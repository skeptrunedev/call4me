**Lincoln Urgent Care in Lincoln, CA is at 77 Lincoln Boulevard, Suite 1, Lincoln, California 95648.** Its [official website](https://www.lincolnurgentcare.com/) lists **(916) 258-2751**, the number this call dialed. On October 7, 2026, the front desk quoted **$180** as the cash pay price for an office visit for an existing patient paying out of pocket.

A call4me customer's assistant made the call. The goal was narrow: ask the self-pay price for a standard sick visit for an uninsured existing patient, without booking anything or giving payment details. It reached a person about 1 minute 7 seconds after the call connected, asked one question, and hung up with the answer.

The recorded greeting also says the clinic is walk-in only: "We do not schedule appointments. Patients are seen on a walk-in basis only." It says the clinic cannot give wait times. The website lists hours of Monday to Saturday 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM and Sunday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and names sister clinics in Granite Bay and Roseville. Those hours come from the site, not the call.

## Asking an urgent care for a cash price

The brief told the assistant to ask only for pricing and to say the patient would call back if questions came up that it could not answer. That kept the call to under two minutes. If you are comparing Lincoln, CA urgent care prices, you can ask each clinic the same short question. The representative's first question was whether the patient had been seen before, so know that answer before you call. Prices for existing and new patients may differ, and this call only got the existing patient price.

The assistant said the patient's full name when only "existing patient" had been asked for. If you want a price without identifying anyone, tell your assistant to answer the question and leave the name out unless staff ask for it.

Ask what the base price includes. Here, staff volunteered the useful split: vitals and sending a prescription are included, while medication cost, x-rays and testing are extra and discussed first.
