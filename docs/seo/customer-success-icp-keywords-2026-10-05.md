# Customer success articles for existing assistant users

October 5, 2026. This followup applies the acceptance criteria in
`docs/seo/icp-plan.md`, including its October 4 targeting audit, to the four
published customer stories. The intended reader uses Claude Code, Codex, Muse,
Instinct, T3 Code or another personal assistant and wants it to complete the
phone step of an ordinary task. The four recordings prove calls and the
reported outcomes. They do not identify or test every customer's client.

Fresh US Google Ahrefs CLI queries checked 110 distinct exact phrases through
six batches, plus five matching ideas reports and available SERPs. Existing assistant intent
comes before volume. Missing difficulty remains unknown. A KD of zero is not
a promise of ranking, particularly on a local or navigational SERP.

### Ohio competition and boundaries

The coverage SERP contains six organic results. Official BWC pages and insurers
are present, but Sheakley's application guide is the third organic result,
DR 37 with zero referring domains, and Plevin and Gallucci's coverage explanation
is the fifth organic result, DR 22 with zero.
These give a more concrete informational opening than KD alone. The query also
contains injury benefit intent, so the article must remain clearly about an
employer applying for coverage.

The article's distinctive ITIN obstacle remains call evidence within that
larger topic. Do not turn the representative's case specific answer into a
general eligibility rule. The application was not submitted and coverage was
not established. The callback mismatch stays disclosed. The task brief should
authorize questions, rather than filing or payment.

### Veterinary competition and boundaries

The how to schedule SERP has nine organic results. A Reddit how to thread is
first. Valley Animal Hospital is DR 2 with one referring domain, German Village
Veterinary Hospital is DR 3 with two, and Newark Veterinary Hospital is DR 5
with zero. Those are mostly local appointment pages; low authority alone does
not remove their local intent advantage. A useful phone script and a real
dropoff example fit the informational part of the query.

The generic `vet appointment` head term remains broader and predominantly local.
Do not trade the precise dropoff query for its larger volume. No completed exam,
clinical urgency assessment, verified street address or exam price was obtained
on this call. The suggested assistant workflow should explicitly ask for those
details and report what remains unanswered, without giving clinical advice.

### Salon competition and boundaries

The salon appointment SERP has eight organic results and concerns booking.
Scott J is DR 33 with zero referring domains; Elite Salon is DR 2 with one;
salon718 is DR 31 with six. Local pages, Booksy and larger brands are also
present. Treat it as a supporting task phrase, rather than evidence that this
national case study will outrank local booking pages everywhere.

`salon cancellation policy` has 100 searches and KD 1, but its SERP mostly
answers salon owners writing policy templates. Do not promote it as an
additional acquisition target for this customer booking story. The salon's
same day cancellation answer can remain a useful confirmed detail.

The assistant confirmed Friday at 2 PM for $60 with the preferred stylist,
but missed the earlier Thursday option. The later call did not confirm a
change. Keep that distinction in the suggested date comparison workflow.

### Oil change competition and boundaries

The cost SERP has nine organic results, including official Les Schwab pages,
Reddit, Facebook, Yelp questions and TractorByNet. The latter has DR 54 and
zero referring domains; the Reddit result has one. A dated real store quote
and a clear explanation of what it includes answer the informational question.

The $100 to $110 estimate applies to one vehicle and a combined oil change and
rotation. It does not establish national pricing, a standalone rotation charge,
a service reservation or completed maintenance. Asking an existing assistant to
get an oil change quote is a suitable task brief; describing an inbound auto
dealer scheduling product is a different audience.

The standalone rotation cost SERP has nine organic results and is mostly
official Les Schwab service or FAQ pages. An independent cost article is
DR 37, with its referring domain count unavailable. Treat the 70 search rotation
term as conditional supporting context: the combined call quote cannot answer
standalone pricing or complimentary service eligibility. Address those only
with current official policy or an explicit unanswered question in the brief.

## Avoid overlap with the existing client guides

Keep one page per setup intent. The task stories link the existing Claude Code
and Codex phone calling guides and can mention Muse, Instinct or T3 as supported
reader contexts where their existing guides support that claim. They should
not repeat installation instructions or retarget `codex mcp`, Claude server
lists, client phone calling setup, or generic calling MCP comparisons.

For every suggested workflow, distinguish the connection guide from the
historical call: linking a Claude Code guide does not establish that this
customer used Claude Code. Returning a call result does not establish a
calendar update, application submission or completed service. Qualified
activation remains the outcome to measure; broader task impressions are not
proof of ICP fit.
