**Ugly Baby is at 364 Grand Street, Brooklyn, NY 11211, and its phone number is (347) 599 1750.** The restaurant's [official website](https://www.uglybabynyc.com) lists that number with the address and says reservations are taken through Resy. The call below went to the same number, and a staff member answered and took a party of 12 by phone.

The site lists dinner hours of Tuesday to Thursday 5 PM to 10 PM, Friday and Saturday 5 PM to 10:30 PM, and Sunday 5 PM to 9:30 PM. It lists no Monday hours. Local press reported that the original Carroll Gardens restaurant on Smith Street closed in 2024, and the official site now lists only the Williamsburg address on Grand Street.

On October 14, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called to ask about a birthday dinner for 12 on Tuesday, October 20, any time after 5 PM. The staff member checked, said after 6:30, then offered **8 PM**. The assistant accepted, gave a callback number and the reservation name, and the staff member repeated "eight for twelve people" on October 20.

The assistant then asked whether a deposit or credit card hold was required. The staff member said they had to check with the manager and stepped away. The recording ends there, before any answer.

## Booking a large party at Ugly Baby NYC

Resy is the restaurant's listed way to reserve, but a table for 12 is the kind of booking worth a phone call, since the time and any group terms come from a person. On this call the staff member did not have the first requested window. A flexible range ("any time after 5") let them land on 8 PM without a back and forth.

Two things would have made this call cleaner. First, ask the money question before giving the name and number, so you never end up holding a table with unknown deposit terms. Second, spell the reservation name when staff repeat it wrong. Here the staff member apologized for a misheard name and the conversation moved on.
