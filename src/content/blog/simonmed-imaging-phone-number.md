**The short answer:** for a heart scan, the SimonMed scheduling phone number to call is **1-844-615-2957**. SimonMed's [coronary CT angiogram page](https://simonmed.com/patients/services/ccta/) lists it under "Call to schedule" and says "For pricing information, please give us a call at 1-844-615-2957." A SimonMed representative called it "our direct number to the cardiology department." The general SimonMed Imaging number is **1-866-614-8555**, shown at the top of every page on [simonmed.com](https://simonmed.com/). The [Van Ness location page](https://simonmed.com/locations/ca/van-ness/) lists **1005 Van Ness Ave., San Francisco, CA 94109**, phone **(415) 229-7825**, fax (415) 229-7826, open Monday to Friday 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Nobody dialed the Van Ness local number on these calls.

**How fast a person answered:**

- **1-844-615-2957:** a short "please hold" message, then a person in **13 to 21 seconds** on all four calls. No menu.
- **1-866-614-8555:** a ten-option phone menu, two submenus and a hold, then a person in **1 minute 44 seconds and 1 minute 49 seconds** on the two calls.

Between October 13 and 15, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant used [call4me](/) to ask about, book and confirm a self-pay **coronary CT angiogram (CCTA) with Cleerly plaque analysis** at SimonMed Van Ness. What SimonMed staff said:

- **Self-pay price at Van Ness: $1,855,** "everything is included in that price": the scan, the Cleerly analysis, the radiologist fee and contrast. Two later calls repeated $1,855, paid at the visit.
- **You need a doctor's order before you can book as a new patient.** "Once the order comes in, they automatically create an account for him and then I can schedule." An out-of-state doctor's order sent by fax was fine.
- **No pre-scan consult.** "No, there's no pre-scan needed."
- **The CPT code staff read out was 75574.** The diagnosis comes from your doctor's order.
- **Prep:** a heart rate medication from your own doctor (staff said metoprolol, "his heart rate has to be 60 or under in order to get a clear read"), no food for 4 hours, plenty of water, and arrive 30 minutes early. **The caffeine rule conflicted:** two representatives said 6 hours, one said 12.
- **No confirmation number.** To check on a booking, "you'll just give the patient's date of birth and last name."

## What these calls didn't establish

- **The scan hasn't happened.** The appointment is booked for December 1, 2026. Nothing here shows the exam, the Cleerly report or the final bill.
- **Prices are as quoted on October 13 to 15, 2026, for Van Ness, self-pay.** No other location was priced, and no insurance pricing was asked about.
- **The "Cleerly" name came from the caller.** Staff said "the Cleerly exam" back and the booking was read as "CCTA with Cleerly", but SimonMed's [CCTA with AI Plaque Analysis page](https://simonmed.com/patients/services/ccta-with-ai-plaque-analysis/) only says "our advanced AI Plaque Analysis software" and doesn't name a vendor.
- **The caffeine rule is unresolved.** 6 hours on two calls, 12 hours on another. Ask your doctor or SimonMed which applies, or go with the longer one.
- **The medication and dose are your doctor's call.** Staff described what the doctor would provide. This isn't medical advice.
- **The fax numbers were spoken by staff, not checked against simonmed.com.** None of them is the Van Ness fax on the location page. Staff gave four different numbers across calls; the order did arrive by the October 15 call.
- **The screening questions weren't finished.** Prior procedures and kidney history were left for the patient to answer at check-in.
- **Phone menu key presses aren't in the recordings,** so the exact digits on the 1-866 line are unknown (see below).

Three other calls from the same request aren't published. One went to a different hospital. One was cut off right after the identity check. One only entered screening details, and call 6 corrected one of them.

## What to have ready

- **Your doctor's order,** and your doctor's fax machine. As a new patient you can't be scheduled until it arrives.
- **The patient's full legal name as it is on the order, and date of birth.** Every call started there. A first name given differently on one call had to be corrected on the next two.
- **Mailing address, phone number and email.** Staff verified them on most calls.
- **Insurance or self-pay.** Every booking call asked.
- **Your doctor's name and office address.** The booking call asked for both, plus a diagnostic code.
- **Height and weight, prior procedures and kidney history** for the screening questions, or plan to answer them at check-in.

## What worked, and what to watch

- **Ask for the cardiology line.** The representative on call 3 offered it unprompted when asked how to check on the order. All four calls to it reached a person in under half a minute.
- **Say the exam name in full, and have them read it back.** The booking was first entered as a different cardiac CT exam. It only came to light when the price was rechecked on call 3, and the representative changed it to the plaque analysis exam.
- **Expect a new fax number on every call, and check the order arrived.** The fax sent around noon wasn't on the chart when call 3 checked shortly after, and the next morning it was. Asking "is the order attached to the appointment?" got a clear yes.
- **Ask prep questions twice.** Arrival was "fifteen minutes... No, actually, no. Thirty minutes" on call 5, and caffeine was 6 or 12 hours depending on who answered.
- **Plan to finish screening at check-in.** SimonMed wouldn't email the questions.
