The highest quote was about $286 more than the lowest, for the same four tires on the same car. That's from one call4me user, whose AI phone assistant called tire shops for one set of tires for a 2024 Toyota Corolla LE in two rounds: October 13 and 14, which we covered in [our Town Fair Tire post](/blog/town-fair-tire-manchester-ct), and October 21, which is new here.

If you're searching "winter tires near me" and planning to call around, the calls show why it's worth it: name the exact tire and size, ask for the price out the door, and check what each shop says that covers.

## Town Fair's price match: three calls, never tested

In round one, Town Fair's representative pitched the price guarantee before quoting: "we will beat any local competitor prices, including Pep Boys, Walmart, Firestone." Firestone had quoted about $655 out the door the same day, about $199 under Town Fair's $853.98.

The assistant called Town Fair back three times and never reached a person:

- **Saturday, October 17, 5:03 pm**, to the number from round one: "We're currently closed." The recording gave Saturday hours ending at 4.
- **Wednesday, October 21, 7:42 am**, to Town Fair's Manchester store: "We're currently closed. We open at 8 AM Monday through Saturday. Please call us again in the morning."
- **7:58 am**, the same store: the hold message, which made the same promise ("We will beat any local competitor, and we'll also match online tire prices. That's why we say, nobody beats Town Fair Tire. Nobody."), and then voicemail. Our caller left a message asking for a call back about a price match.

So we can't tell you whether Town Fair would have beaten Firestone's quote. Its [price match page](https://www.townfairtire.com/tire-price-match/) describes an "After Purchase Price Protection program" that "allows you to save money up to 30 days after the purchase of your new tires," with money back if you find "the same tires somewhere else for a cheaper price," and says, "We will always beat the competition's price!" The page also has a "Found A Lower Price? WE WILL BEAT IT!" box with a "Submit Lower Price" button. Those are the site's words, not something a Town Fair employee confirmed on these calls.

## The $80 Bridgestone rebate: four shops said it applied

Our caller asked most of the shops about "the eighty dollar October Bridgestone rebate." Firestone said "Yes." Pep Boys said "it should definitely apply." VIP said "it does apply." At Town Fair, the representative brought up the rebate first, said it covered the Blizzak line, and said it ran from 10/1 to 10/31. Costco, in round one, said its Bridgestone promotion should come "probably like late October."

Nobody read out Bridgestone's own terms, so treat a shop's yes as a starting point. October is over, so whatever rebate is running when you buy, ask about it by name and read the manufacturer's fine print before you count on it.

## Winter tires near me: how to price them before holiday travel

Everything here comes from what these shops said on these calls.

1. **Name the exact tire and size, every time.** Asked for "snow tires" in round one, VIP quoted a Cooper at $684 plus tax. Asked for the Blizzak WS90 by name eight days later, it quoted $940.86. You can only compare quotes for the same tire.
2. **Ask for out the door, then listen to what comes back.** Pep Boys answered "tax, labor and everything." Town Fair listed every fee down to a $2.39 supply fee. Costco gave its price before tax, VIP said "Four tires installed. Mount and balance," and Firestone gave a round "about 655." If the answer doesn't say tax, ask.
3. **Ask if the tire is in stock, and if not, when it would come in.** On October 21, Pep Boys and VIP would both have had to order the WS90, and two other shops couldn't find it in this size at all. If you want snow tires on before a holiday drive, ask early.
4. **Call when they're open, especially for a price match.** All three calls back to Town Fair landed outside its hours: once after its Saturday closing, twice before its 8 am opening. The price match never got tested.
5. **Have the other quote ready.** Town Fair's pitch named Pep Boys, Walmart and Firestone, and its site has a form for a lower price. Give the shop name and the out-the-door total.
6. **Ask about the rebate by name, then read the terms.** Four shops said the Bridgestone rebate applied, but nobody read Bridgestone's terms on the phone.
7. **Ask about storage.** Neither Pep Boys nor VIP stores tires off season. Pep Boys offered to bag your old tires for you to take home. In round one, Town Fair's pitch included "free snow changeovers."
8. **Check the shop sells what you need.** Hoosier Tire East only sells racing tires.

## Where our caller fell short

- **It called Town Fair outside its hours, three times.** The October 21 tries were at 7:42 and 7:58 am, and the first of them got a recording that the store opens at 8.
- **It said the size wrong twice.** At Hoosier Tire East and in the first Tire Guys voicemail, it said "two o five fifty R sixteen," which is 205/50R16, not 205/55R16. Hoosier only sells racing tires, so that call didn't hinge on it, but the voicemail asked about the wrong size.
- **It stayed on voicemail too long.** After its first Tire Guys message, it stayed on the line in silence until the voicemail system said, "You have reached the maximum time permitted for recording your message," three minutes after the message started. On the second call it waited through the whole greeting again, and the call ran about six minutes with no one on the line.
- **It didn't ask VIP about tax.** The $940.86 quote was for "Four tires installed. Mount and balance," and our caller never asked whether that included tax.
- **It can't text.** At the shop where the person was away, they asked for a text. Our caller said "I don't have texting" and gave a callback number instead.
