**The short answer:** in mid-November 2026 the iPhone Duo was sold out at almost every store we heard from. On Saturday, November 14, a call4me user's AI phone assistant called an Apple Store in San Francisco at 10:05 am. The sales specialist checked and said, "it's showing me that it's out of stock," then checked the rest of the state: "No, look like the whole California is out of stock." A restock date? "We don't have like a specific date. It's at random right now." The one yes came at 10:19 am from a Best Buy in San Francisco: "for the iPhone Duo, they do have it at the store."

The user's ask, as the assistant put it to a second San Francisco Apple Store: any iPhone Duo, "any color, any storage, unlocked or carrier," that someone could walk in and buy that day.

What it took:

1. **Seven calls just after midnight.** Every Apple, Best Buy, Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile line answered with a recording or an AI assistant, and none of them checked stock.
2. **An Apple Store in San Francisco at 10:05 am:** out of stock there and, the specialist said, in all of California, with no restock date.
3. **Three more Apple Stores** (a second one in San Francisco, one in Emeryville and one in Sacramento) reached a specialist, but every call ended before a stock answer. Most of that was our fault, more below.
4. **A Best Buy in San Francisco:** after three requests for a SKU number and three and a half minutes on hold, a Best Buy Virtual Sales agent said the store had it.

Fourteen of these calls were made by one call4me user in the Bay Area. Two more come from two other call4me users: one call to Apple's sales line the night before the iPhone Duo went on sale, and one to an Apple Store in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, about one exact configuration. Our caller didn't buy anything or give a card. The user's name, the store staff's names and the location details in the stores' greetings are muted in the audio and shown in brackets in the transcripts. Stock and hours are what each store said that day, and they can change any day, especially around Black Friday.

## Apple: sold out in California, restocks at random

At 10:05 am, five minutes after the first San Francisco Apple Store opened, our caller got through Apple's phone system to a sales specialist in 1 minute 19 seconds. The specialist asked two questions ("is this for business or personal" and "how long have you been looking for the Duo"), then gave the answer: "it's showing me that it's out of stock. We don't have none in the store right now." Asked about other Bay Area and Northern California stores, the specialist checked for about 25 seconds and came back with "No, look like the whole California is out of stock." On a shipment that week: "We don't have like a specific date. It's at random right now."

Best Buy's line answers with an AI customer care assistant that asks for a product number first: "It's quickest if you have the item's seven or eight-digit SKU number. It also warned, "the agents don't have more product availability info than what's online."

At 5 minutes 36 seconds a Best Buy Virtual Sales agent picked up, checked for about 30 seconds, and said, "Okay, for the iPhone Duo, they do have it at the store." The recording catches the start of the next sentence, "However, only...", and then our caller's side of the call dropped. We never heard the rest.

## Where to buy an iPhone Duo: Apple, Best Buy and carrier stores

Apple's own [iPhone Duo page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/) says "iPhone Duo is available on apple.com and at select carriers and partner stores." Its [buy page](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-duo) lists the unlocked phone at $1,999 for 256GB, $2,199 for 512GB, $2,599 for 1TB and $3,199 for 2TB, in Night Sky and Star White. Apple's [September announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/) set pre-orders for Friday, October 16, "with availability beginning Friday, October 23." That's what Apple publishes. Here's what its stores and the carriers said on the phone:

- **Apple Stores** at 12:24 am all said, "The Apple Store is currently closed." Each one read its hours (all three open at 10 am on Saturdays), offered help with "product information or product availability," and then ended the call with "Unfortunately, we are currently closed."
- **Verizon:** "Thank you for calling Verizon. Our store is currently closed," then Verizon's AI assistant: "Verizon stores do not publish real-time local inventory, and iPhone Duo availability varies by location."
- **AT&T:** a closed-store menu with the store's hours, then AT&T's main line: "what's the account number, or phone number on the account."
- **T-Mobile:** the store's menu does have a "check inventory" option. At 12:27 am it said, "The store is not open yet. Please call back after 10 AM today."

Our caller didn't call the carrier stores back during the day, so we don't know what they had.

## Where our caller fell short

- **It dropped five calls in the middle of the conversation.** At the second San Francisco Apple Store, the Emeryville and Sacramento (10:11 am) stores and both Best Buy calls, our call records show our caller's side closed the connection while the store was still talking. The worst one was Best Buy at 10:19 am, on "However, only...". That's a bug on our side, not the stores'.
- **It started at midnight.** Seven calls between 12:24 and 12:27 am reached only recordings and AI assistants. None checked stock, and several of the lines said the store was closed.
- **It answered every sales question at the second San Francisco Apple Store** instead of asking the specialist to check stock first, and got no answer in five minutes.
- **It didn't have a Best Buy SKU.** Both Best Buy calls spent 1 and a half to 2 minutes on "What's the SKU number?" before a 2 and a half to 3 and a half minute hold for an agent.
- **The 11:14 am Sacramento call ran out of time** while the specialist was still looking up the Star White 256GB.

## How to find an iPhone Duo in stock before Black Friday or Christmas

Everything here comes from what these calls showed.

1. **Call after the store opens.** Before opening, Apple, Best Buy, Verizon, AT&T and T-Mobile all answered with recordings or AI assistants. The three San Francisco Apple Stores' recordings all gave a 10 am Saturday opening, and T-Mobile said to call back after 10. Holiday hours can differ, so check the store's page first.
2. **At Apple, two answers get you to a specialist.** On every Northern California call, the Apple Store's line asked "home or business use?" and "Are you calling about financing with your current wireless carrier?" Saying home, and no, got a sales specialist on the line between 1 minute 19 seconds and 2 minutes 31 seconds into the call, all five times during store hours. In Pennsylvania it took 2 minutes 42 seconds.
3. **Name the store, then ask about nearby ones.** The specialist may be answering for many stores. The Sacramento store's specialist asked which location to check, and the first San Francisco store's checked the whole state in about 25 seconds.
4. **Ask for the stock check before anything else.** Apple's specialists start with sales questions. Say what you want up front ("any iPhone Duo, any color or storage, no trade-in, no financing, no accessories") and ask them to check stock first.
5. **At Best Buy, have the SKU ready.** Find the iPhone Duo on bestbuy.com and copy the 7 or 8 digit SKU from the product page. Without it, the AI assistant couldn't match "iPhone Duo" to a product, and its own hold message says bestbuy.com "has the most current product availability information."
6. **When someone says yes, get it all in that call.** Storage, color, unlocked or carrier, price, how many, and whether they'll hold it. The Best Buy agent's next words after "they do have it at the store" were "However, only...", and the callback an hour later started from zero.
7. **Restocks are random, so check pickup often.** "It's at random right now," the San Francisco specialist said. If Apple's site or app shows pickup at a store, reserving it there beats calling: just reaching a specialist took one to three minutes, and the 11:14 am Sacramento call ran out of time while the specialist checked.

## What to say when you call

> Hi, could you check whether [store] has any iPhone Duo in stock to buy today? Any color or storage is fine. It's for personal use, no trade-in and no financing. If you don't have one, could you check the nearest other stores?

If they have one: "Which storage and color, is it unlocked or on a carrier, what's the price, how many do you have, and could you hold it under [name] until [time]?"
