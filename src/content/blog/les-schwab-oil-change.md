**Yes, Les Schwab does oil changes at select locations.** Check your particular store before going. The company's [official oil change page](https://www.lesschwab.com/more/oil-changes) says availability is limited to select locations and that prices may vary. It lists tire rotation among the services included with its full service oil change.

On October 3, 2026, a call4me assistant phoned the [McKinleyville store](https://www.lesschwab.com/stores/ca/mckinleyville/2210-central-ave) for a customer who wanted an oil change and tire rotation for a 2009 Toyota Tacoma. The representative estimated **$100 to $110 total** and accepted a plan to drop the truck off around **9 AM Monday** as a walk in.

**That was a price estimate and a walk in plan.** Monday's reserved appointments were already full. The call did not secure a reserved appointment or prove the work was later completed.

## How much does a Les Schwab oil change cost?

This call produced a **$100 to $110 estimate for an oil change and tire rotation together**, for one truck at one location. It did not establish a standalone tire rotation price, specify an oil type or give a fixed national price.

To get a quote that fits your vehicle, provide the year, make and model. Ask which oil and service package the quote covers, whether the rotation is included, and whether taxes or other charges are included. The written service page lists different oil change packages, so make sure the quote is for the work you want. [Check the current packages with Les Schwab.](https://www.lesschwab.com/more/oil-changes)

Keep the estimate dated. A historical phone quote is useful evidence of what the store said, but a later visit needs its own price confirmation.

## Can you walk in when appointments are full?

At this store, the representative said yes. The Monday schedule was full, but walk ins were accepted and a morning dropoff was an option. The representative estimated **at least one to two hours**, depending on the queue. That was an estimate of the wait on the call, rather than a guaranteed completion time.

The customer could leave the truck and receive a call when it was ready. Dropping off a vehicle around 9 AM did not reserve a service bay for 9 AM.

The representative estimated opening time as around 7 or 7:30 AM. The [store's published weekday hours](https://www.lesschwab.com/stores/ca/mckinleyville/2210-central-ave) currently list 7 AM. Check the current store listing before planning an early arrival.

## What to ask before an oil change and tire rotation

1. **Does this location do oil changes?** Availability at one Les Schwab does not establish availability at another.
2. **Can you service this vehicle?** Give the year, make and model, plus the service you want.
3. **What does the estimate include?** Ask about the oil type, filter, rotation, taxes and other charges.
4. **Is this a reserved appointment or a walk in?** Get the distinction clearly before arranging your day.
5. **Can I leave the vehicle?** Confirm when to arrive, the likely queue and how the store will contact you.
6. **What needs approval before extra work?** Give a clear instruction to call you before work beyond the agreed service.

## A call script for a store quote

> Hi, does this location do oil changes? I have a [year, make and model] and need an oil change and tire rotation. What would the total estimate be, and what oil and services does it include? Do you have appointments on [day], or could I leave the vehicle as a walk in?

If the schedule is full:

> Would a dropoff around [time] work? What wait should I expect, and can you call me when it's ready? Please confirm whether that is a walk in or a reserved appointment.
