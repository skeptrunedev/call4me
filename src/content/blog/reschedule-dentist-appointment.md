## How to reschedule a dentist appointment, step by step

1. **Call during office hours.** Front desks handle scheduling; you rarely need anyone else.
2. **Say what you're moving first:** "I'm calling to move [patient]'s Thursday 3:30 appointment."
3. **Offer windows, not a single time.** "Friday after 3, or any time Monday" let the office find a slot in one pass.
4. **Spell the name if asked.** The first question back was the patient's name.
5. **Decide what matters more: the day, or the dentist.** On this call Friday meant a different provider; staying with the same dentist meant Monday. Know your answer before you call.
6. **Repeat the new time back** so both sides agree: "Can we move it to Monday, October 5th at noon?"
7. **Wait for the confirmation.** The office said it had rescheduled and confirmed it, which is what you want to hear before hanging up.

## What to say: a script that worked

> Hi, I'm calling to see if we can move [patient name]'s [day] appointment at [time] to [first choice] or [second choice]. [Patient name] would prefer to keep the same dentist if possible.

If they offer a different provider: "Let's look at [other day] instead, preferably with the same provider." When they offer a time: "Yes, that works. Can we move it to [day, date] at [time]?"

## Cancelling instead of rescheduling

Same call, different ask: give the name and the appointment, and say you'd like to cancel. Two questions worth asking while you have them on the phone:

- **Is there a cancellation fee or a notice period?** Policies vary by office, so ask rather than assume.
- **Can you put me on a cancellation list** if you'd rather move the appointment than drop it?
