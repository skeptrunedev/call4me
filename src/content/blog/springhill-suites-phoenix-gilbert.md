**SpringHill Suites by Marriott Phoenix Gilbert is at 1801 South San Tan Village Parkway, Gilbert, Arizona 85295, and its phone number is +1 480-996-0800.** That is the number Marriott lists on the hotel's [official Marriott page](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/phxgh-springhill-suites-phoenix-gilbert/overview/), and it is the number this call dialed. The hotel's own menu answers first: **press 1** for Marriott central reservations, **press 2 or stay on the line** for the front desk, **press 3** for the director of sales.

On October 16, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called the SpringHill Suites Gilbert front desk for a guest who stays there often. He wanted three nights, Monday October 19 through Wednesday October 21, checking out Thursday the 22nd. ## When SpringHill Suites Gilbert shows sold out online

On this call, the front desk confirmed what Marriott.com showed. It had nothing for Tuesday and offered a waitlist place and a manager callback instead. If you call about a sold out night yourself:

- **Ask for the front desk** (press 2). That's where this call got the waitlist offer.
- **Give the guest's last name and Bonvoy number.** That's what the desk took for the waitlist.
- **Give the callback number you actually want.** The desk will offer to use the number you're calling from. Say so if that isn't the right one.
- **Ask about the other nights separately.** This call only waitlisted Tuesday and didn't check whether Monday and Wednesday could be booked on their own.
- **Ask for the rate**, and whether a waitlisted night would come at that rate if a room opens up. This call didn't get either.

For group blocks or a corporate rate, the menu's press 3 reaches the director of sales directly.
