## Getting a snow tire quote you can compare

The useful quotes here had three things in common: the exact tire model, the exact size, and an explicit ask for the out-the-door price with mounting, balancing and fees. When the assistant asked for "snow tires" without a model, Tires International picked a different tire, which can't be compared line for line with the Blizzak quotes.

Even with the same question, shops answered differently. Town Fair itemized and included tax. Costco gave pre-tax prices. Firestone gave a rounded figure. If you're comparing, ask each shop the same three things: the total with tax, what fees it includes, and whether the tire is in stock.

For the rebate, the shop's answer is a starting point. Read the manufacturer's terms yourself before you buy, and ask the shop to put the tires and installation on one invoice if the rebate needs that.

If Town Fair Tire is where you want to buy, its price-beating pitch is worth using. Have the other written quotes ready and ask them to beat one.
