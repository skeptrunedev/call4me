**The Best Buy customer service number is 1 888 237 8289.** Best Buy lists it on its [official contact page](https://www.bestbuy.com/contact-us) and [support page](https://www.bestbuy.com/support). On our two recorded calls, an AI customer care assistant answered first. Explaining that we had a return question and asking for a live agent led to a transfer both times.

We called on **October 24, 2026**, at about **5:24 and 5:31 pm Central**, using [call4me](/). A person answered **4 minutes 19 seconds** into the first recording and **3 minutes 15 seconds** into the second. Those are the total times to a person, including the automated conversation and hold, not average wait times.

These were general inquiries for our [Best Buy return policy guide](/blog/best-buy-return-policy). We did not have an order or account to change. We checked Best Buy's official contact pages again on **November 2**, but did not place another call for this article.

## How to speak to a person at Best Buy

Here is the route that worked for our returns inquiries:

1. **Call 1 888 237 8289 and explain the issue.** The greeting introduced an AI customer care assistant and asked what it could help with.
2. **Ask for a live agent.** On the first call, the assistant asked whether we wanted general information or help with a particular purchase. We said it was a general question for a guide. For your own order, describe the actual issue instead.
3. **Answer its routing question.** On the second call, it asked whether the issue involved changing an order, a return or something else. We answered that it was about a return.
4. **If you still need a person, say so.** On the second call, the assistant offered information and a text link. We declined the text and repeated the request for a live agent. On the first call, it offered a transfer after it could not establish who could approve a late return exception.
5. **Stay on the line after the transfer.** A recorded notice played, followed by hold audio. A person then answered.

We did not press a keypad shortcut. Our calls establish this voice route for return questions on the date tested. They do not establish the route for Geek Squad repairs, delivery changes, credit cards or every other department.

## What should you have ready?

For a general question, tell the assistant that it is general. Our callers reached people without supplying an order number or inventing a purchase.

For your own purchase, gather the order or receipt and the question you need resolved. One representative said a purchase lookup used the customer's phone number, first and last name, and email. We did not test a real lookup, so the calls do not establish that those details always suffice or that every purchase can be found.

The AI assistant on the second call said it could not see an account associated with the calling number. We did not test calling from a number linked to a Best Buy account, and cannot say whether that changes the route or the wait.

Keep the request specific: identify the purchase or service, explain what happened and say what answer you need. If you are asking about an exception, ask who can approve it and whether you need to visit a store. Our [return policy guide](/blog/best-buy-return-policy) separates what the representatives said from Best Buy's published rules.

## Phone hours and other contact options

Best Buy's [contact page](https://www.bestbuy.com/contact-us) offers routes for orders and purchases, accounts, membership, product repair and support, stores, and other questions. It also offers chat. The [support page](https://www.bestbuy.com/support) has a help topic search and lists the same phone number.

Neither of those retrieved pages stated phone hours when we checked on November 2. Our two calls reached staff on a Saturday evening in October. That does not establish round the clock availability, holiday hours or the hours of another department. We did not test the chat service.
