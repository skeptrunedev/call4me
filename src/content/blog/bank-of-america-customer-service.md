**The short answer:** the Bank of America customer service number is **800.432.1000** (1-800-432-1000). Bank of America's [checking and savings contact page](https://www.bankofamerica.com/customer-service/contact-us/checking-savings/) lists it for general account information, reporting a lost or stolen ATM or debit card, and opening an account. It's staffed **Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 11 p.m. ET** and **Saturday and Sunday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. ET**. The Spanish line is 800.688.6086, and from outside the US you can call 1.315.724.4022 collect. The page also says "Your card or account number will be required."

We didn't have one, and this got us to a person anyway:

1. At the prompt for your telephone access ID, card, or account number, **say "agent"**. It refused twice with a security message. The third time it moved on.
2. When it asks you to "tell me what you're calling about", **say "open a new checking account"**.
3. **Say "no"** when it asks about a business account.
4. When it asks for your account number, **say you don't have one**, then confirm "that's right" when it asks "You said you don't know it?"

It said "Please hold while I connect you", and a representative in Scranton, Pennsylvania answered **2 minutes 5 seconds** after the call connected. She told us:

- **The fastest way to a person is the app.** In the Bank of America mobile app, use **Erica** and type **"connect me to an agent"**. She said that "pushes them more towards the front of the line."
- **Hours depend on the department.** "Some departments are 24-7, like fraud and certain servicing departments."
- **Callbacks: maybe.** She thought "there is some sort of callback option for some of the departments", but said she wasn't sure.
- **Have whatever relates to your question ready.** "Any pertinent information in regards to what they're discussing."
- **Not a customer yet? You can open a checking account online or by phone,** "whatever is easier for them."
- **A replacement debit card takes "about four to six business days."**

We called on Friday, October 16, 2026, at about 3:35 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Bank of America phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 800.432.1000.** "Welcome to Bank of America. This call may be recorded."
2. **"To get started, please say or enter your telephone access ID, card, or account number."** Customers enter their number here. Without one, say "agent".
3. **"For security, you must provide required information to speak with an associate or use the automated service."** It played this twice for us, after our first and second "agent". After the third, it moved on.
4. **"In a few words, please tell me what you're calling about."** Say "open a new checking account".
5. **"Okay. A new checking account. Would you like to open a business account today?"** Say no.
6. **"Please say or enter your full account number."** Say you don't have one.
7. **"You said you don't know it. Is that right?"** Say yes, that's right.
8. **"Please hold while I connect you."** Then: "All of our associates are currently assisting other customers. Please stay on the line. Your call will be answered in the order that it was received." Our hold, from "please hold" to a person, was about 30 seconds.
9. **A representative picks up** and asks for your first and last name.

## What to have ready

- **Your card or account number,** if you have one. Bank of America's contact page says it "will be required", and it's the first thing the phone menu asks for.
- **Anything related to what you're calling about.** That's what the representative told us to have handy.
- **The Bank of America app,** if you'd rather skip the phone menu. The representative's tip was to ask Erica to "connect me to an agent".

## Things that don't work

On October 16, 2026, we called this number twice. On our first call:

- **Saying "agent" at the account number prompt** got the security message, then the "what you're calling about" prompt.
- **Saying "I'm not a customer. I just have a general question"** at that prompt got "I will be unable to assist you until your account is securely verified. Please enter your account number."
- **Saying "speak to a person"** and saying we didn't have an account number didn't get past it. It offered to wait while we looked for the number, asked for it again, and then ended the call: "For the security of your account, I am unable to complete your request until you are able to provide the required information. Please try your call again later when you have this information available."

That call lasted about 4 minutes. Saying "open a new checking account" instead of "general question" is what got us to a person on the second call.
