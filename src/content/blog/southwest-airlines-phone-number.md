**The short answer:** the Southwest Airlines phone number is **1-800-435-9792** (1-800-I-FLY-SWA). An **automated voice menu** answers and asks what you're calling about. We said **"Representative"** at the first prompt, heard "All our representatives are assisting other callers at this time," and **a live reservations agent answered 4 minutes 10 seconds after Southwest picked up.** But she **wouldn't answer general questions**: "I have to answer phone lines for customers needing assistance, so I can't answer general questions." So call this number when you have a booking or a trip to sort out. For general policy questions (refund timing, accessibility, Rapid Rewards), Southwest's written pages below are the faster source, and Rapid Rewards has its own line, **1-800-445-5764**. On a later call with a real booking, a call4me customer's assistant said **"existing reservation"**, reached an agent in **46 seconds**, and the agent [added a Known Traveler Number to the reservation](#how-to-add-a-known-traveler-number-to-southwest-by-phone) over the phone.

The number is listed as **"General Questions: 1-800-I-FLY-SWA (1-800-435-9792)"** on Southwest's [More phone numbers and contact options](https://support.southwest.com/helpcenter/article/More-phone-numbers-contact-options) help article, which we loaded on October 13, 2026. Southwest's [Customer Service Plan](https://www.southwest.com/swa-resources/pdfs/corporate-commitments/customer-service-plan.pdf) (effective June 4, 2026) gives the same number for "speaking with a Customer Representative." The old southwest.com/contact-us address now redirects to the [Southwest Help Center](https://support.southwest.com/helpcenter/s/), which doesn't show the number on its front page.

Here's what happened on our call:

1. Call 1-800-435-9792. A recording notice, then "Para continuar en español, oprima el número ocho" (press 8 for Spanish).
2. "In a few words, tell me what you're calling about. You can say Rapid Rewards, a new reservation, an existing reservation, flight status, bags, check-in, or credit card." **We said "Representative."**
3. A fare message, then "All our representatives are assisting other callers at this time. Please remain on the line for the next available representative."
4. **Hold music for about 3 minutes 20 seconds.**
5. A reservations agent answered at **4:10**.

**What the agent told us:** that this is a reservations department and she couldn't take general questions. None of our four questions (live-agent hours, callbacks, refund timing, what to have ready) was answered on the phone. This was a general question for this guide, not about any reservation or account, and we gave no booking or personal details. This page is what we heard, plus what Southwest's own website says.

## The Southwest phone menu, step by step

It's a speech menu. The only keypad option we heard was 8 for Spanish.

1. **Call 1-800-435-9792.** "Hi, thank you for calling Southwest Airlines. Your call will be recorded and transcribed for training and quality purposes, and any data collected will be used in accordance with our privacy policy, which can be found on southwest.com."
2. **Spanish:** "Para continuar en español, oprima el número ocho."
3. **"In a few words, tell me what you're calling about. You can say Rapid Rewards, a new reservation, an existing reservation, flight status, bags, check-in, or credit card."** We said "Representative." It didn't ask a follow-up question.
4. **"To receive the lowest fare, please specifically request it from a representative or shop online."**
5. **"All our representatives are assisting other callers at this time. Please remain on the line for the next available representative."** Then hold music until a person answered.

Saying "representative" sent us straight to the queue. On a customer's later call, saying "existing reservation" played the same fare message and then reached an agent at 0:46, with no hold. One call each can't tell whether that was the menu choice or a quieter evening queue, and we don't know whether "Rapid Rewards" reaches a different team.

## What the agent said, and what wasn't answered

The agent's words are hers, as recorded. She didn't give any policy information.

- **"Okay, this is a reservation department, I can't do a survey."** Our caller had opened by saying it was putting together a consumer guide. One transcription hears "reservations department."
- After our caller asked about the line's hours, she asked who was calling, and who the person it was calling for was.
- **"All right, ma'am, I have to answer phone lines for customers needing assistance, so I can't answer general questions. That's not referring to a call. I mean, a reservation."**
- When our caller tried once more, she asked if there was anything else she could help with, and the call ended politely.

Not answered on the phone:

- **What hours the line has live agents, and in what time zone.** Neither Southwest page we loaded lists hours for this number.
- **Whether there's a callback option.** None was offered during our hold, and the pages we loaded don't mention one.
- **How long a refund takes.** Southwest answers this in writing ([below](#southwest-refund-timing-from-southwestcom)).
- **What to have ready.** Not asked before the call ended.

## How to add a Known Traveler Number to Southwest by phone

On Wednesday evening, October 14, 2026 (Pacific time), a call4me customer's assistant called the same number, 1-800-435-9792, to add a Known Traveler Number (KTN) to an existing Southwest reservation the night before the flight, so TSA PreCheck would show for one traveler. The account owner approved publishing the recording. This time the menu was quick: our caller said **"Existing reservation"** at 0:33, heard the same fare message, and **an agent answered at 0:46**, with no hold music.

She asked for the confirmation number, checked that the booking was for a party of five, and asked for the traveler's name. Then she asked, **"Okay, what is her known traveler number?"**, read the nine digits back, and after about two minutes of lookup said: **"Okay, so I re-validated [traveler]'s ticket, so it should show the TSA pre-check when she checks in."** The whole call took 5 minutes 5 seconds.

### What the call established

| Question | What the recording shows |
|---|---|
| Can a Southwest agent add a Known Traveler Number after booking? | Yes. She asked for the number, read it back, and entered it on the existing reservation. |
| What she asked for | The confirmation number, the traveler's name, and the KTN. She didn't ask for a date of birth or anything else on this call. |
| One traveler on a group booking | The reservation was for five people. She added the number for one traveler ("TSA for [traveler]"). |
| Time to a person | 0:46 from the greeting, 13 seconds after saying "existing reservation." No hold queue. |
| Time for the change | About 2 minutes of lookup pauses, from the readback at 2:37 to her answer at 4:40. |
| What she said happens next | The ticket was "re-validated," and PreCheck "should show" when the traveler checks in. |
| Cost | No fee was mentioned, and our caller didn't ask. |

### What this call didn't confirm

- **A reissued boarding pass.** Our caller asked to "reissue just her boarding pass so that pre-check shows up." The agent didn't say she reissued a boarding pass or redid check-in. Her words were "re-validated" and "should show."
- **That PreCheck actually appeared.** The call ended before check-in, and we don't know what the traveler's boarding pass showed.
- **What "simultaneous changes" meant.** Midway through, the agent said "I've got simultaneous changes. What's going on?" The recording doesn't explain it.
- **Our caller's stumbles.** It introduced itself as "admin agent's assistant," and after saying the traveler's name it offered "the corrected name in just a sec" and never gave one. The agent found the traveler anyway.

Southwest also lets you do this yourself. Its [TSA PreCheck help article](https://support.southwest.com/helpcenter/s/article/tsa-precheck), checked October 17, 2026, says to add your Known Traveler Number through Manage Reservation "at least 60 minutes prior to departure," that "You'll then need to re-print or refresh your boarding pass," and to "Make sure that your Known Traveler Number, name, and date of birth are accurate." Its [Adding Known Traveler Number](https://support.southwest.com/helpcenter/article/Adding-Known-Traveler-Number) article says a KTN saved under "Add traveler info" in your Rapid Rewards profile updates "Any previously booked reservations linked to your account." The phone is the route when that isn't working, or when you're sorting out one traveler on someone else's booking, as here.

## Southwest refund timing, from southwest.com

From Southwest's [Method-of-payment Refund](https://support.southwest.com/helpcenter/article/How-will-I-receive-a-method-of-payment-refund) help article, checked October 13, 2026. Times count from the date Southwest determines a refund is due:

| Paid with | What Southwest says |
|---|---|
| Credit card | "reasonable efforts to process eligible refunds to the same account no later than seven business days." Your card company "may then take up to 10 business days to post the credit," and you'll see it "within one to two billing statements." |
| Apple Pay or PayPal | "no later than seven business days" |
| Klarna | "no later than seven business days," then Klarna applies it under its own terms |
| Cash | refunded by check, "no later than 20 calendar days" |

Southwest's [Customer Service Plan](https://www.southwest.com/swa-resources/pdfs/corporate-commitments/customer-service-plan.pdf) says the same for cards and cash. Its [Refund Policy](https://support.southwest.com/helpcenter/article/refund-policy) page says "Choice Extra and Choice Preferred tickets are refundable," while "Choice tickets are non-refundable" and "Basic tickets are non-refundable," and that after a significant disruption you are "eligible for a method-of-payment refund," even for non-refundable tickets.
