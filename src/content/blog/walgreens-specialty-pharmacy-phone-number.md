**The short answer:** the Walgreens Specialty Pharmacy phone number for general questions is **888-782-8443**. Walgreens' [specialty pharmacy page](https://www.walgreens.com/topic/pharmacy/specialty-pharmacy.jsp) says: "Questions? Call our Walgreens Specialty Pharmacy retail support center at 888-782-8443 Monday through Friday 8 am to 10 pm ET." We did not call that number for this guide.

Each Walgreens Specialty Pharmacy store also has its own line, and that is the one to call about something booked at that store. The call on this page went to the **Walgreens Specialty Pharmacy in Sacramento, at 1020 29th St, Sacramento, CA 95816**. Its [Walgreens store page](https://www.walgreens.com/locator/walgreens-1020+29th+st-sacramento-ca-95816/id=15987) lists **916-738-3300**, the number that was dialed, and pharmacy hours of **8 am to 5:30 pm, Monday to Friday, closed Saturday and Sunday**. To find a Walgreens specialty pharmacy near you and its direct line, use the Walgreens store locator.

On the Sacramento line, this path reached a person:

1. At "How can I help you today", the assistant said "representative". The system read out a keypad menu instead of transferring.
2. **Choose 4**, "to speak with the pharmacy staff". The assistant said "let's do four", and the system transferred the call.
3. A recorded note gave the hours, then a short silence and ringing played while the call transferred.

**A member of the pharmacy staff answered 1 minute 23 seconds into the call.** There was no hold message.

On Thursday, October 8, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called that store to cancel a flu shot appointment for the next day. Staff found the booking, asked whether it should be rescheduled, and said they would cancel it once the assistant said no.

## Calling a Walgreens specialty pharmacy store: what to have ready

- **The store's own number.** The flu shot was booked at this store, and its line went straight to that store's staff after option 4.
- **The patient's full name and date of birth.** Staff asked who the appointment was for, then for the date of birth, before cancelling.
- **The appointment's date, time and type.** Staff confirmed it was "for the flu shot" before ending the call.
- **A clear answer on rescheduling.** Staff offered it first. Decide before calling whether you want a new time.
- **Walgreens Specialty Pharmacy hours for your store.** The Sacramento pharmacy closes at 5:30 pm on weekdays and is closed on weekends, per its store page and the recorded message.
