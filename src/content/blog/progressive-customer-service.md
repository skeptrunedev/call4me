**The short answer:** the Progressive customer service number is **1-888-671-4405**. Progressive's [contact page](https://www.progressive.com/contact-us/) lists it as **General Customer Service** and says "We're always open! You can call us 24/7." A voice menu answers first. Say you want a quote on a **new policy**, say the state your car is parked in, and stay on the line. **A live agent answered 1 minute 30 seconds after the call connected.** We pressed no keys.

The same page lists the things that number handles: "Report an accident, File a claim, Make a change to my policy, Check my bill, Start a quote, Buy a policy, Need roadside assistance."

This got us to a person:

1. When it says "You can speak to me in complete sentences" and asks how it can help, **say you want an auto insurance quote**.
2. When it asks whether it's a new policy, an existing quote, or a change to an existing policy, **say "new policy"**.
3. When it asks "What state is your vehicle parked in?", **say your state**.
4. **Stay on the line** through a disclosure about how quotes are priced.

What the agent told us:

- **There's no callback option.** "So there's no callback option."
- **The fastest way to a person is this number and the prompts.** Call "the same number you call and just following the prompts from the system when you first call in."
- **Quotes and general customer service are open 24/7.** "We're open 24-7 for new quotes and general customer service."
- **You can get an auto quote by phone.** Have your name, address, email, the vehicle's year, make and model, the VIN, the finance company if it's financed, everyone in the home over 15 or 16 (depending on the state), the coverage you want, and your driver's license number.
- **For claims, call the same number and ask for claims.** The agent wasn't in the claims department and couldn't say how long a claim takes, but said callers "will be able to call the same number" and get transferred to the claims team.

We called on Friday, October 16, 2026, at about 3:33 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Progressive customer service phone tree, step by step

It's all voice. The menu said "Press 1" once, for more about its information practices, and we didn't.

1. **Call 1-888-671-4405.** "Hello, thank you for calling Progressive. Your call may be recorded or monitored. You can speak to me in complete sentences. Si quiere hablar en español, solo diga español."
2. **"How can I help you?"** Our caller said it wanted general info on reaching a person for a new auto insurance quote by phone.
3. **"I can help you with a quote. Are you calling to get a quote on a new policy, help with an existing quote, or to quote a change on an existing policy?"** We said "New policy."
4. **"What state is your vehicle parked in?"** We said California.
5. **"Please hold while I get you to someone to assist with a quote."** Then a recorded disclosure: Progressive's companies sell directly by phone or online and through independent agents and brokers, "prices vary based on how you buy", and "By seeking a quote, you will be consenting to this collection and use of information even if it is protected by a security freeze." It offers "Press 1 to hear more about our information practices."
6. **"Please be aware immediate coverage may not be available."**
7. **A person.** "Thank you for calling Progressive. My name is [name removed]. How can I help today?" About 7 seconds after the last recording.

This path goes to the quote team, since it's the new policy route. The menu never asked for a policy number. If you already have a policy and need a claim, a bill or a change, the menu likely routes you differently. We didn't test that.

## What to have ready

For a quote by phone, this is the agent's list:

- **Your name, address and email address.**
- **The vehicle's year, make and model, and its VIN.**
- **The finance company,** if the car is financed.
- **Everyone who lives in your home over 15 or 16,** depending on the state.
- **The coverage you want.**
- **Your driver's license number.**

The menu also asks **what state your vehicle is parked in**.

## Things that don't work

- **Asking the quote agent about claims.** The agent wasn't in the claims department and couldn't say how long a typical claim takes. The advice was to call the same number and get transferred to claims.
- **Waiting for a callback.** The agent said there isn't one.
