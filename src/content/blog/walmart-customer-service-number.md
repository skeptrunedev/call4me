**The short answer:** Walmart's customer service number is **1-800-925-6278 (1-800-WALMART)**. **The "Walmart AI agent" answers, not a person.** To reach a real person, **ask for a representative, and when it asks for an order number, say you don't have one and ask again.** On our call it transferred us after the second request, a recording said there was one caller ahead of us, and **a live representative answered 1 minute 15 seconds into the call.**

**One thing to know first:** earlier the same day, the same line played "I'm sorry. We cannot take your call at this time" and hung up within about 13 seconds, three times. All three of those calls came from one of our phone numbers. Both calls from our other number were answered by the AI agent. Walmart's line refused calls from one of our numbers and answered from another; if you hear that message, try calling from a different phone. That's what we observed on one day, not a Walmart policy we can confirm.

Walmart's [contact page](https://corporate.walmart.com/about/contact) says: "You may also contact our customer service team at 1-800-925-6278 (1-800-WALMART)." Its [Ask Walmart FAQ](https://corporate.walmart.com/askwalmart) says "You can also reach us by calling 800-Walmart (925-6278)."

Here's what got us from the AI agent to a person:

1. Call 1-800-925-6278. "Hi, I'm the Walmart AI agent. I'm not perfect, and this call is being recorded to improve quality."
2. It asks what it can help with. **Say "Could I speak with a representative, please?"**
3. It asks whether there's a specific order or issue, and for an order number "so I can make sure you get to the right person." **Say you don't have an order number and ask for a live representative again.**
4. "I'm transferring you to the customer support team." Then: "There's one caller ahead of you. Your estimated wait time is approximately one minute."
5. A representative answered about 15 seconds after that message.

**What the representative told us:**

- **Hours: two different answers in one breath.** "It is 24/7 available, chat and phone calls also." Then: "We operate daily from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m. Central time." Walmart's AI agent, on a separate call three minutes earlier, gave a third answer: 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. Central, seven days a week, except Thanksgiving and Christmas. [More below](#hours-three-answers).
- **Refunds for online orders:** "it may take up to seven days," or "up to 10 business days" if the refund is escalated to the account review or billing team.
- **Store purchases go to the store.** "If your concern is about your purchase in the store, physical store, you need to go to the customer service desk at the store. But if it's online orders, you need to call 1-800-WALMART."
- **What to have ready for an online order:** "the complete order number," "the specific name of the item," and "the first four letters of the email and also the last four digits of the phone number."

We called on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, at about 4:48 pm Pacific (6:48 pm Central), with [call4me](/) placing the call. It was a general question for this guide, not about any order or account. This page is what we heard.

## The Walmart phone tree, step by step

It's a voice AI. We didn't hear any keypad options.

1. **Call 1-800-925-6278.** "Hi, I'm the Walmart AI agent. I'm not perfect, and this call is being recorded to improve quality."
2. **The opening question.** On our earlier call from the same number, it said "Para ayuda en español solo pregunte" and asked: "Can I help with tracking an order, making a return, or something else today?" On this call it opened with "I'm glad we could answer your general Walmart questions. Thanks for being a Walmart customer. What else can I help you with today?" It seemed to remember our call from three minutes before.
3. **Ask for a representative.** "Could I speak with a representative, please?"
4. **The order number check.** "Okay, just before I connect you, is there a specific order or issue you wanna talk about? If you have an order number handy, could you share it with me? That way, I can make sure you get to the right person." If your call is about an order, this is when to give the number. We had none.
5. **Ask again.** "It's just a general inquiry for a consumer guide. I don't have an order number. Could I get a live representative, please?"
6. **The transfer.** "Just a sec. I'm transferring you to the customer support team to help you with your issue." Then: "There's one caller ahead of you. Your estimated wait time is approximately one minute. This call and your voice may be recorded for quality or other business purposes, including fraud prevention."
7. **A person.** "Hello, thank you for calling walmart.com."

## Refunds: the representative versus the AI agent

**The representative:** "For online orders, it may take up to seven days. Usually if it's escalated to our account review team or to our billing team, it will take up to 10 business days for the reversal of the money to the account."

**The AI agent:** "For an online order return, refunds to a credit or debit card, PayPal, pay by bank, or OnePay Later typically post within seven days. Refunds to a Walmart gift card, Walmart Cash, or Walmart balance are typically available within three hours."

They agree on seven days for a card. Only the AI mentioned the faster gift card and Walmart Cash option, and only the representative mentioned the 10 business day case. We didn't check either against walmart.com's written return policy.

## Beware of fake Walmart calls

Walmart's [fraud alerts page](https://corporate.walmart.com/privacy-security/fraud-alerts) says "A trusted company will never ask a customer for highly sensitive information during a call they initiated," and "Don't trust the Caller ID. Fraudsters can manipulate the Caller ID to have it display a legitimate business' name." It also says "Other businesses do not accept payments in the form of Walmart Gift Cards," and "you will never be asked to pay your utility bills, bail money, or bills with Walmart Gift Cards."

**Where to report it**, from the same page: a scam involving Walmart Gift Cards goes to **(888) 537-5503**. A fake Walmart email goes to OnlineAbuse@walmart.com, sent "as an attachment," not forwarded or pasted. We didn't call the gift card line.

The representative said that for an online order, the agent will ask for the first four letters of your email and the last four digits of your phone number. That was on a call we placed to Walmart's published number. Nobody asked us for a password, a card number or a code.
