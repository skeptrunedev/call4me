## How to apply for Ohio workers compensation insurance

BWC's [official application and instructions](https://dam.assets.ohio.gov/image/upload/info.bwc.ohio.gov/forms/U-3.pdf) describe online, mail and in person submission. The instructions list a $120 nonrefundable application fee. For mail submissions, they require a completed, signed application with a check or money order. They also explain when coverage begins following a successful submission or receipt of the application and fee.

Prepare the business identity, contact information, owner or officer details, description of operations and estimated payroll requested by the form. Its owner information section labels the identity field as a Social Security number. The published instructions do not explain an ITIN alternative. That was the gap this customer needed BWC to answer.

For a blocked field, call **800 644 6292**, the number printed on the application. Explain which field is failing, whose information it requests and the exact problem with the online submission. Ask for the correct submission route, required attachments and payment method. Keep the answer with your application records.

## What to ask when the online application rejects an identifier

Use a narrow question that a representative can answer:

> I'm applying for Ohio workers compensation insurance. The owner is a foreign individual with an ITIN and no SSN. The online owner identity field rejects the ITIN. How should we complete that field, and which submission method should we use?

Then check the parts that determine whether you can act on the answer:

1. **Which form and section should we use?** Distinguish the business tax ID from the owner's identity field.
2. **What identifier belongs in that field in our situation?** Ask for clarification if the spoken answer differs from the printed label.
3. **Are additional documents needed?** Get a specific answer for your application rather than assuming the phone explanation covers every case.
4. **How do we submit and pay?** Confirm the destination and payment method for the route you choose.
5. **How will we know the application was accepted and coverage began?** An answered phone question is not proof of coverage.
