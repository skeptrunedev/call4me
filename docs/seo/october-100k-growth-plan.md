# Call4me October audience growth plan

Planning date: October 6, 2026. Nick confirmed the target is 100,000 total
October website visitors by October 31, with a maximum cash budget of $5,000.
The audience is people using an existing personal assistant or coding agent
who want it to make outbound calls on their behalf.

This plan is being built from the current traffic audit, fresh Ahrefs CLI data,
and verified distribution options. It does not authorize ad spend or outreach.

The October 6 Ahrefs refresh still reports zero estimated organic traffic,
zero ranking keywords and zero tracked AI citations. Search Console already
records real Google clicks, so these Ahrefs estimates are not a measurement of
all traffic. The October 6 snapshot contains 29 Google clicks dated October 1
through October 6, with October 5 onward incomplete.

Current keyword estimates reinforce the audience constraint: `codex mcp` has
1,500 monthly US searches, `claude personal assistant` 150 and `claude code
personal assistant` 40. These overlapping and adjacent terms cannot be summed
into an October visitor forecast. More generic consumer content cannot by
itself establish the requested ICP fit.

Evidence and the full traffic audit are being assembled in the ignored
`scratch/october-100k-plan-2026-10-06/` directory. Final channel allocations,
measurement definitions and decision dates will follow that audit.
