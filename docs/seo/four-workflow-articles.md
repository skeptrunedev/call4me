# Four agent calling workflows

October 4, 2026. Nick requested parallel execution of four new articles.

## Acceptance criteria

* T3 Code: verify the provider and configuration used by a real T3 thread.
  Distinguish provider tool discovery from successful dialing and task completion.
* Scheduling: verify actual schedule tools and existing pending or canceled
  records. Future business calls remain pending until their results arrive.
* Phone menus: separate keypad action, destination, voicemail behavior and task
  success. Historical calls are case studies, not a controlled reliability test.
* Twilio: test the official public documentation MCP and explain the additional
  execution and conversational voice components accurately.
* Each page must solve a distinct job, cite current primary sources and link to
  related setup and evidence pages. No invented outcomes or customer identities.
* Credentials and raw session traces stay outside git. Existing reviewed audio
  may be reused with its original evidence boundaries.
* Root owns T3 and integration review. Three subagents own separate scheduling,
  phone menu and Twilio pages and their images. They do not commit or deploy.
* Verify builds, tests, article rendering, mobile layout, metadata, links and
  media. Publish through main and confirm the production pages.
