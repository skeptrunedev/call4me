**The San Francisco Public Library Main Library phone number is 415 557 4400.** The [official branch page](https://sfpl.org/locations/main-library) lists the building at **100 Larkin Street**. On our recorded call, the menu announced **3 to speak with library staff** and **2 for the Main Library reference desk**.

We called on **October 6, 2026, at 4:22 pm Pacific**, using Meta Muse connected to [call4me](/). A staff member answered about **48 seconds into the recording** and explained where adults can quietly use a laptop, find outlets and access WiFi. We did not reserve a seat or visit the library to test the connection.

This article uses that previously published call. We checked the official contact information and regular hours on November 2; we did not make a new call today.

## Main Library hours and contact options

These regular hours appear on the [Main Library page](https://sfpl.org/locations/main-library). Check that page for your visit date and any special closure.

| Day | Regular hours, Pacific time |
| :--- | :--- |

| Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday | 9 am to 8 pm |
| Friday | Noon to 6 pm |

The branch page also lists **ask@sfpl.libanswers.com**. SFPL's [contact page](https://sfpl.org/about-us/contact-us) gives **415 557 4433** as the TTY number. Other branches have their own numbers in the [locations directory](https://sfpl.org/locations), so choose the branch you plan to visit.

## Can you work on a laptop at the Main Library?

The staff member said an adult could quietly use their own laptop in general seating. Asked where to find outlets, they directed us to **floors three, four and five**. For the question about noise rules or seating time limits, the answer was to keep the volume low.

That is useful guidance for a quiet laptop visit. It does not establish that a seat or outlet will be free when you arrive, or that calls and meetings are allowed in the seating area. We did not inspect the building or perform a full review of its conduct policies.

Private study rooms have a separate rule. The [official study room page](https://sfpl.org/services/meeting-rooms/study-rooms) says they are taken in person when available, cannot be reserved and are not intended for sessions lasting several hours. Asking about general seating is different from trying to book a room.

## Does the WiFi require a password or library card?

On the call, the staff member said the WiFi had no password and anyone could use it. The caller's question about a card ended with an interrupted “or for just,” so the answer does **not** separately establish a library card rule for seating.

SFPL's [published WiFi FAQ](https://sfpl.libanswers.com/faq/88545) names the network as **#SFLibraryWiFi**. That FAQ is dated January 14, 2019, and includes older device instructions. We could not resolve every letter of the network name from the call, and did not check the currently displayed network on a device. Confirm the name with staff if it differs when you arrive.

A staff answer about guest access is not a WiFi speed test. Our call did not check signal quality, upload speed, login screens or whether a particular application would work.
