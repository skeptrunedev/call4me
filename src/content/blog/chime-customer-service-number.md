**The short answer:** Chime's customer service number is **(844) 244-6363**. Chime's help article [How do I contact Chime customer service?](https://help.chime.com/how-do-i-contact-chime-customer-service-731cb26b) says: "The quickest way to contact us is to call (844) 244-6363," and "Our Chime Member Services team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week." **The representative we reached said the same: "We work 24/7."**

An automated menu answers, not a person. This got us to a live representative:

1. At the main menu, **press 9** ("For all other questions, press nine").
2. At the next message, which points you to help.chime.com, **press 0** ("To connect with an agent for further assistance, press zero").
3. **Stay on the line.** "Alright, you'll be connected with an agent shortly."

**A live Chime representative answered 1 minute 21 seconds into the call.** It never asked us for a card number, account number or Social Security number on the way.

What the representative told us:

- **Hours: 24/7.** Same as Chime's help article.
- **No callbacks.** "We don't have an option to give members a call back." You wait on the line.
- **What to have ready:** "their personal information like Social Security number, date of birth, and first and last name, the full name."
- **To dispute a debit card charge, call.** "They can give us a call and we can get them connected to a team of specialists, and they'll be able to file a dispute for them." **"The maximum time it takes for a dispute to be settled is like 90 days."**
- **If your card is lost or stolen,** order a replacement in the Chime app if you can get into it, or call, "and we can replace the card on members' behalf."

**Chime won't ask for your login.** Chime's help article [Is this communication from Chime?](https://help.chime.com/is-this-communication-from-chime-fd81c96b) says: "We never ask for personal details like your full SSN, card number, or login credentials." The same article says Chime will never ask for your username or password, your PIN, or your account or routing numbers, and will never "request money in exchange for services." It lists (844) 244-6363 as Chime's phone line.

## The Chime customer service phone tree, step by step

This is the menu as it played on October 22, 2026, calling (844) 244-6363 from a number with no Chime account.

1. **Call (844) 244-6363.** "Thanks for calling Chime. One moment while we locate an account with your phone number."
2. **The main menu:** "If you have an account with Chime, press one. If you are interested in joining Chime or want more info on product and services, press two. To report fraudulent or unauthorized account activity, press three. To verify a Chime member's bank account, press four. For all other questions, press nine. Press star to hear these options again." **Press 9.**
3. **"For support with other questions, please visit our Help Center at help.chime.com. Press star to repeat this message. Press eight to go to the main menu. To connect with an agent for further assistance, press zero. To end this call, please hang up."** **Press 0.**
4. **"To provide you with the best assistance, let me connect you with an agent. Please stay on the line while I find the right team for you."** Then: "Alright, you'll be connected with an agent shortly." Then: "Your call may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance purposes."
5. **A person,** about 8 seconds after the recording notice: "We appreciate your business with Chime. This is [name removed]. May I please have your first and last name?"

Keypad tones don't show in our recording. The menu answered as if we pressed 9 and then 0: the "all other questions" message played after the main menu, and the transfer to an agent followed the "press zero" option.

## What to have ready

- **Your full name,** first and last. It's the first thing the representative asked for.
- **Your date of birth and Social Security number.** That's what the representative said callers should have ready. Chime's help article says Chime never asks for your *full* SSN, so we don't know how much of it they ask for on the call. Don't give your PIN, password or login to anyone who calls you.
- **The phone number on your Chime account,** if you can call from it. The menu starts by trying to "locate an account with your phone number." We called from a number with no account, so we don't know what changes when it finds one.

## What we didn't test

- **Pressing 1** ("If you have an account with Chime"). We have no account, so we went through "all other questions". The representative said members go "through the IVR" and "once you enter the information, you'll be connected to a live agent." We don't know what information that branch asks for.
- **Pressing 3** to report fraud or unauthorized activity. We don't know whether it goes to the same team or straight to the dispute specialists the representative mentioned.
- **Disputing in the app.** The representative only described disputing by phone. We didn't ask whether the app has a way to start one, and we didn't check.

## How to reach Chime without calling

Chime's help article says: "You can also chat with us from your Chime app: Tap **Profile** > **Help Center** > **Chat**." It also says "The easiest way to reach us is through the Chime app." For a lost or stolen card, the representative said you can order a replacement in the app.
