**The short answer:** Uber Eats doesn't have a customer service phone number. Uber's [Uber Eats help center](https://help.uber.com/ubereats/restaurants) says so at the top: "While we don't offer a customer service phone number for Uber Eats, you can log in to your account to get personalized support." Uber's [How to contact support](https://help.uber.com/ubereats/restaurants/article/how-to-contact-support?nodeId=7ad3ef19-012f-43e6-a711-113935b0cb7b) page sends you to **Help in the app**, and says only Diamond members get a premium support line, through a "Call us" button once you're logged in.

The only Uber Eats support number on Uber's help center is **1-833-ASK-EATS (1-833-275-3287)**, on its [Contact us page for merchants and restaurants](https://help.uber.com/en/merchants-and-restaurants/article/contact-us?nodeId=8b232649-2bda-440c-b1c3-9757f81cf0c2). It's the line for restaurants and stores, not people who ordered food.

We called it anyway, twice, on Thursday, October 29, 2026. Here's what happens:

1. A recording: "Thank you for calling Uber Merchant Support. This is an automated recorded call."
2. **An automated assistant answers** and says "this line is just for restaurants and merchants." It told us "There isn't a phone number for customers" and to use the Uber Eats app or website.
3. **We asked twice for a live person,** and it said "Let me connect you to a team member."
4. **A live Uber Eats Merchant Support agent answered 2 minutes 27 seconds into the call.**

What the agent told us:

- **There's no phone number just for customers.** "We don't have like a specific phone number for customers." The fastest way is to "go in their app and press help," where you chat with support.
- **If a customer does call this line,** "if the customer say, like I'm a customer, I need to resolve the problem, we are able to transfer them very quick." We didn't ask to be transferred, so we don't know where that goes or how long it takes.
- **Missing or wrong items get refunded through the app.** You "create a report or request a refund for a specific item," and "the system refunds them automatically" with a message confirming it. She added that this is "not my department."
- **Uber Eats doesn't usually call customers back.** "It's not like usual that we contact the customers," and "person with person, no, not really."
- **The line is 24/7.** After checking, she came back with "We have 24-7 for customers and merchants." Uber's merchant page also says 1-833-ASK-EATS is for contacting "Uber Support 24/7."

We called at about 2:49 pm Eastern with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard. If you're after Uber rides rather than Uber Eats, see our [Uber customer service number](/blog/uber-customer-service-number) guide: riders don't get a regular phone line either, and Uber's premium rider line asks for the phone number on your account.

## The 1-833-ASK-EATS phone tree, step by step

This is the line as it played on October 29, 2026, calling 1-833-275-3287.

1. **Call 1-833-275-3287.** "Thank you for calling Uber Merchant Support. This is an automated recorded call. Just a moment while I connect you." There was no keypad menu on either of our calls.
2. **An automated assistant:** "Hello, thanks for calling Uber Merchant Support. How can I help you?" You talk to it in plain sentences.
3. **Ask for a live representative.** The first time, it answered the question itself: "There's no phone support for customers, just the app or website." When our caller asked again, for the fastest way to reach a person, it said "Let me connect you to a team member."
4. **A person**, about 10 seconds later: "Hello, thanks for calling Uber Eats Merchant Support." That was 2 minutes 27 seconds into the call.

On our first call, a couple of minutes earlier, the assistant said "I can only answer questions about merchant support," our caller didn't ask for a person, and the call ended after 2 minutes without reaching one.

Uber's merchant help page about [reaching a partnership manager](https://help.uber.com/en/merchants-and-restaurants/article/i-want-to-talk-to-someone-over-the-phone-how-do-i-reach-a-partnership-manager?nodeId=6046ea3b-7fee-4745-a361-09756c96f18f) describes a keypad menu on this number (press 1 for merchants, then 3 to sign up a new store). We didn't hear that menu on either call.

## How to get help with an Uber Eats order

From Uber's help pages and what the agent said:

- **Open the order in the Uber Eats app and tap Help.** Uber's contact page says you can "contact support 24/7" through its help articles: wrong or missing items, food quality, a damaged order, an order that never arrived, and problems with a delivery person.
- **Report it within 48 hours.** Uber's [Wrong or missing items](https://help.uber.com/ubereats/restaurants/article/wrong-or-missing-items?nodeId=beb06041-6d9c-44de-9ecf-2a9fa80c3bc9) page asks you to contact them within that window, or they "may be unable to provide a refund." Name the exact items, and add a photo if you got the wrong item. Uber says it won't replace the order, but you may be eligible for a refund.
- **Look for the confirmation message.** The agent said refunds for an item come through automatically once you request them, with a message confirming it. Don't wait for a phone call: she said Uber Eats doesn't usually call customers.
- **If you're a Diamond member,** Uber's help center says you get a premium support line through "Call us" in the app. We aren't members, so we couldn't see what number it dials.

## Things that don't work

On October 29, 2026:

- **Expecting 1-833-ASK-EATS to handle your order.** The assistant said "If a customer calls this line by mistake, we can't assist them directly." The agent said they can transfer customers, but we didn't test it.
- **Asking the assistant for a person once.** The first time we asked, it answered the question itself. The second ask, phrased as a question about reaching a person, got us transferred.
- **Waiting for a callback.** The agent said Uber Eats doesn't usually call customers about order issues.

Watch out for other "Uber Eats customer service" numbers in search results. Uber's own Uber Eats help pages list none for customers, so take any number from Uber's site or app, not from a directory site or a search ad.
