**The short answer:** for an **AAA tow reimbursement** from **AAA Northeast**, you mail a paper request, not a phone claim. AAA Northeast's [reimbursement page](https://northeast.aaa.com/automotive/services/reimbursement-forms.html) links a printable [Roadside Assistance Reimbursement Request form](https://northeast.aaa.com/content/dam/aaa-ne-web/pdf/automotive/aaa-reimbursement-form-fillable-September-2026.pdf). Send it with the **original itemized paid receipt** to:

> AAA Northeast, Attn: Reimbursements, 110 Royal Little Drive, Providence, RI 02904

The form says to submit the original receipt **within 30 days of the date of service**, allows **3 weeks for processing**, and says "For questions please call **1-844-765-6428**." That is the number a call4me customer's assistant dialed on Thursday, October 15, 2026. A recorded announcement read out the same mailing instructions, and a **live person answered 1 minute 8 seconds** into the call. There was no menu to press through.

AAA is a group of regional clubs, so this only applies to AAA Northeast members. The membership on this call was based in New York, and the reimbursement address is still the Rhode Island one. If you're in another club, see our [AAA Mountain West insurance call](/blog/aaa-insurance-customer-service) for how different the numbers are, and use your own club's site.

## The AAA tow reimbursement form, and what goes with it

The phone announcement and the form agree on the address. The form asks for more than the announcement does, so fill it in rather than writing a letter:

- **Membership number, member's name, mailing address, contact phone and email.**
- **Whether a member was in the vehicle**, the vehicle driver, type, year, make and model.
- **Service date and time, type of service** (tow, winch, flat tire and so on), towing mileage and **amount requested**.
- **Whether you tried AAA first**, and how: phone, text, AAA.com or the app, and the number you called from. The form says that "to be eligible for reimbursement members must be in good standing and have attempted to secure service from AAA first, unless AAA is legally prohibited from providing service on the roadway."
- **Breakdown location, where the car was towed, the provider's name, your insurance carrier, the breakdown reason**, and whether police were involved.

The receipt matters more than anything else. AAA Northeast's FAQ, attached to the form, says:

- **"An itemized receipt on the garage letterhead is required."** A credit card slip or an emailed payment confirmation is not enough: "If you only received a credit card or emailed receipt, please contact the provider for an itemized receipt."
- **"The receipt should be in a member's name."** A legible copy is accepted, though they may ask for the original.
- **Accidents are not covered.** "AAA memberships are not an automobile liability insurance contract. This is also not a contract for towing insurance." Accident costs go to your auto insurer.

That is why this customer's assistant also called the tow company: once to confirm the tow and get an itemized receipt, and once to have the paid invoice reissued in the member's name.

## Calling the AAA Northeast reimbursement line

1. **Call 1-844-765-6428.** A greeting says the call may be recorded, then: "You have reached the reimbursement department."
2. **An announcement reads the mailing instructions** in full, including the Providence address and "Please specify attention reimbursements."
3. **"For all other questions, please remain on the line and a representative will assist you shortly. To hear this announcement again, please press one."** Don't press anything.
4. **A person answered at 1:08:** "AAA reimbursements, how can I help?" The first thing the representative asked was the city and state the membership is based in.

Have your **membership number** ready (the representative asked for the digits after the club number "on the card") and be ready to say who you are. When the assistant said it was calling on the member's behalf but had no name of its own to give, the representative said: "I can't verify if you would be eligible to speak regarding his account." The representative would still answer general questions about the process.

## What these calls did not settle

- **Whether the associate member was covered on the tow date.** AAA Northeast would not say without the member, or a caller it could identify, on the line. The assistant tried to ring the customer in, and he didn't join.
- **Whether a rental car, a breakdown in Hawaii, or the cause of the breakdown changes anything.** The assistant started asking about the rental, then the call reset and ended before an answer.
- **How much AAA would pay back, the postmark question, a representative name or a reference number.** None came up before the recording ends. The amount rules and the 30 day deadline above come from the form, not the call.
- **That the reimbursement was paid,** or even filed. These calls gathered instructions and paperwork only.
- **That the right emails arrived.** On both tow company calls, the address read back by the employee did not match what the caller spelled, according to the saved call text and the source transcriptions, and the caller agreed anyway ("Exactly", "Yeah, that's it"). The recordings also don't show what was in the reissued invoice.
