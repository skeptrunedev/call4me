**The short answer:** oral surgery at Fort Leonard Wood is handled by the **hospital dental clinic**, which Fort Leonard Wood's [official dental page](https://leonard-wood.tricare.mil/Health-Services/Dental) lists as the **Hospital Oral Maxillofacial Department, 573-596-0198**, at General Leonard Wood Community Hospital, 4234 Illinois Ave. It's open **Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 3:30 p.m., by appointment only**. The **Roll Dental Clinic** (permanent party) is **573-596-0364**, at 6958 Nebraska Ave, Building 1608, with sick call Monday to Friday 7 to 9 a.m.

Both numbers were answered by a person, with no phone menu, about a second after the call connected.

On October 8, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called both lines to check on a surgery referral that a dental clinic at Fort Leavenworth was supposed to have sent about three weeks earlier. If it had arrived, the plan was to book the surgery for late January or February 2027. Here's what the two calls established:

- **Roll Dental Clinic doesn't do oral surgery.** "The oral surgery, that would be over at the hospital." Staff read out 573-596-0198 and said it was the right place to check on the referral "as far as for oral surgery."
- **The hospital clinic usually gets referrals by email.** The staff member said she'd been dealing with the Fort Leavenworth clinic "for the past five, six years", and "usually I get the emails, and I don't have it."
- **The other route is PowerChart.** "The only other thing you could do is put it in the pool in PowerChart, if the provider knows how to do that." She also asked whether the sending clinic used Dentrix and PowerChart.
- **The fix she offered:** have the referring clinic email the referral to her directly. She spelled out her health.mil address (muted in the recording).
- **No surgery was booked.** The referral wasn't on file, so there was nothing to schedule yet. She took the patient's DoD ID and a callback number and said she'd look at his notes and call back if she had questions.

The dialed numbers match what Fort Leonard Wood's official dental page lists for each clinic. That page refused automated requests when we checked on October 16, so we read it through the [Internet Archive's June 2026 copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20260613211838/https://leonard-wood.tricare.mil/Health-Services/Dental).

## What these calls didn't do

- **No appointment exists.** The referral wasn't found, so the January to February 2027 window was never discussed with the clinic.
- **The referral wasn't confirmed as lost.** She said the oral surgeon wasn't in that day or the day before, and allowed that it might have been sent "yesterday". Whether it was ever sent is still open.
- **Nothing was resent during the call.** The next step depends on the Fort Leavenworth clinic emailing it, which the assistant said it would ask for. These recordings don't show that happening.
- **The callback is a maybe.** She said she'd call back "if I have any questions", not that she would call in any case.
- **The caller never gave a name.** The staff member asked three times, and each time the assistant answered only "his assistant". It worked here, but have your caller say who it is and who it's calling for.
- **Eligibility and wait times weren't asked about.**

## Calling about a dental referral at Fort Leonard Wood

Start with the hospital line if the referral is for oral surgery. The first call shows that Roll Dental Clinic sends oral surgery questions there.

Have the patient's DoD ID ready. It's what the hospital clinic asked for before it looked up his notes. Know which clinic sent the referral, and when. Staff asked how it was sent before anything else, so it helps to find out from the referring clinic whether they emailed it or put it in PowerChart.

If the referral isn't there, ask who it should go to, then pass that to the referring clinic. In this call the hospital clinic wanted it emailed straight to its staff.
