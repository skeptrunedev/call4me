## Is lifetime alignment worth the extra cost?

Ask your assistant to compare the two offers against how long you expect to keep the car and the shop's actual terms. The Franklin Street employee described lifetime coverage as repeat alignments for as long as you own the vehicle. They put the difference at about $80.

A useful followup is: which future visits would be covered, and what would still be charged separately? Ask about inspection requirements, exclusions, modifications, participating locations and transferability before assigning value to a lifetime plan. Our inquiry did not settle those terms.

Keep repairs separate from alignment coverage. A suspension check is not a promise that replacement parts or labor are included. Have the assistant request a separate estimate for any recommended work and bring it back for your approval.
