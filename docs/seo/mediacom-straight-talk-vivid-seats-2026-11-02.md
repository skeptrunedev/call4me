Official contact pages and the actual recordings support the contact details. Mediacom publishes round the clock broadband customer service. Straight Talk's contact page labels its displayed hours as chat hours. Vivid Seats' existing orders page requires sign in and promotes app chat; its public SMS terms supply the number tested. The articles distinguish published information from what the calls established.

The recording review workflow uses exact provider leg matching, complete source and export transcriptions from two independent models, source excerpts around identifying words, and decoded duration and silence checks. Original timelines are retained. Staff identifying audio is muted in the Mediacom and Straight Talk recordings. Vivid Seats contains no private identifier and has no mute or cut. Source recordings, credentials and provider associations remain outside the repository. No human listening claim is made by the automated review.

Add each actual call once to the public CSV and directory. Mediacom and Straight Talk contribute timed human results. Vivid Seats contributes a recorded automated menu result and no human time. Linking an existing recording from another guide must not create another call observation.
