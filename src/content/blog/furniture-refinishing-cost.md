**Furniture refinishing cost varies with the piece and the work included.** Dale's in Saint Paul publishes a dining chair range of **$90 to $200**, while Murphy's in Salem lists **$275 to $350** for stripping and refinishing a chair. These are individual shops' published prices, not a national average or quotes for your furniture. Sources: [Dale's pricing](https://dalesfurniturerefinishing.com/pricing/) and [Murphy's pricing](https://www.murphysrefinishing.com/pricing/).

We checked three official shop pages on October 7, 2026. No phone price was obtained for this guide.

## Use an AI agent to get furniture refinishing quotes

To request estimates with [call4me](/), [connect your agent](/mcp) using the [Claude Code guide](/blog/claude-code-phone-calls) or [Codex guide](/blog/codex-phone-calls). Our [calling workflow guide](/blog/ai-agent-that-makes-phone-calls) explains how to check the results. Adapt this brief:

> Find furniture refinishing shops near [city or ZIP code] for my [piece, quantity and dimensions]. The visible condition is [damage or wear], and I want [desired finish and surfaces to refinish]. Check official websites, then use call4me to call up to [number] suitable shops. Ask whether they handle this material and work, what photos they need, where I should send them, and whether an inspection is required. Ask for an estimate separating finish removal, repairs, refinishing, transport, taxes and other charges. Confirm whether leaves, legs, drawers and hardware are included. Ask about the earliest start and expected completion. Return a table with sources, quote dates, work included, exclusions and missing answers. Do not send photos, book collection, pay a deposit or authorize work yet.

Supply real measurements and describe uncertain materials as uncertain. After the calls, approve the photos and recipient before your assistant sends an estimate request through an available email or messaging tool. A shop that requests photographs has given you a next step, not a confirmed price.

## Is stripping included in the price?

**Check the column heading before comparing numbers.** Murphy's lists dining tables at $400 to $500 for stripping alone and $975 to $1,275 for stripping plus refinishing. It also lists table leaves separately at $100 to $175 each for the complete process. Those are different scopes, even within one shop's [price list](https://www.murphysrefinishing.com/pricing/).

## Repair, refinishing or reupholstery?

Material identification matters. Weathersby says it works with solid wood and suitable veneer, while laminate and particle board generally need a different approach. Ask the shop to assess your piece rather than treating a photograph as conclusive. [Weathersby material guidance](https://weathersbyguildsa-aus.com/services/furniture-refinishing)

Wood finishing and replacing fabric are separate requests. If a couch needs new upholstery as well as attention to its wooden legs, collect those charges separately. Our [couch reupholstery cost guide](/blog/couch-reupholstery-cost) covers the fabric and upholstery side of the comparison.
