**Zan Atelier is at 1756 West Lawrence Avenue, Chicago, IL 60640.** Its [official website](https://zanatelierchicago.com/) lists **+1 (773) 348-8666** as its only phone number and says to call that number to schedule an appointment. That is the number the call below dialed. The site lists hours of Monday to Friday, 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM, closed Saturday and Sunday.

On Thursday, October 1, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called Zan Atelier to ask whether the shop would tailor a short white coat, the kind medical students wear, so it fit better. It also asked about price, turnaround time and whether the customer could walk in that day or the next.

A person answered right away. The short version: **yes, they do alterations on a coat like that, but you need an appointment.** There were no openings on Thursday or Friday, and the next opening was Monday.

## What the call did not settle

- **No appointment was booked.** The assistant learned that Monday was the next opening but did not reserve it.
- **No price for the white coat itself.** Taking in the sides or shortening sleeves were mentioned by the assistant, but staff would not price them without seeing the coat.
- **Hours were not answered on the call.** The assistant asked, but staff answered the walk-in question instead. The hours above come from the shop's website.
- **Rush service was not discussed.** "Sooner or later" than 14 business days was mentioned, without a rush fee or a guaranteed date.

## Getting a white coat altered: plan for the appointment

Medical and nursing students usually need a white coat to fit by a fixed date, such as a white coat ceremony or the first day of rotations. At Zan Atelier, the call points to three things to plan around:

1. **Book first.** The shop works by appointment, and on a Thursday the next opening was the following Monday.
2. **Bring the coat.** Staff would not price the job over the phone. The $80 jacket shortening and $30 trouser hem give a sense of the range, not a quote.
3. **Count business days.** About 14 business days is close to three calendar weeks. If you have a deadline, say it at the fitting and ask whether it can be done sooner.
