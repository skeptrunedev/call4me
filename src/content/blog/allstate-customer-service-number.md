**The short answer:** the Allstate customer service number is **800-726-6033**. Allstate's [call center page](https://www.allstate.com/help-support/contact/call-center) lists it under "Get 24/7 support" and says its licensed agents "are available any time." The agent we reached confirmed it's the number for **existing customers' billing and policy questions**, and said claims can go through it as well: "there's not like a direct line to claims," you call it and ask for claims in the automated system. Allstate's own [claims page](https://www.allstate.com/help-support/claims) does list a separate claims number, **800-255-7828** (1-800-ALLSTATE), for auto and property claims, and it's the one to call if you're not an Allstate customer.

To reach a person, this got us through in 2 minutes 9 seconds:

1. Say **"English"** (or "español").
2. At "will you need help with claims?", **say "no"** (say "yes" if it's a claim).
3. Say **why you're calling** in a sentence.
4. **Answer the quote questions:** the ZIP code where you want coverage, then whether you own your home.

After a privacy disclosure and about 7 seconds on hold, a **licensed insurance agent** picked up. They told us:

- **Allstate is writing new auto policies in California.** "Yes," with no hesitation.
- **Phone, online or a local agent all work for a quote.** "Neither one is easier than the other."
- **For a quote, have your driver's license, your VIN, and the names of everyone who'll be on the policy.**
- **Claims can go through the same number:** ask for claims in the automated system. Allstate's claims page lists 800-255-7828 for auto and property claims.
- **Chat is open 24/7** in the Allstate app and on the website. The agent wasn't sure the phone lines are: "It depends on the department."

We called on Friday, October 2, 2026, at about 2:18 pm Pacific (5:18 pm Eastern), with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Allstate customer service phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 800-726-6033.** "Thanks for calling Allstate, where you're in good hands. Para español, diga español." Say "English" or just wait.
2. **"This call will be monitored and recorded. First off, will you need help with claims? Say yes or no."** Say "yes" for a claim. We said "no".
3. **"In a few words, tell me why you're calling today."** We asked how to get an auto quote in California and whether this number handles billing and policy questions.
4. **"Let's get you to an agent. Just a few quick questions first to tailor your quote."** Even though half our question was about existing customers, the menu sent us down the quote path.
5. **"What's the zip code where you want coverage?"** Say it, or type it. If you don't, it asks again, then offers the keypad, "or if you don't have it, press pound."
6. **"Is this for Michigan?"** This came when we didn't give a ZIP. Our call4me number has a 248 area code, which is Michigan, so the menu seems to guess your state from the number you call from. We said "no" and it moved on.
7. **"Do you own your home?"** Any answer works. Ours was "I'm not sure."
8. **A disclosure plays:** by continuing, "you are authorizing us to collect information like credit, driving, prior insurance and claims history for you and others in your household," and Allstate may share it with affiliates and partners.
9. **"Please hold while we connect you."** About 7 seconds later a licensed insurance agent answered, 2 minutes 9 seconds after dialing.

## What to have ready

- **Your ZIP code.** The menu asks for it first, twice, and then on the keypad. Having it skips the Michigan guess.
- **Your driver's license and your car's VIN,** if you want a quote. That's what the agent said to have ready.
- **The names of everyone who'll be on the policy.**
- **Whether you own your home.** The menu asks right before it connects you.

## Things that don't work

On October 2, 2026:

- **Asking for a representative at the ZIP prompt.** "I don't have a zip. Can I speak to a representative?" got "I still didn't get that." The menu wants an answer, not a request.
- **Asking for an agent at the home question.** "Can I please speak to an agent?" was ignored, but the menu continued anyway and connected us right after the disclosure.

Answering the questions, even with "no" or "I'm not sure", is what got through. Each answer moved the menu forward, and the transfer came right after the last one.
