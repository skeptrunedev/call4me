**The Ritz-Carlton, Bachelor Gulch is at 0130 Daybreak Ridge, Avon, Colorado 81620, in the Bachelor Gulch area of Beaver Creek.** The hotel's [official page](https://www.ritzcarlton.com/en/hotels/whrrz-the-ritz-carlton-bachelor-gulch/overview/) lists **+1 970-748-6200** as the hotel phone and **+1 800-576-5582** as the toll free number. The 970 number is the one dialed on this call. A staff member answered it directly, with no phone menu.

**If you call to check a booking, have the confirmation number ready.** On this call, staff said the hotel would not look up a reservation by guest name and dates: "Ritz Carlton has a strong policy for privacy and security, and what we must have is the confirmation number." Once they had one number, they found all three rooms.

On October 2, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called the hotel to confirm an existing booking for three rooms, Friday, December 4 to Tuesday, December 8, 2026: one king room and two rooms with two queen beds, plus a crib. The assistant was told to confirm only and change nothing, except to add a crib request if one was missing.

## Before you call Bachelor Gulch about a booking

The one thing that slowed this call down was the missing confirmation number. The assistant did not have it at the start, asked the customer, and kept staff waiting for about a minute while it checked. Staff were clear that a name and dates would not be enough. If you only have one of several room confirmations, that was fine here: "one of those confirmation numbers is sufficient" to pull up all three rooms booked together.

Staff also asked for the last name spelled before the search worked, so have the exact spelling on the booking ready too.

Say up front what you want done with each request. Here, the crib was already on a two queen room, the customer's instruction put it in the king room, and the result was a note on both. If you want the crib moved rather than added, say "move", and ask staff to read back which room has it.

Marriott's [hotel information page](https://www.marriott.com/stay/hotel-info/whrrz) lists check in at 4:00 PM and check out at 11:00 AM. The call did not discuss either time.
