**The short answer:** to lower a DIRECTV bill, the account holder calls **800-531-5000**, gets past the AI agent to a person, and asks to move to a cheaper package, drop paid add-ons, or speak with retention. A live DIRECTV representative told us on October 24, 2026:

- **There's no retention option in the phone menu.** "It's not available in the phone menu itself." You call customer service or billing, then "you can request to speak with somebody [in] retention, but there's not a direct way. You'd have to speak with somebody first."
- **Three ways to save:** a cheaper **TV package** ("it depends on what channels they'd like to keep"), fewer **receivers**, since "there's a DIRECTV app that's available" as a way "to save money", and **DIRECTV PERKS** "online to check out, as well as other offers available."
- **The account holder has to make the call.** Asked if someone else could, he said: "Account holder, directly."
- **Have the phone number or account number ready.** "Phone number would recognize the account. If not, the account number would work."

DIRECTV's AI agent, which answers first, added two more: **remove paid add-ons** "such as premium channels, sports options, or streaming subscriptions," and on satellite, ask whether "an eligible package without local channels would reduce the price." It warned that "switching package types can affect existing promotions or discounts."

DIRECTV's own [PERKS page](https://www.directv.com/insider/directv-perks/) says the loyalty program is "free to join for eligible new and current residential DIRECTV customers," that members earn tokens "through everyday actions like paying bills on time," and that you can "use tokens for bill credits, movie rentals, reward cards, premium content, sweepstakes entries, and more."

We called on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at about 5:29 pm Central, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. We're not DIRECTV customers and gave no account details, so nobody quoted us a price. This page is what we heard.

## The DIRECTV number to call

DIRECTV's [contact page](https://www.directv.com/support/contact-us/) lists one number, "Call us at 800.531.5000," with "Business hours: Mon-Sun, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM CT". The representative gave different hours: "Monday through Friday 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. And then weekends 7 a.m. to 8 p.m." He didn't say which time zone. If you call near either end of the day, go by the website's 8 am to 9 pm Central.

## How we got past the AI agent to a person

An AI agent, "Eva, DIRECTV's AI agent," answers. This got us to a person in about 2 minutes:

1. **"How can I help you today?"** We said: "I'd like to talk to a live agent about lowering a bill or canceling a service."
2. **"Please say or enter the phone number associated with your DIRECTV service."** We said we didn't have it and asked for a live agent. As the account holder, you'd give it here.
3. **"Do you have your DIRECTV account number?"** Same answer.
4. **"Do you have a satellite dish?"** We said "satellite."
5. **"Is this request to cancel because you are moving?"** We said no.
6. **"One moment while I connect you with an agent."** It announced a wait of "about five seconds."
7. **A representative answered 2 minutes 1 second into the recording.**

That's close to the route on our earlier [DIRECTV customer service call](/blog/directv-customer-service), where saying "cancel service" also reached a person.

**On our first call that evening we never reached a person, and that was our caller's fault.** Eva answered our questions herself, then asked "Would you like me to connect you with a live agent now?" Our caller said "Not yet," kept asking Eva, and ended the call without a person. If Eva offers you a live agent, say yes.

## What to say: a script

> Hi, I'm the account holder. My bill has gone up to [amount] and I'm thinking about my options. Can you go through cheaper packages with the channels I actually watch, take off any add-ons I'm not using, and tell me whether I have any receivers I don't need? If there's nothing better, I'd like to speak with retention.

Before you call: sign in and write down your package, your add-ons and premium channels, and how many receivers you have. Have the phone number on the account or your account number in front of you. Eva said a four-digit PIN "may be requested."
