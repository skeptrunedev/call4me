**The short answer:** call **1-800-430-3376**, The Home Depot's online orders and customer support line. It's open **7 days a week, 6 a.m. to 2 a.m. Eastern.** A voice assistant answers. **Ask for a representative, say no to texting instead, and stay on the line.** On our call, **a live representative answered 1 minute 34 seconds after the call connected.**

The better-known number, **1-800-HOME-DEPOT (1-800-466-3337)**, didn't get us to anyone. Earlier the same day it put us through two keypad menus, said our hold "may be less than three minutes," played hold music until about 7 minutes in, went silent for about 15 minutes, and then sent us to a customer satisfaction survey. No person ever came on the line.

Both numbers are on homedepot.com. Its [Customer Support Answers page](https://www.homedepot.com/c/Customer_Support_Answers) says to call "800-430-3376" and lists "Online orders: 1-800-430-3376" with "We're available 7 days a week, 6am - 2am ET." The same page and the [Customer Service Center](https://www.homedepot.com/c/customer_service) list three lines:

| Line | Number | What homedepot.com says it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Online orders | 1-800-430-3376 | Online orders |
| 1-800-HOME-DEPOT | 1-800-466-3337 | In-store purchases ("Customer Care" in the phone directory) |
| Major appliances | 1-800-455-3869 | "Most appliances eligible for installation services" |

The one hours line on those pages covers all three. Home Depot's [corporate contact page](https://corporate.homedepot.com/page/contact-us) prints 1-800-HOME-DEPOT (1-800-466-3337).

Here's what got us to a person on 1-800-430-3376:

1. A voice assistant answers: "How can I help you today?" **Say you'd like to speak with a representative** (we also said we had general questions about the online orders line).
2. "I'll connect you with someone that can help. Would you like to text with us instead of speaking on the phone?" **Say no thanks, you'll stay on the line.**
3. "Okay, just stay on the line and I'll connect you." About half a minute later, a recording: "All of our representatives are currently assisting other customers."
4. A representative answered about 26 seconds after that, 1:34 into the call.

**What the representative told us** (her answers, not official policy; [see how they compare with homedepot.com](#the-representative-versus-homedepotcom)):

- **Hours:** "From six AM up until 2 AM, Mondays to Sundays."
- **Return windows:** about 48 hours for major appliances, about 7 days for air conditioners, dehumidifiers and "most lawn mowers," and "between thirty days or sixty days or ninety days" for most other things. A full year if you paid with a Home Depot credit card.
- **Receipts:** "Not necessarily." She said they can find purchases by order number or phone number.
- **Online orders:** you can return them to your nearest store, which she called the quickest option, with an "instant credit."
- **Refund timing:** three to five business days for a UPS or carrier return, up to ten business days for major appliances.
- **Checking a gift card:** call the gift card and credit card department at 800-677-0232. homedepot.com lists that number for consumer credit card accounts, and sends gift card scam reports to 1-800-HOME-DEPOT ([see below](#gift-cards-and-scams)).

We called on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, at about 3:36 pm and 4:20 pm Pacific (6:36 and 7:20 pm Eastern), with [call4me](/) placing the calls. These were general questions for this guide, not about any order, purchase or account, and we gave no personal details. This page is what we heard.

## The Home Depot phone menus, step by step

**On 1-800-430-3376 (online orders),** there was no keypad menu on our call:

1. "Thank you for calling The Home Depot. This call may be recorded or used by Home Depot and its authorized vendors. How can I help you today?"
2. We asked for a representative. "I'll connect you with someone that can help. Would you like to text with us instead of speaking on the phone?"
3. We said we'd stay on the line. "Okay, just stay on the line and I'll connect you."
4. Hold, then "All of our representatives are currently assisting other customers. Please hold and your call will be answered in the order it was received." Then a person.

**On 1-800-466-3337 (1-800-HOME-DEPOT),** the voice assistant said "Para español, oprima 9" and "I can help with order management, installation services, and more." It didn't understand "a general question." Saying "representative" brought up this keypad menu:

After customer care, it read more menus: online orders, appliance issues, credit card, installation or special orders, store experience; then large appliances, other online products, your local store, credit card; then an offer to text instead; then place an order, an order you've already placed, or self-service. ## The representative versus homedepot.com

We checked each answer against Home Depot's [Return Policy](https://www.homedepot.com/c/Return_Policy), [Refund Policy](https://www.homedepot.com/c/refund-policy), [Gift Card FAQ](https://www.homedepot.com/c/Gift_Card_FAQ) and [phone directory](https://www.homedepot.com/c/view-directory), all loaded October 13, 2026. Some match, some don't.

| Topic | The representative said | homedepot.com says |
|---|---|---|
| Phone hours | 6 a.m. to 2 a.m., Monday to Sunday | 7 days a week, 6 a.m. to 2 a.m. ET (the Return Policy adds "closed Christmas Day") |
| Most items | "Between thirty days or sixty days or ninety days" | "Most merchandise can be returned within 90 days with proof of purchase." The page lists 90, 30, 7 days, 48 hours and 365 days; we saw no 60-day group |
| Major appliances | "About forty eight hours" | 48 hours, for "refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, dishwashers, and over-the-range or built-in microwaves" |
| Air conditioners, dehumidifiers | "About seven days" | 7 days, unused and in the original factory-sealed packaging |
| Lawn mowers | "Most lawn mowers have a one week return window" | Gas-powered mowers are on the **30-day** list. We didn't find mowers in the 7-day group |
| Home Depot credit card | 365 days | 365 days for the Consumer Credit Card, Pro Xtra Credit Card or Commercial Account, but **not** for the 48-hour, 7-day or 30-day items |
| Receipt required? | "Not necessarily." Purchases can be found by order number or phone number | Returns are "within 90 days with proof of purchase." Stores can look up an online purchase "with your credit card, order number, or email." Card and check receipts "may be located in our system within 90 days." Returns without a receipt need a government photo ID and "may be refunded as a Store Credit at the lowest advertised price" |
| Online order to a store | Yes | Yes: "Take your return back to any The Home Depot store for free, even if you purchased online." Bring the receipt or shipping confirmation email, the card you paid with and a valid ID. Major appliances are excluded |
| In-store refund | Instant, "through a store credit" | In-store returns are "processed instantly," and refunds go "to the original form of payment" unless noted. Store credit is for gift card or store credit purchases, or returns without proof of purchase |
| UPS or carrier return | "Three to five business days" | "About 7 business days to process your return," then "up to 10 business days for your bank" |
| Major appliance refund | "Up to ten business days" | Up to 12 business days for Home Depot to process, plus your bank's time |

**The refund timing is where they differ most.** The website's two steps add up to longer than what the representative told us for a mailed return. The Refund Policy page lists bank times too: up to 10 business days for a credit or debit card, up to 30 business days for a prepaid debit card, instant for cash, and up to 24 hours for a gift card, which is refunded as store credit. For the exact policy, we'd trust the written pages over one phone answer.

## Gift cards and scams

We asked how to check whether a Home Depot gift card is authentic and what to do about a suspected scam.

**The representative:** "They should call our gift card department. So, we do have a Home Depot credit card and gift card department, which can just authenticate that for you." She first said "I think an 833," checked, and then gave **800-677-0232**, saying you can call it "anytime" to verify "any gift cards, coupons, or any Home Depot credit cards."

**homedepot.com:** the [phone directory](https://www.homedepot.com/c/view-directory) lists 1-800-677-0232 as "Credit Card: Consumer Accounts." It doesn't list a gift card verification line. The [Gift Card FAQ](https://www.homedepot.com/c/Gift_Card_FAQ) says:

- To check a balance, ask a cashier at any store, or go to homedepot.com/gift-cards/balance (account login required).
- If you're the victim of a gift card scam involving a Home Depot gift card, "contact our Customer Care team to report the scam at 1-800-HOME-DEPOT (1-800-466-3337)."
- "Never give your gift card number to anyone over the phone." For more help reporting a scam, it gives the Federal Trade Commission at 877-382-4357.

The [Gift Card Scams page](https://www.homedepot.com/c/gift_card_scams) says "no legitimate transaction will ever require you to pay specifically with gift cards," and that government agencies "will not ask you to purchase gift cards for any reason." We didn't call 800-677-0232, so we can't tell you whether that line handles gift cards.
