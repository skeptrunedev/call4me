**The short answer:** the Airbnb customer service number is **1-844-234-2500**. Airbnb's help center says "You can always call us directly at 1-844-234-2500" ([source](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/971)). An automated voice answers first. To reach a person: **press 2** if your phone number isn't on an Airbnb account (press 1 if it's on one under another number), **say "agent"** when it asks why you're calling, then **press 0** after it reads you a help message. **A live support specialist picked up 1 minute 56 seconds after we dialed.**

Airbnb's [contact article](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1542) lists a second number: "You can also call us at +1-415-800-5959." It says to contact Airbnb "by messaging or chat" first. We called the 844 number.

The representative told us:

- **If a host cancels at the last minute, Airbnb helps you find another listing and processes a full refund.** "We can help provide him another listing to find another listing, and we will process a full refund." He added that the host "will have consequences."
- **It depends on who cancels.** His first answer was "it depends on the cancellation of the host or the guest."
- **Refunds to a Visa or Mastercard take 3 to 5 business days.** "It depends on your mode of payment."
- **Have your first and last name and your reservation code ready.** The name is "for verification purposes." His first words to us were a request for the email address on the account.
- **Phone support is open 24/7.** "Yeah, we are open 24-7."
- **On callbacks, his answer was about messages, not phone holds.** "If they put it in the note that they need a callback, definitely specialists will call back."

On an earlier call the same afternoon, a different representative said 1-844-234-2500 is "the only phone number that we have" if you're calling from the US, that **English support is 24/7**, and that other languages "may vary from country to country." He listed **Spanish, Chinese, Italian and German**.

We called on Friday, October 16, 2026, at about 3:39 pm Eastern (the earlier call was at about 3:33 pm), with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Airbnb customer service phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 1-844-234-2500.** "Hi, thanks for calling Airbnb." It looks up the number you're calling from. Ours wasn't on an account, so it said: "We weren't able to find an Airbnb account associated with the number you're calling from."
2. **"If you have an Airbnb account associated with another phone number, please press 1. If not, please press 2."** We pressed 2. If you call from the phone number on your account, you may skip this step. We didn't test that.
3. **"In a few sentences, please let us know why you're calling today."** It gives examples: "cancel a reservation or refund" or "check my reservation status." We said "Agent."
4. **It guesses a topic and reads a help message.** It said "Got it. I'll explain that and send you a message with more details," then read tips for logging in. On our earlier call, the same word (per our call log) got a message about declined reservation requests and Instant Book instead.
5. **"If you still need help, don't worry. Just return to the call and press 0 to be transferred to a specialist."** Press 0. It says it waits two minutes before disconnecting.
6. **A recording notice.** "We're going to record this call to help us with training and quality."
7. **The transfer.** "Please wait while I transfer your call to a representative." About 10 seconds later, a support specialist answered.

## What to have ready

- **The email address on your Airbnb account.** It was the representative's first question.
- **Your first and last name,** "for verification purposes."
- **Your reservation code,** "so that the agent or the specialist will know what's going on on that reservation."
- **How you paid,** if you're asking about a refund. The timing he gave, 3 to 5 business days, was for Visa or Mastercard.
- **The phone number on your account.** If you call from it, the system may find your account. If not, you'll need to press 1 or 2 first.

## Things that don't work

On October 16, 2026, at the "why you're calling today" prompt:

- **Saying "representative".** On our first call it answered "We didn't quite get that. Please try again."
- **Expecting "agent" to transfer you right away.** It didn't. Both times, the system picked a topic we hadn't asked about and read a help message first. The transfer came after the "press 0" offer at the end of that message.
