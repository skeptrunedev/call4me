**The short answer:** Fifth Third Bank customer service is **800-972-3030**. Fifth Third's [customer service page](https://www.53.com/content/fifth-third/en/customer-service.html) says "You can reach us at 800-972-3030, Monday through Friday", and its "Call Us" box lists the same number for Customer Service, plus:

- **Business:** 877-534-2264
- **Commercial:** 866-475-0729
- **International:** 513-579-5353

**The hours on that page disagree with themselves.** The FAQ says Monday to Friday 8 am to 6 pm Eastern and Saturday 10 am to 4 pm Eastern. The "Call us" block on the same page says Monday to Friday 8 am to 8 pm Eastern, Saturday 8 am to 5 pm Eastern, closed Sunday, with lost or stolen cards handled 24/7. We called on a Thursday afternoon, inside both windows.

**We reached a live human representative, not a virtual assistant.** Here's how it went without an account number:

1. An automated menu offers a Spanish option, then: press 1 if you're a Direct Express customer, press star to report a lost or stolen card, "otherwise, please remain on the line." **Stay on the line.**
2. It asks: "Please enter your full account or card number." Saying "representative" didn't work. It said "Let's try again."
3. **Don't enter a number.** After the third prompt, it said "Your call is being transferred due to invalid entries." (Our caller's brief allowed pressing 0. Key presses don't show in the recording or our transcript, so we can't say whether it did.)
4. A hold message mentioned "increased call volume related to the Comerica integration" and longer than normal hold times. Ours was short.
5. **A representative answered 1 minute 44 seconds into the recording**, about 33 seconds after the transfer message. The first thing they asked was to confirm an account number or Social Security number. When we said we only had general questions, they said "Sure" and took them.

**What the representative told us:**

- **Monthly fee:** "For a personal checking account, there's actually no fee." Then: "Monthly fee." They didn't name a specific account, and we didn't ask which one. We couldn't confirm this against a checking account page on 53.com, so treat it as what one representative said on the phone, not as a published price.
- **Overdraft:** a fee may be charged if the account goes negative and you don't pay the balance: "that is when you will be assessed for the overdraft fee." No amount was given, and we didn't ask for one.
- **Verifying an existing customer:** "we need to have the whole account number or the whole social security number."

We called on Thursday, October 8, 2026, at about 4:12 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Fifth Third customer service phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 800-972-3030.** "Welcome to Fifth Third, where we're making banking a Fifth Third better. Para Español, oprima la estrella."
2. **"If you are a Direct Express customer, press 1. If you would like to report a card lost or stolen, please press star. Otherwise, please remain on the line for further assistance."** For anything else, wait.
3. **"Please enter your full account or card number."** Customers key in a number here. We had no account number to enter, and saying "representative" got "Let's try again." The prompt played three times.
4. **"Your call is being transferred due to invalid entries."** After the third prompt without a valid number, the menu sends you on.
5. **A hold notice:** "Due to increased call volume related to the Comerica integration, you may experience longer than normal hold times." Then a recording notice and "Please hold for a brief moment while we transfer you to the next available customer service professional."
6. **A representative picks up** and asks you to confirm with your account number or Social Security number.

## What to have ready

- **Your full account number or full Social Security number,** if you're an existing customer. The representative said that's what they need to verify the caller, and it's the first thing they asked us for.
- **Your full account or card number** for the automated menu, if you want to use it.
- **Nothing,** if you only have general questions. We got answers without giving any account details.

## Things that don't work

On October 8, 2026, at the "Please enter your full account or card number" prompt:

What got us through was not entering a valid number until the menu gave up and transferred the call.
