**The short answer:** to dispute a charge on a **Wells Fargo credit card**, call **1-800-642-4720**. Wells Fargo's [credit card FAQ](https://www.wellsfargo.com/help/credit-cards/credit-card-faqs/) says: "To initiate a dispute please call 1-800-642-4720 or sign on to Wells Fargo Online and access Track Claims in Account Services." The same page calls 1-800-642-4720 "our 24-hour Customer Service number." For a dispute you've already filed, it lists **1-800-390-0533**.

**Debit card claims use other numbers.** Wells Fargo's [claims page](https://www.wellsfargo.com/debit-card/claims/) lists **1-800-548-9554** (Monday to Friday, 7 am to midnight Eastern) for signature and Visa debit purchases and unauthorized transactions, and **1-877-230-8708, option 3** (Monday to Friday, 7 am to 7 pm Eastern) for ATM and non-fraud PIN purchases. More [below](#wells-fargo-debit-card-disputes).

This got us to a person on the credit card line, with no card:

1. At "please provide your full account or card number", **say "I don't have it."** The menu itself says: "If you don't have this information, say, I don't have it."
2. At "In a few words, please tell me the reason for your call", **say you want to dispute a charge.**
3. At "please say the type of account", **say "my credit card"**, then **"personal account."**
4. "Give me a moment to connect you to someone that can help." **A live representative answered 2 minutes 52 seconds into the call.**

What the representative told us:

- **You verify first, then file.** "We need to verify the account using basic information that they do have on file, and then after the verification, that's when the time that they can request to file a dispute."
- **Provisional credit: yes.** "Yes, ma'am, they will."
- **Timeline: "60 days, actually, is the timeline for that."**
- **Online works too.** "They can actually have it online if they want to, or they can just give us a call and have it disputed, or ask for assistance in filing a dispute."
- **Hours, as he gave them:** "Mondays to Fridays from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. Central Time," after first saying 8 am to 6 pm. We reached him at 5:27 pm Central on a Saturday, and Wells Fargo's site calls the line 24-hour, so treat his hours with care.

**One answer was wrong.** We asked if there's a time limit, and he said "No, ma'am." There is one. Under federal law your credit card billing error rights depend on a **written** notice that reaches the card company **within 60 days** after the first statement showing the charge ([FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/using-credit-cards-and-disputing-charges); see [our general guide](/blog/how-to-dispute-a-charge)). Call early and follow up in writing.

We called on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at 6:24 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. We don't have a Wells Fargo account, said so, and gave no card or personal details. This page is what we heard.

## The Wells Fargo credit card phone tree, step by step

This is the menu as it played on October 24, 2026, calling 1-800-642-4720 with no card.

1. **Call 1-800-642-4720.** "Thank you for calling Wells Fargo. This call may be recorded, monitored, or analyzed by Wells Fargo and its service providers." Spanish: "oprima nueve."
2. **"To get started, please provide your full account or card number."** Cardholders enter it on the keypad.
3. **Without one:** "I couldn't find anything. If you need more time to find it, just say, hold on." Then "I still couldn't find anything."
4. **"To get you to the right place, I'll need to collect this information before I transfer you. If you don't have this information, say, I don't have it."** We said "I don't have it."
5. **"In a few words, please tell me the reason for your call."** We said "General question about disputing a charge."
6. **"To route you correctly, please say the type of account you need help with. You can say things like my checking account or my credit card."** We said "My credit card."
7. **"Which is this? A personal account or a business account?"** We said "Personal account."
8. **"Give me a moment to connect you to someone that can help."** An ADA notice plays, with 1-800-642-4720 read out, then a second recording notice.
9. **A representative answers** 2 minutes 52 seconds in.

## Wells Fargo debit card disputes

From Wells Fargo's page [Understanding the Claims Process](https://www.wellsfargo.com/debit-card/claims/):

- **Online:** open a claim after signing on to Wells Fargo Online, and check its status there.
- **By phone:** 1-800-548-9554 for "Transactions you authorized with your signature, Visa card purchases, or unauthorized transactions", Monday to Friday, 7 am to midnight Eastern. 1-877-230-8708, option 3, for ATM transactions and non-fraud PIN purchases, Monday to Friday, 7 am to 7 pm Eastern. Any other claim: 1-800-869-3557.
- **Timing:** "Within 10 business days, your claim will be resolved" or "your account will receive a temporary credit if additional investigation is needed."

We didn't call the debit lines.

## What to have ready

- **Your Wells Fargo card or account number.** It's the first prompt.
- **The details on file** that Wells Fargo uses to verify you.
- **The charge:** merchant, date, amount, and why you're disputing it.
- **The merchant's terms.** Wells Fargo's FAQ says the merchant's terms and conditions "can impact your dispute," for example its rules on canceling or returns.
