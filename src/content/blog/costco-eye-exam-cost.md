**A glasses eye exam at Bayside Family Eyecare inside Costco Santa Clara was quoted at $99 on October 6, 2026.** The representative said the health check was optional. The practice's [booking page](https://baysidefamilyeyecare.setmore.com/book) lists a **$138 total package with Optomap retinal imaging**, alongside the $99 glasses exam without imaging.

That is one independent doctor's price, not a nationwide Costco rate. [Costco directs people to call their local warehouse or independent optometrist](https://www.costco.com/f/-/optical) to arrange an exam. We used [call4me](/) to ask two Bay Area offices about prices. Santa Clara answered; Mountain View reached a recorded greeting. We did not book an appointment or receive care.

## How much is a Costco eye exam without insurance?

Here is what the Santa Clara practice quoted for someone paying without insurance:

| Exam | Price quoted October 6 | What we verified |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Adult glasses exam including refraction | $99 | We asked for that service; staff gave this price |
| Glasses exam with the optional health check | $138 | Staff described the health check as optional; the booking page identifies the package as including Optomap |
| Spherical contact lens exam and fitting | $149 | Staff quote; the booking page specifies established wearers |
| Contact lens exam for astigmatism | $169 | Staff quote; the booking page specifies established wearers |
| Multifocal contact lens exam | $199 | Staff quote, corroborated by the booking page |

The $138 listing is a package total, **not a $138 imaging surcharge on top of $99**. The call did not establish a separate imaging price for a contact lens exam, insurance reimbursement, or every possible additional charge. Ask for the total for the exact exam you need.

The representative also mentioned monovision in the $199 answer. We did not separately corroborate a monovision listing on the booking page, so confirm that service and price with the practice. Prices may change after this dated inquiry.

## Does the contact lens exam include fitting and followups?

We asked for the price of a standard soft contact lens exam and fitting, then asked about followups. Staff said **followups are complimentary within three months**. That matches the practice's [followup listing](https://baysidefamilyeyecare.setmore.com/book).

The same booking page describes the listed contact exams as being for established wearers and separately lists a **$39 training fee for new wearers**. That fee is website evidence, not something answered on our call. Confirm the combined price if you are new to contacts, need training or want imaging added. We did not establish that a glasses exam, contact fitting and imaging all fit inside the lowest listed price.

## Do you need a Costco membership for an eye exam?

**Santa Clara staff said no.** Mountain View's [practice contact page](https://www.hanhtranoptometry.com/contact-us.html) also says membership is not required for an exam. Those statements concern the doctor's exam, not the requirements for buying glasses or contacts from Costco Optical.

Ask the practice about its own insurance arrangements. An optical department's insurance statement does not establish coverage for an independent doctor's exam. Our calls requested prices without insurance and did not check a policy.

We called around 6:14 PM Pacific on Tuesday, October 6. Mountain View's website listed a 6:30 PM weekday close, but the greeting said **6 PM**. That conflict is why we cannot treat warehouse hours, or even a practice's web listing, as proof a person will answer. We left no message.

Santa Clara provides an [online booking page](https://baysidefamilyeyecare.setmore.com/book). Its existence is a website finding; we did not verify a live appointment slot or complete a booking.
