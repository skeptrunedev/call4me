**The short answer:** to dispute a charge on a Chase credit card by phone, call Chase **Card Services at 1-800-432-3117**, the "Card Customer Service" number on [Chase's customer service page](https://www.chase.com/digital/customer-service). It's also the number on the back of most Chase credit cards. **The specialist we reached said the line is open 24/7.** Chase's [dispute page](https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/dispute) says the fastest route is online: "Sign in to your Chase account, find the transaction, choose the arrow and follow the instructions."

How a dispute by phone goes, from our call and Chase's page:

1. **Wait until the charge posts.** Chase says pending credit card charges generally post in 1 to 3 business days, and you can dispute after that. The specialist told us the same: you can dispute "as long as it's not pending."
2. **Call 1-800-432-3117 and enter your 16-digit card number.** After the pound key, the menu offers: "For an unrecognized purchase, or to file a dispute, press three."
3. **Tell the specialist the amount and why you're disputing it.** That's what they said to have ready: "the amount and the details why you want to dispute it."
4. **Chase works it with the merchant.** "We just need to gather information and call the merchant on your behalf to get a refund."
5. **Expect about 30 days.** "In most cases, within 30 days. It might take longer, depending on the case." Chase's page says disputes "may take up to 90 days."

**Debit card disputes go somewhere else.** "You'll have to contact the debit card department." Chase's dispute page lists **1-866-564-2262** for debit card pending charges, and Chase's customer service page lists **1-800-935-9935** for personal banking.

**Don't wait on the deadline.** The specialist didn't give one, but Chase's own page says to submit "within 60 days of the transaction first appearing on your statement." Federal law ties your strongest credit card rights to that same 60 days (more in our [general guide to disputing a charge](/blog/how-to-dispute-a-charge)).

## The Chase Card Services phone tree, step by step

This is the menu as it played on October 24, 2026, calling 1-800-432-3117 with no card.

1. **Call 1-800-432-3117.** "This call will be monitored and recorded, and your voice may be used for verification." Then: "Welcome to Chase Card Services."
2. **"Please enter your full 16-digit credit card account number. If you need more time, press star."** Cardmembers enter the card number here.
3. **"If you are calling to report your card lost, stolen, or damaged, or to check the status of an application, press the pound key."**
4. **The pound key list:** "If you are calling to report your card lost or stolen, press 1. If you are calling to replace a worn or damaged card, press 2. For an unrecognized purchase, or to file a dispute, press three. To check application status, press 4." This is the dispute option. We didn't press 3, since we had no card to dispute on.
5. **"If you do not have your account number available, press 1."** Then: "Please enter your 9-digit Social Security number."
6. **We said "I don't have that. Agent."** The menu answered: "To speak with a customer service specialist, press or say 0." Our caller said "0".
7. **"Okay, I'm connecting you with a customer service specialist."** A business card option plays ("press or say 1"), then a short welcome message.
8. **A specialist answers,** 2 minutes 14 seconds in, and asks who's calling.

## What the specialist told us

- **How it works:** Chase gathers the details "and call[s] the merchant on your behalf to get a refund."
- **What to have ready:** "the amount and the details why you want to dispute it."
- **When you can dispute:** "As long as it's not pending." We asked how long after a charge you have, and that was the whole answer. Chase's page gives the real limit: 60 days from the statement.
- **Temporary credit:** "In some cases, we put temporary credit. In some cases, it will be put on hold, so it won't earn interest."
- **How long it takes:** "In most cases, within 30 days. It might take longer, depending on the case."
- **Online or app:** "You can do it online or in the app." We asked whether that's faster, and the specialist didn't say.
- **Hours:** "We're open 24-7." We reached a person at 6:29 pm Eastern on a Saturday.
- **Debit cards:** a separate department.

## What Chase's dispute page adds

Chase's page [Dispute a charge](https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/dispute) says:

- **Contact the merchant first,** and keep track of "the date you contact them, who you speak with, and their response." Chase may ask for it.
- **Deadline:** submit "within 60 days of the transaction first appearing on your statement."
- **Resolution:** disputes "may take up to 90 days for credit card and/or debit card disputes."
- **Credit cards:** you don't have to pay the disputed amount, or related interest and fees, while it's investigated. You still owe at least the minimum on the rest of your balance.
- **Debit cards:** "Most of the time, a provisional credit will be applied to the account while we investigate the debit card dispute." That "usually occurs within 48 hours but may take up to 10 business days."
- **Lost, stolen or unauthorized use** of a credit card: 1-800-955-9060.

## What to have ready

- **Your 16-digit Chase card number.** It's the first prompt.
- **The last 4 or full Social Security number,** in case the menu asks for it.
- **The charge amount, date and merchant,** and why you're disputing it.
- **Your notes from contacting the merchant:** the date, who you spoke with, what they said.
- **Receipts, emails or tracking numbers** that support your side.
