**To cancel Factor meals, sign in on a desktop browser and open Plan Settings. Under Status, choose Deactivate My Plan, finish the cancellation prompts, and select Deactivate Now.** Those are the steps in [Factor's current cancellation guide](https://www.factor75.com/about/how-to-cancel-factor-subscription). You can also call **(888) 573 5727**; a representative told us phone cancellation needs the account holder's full name and email.

**Check the next order separately before you finish.** On our second call, the representative said cancelling the account does not itself cancel an upcoming order. Their advice was to cancel or skip that order, save the change, then cancel the subscription. If the order has passed its cutoff, the first representative said the box will still be processed.

There is a deadline conflict to know about. Factor's website gives **11:59 pm PST, five days before delivery** in its skipping instructions. Both representatives gave **9:59 pm Pacific**, and the second also said a week before delivery. Those answers did not reconcile. Ask support to confirm the cutoff for your actual delivery date and whether the next order can still be stopped.

We called on Saturday, October 3, 2026, at about 11 am Pacific. [call4me](/) reached a human on both calls. These were general inquiries for this guide: we did not have a Factor subscription to cancel, and no account or order was changed.

## How to cancel a Factor subscription online

On a desktop or laptop browser:

1. Sign in to your Factor account and open Plan Settings.
2. Find Status and choose Deactivate My Plan.
3. If you want to continue rather than accept an offer, select Cancel Anyway.
4. Choose your reason, then select Deactivate Now.
5. Check that the plan is deactivated and keep the confirmation email. Our first representative said the email should arrive within 24 hours.

The button labels come from [Factor's guide](https://www.factor75.com/about/how-to-cancel-factor-subscription). We did not sign in to a subscription to test the screens. If the controls are missing or you cannot tell whether the plan is deactivated, ask support to check its status and the next scheduled order.

## Can you cancel Factor by phone?

Yes. Both representatives answered general cancellation questions; the first said support can help cancel the plan after finding the account. Have the **account holder's full name and account email** ready.

Call **(888) 573 5727**. Factor publishes this number on its [consumer bundles page](https://www.factor75.com/pages/add-ons/bundles), with hours of **6 am to 11 pm Eastern on weekdays** and **7 am to 7 pm Eastern on Saturday and Sunday**. We reached people during the Saturday window.

### The Factor customer service phone menu

The menu we heard on both calls:

1. Select **1 for Factor ready meals**. Option 2 was for Factor supplements.
2. Enter the requested phone number, followed by the pound key. For an actual cancellation, have the number associated with the account ready.
3. Answer whether you are the account holder: **1 for yes, 2 for no**. Answer truthfully when calling for someone else.
4. Select **4 for account cancellation**. The other options were delivery, ingredients, technical support and other inquiries.
5. The system transfers you to the next available representative. Explain what you want cancelled and complete any verification they require.

Our general inquiries reached people without pretending to have a subscription. That does not establish that someone calling as an assistant can change every customer's account without further authorization. Follow the actual prompts if the menu changes.

## When do you need to cancel Factor to avoid the next box?

**Confirm the deadline for your delivery schedule before relying on a general time.** Our calls and the website disagreed:

| Source checked October 3 | What it said |
|---|---|
| Factor's consumer website, in the skip section | 11:59 pm PST, five days before delivery |
| First support call | 9:59 pm Pacific; cutoff day depends on delivery day |
| Second support call | 9:59 pm PST, a week before delivery; also confirmed weekday examples that did not consistently fit that interval |

The first representative gave Monday as the cutoff for Saturday delivery, Tuesday for Sunday delivery, Wednesday for Monday or Tuesday delivery, and Thursday for Wednesday delivery. We asked again on the second call, but did not get an explanation that resolved the difference between the stated interval, the examples and the website.

We cannot establish a universal cancellation deadline from those answers. Tell support your actual delivery day, ask for the exact date, time and timezone, and ask whether cancelling now will stop the specific upcoming order. Do this while there is still time to act, rather than waiting until either published time.

### Will the next box still arrive after you cancel?

It can. The first representative said an order already beyond its cutoff will keep progressing. The second emphasized that changing the subscription and stopping an order are separate actions.

Before ending the call, ask support to confirm both:

* The subscription is deactivated and will not automatically resume.
* The next order is skipped or cancelled, or it is already too late to stop it.

We did not ask about a damaged delivery or test refund eligibility for a real order. Do not assume subscription cancellation also grants a refund for an already processed box.

## How to skip or pause Factor instead of cancelling

Skipping a week prevents that week's order; it does not mean you cancelled the plan. Our first representative made that distinction explicitly.

Factor's [website instructions](https://www.factor75.com/about/how-to-cancel-factor-subscription) say to select the delivery date, open Edit Delivery and choose Skip Week. In the app, choose the week, open the three dots beside its delivery date and select Skip Week. Finish the action and check the week is marked as skipped.

For a longer stop, the website describes plan deactivation as skipping deliveries indefinitely, with the option to reactivate later. Decide whether you want one week skipped or the subscription stopped, and ask support to verify the corresponding status.

## How to cancel Factor in the app, and what Delete Account means

Factor's current website lists an iOS route through Settings, Personal Information, Delete Account, then Continue to deactivate. The page describes the result as plan deactivation.

Our calls did not establish when that route deletes personal data. The first representative described cancelling the subscription before a separate deletion request that can take weeks. The second said a Delete Account action deletes data, but our question shortened the website's button wording to Continue. We did not test the actual iOS screens.

**If your goal is to stop deliveries, use the desktop plan deactivation route above or ask support to do it and confirm the next order's status.** If you also want your personal data deleted, request that separately and ask what will be removed and when. These calls do not prove immediate data deletion or an Android cancellation path.

## How do you confirm Factor is cancelled?

Our first representative said a confirmation email is sent within **24 hours** after cancellation. Keep it alongside the status shown in your account. If no email arrives, contact support and ask them to confirm the subscription status and send confirmation again.

A skipped delivery is evidence about one order. It is not proof that the entire subscription is deactivated. Confirm both the plan and the next order when you are trying to stop future deliveries.

## Have your agent make this call

After [installing call4me](/mcp), give your agent the account information privately and use this prompt:

> Call Factor at (888) 573 5727. Choose ready meals, answer the account holder question truthfully, then choose account cancellation. I want my subscription deactivated. My account name is [your name] and my email is [your account email]. Before making the change, confirm the exact cutoff for my delivery on [date], including the timezone. Check whether my next order can still be cancelled or skipped, and stop it if possible before deactivating the subscription. If it is already too late, tell me whether it will still be delivered and charged. Decline offers to keep the plan. Do not delete my personal data or buy anything. Confirm both the plan status and the next order status, and ask for the confirmation email. Ask me if further verification is needed.
