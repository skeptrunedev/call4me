**The short answer:** to set an EJ-125 judgment debtor exam date at Ventura Superior Court, no reservation is needed: the civil clerk said to pick any Monday through Thursday at 8:30. The civil clerk's line is **805-289-8525**. The court's [phone directory](https://ventura.courts.ca.gov/general-information/phone-directory) lists that number as "Civil" under the Ventura Courthouse, next to the main number, **805-289-8900**. The recorded greeting gives the Civil Department's location as **800 South Victoria Avenue, Room 210, Ventura**, and its office and telephone hours as **8 a.m. to 4 p.m., Monday through Friday, except holidays**. The court's [civil page](https://www.ventura.courts.ca.gov/civil.html) lists the same clerk's office hours.

**Press 3 to speak with a clerk.** On this call a live clerk answered **1 minute 16 seconds** into the recording, with no hold message.

What the clerk said about an EJ-125 judgment debtor exam in that civil case:

- **No reservation is needed.** "There's no reservations for those type of cases."
- **Pick a date yourself.** "You just need to pick any day Monday through, uh, Thursday at 8:30."
- **Write it in the hearing box.** Asked whether the filer can put a Monday to Thursday 8:30 date in the hearing box when filing the EJ-125, the clerk said "Correct."
- **Same department.** Asked whether it would stay in Department 40, the case's department, the clerk said "Correct."
- **Nothing else holds the date.** Asked whether anything else was needed to hold the date ahead of filing, the clerk said "No."

## What the call did not settle

- **No date was set or confirmed.** The clerk described the process. Nothing was reserved, and the EJ-125 was not filed on the call.
- **Service timing wasn't discussed.** The clerk didn't say how far out to pick the date. The current Judicial Council [EJ-125 form](https://www.courts.ca.gov/documents/ej125.pdf) (Rev. Oct. 1, 2024) says the order "must be served no less than 30 days before the date set for the examination," or 10 days for an examination of someone other than the judgment debtor. Count that before you choose a Monday to Thursday date.
- **One department, one case.** The answer was about one civil case assigned to Department 40. The clerk didn't say whether every civil department, small claims or a different courthouse works the same way.
- **The 8:30 time is as spoken.** The clerk said "Thursday at 8:30" without saying a.m. or p.m.
- **Who the clerk needed.** Before answering, the clerk asked for the name of the party to be examined, not only the case name. Have that ready.

The court's [civil page](https://www.ventura.courts.ca.gov/civil.html) says ex parte hearings and Law and Motion matters are limited per day and must be reserved, and points people to this same number. The clerk's answer was that a debtor's exam is not one of those: there was nothing to reserve.

## How the call went

The customer's brief asked three things: whether the clerk assigns the EJ-125 hearing date at filing or a date has to be reserved first, how to reserve one if so, and what to write in the date box. The first answer made the other two simple.

A few things worth knowing for your own call:

- **The clerk asked for the examinee first.** Giving the full case caption wasn't enough. The clerk wanted the name of the party to be examined before answering.
- **The caller confirmed each point back.** It repeated the days, the time, the hearing box and the department, and the clerk said "Correct" to each.
- **The call4me summary went one step further than the clerk.** Its recap said to pick a date "far enough out for service." That's sensible, but the clerk didn't say it. We left it out of the answers above and put the form's 30 day rule in the limits instead.
