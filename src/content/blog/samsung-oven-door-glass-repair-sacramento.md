## Order the glass before you book

The useful answer here wasn't the price. It was that this shop won't source the part. If you call around for Samsung appliance repair in Sacramento with a broken oven door, ask each company early whether they supply the glass, because the answer changes what you need to do first.

If they don't, find the exact outer glass panel for your model number before booking, and check it for cracks when it arrives. Then ask the shop for the labor price with your part, whether that includes the visit, and what they charge if the panel turns out to be the wrong one.

A referral line like this one also means the first voice isn't the shop doing the work. Ask for the name of the company you're being sent to, as this call did, so you can look it up before anyone comes to your home. When you're comparing the best appliance repair in Sacramento for a job like this, that name, the labor price and the part policy are the three things to write down.
