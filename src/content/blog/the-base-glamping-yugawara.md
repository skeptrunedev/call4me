**The Base Glamping Yugawara accepts luggage sent ahead by takkyubin (宅急便), and staff can ship it out again at check-out.** Write your **check-in date and your name** on the parcel, and say in the notes field of your booking that luggage is coming ahead. The property's [official site](https://thebase-gp.com/yugawara) lists **0465-20-8112** (from abroad, **+81 465-20-8112**), answered 9:00 to 18:00 Japan time, at 473-1 Miyakami, Yugawara, Kanagawa, in the Yugawara onsen town. That is the number the call dialed.

On Wednesday, October 14, 2026, at about 2:54 pm Japan time, a call4me customer's assistant called that number and spoke Japanese throughout. A staff member answered directly. There was no phone menu. The whole call took about two minutes.

What staff said:

- **Sending luggage ahead before check-in: yes.** "はい、大丈夫です" (Yes, that's fine).
- **Label it with the check-in date and the guest's name.** Staff asked for both "必ず" (without fail).
- **Mention it when you book.** The customer had not booked yet. Staff asked them to write something like "宅急便を事前にお送りします" (I'm sending luggage ahead by takkyubin) in the reservation's memo field.
- **Shipping out at check-out: yes.** "発送もできますので、対応いたします" (We can ship it too, so we'll handle it).
- **Bring the outgoing luggage to the front desk.** Staff confirmed that was the way to do it.

## What this call did not confirm

- **No carrier was named.** The question used 宅急便, which is Yamato's service name but also a common word for parcel delivery. Staff did not say whether Sagawa or Japan Post parcels are handled the same way.
- **No fees, size or weight limits, number of bags, or how many days ahead a parcel may arrive** were discussed. Nor was how the outgoing shipment is paid for.
- **The advance notice answer was not a clean yes or no.** The assistant asked whether advance notice was unnecessary, and staff answered "はい、お願いいたします", which can be read either way. The useful instruction came right after: use the memo field when you book.
- **No reservation was made or changed.** The assistant said it would contact the property again after booking.

## How to ask a Japanese hotel about luggage forwarding

Two properties, two slightly different sets of rules. The Base Glamping Yugawara wanted the check-in date on the label and a note in the booking. Anran wanted only the guest's name, timed to arrive on the check-in day. If you are forwarding luggage between Yugawara hotels and the rest of your trip in Japan, ask each property rather than assuming the same rules apply.

Questions worth covering in one call:

1. Can you receive luggage before check-in, and how many days early can it arrive?
2. What should the label say: guest name, check-in date, booking number?
3. Do you need notice, and where: the booking notes, an email, a phone call?
4. Can you ship luggage out at check-out, with which carrier, and how is it paid?
5. Any size, weight or count limits?

Both calls were in Japanese, with the assistant using polite phrasing throughout.
