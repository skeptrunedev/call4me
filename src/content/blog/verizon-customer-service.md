**The short answer:** the Verizon customer service number for wireless, the Verizon phone number to call about your plan or a line, is **800-922-0204**. Verizon's support pages list it under "Speak to an agent", open **8 am to 7 pm Monday to Saturday** and **8 am to 5 pm Sunday**.

**Verizon's AI assistant** answers. Before it does anything with your account it wants **the mobile number on the account**, then it checks it's you. It offers a **text confirmation** to that phone first. Ours expired, so it switched to the **Account PIN**, and the PIN worked. Have both ready. To reach a person, say **"An agent, please."** On our call, "Could I just speak with a representative, please?" didn't work, and "An agent, please" did.

We reached a person 6 minutes 34 seconds into the call. They said switching our 3 lines from **5G Do More** to **Unlimited Welcome** would take the bill from **$219.08** to about **$170.58 a month**, including surcharges, government taxes and fees. That's about **$48 a month** less. These were this account's numbers on October 1, 2026. Yours will be different.

Our call hit call4me's 25 minute limit mid-question, and about a minute later **the same representative called us back**. On that call they said the line we asked about had **no device payment balance or agreement**, only the plan, and that to keep the number **you start the port-out yourself**, and Verizon disconnects the line on its own once the number has moved. That call is [below](#verizon-called-us-back).

We called on Thursday, October 1, 2026, at about 2:11 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking questions on my family's Verizon account: 3 lines on 5G Do More. It asked about cheaper plans and about cancelling one line. Nothing was changed. Earlier that day we also called without an account, and the AI assistant wouldn't go any further. That call is [further down](#if-you-call-without-an-account).

## What Verizon told us

These are what the AI assistant and the representative said about this account on October 1, 2026. Prices and the bill are this account's, on that date.

**The plans, from the AI assistant, for one line on 5G Do More at $69 a month:**

| Plan | Per month, as read out for that line |
|---|---|
| 5G Do More (current) | $69 |
| Unlimited Welcome | $50 |
| Verizon Simplicity | $55 |
| Unlimited Plus | $65 |

The AI assistant couldn't give a 3 line total with taxes and fees. The representative did.

**Switching all 3 lines to Unlimited Welcome, from the representative:**

- After the second hold: "if ever that you've decided to change your plan from 5G Do More to Unlimited Welcome, you can actually save up to $50."
- The current bill on 5G Do More: **$219.08**.
- On Unlimited Welcome: "around $170 and some change", then after another hold, **$170.58**. Asked if that included taxes and fees, they said it already included "surcharges, government taxes, and everything."
- That's **$48.50 a month less**.

**What you'd give up, in the representative's words:** "To tell you honestly, there is no much difference between the two." Unlimited Welcome has "unlimited calls, text and data, and international Mexico and Canada included." 5G Do More also includes "cloud storage and Disney Plus." If someone on the account wants those, "you could actually add them back as a perk."

**Before giving the number, they asked about usage.** "How do you usually use your data allowances?" Our caller said it was light: mostly calls, texts and web. They also asked: "Is there any family member that is still not registered on your account?"

**The pitch to keep the line:** when our caller said we might drop a line, the representative said "your bill will go down if you would add more line rather than canceling another line."

**Cancelling a line and the multi-line discount:** asked what cancelling one line would do, the representative said "there will not be much of a change because your account actually subscribed into multi-line discount, meaning to say, the more line you add, the more discount you can have." Their example: Unlimited Welcome "would be just $50 per month, but if you would remove a one line there, it could actually increase up to $10 more." So dropping a line could raise the price of each remaining line by up to about $10.

**What we didn't get on this call.** The call hit our 25 minute limit while our caller was asking about cancelling: any device payment balance, fees, when it takes effect, and keeping or porting the number. The representative's callback answered the balance and porting questions. Neither call answered whether there's a fee or when a cancellation takes effect. Verizon's own rules on cancelling are [below](#cancelling-a-verizon-line).

## What to have ready before you call

- The **mobile number on the account**. The AI assistant asks for it before anything else about the account.
- **The phone that gets the text**, if you want to confirm by text. Our first text expired while we waited, and the second didn't go through.
- Your **Account PIN**. It's what got us in.
- **Which line** you're calling about, by its last four digits.
- The words **"An agent, please"** when the AI assistant can't answer.
- To cancel a line: the **Account Owner's name, phone number and Account PIN**, or the **last 4 digits of the owner's Social Security number**. This is from Verizon's [cancel a line page](https://www.verizon.com/support/disconnect-cancel-mobile/).

## Changing your Verizon plan

From Verizon's [plan change FAQ](https://www.verizon.com/support/understand-and-change-your-plan-faqs/):

- "There's no cost to change your plan or your allowances."
- "Changing plans doesn't affect your upgrade eligibility or contract end date."
- You can't change your plan while an order is pending.

## Cancelling a Verizon line

From Verizon's [cancel a line page](https://www.verizon.com/support/disconnect-cancel-mobile/):

- You need the Account Owner's name, phone number and Account PIN, or the last 4 digits of the owner's Social Security number.
- "You must pay off any outstanding Device Payment balances before disconnecting the associated lines."
- The cancellation takes effect on the last day of the billing period.
- You can reverse it until the day before the disconnection date.
