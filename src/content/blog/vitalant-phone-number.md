**The short answer:** to get off the Vitalant call list, call the Vitalant phone number, **877-258-4825** (877-25-VITAL), and ask a person to remove your number; allow 10 to 12 days. Vitalant's [contact page](https://www.vitalant.org/contact-us) lists it "For Appointments", open **Mon. to Fri. 7 AM to 8 PM MT** and **Sat. to Sun. 9 AM to 7 PM MT**. The same page lists a separate **Emergency Hotline, 800-821-6277**, for donors who gave blood today and worry it "may not be suitable for transfusion".

**How to get off the Vitalant call list:** call 877-258-4825 and ask a person to remove your phone number from donor recruitment calls. Vitalant's [communication preferences page](https://www.vitalant.org/resources/help/communication-preferences) offers the same thing: "You also may call our Customer Contact Center at 877-258-4825 and they will be happy to help." You can also do it yourself online: log in to your donor account, open the "PREFERENCES" tab, uncheck phone calls, and click "Submit now" for that channel. The [privacy policy](https://www.vitalant.org/privacy-policy) adds donorsupport@vitalant.org for opt-out requests.

4. **A person answered 5 minutes 31 seconds into the call.** It was about 7 seconds after that prompt.

The representative asked for the phone number, had the last four digits repeated, and then said: **"I just removed that number from our call list."** He added that it takes "about 10 to 12 days" to fully remove it, so "a couple calls may slip through during that time."

## How to stop Vitalant blood donation calls

Before you call, have the **phone number that's getting the calls** ready. It was the only thing the representative asked for. He didn't ask for a donor ID, a date of birth or anything about past donations.

On the call, it helps to:

- **Name every channel you want stopped.** Ask about phone calls, voicemails, texts and mail by name. This call only established the call list.
- **Ask how long it takes.** Here it was 10 to 12 days, so a call or two in the next week and a half doesn't mean it failed.
- **Ask for a reference or confirmation,** if you want something to point to later. This call didn't get one.

If you have a donor account, the online route on Vitalant's [communication preferences page](https://www.vitalant.org/resources/help/communication-preferences) (the vitalant donor login, then the "PREFERENCES" tab) lets you uncheck each channel yourself. Remember to click "Submit now" for each one.
