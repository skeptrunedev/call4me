## What these calls did not settle

- **No room was booked or held** at any of these five venues. Sassafraz's room was taken, and the others offered availability without a hold.
- **No written proposal is shown.** Every price here is a spoken figure from a dated call. Menus, wine, tax, service charges, deposits and cancellation terms were not confirmed in writing.
- **Sassafraz's numbers need a breakdown.** The $95 average, the $125 figure and the C$169.50 all in estimate were not reconciled against the C$2,200 minimum on the call.
- **Room capacity differs between calls and websites** at Ki (16 on the call, 24 in the package) and La Plume (the 24 seat room is listed as semi-private). Volos's 14 seat Celebration Table was not mentioned.
- **The Ki and La Plume calls ended mid conversation**, before an email or events contact was confirmed.
