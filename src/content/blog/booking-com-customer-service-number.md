**The short answer:** the Booking.com customer service number its own Help Center shows to visitors from the US is **+44 20 3320 2624**. It's a London number, so from the US you dial **011 44 20 3320 2624**, and international call rates may apply. Booking.com's [customer service page](https://www.booking.com/customer-service.html) says "For anything urgent, you can call us 24/7 at a local or international phone number," and the agent we reached confirmed: "We are open 24/7." **There's no phone menu.** You go straight into a queue, and **a live agent answered 4 minutes 10 seconds after the line picked up.**

Booking.com doesn't print one phone number across its site. Its Help Center's ["Speak to someone" page](https://secure.booking.com/help/csPhoneNumbers), opened from the US without signing in on October 29, 2026, listed one number: "Customer Service (English) +44 20 3320 2624." The page says it's for people who "don't have an account or booking," and adds: "For faster support or different languages, sign in with your booking details." So if you have a booking, sign in at booking.com/help or open the app first; it may show you a different number. We had no booking, so this is the number we called. When we asked the agent whether there's a US or toll free number people should call instead, they said "this is the only contact number that we have so they can reach us."

**Watch out for fake numbers.** Search results for "Booking.com phone number" are full of US toll free numbers on PDFs and directory pages, and we found none of them on booking.com. Booking.com's own [scam guide](https://www.booking.com/articles/avoid-hotel-scams.html) warns that "Scammers sometimes phone, text, or message pretending to be customer service agents," and says to find contact details "directly on our official site."

What the agent told us:

- **Phone support is 24/7.** "We are open 24/7."
- **Have your confirmation number and PIN ready.** Both are in the top section of your confirmation email, the recording says. The first thing the agent asked was whether we were the property or "the guest who made the reservation."
- **If a property cancels on you, Booking.com can move you.** "We are going to relocate the guest into an alternative property and we can refund the price difference of the alternative property and the original property."
- **Refunds take 7 to 12 days if Booking.com took the payment,** and **15 business days if the property took it.** "The refund will take usually 7 to 12 days if the payment facilitated is Booking.com. However, if the payment facilitated for that is the accommodation, the time frame for the refund is 15 business days."
- **No callbacks on request.** If your call drops while they're working on your case, "we can call the guest directly." But if you ask them to call you back later, "we are not able to call them back since we are taking calls real time."

## What happens when you call Booking.com

1. **Call +44 20 3320 2624** (from the US, 011 44 20 3320 2624). A recording says "Thank you for calling Booking.com" and suggests you sign in online "to add, change, or even cancel your booking."
2. **A recording notice.** "Your call may be recorded for training purposes."
3. **"Please keep your reservation number and PIN handy. It's in the top section of your confirmation email."**
4. **You're put in the queue.** "We'll assist you as soon as a customer service representative becomes available. The current average waiting time is less than five minutes." There are no options to press.
5. **Hold.** About every 90 seconds a message repeats: "To keep waiting, please continue to hold. You can also visit Booking.com slash help to use live chat or send us a message."
6. **An agent answers.** On our call, 4 minutes 10 seconds after the line picked up. They ask whether you're the property or the guest.

## What to have ready

- **Your confirmation number and PIN.** The recording asks for them before you reach anyone, and the agent named them when we asked what to have ready.
- **Your confirmation email.** Both numbers are "in the top section" of it.
- **Whether you're the guest who made the reservation.** It's the agent's first question.

## If the property cancels or won't honor your booking

The agent said Booking.com will "relocate the guest into an alternative property" and refund "the price difference of the alternative property and the original property." Have your confirmation number and PIN ready when you call.

## How refunds work, per the agent

- **If Booking.com took your payment:** "usually 7 to 12 days."
- **If the property took your payment:** "15 business days."

We didn't ask how to check a refund's status.
