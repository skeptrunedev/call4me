**Dog teeth cleaning cost depends on what the clinic includes.** On October 7, 2026, we called three Houston area clinics: Houston Humane Society quoted cleaning from **$250 plus a required $50 consultation**, Houston Pet Dental quoted a **$519 base package**, and Chewy Vet Care Meyerland quoted a **$705 to $755 base package**. These are local starting prices from general inquiries, not national averages or final estimates for a particular dog.

The most useful finding was the difference in coverage. Houston Pet Dental included bloodwork and dental X rays in its base price. Chewy included dental X rays but charged separately for bloodwork. Houston Humane Society said it did not offer X rays. Comparing the advertised number alone would miss those differences.

Houston Humane Society's standard starting fees add up to **$300 before extras**. Chewy's quoted dental range plus the $85 exam adds up to **$790 to $840 before bloodwork and other extras**. Those are calculations from the stated fees, not complete treatment estimates. None of the calls established every possible charge for an individual pet.

The clinics' published pages provide a second source for the headline fees: [Houston Humane Society dental care](https://www.houstonhumane.org/clinic/dental-care), [Houston Pet Dental's consultation page](https://houstonpetdentals.com/complimentary-exam/) and [Chewy Meyerland services and pricing](https://www.chewy.com/vet-care/vet-near-me/tx/houston/8845-w-loop-south-suite-a/veterinary-services). ## Why the cheapest starting price may not cover the same care

Ask for a list of services before choosing by price. In these calls, the largest differences were whether bloodwork was included, whether dental X rays were available, and whether an exam was charged separately. Larger dogs could also cost more at Houston Pet Dental because of additional anesthesia time.

These are consequential differences to discuss with a veterinarian. [AAHA's dental guidance](https://www.aaha.org/resources/2019-aaha-dental-care-guidelines-for-dogs-and-cats/role-of-anesthesia/) describes a comprehensive examination under anesthesia and dental radiographs as part of evaluating and treating the mouth. Its [radiography guidance](https://www.aaha.org/resources/2019-aaha-dental-care-guidelines-for-dogs-and-cats/unconscious/) recommends images of the entire mouth for dental patients. A phone quote does not establish whether a particular package is appropriate for your pet.

The Houston Humane Society representative used the word “sedation,” while the clinic's website describes dental exams under anesthesia. We have preserved that wording rather than deciding what protocol the clinic uses. Ask its veterinary team to clarify the procedure and imaging arrangements before booking treatment.

## How much does cat teeth cleaning cost?

**Houston Humane Society explicitly confirmed the same $250 starting cleaning fee and separate $50 consultation for dogs and cats.** The final estimate depends on the examination and required services. Staff said bloodwork, extractions and medication cost extra.

Houston Pet Dental's published consultation page covers both dogs and cats and lists the $519 procedure. Our call confirmed the base package but did not separately ask for a cat price. The Chewy call also did not establish separate dog and cat rates. Give your assistant the species when requesting a quote rather than assuming every general price applies identically.

Use the same comparison prompt for a cat, including the cat's actual age and weight. Let the veterinarian determine the examination, testing and treatment needed.

## How do you get an itemized dental estimate?

All three clinics directed us to an examination or consultation for an individual estimate. Houston Pet Dental said it provides an itemized list at the complimentary assessment. Houston Humane Society said its $50 consultation establishes the dental requirements and total. Chewy said the doctor discusses the case and provides a quote during the exam.

Ask your assistant to collect the appointment fee, what records the clinic needs, and how you will receive the estimate. Ask which charges are fixed, which depend on findings, and how the clinic will contact you before additional work. Keep the consultation booking separate from permission for treatment.

Availability also matters. Houston Pet Dental said it was booked until January during our October 7 call; it did not state the year. Houston Humane Society mentioned $60 off dental cleaning during October, but we did not establish eligibility or other conditions. Neither detail should be treated as a current offer or available slot without checking again.

## How much is the vet visit before the cleaning?

Chewy Meyerland quoted about **$85 for the exam alone**, excluding tests, vaccines and medication. Houston Humane Society's **$50 fee was specifically for the dental consultation**. Houston Pet Dental's initial dental consultation was free. These are different visit types, so ask what appointment the quoted fee covers.

If you also need routine care, have your assistant request that exam price separately. For a different specialist task, our [veterinary behavior consultation call](/blog/veterinary-behavior-consultation-call) shows how to ask about a consultation fee and availability.
