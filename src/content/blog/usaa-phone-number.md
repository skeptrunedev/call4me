**The short answer:** the USAA phone number, the main line for USAA customer service, is **800-531-8722** (800-531-USAA). You can also dial **210-531-8722**, or **#8722** from a mobile phone. USAA's [support page](https://www.usaa.com/support/) says "You can reach any department through our main number." It's open **Monday to Friday 6 am to 10 pm Central** and **Saturday 8 am to 8 pm Central**, and closed Sunday ("Days and times may vary").

If you're not a member and want to join, this got us to a person:

1. At the prompt for your Social Security or member number, **press 1** ("If you're not a member").
2. **Press 2** ("interested in joining USAA").
3. When it asks for a product, **say "membership"**.

After a short disclosure and a hold announced as "approximately two minutes", someone from the **eligibility department** picked up. On our call that was 3 minutes 28 seconds after dialing. They told us:

- **Most members qualify through military service**, "hopefully with an honorable or under honorable discharge."
- **A child of a USAA member can join, and so can a spouse.** Siblings and other relatives can't.
- **Some areas of federal government may be eligible.** That's assessed person by person.
- **No documents up front.** "We do have a system that does check for eligibility." A **DD-214** or other military documents, or an **ID card**, may be asked for later.
- **Membership is free.** You only pay for products you choose to buy, like insurance. USAA's [membership page](https://www.usaa.com/membership/about/) says the same: "creating a member account with USAA is free."
- **You can join at usaa.com or by phone.** It's the same process either way, and by phone it takes "five minutes, maybe a little more" if nothing comes up.

## The USAA customer service phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 800-531-8722.** "Welcome to USAA. Para español, marque estrella dos." Then: "We monitor and record calls in line with our privacy notice."
2. **"To reach an extension, press the pound key. Otherwise, please enter your Social Security or USAA member number. If you're not a member, press 1."** Members enter their number here. To join, press 1.
3. **"Alright, you're not a member."** Then: "If you're a business or need help with a claim, press 1. If you're interested in joining USAA or would like more information about our products and services, press 2." Press 2.
4. **"Tell me just one of the products you're calling about."** It lists auto insurance, homeowners insurance, credit card, checking and auto loan, "or say membership questions or more options."
5. **Say "membership".** Saying the offered "membership questions" didn't work for us (see below). Plain "membership" got "Sorry" the first time and went through the second time.
6. **A disclosure plays.** It says USAA collects your name, contact information, date of birth, occupation and military service details to check eligibility and to market its products.
7. **A hold.** It announced "approximately two minutes" and offered a survey after the call. A person answered about 30 seconds after that announcement.
8. **The eligibility department picks up.** Our representative asked where we were calling from and whether we were calling for ourselves or for general information.

## What to have ready

- **The state you're calling from.** It was the representative's first question.
- **Your military service details,** if that's how you qualify. The disclosure lists "military service details", and the representative said applicants "give us their military information."
- **Your parent's or spouse's USAA membership,** if you're joining through family.
- **Your DD-214 or other military documents, and an ID,** in case they're asked for after your account is set up. Nothing was needed up front.

## Things that don't work

On October 1, 2026, at the "which product" prompt:

- **Saying "membership questions".** The menu offers it, but saying it just got "What product are you mainly calling about?" again.
- **Saying "representative".** Our call log shows our caller said it right after the first "Sorry". The word didn't make it onto the recording (the line is silent there for about 2 seconds), but the menu's answer did: "Sorry, for what product?"

Saying "membership" a second time is what worked. We didn't try "more options".
