**The short answer:** the Netflix customer service number for the US is **1-844-569-7700**. It's the number Netflix's own [Help Center contact page](https://help.netflix.com/en/contactus) gave us under "Call Us" on October 29, 2026, signed out, with "Wait time: less than 1 minute". **The live agent we reached said Netflix phone support is open 24/7:** "Yeah, we are open 24-7."

**A virtual agent answers first.** There's no keypad menu. It introduces itself ("I'm a virtual agent") and asks how it can help. What got us through:

1. **Say you want a live agent.** It offered to help with "billing questions, streaming issues, account changes, or device setup" first.
2. **Ask again for a representative.** On the second request it said "Please hold on for a moment while I connect you with a live agent" and "Your call will be answered in about 30 seconds."
3. **A live Netflix support agent answered 1 minute 20 seconds after the call connected.**

**Is this the real Netflix phone number?** Yes: help.netflix.com showed it to us, we called it on October 29, 2026 and reached Netflix support, and the agent read the same number back to us as the one to call. Also written 1 (844) 569-7700 or 844.569.7700. Directory sites list other numbers for Netflix. This is the one Netflix's site gave us, and the only one we called.

What the agent told us:

- **Phone support is 24/7 in the US.** "Yeah, we are open 24-7."
- **The fastest way to a person is this number.** Asked whether calling, the app or chat is fastest, she said "I can give you a phone number" and read out 1-844-569-7700 twice.
- **Have your email ready.** She asked for our email "for account reference" right away. Asked what members need, she said "It's just only an email."
- **You can change your plan by phone.** "You can change your plan on the account anytime, and also you can give us a call to change the plan." She said you can cancel "anytime" and rejoin "anytime", but didn't say whether support cancels by phone.
- **For a charge you don't recognize, she said to contact your bank.** "They should need to contact the bank," and "you can file a dispute." Netflix's own help page says something different for some cases. See [below](#a-netflix-charge-you-dont-recognize).
- **No callbacks.** Asked whether Netflix calls members back or offers a callback, she said "No" and "We don't have any give a call."

## The Netflix customer service phone line, step by step

This is how the call went on October 29, 2026, calling 1-844-569-7700.

1. **Call 1-844-569-7700.** "Hi, thanks for calling Netflix. I'm a virtual agent, and this call may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance and improvement of our service. How can I help?"
2. **Ask for a live agent.** Our caller said it had a general question and asked to be connected with someone. The virtual agent answered: "I hear you're looking to speak with a live agent. Before I get you there, I can help with things like billing questions, streaming issues, account changes, or device setup. Is there something specific you're trying to sort out today?"
3. **Ask again.** Our caller repeated that it was a general question and asked for a representative. "I understand you have a general question about phone support. Please hold on for a moment while I connect you with a live agent."
4. **A short hold.** "Your call will be answered in about 30 seconds," then a recording notice and "Please stay online at the end of the call for a brief survey."
5. **A person:** "Netflix support, this is [name removed]. How can I help?" That was 1 minute 20 seconds into the call, about 22 seconds after the "about 30 seconds" message.
6. **After you hang up with the agent,** a keypad survey starts: "How would you rate your experience with Netflix customer service? Press a number between 1 and 5."

We only tested asking for a live agent with a general question. If you tell the virtual agent about a specific billing or streaming problem, it may try to handle it itself first.

## Cancelling or changing your Netflix plan

The agent said you can change your plan on your account "anytime", and "also you can give us a call to change the plan." She said you can cancel "anytime" and rejoin "anytime", but didn't say whether an agent can cancel for you by phone.

Netflix's own [cancel article](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/407) says to go to the **Manage your membership** page, sign in, and tap **Cancel**, then **Finish Cancellation**. It says "This is the only way to cancel your account and end your membership," and that signing out or deleting the app doesn't cancel it. If you cancel mid cycle, "you can use Netflix until the end of the billing period." If you don't see a cancel option, you're billed through a partner, and "you'll need to cancel the account with your payment partner."

If you'd rather have someone else deal with it, see [cancelling a subscription by phone](/cancel-subscription).

## A Netflix charge you don't recognize

The agent's answer was to contact your bank: "They should need to contact the bank," and "you can file a dispute."

Netflix's [unrecognized charges page](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/1019/us) covers more cases:

- **You never signed up,** or think someone is using your payment information without your consent: "please contact us immediately" (it links to the contact page).
- **Charged on an expired card:** "Banks may automatically update credit or debit card numbers when a new card is issued."
- **A different amount than expected, charged after cancelling, or charged twice:** each has its own Netflix help article, linked from that page.

For disputing a charge with your card issuer, see [how to dispute a charge](/dispute-a-charge).

## Callbacks and calls claiming to be Netflix

Our caller asked two things at once: whether Netflix offers a callback, and about unprompted calls people might worry about. The agent said "No" and "We don't have any give a call." We read that as no callbacks, but because the question was doubled up, we can't say which part she was answering.

Netflix's [phishing article](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/65674) says Netflix will "never ask you to share your personal information in a text or email," including card numbers, bank details and passwords, and will "never ask for payment through a 3rd party vendor or website." For a phone number posing as Netflix customer service, it says not to share any information and to email the number to phishing@netflix.com. Get the number from help.netflix.com, not from a search ad or a directory.
