**LA Fitness's official FAQ lists cancellation through your online account, in person at a club, or by written notice mailed to P.O. Box 54170, Irvine, CA 92619, for most monthly memberships.** Other options can depend on the agreement or enrollment state. Start with the [official membership FAQ](https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/MembershipQuestions.aspx/1000) and your own agreement.

We used [call4me](/) to ask the Irvine Jamboree club about cancellation on October 6, 2026. The representative said **in person only**, with a sales counselor and confirmation by email. That conflicts with the published alternatives. A second call received no speech from the business, so we ended it without resolving the disagreement. Both recordings are below.

These were general questions. We did not cancel a membership, test a signed in account or establish a rule for every club. If your existing assistant is helping manage subscriptions, it can check the instructions for your account and return the answer to your task. ## How to cancel LA Fitness membership online

Use the online account linked from the [official FAQ](https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/MembershipQuestions.aspx/1000) and look for the cancellation option for your membership. We did not test a signed in flow, so this guide does not invent button labels or promise that the option appears for every agreement.

If the option is missing, contact your club or [member services](https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/ContactUs.aspx?submenu=MemberServices). Explain your membership type and ask which route applies. Save the submitted request and confirmation. Reaching support is not itself a completed cancellation.

## Can you cancel at the club or by phone?

The person we reached at **Irvine Jamboree, 2880 Michelle Drive**, said any sales counselor could handle an in person cancellation; a manager was not required. They said confirmation would be emailed to the address on the account. We did not visit the club or receive that email ourselves.

When we asked explicitly about phone cancellation, staff said they could not do it by phone. The [official club page](https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/clubhome.aspx?clubid=224) lists **(714) 505 8958**. Our first call reached staff after an initial wait, with no demonstrated keypad route to reproduce.

That call establishes what one representative told us. It does not invalidate the online and mail routes on the official site. For another location, use the [club directory](https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/findclub.aspx) and confirm its own contact details.

## What about cancellation by mail and the next billing date?

The official FAQ recommends keeping proof of mailed notice. It says a postmark at least **five business days before the next billing date** should prevent further billing; a later notice may produce another charge that it says will be refunded. Read the [current wording](https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/MembershipQuestions.aspx/1000) before relying on it for your account.

Our representative instead recommended coming in about a month before billing and referred to dues paid for the first and last month at signup. We could not establish whether that was advice about prepaid access or a required notice period. **Do not treat that phone answer as a universal one month cancellation rule.**

Ask support to distinguish three dates: when it receives the request, the final recurring payment, and the last day you can use the club. Keep any prepaid last month separate from a claim that another charge is required.

## What if you have personal training or an initial commitment?

The FAQ distinguishes agreements still within an initial term and warns that early termination can involve a fee. Consult the [terms described there](https://www.lafitness.com/Pages/MembershipQuestions.aspx/1000) and your actual agreement. We did not ask about personal training cancellation, so the gym membership conversation should not be presented as a tested personal training cancellation process.

Ask which agreements are attached to the account, which one your request affects, and whether each needs its own action. The calls did not verify a fee amount or any exception for a particular member.
