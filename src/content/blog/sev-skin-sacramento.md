**The short answer from the call:** the Sacramento studio was running the free first lip flip offer, and having had a laser facial there before does not disqualify you. The staff member said the only condition is that you have never had an injections appointment at SEV Skin.

## What the call did not confirm

- **Nothing was booked.** The brief said price questions only, and no appointment was made.
- **No price was quoted.** Because the lip flip was free for new injection clients, the assistant did not ask about units or a per unit price. The $11 per unit figure above is from SEV Skin's website, not the call.
- **No expiration date.** The staff member said there was no fine print the clients had to act on, but did not say when the promotion ends. "At this moment" is all the call established.
- **Eligibility was not checked against an account.** The staff member answered the general rule. Nobody looked up either client's history, so "qualifies" means "qualifies if they really have never had injections there."
- **Whether you can choose Botox or Xeomin for the lip flip itself** was left to the nurse. The answer started with "I think."

## Asking about an intro offer when you have been there before

Intro offers at multi service studios like SEV Skin are usually "new to each service," not "new to the studio." That is what made this call worth placing: a laser facial in the past did not count against the lip flip, because it is a different service. If you have had any injection there, though, the staff member's own rule says you would not qualify.

Say exactly what you have had before, and ask the yes or no question directly. Then ask about the fine print: whether a membership or package is required, whether there is a consultation fee, and whether the offer has an end date. This call got clear answers on the first two and nothing on the third.
