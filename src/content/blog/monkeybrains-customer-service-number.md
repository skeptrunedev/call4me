**The Monkeybrains customer service number is 415 974 1313.** Its [official support page](https://www.monkeybrains.net/support/) lists office hours as **Monday through Friday, 9 am to 5 pm**. In our recorded call, the menu offered **1 for new residential service and scheduling, 2 for technical support, 3 for billing, and 5 for commercial and event services**.

We used [call4me](/) to ask about home internet for one San Francisco apartment on **September 28, 2026**. A representative checked whether the building could be served, explained the installation requirements and offered arrival windows. The caller left to check with the property manager. **No installation was booked and no service was activated.**

The recording is from September. We checked the official contact and service pages again on November 2; we did not place a new call for this article.

## Which menu option should you use?

| What you need | Option announced on our call |
| :--- | :--- |
| New residential service or installation scheduling | 1 |
| Technical support | 2 |

| New commercial or event service | 5 |

The current [Monkeybrains policies page](https://www.monkeybrains.net/policies/) also directs residential inquiries to extension 1, business inquiries to extension 5 and moving or termination questions to billing at extension 3.

Our call tested a residential inquiry. It did not test technical support or billing, and it does not establish how long either department takes to answer. Listen to the current menu before selecting an option.

## What to have ready before asking about installation

Give the representative your **street address and unit**, whether the request is **residential or business**, and whether you have already submitted an inquiry. In our call, finding the earlier email helped the representative identify the right unit.

Ask these questions for your own building:

1. Can you serve this exact address and unit?
2. Is the building already connected, or would this be a new installation?
3. What approval and building access do you need?
4. What equipment must I supply?
5. What appointment windows are available, and what must happen before I can book one?

Monkeybrains' [residential page](https://www.monkeybrains.net/residential/) says coverage maps do not guarantee service at an address and that a representative reviews eligibility. It also states that building ownership or management approval is required before installation. Our call illustrates why an address check matters even when a neighborhood is covered.

For an existing connection problem, the [support page](https://www.monkeybrains.net/support/) has an outage map and a contact form. Its map describes whole buildings that are offline, so a missing outage marker does not rule out a problem in one unit. Choose the technical support option rather than the new service route we tested.
