**The short answer:** the FPL phone number for customer service is **1-888-988-8249**. FPL lists its hours as "Mon. to Fri., 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. Sat., 9 a.m. to 5 p.m." To report an outage, call **1-800-468-8243**, "Anytime" ([fpl.com](https://www.fpl.com/support/contact.html)). In Northwest Florida the number is **1-888-326-4853**, Monday to Friday 7 am to 6 pm and Saturday 8 am to 4 pm ([fpl.com](https://www.fpl.com/northwest/support/contact-us.html)).

**To reach a person,** go through the new service signup. This is the route that worked on our fifth call:

1. Say **"Residential"**.
2. At "Please say or enter the FPL account number or your social security number", say **"Open a new account"**. If it doesn't catch that, it reads out keys: **press 3** to open a new account.
3. "Do you have an existing account that you would like to transfer to a new address?" Say **"No"**.
4. "To begin new service, we will walk you through a three-step process." Say **"Get started"**.
5. At "What's the zip code?", our caller said **"Agent"** ("Sorry. That option is not available.") and then **"Representative"**. FPL read a safety notice and said "your information has been saved for the next step."
6. Our caller said **"Speak to a person"**, and FPL said "Your call is being routed to the next available agent." An agent answered about 10 seconds later.

Asking for a representative anywhere else got "Sorry. That option is not available." It took us five calls to find this route.

**What the agent said:** you can call any time, "even the same day", because FPL connects service remotely, which "takes between a couple of minutes to four hours." To start service they need your **name, Social Security number, phone number, email address** and the **service address**. The Social Security number is for a **credit review**, which decides whether you pay a deposit. If you do, it's due **10 days after the account is established**.

We made these calls on Thursday, October 1, 2026, with [call4me](/) placing them. We had no new address to start service at. We only asked how it works.

## What the FPL agent told us

The agent asked for the address where we wanted service connected. We said we didn't have one yet, and asked what a new customer needs.

- **How far ahead to call:** "They can call at any time, even though the same day, because we connect the service remotely, and that it takes between a couple of minutes to four hours."
- **What they need:** "the name, the social security number, phone number, and email address, and of course, the address." And: "That's the only thing that we need, and we connect the service at the moment."
- **Whether there's a deposit:** "That's the reason why we ask the social security number. At the time that we place the social, the system do a credit review, and that is what depends if the system charge a deposit or not."
- **When the deposit is due:** "Ten days after the account is established."
- **A connection fee:** we asked "is there a connection fee or any deposit that might be due?" The agent answered about the deposit and didn't mention a connection fee. So we don't know if there is one.
- **How much the deposit is:** we didn't ask. FPL's own rules are [below](#how-the-fpl-deposit-works).

FPL's deposit page says the deposit is "due once the account is opened, sometimes before your service is connected". The agent said 10 days after. Both are below, as each one said it.

## What to have ready before you call

- Your **name, phone number and email address**.
- Your **full Social Security number**. The agent said it's used for the credit review that decides the deposit. FPL's site says to start service by phone you need "either your full social security number, or your FPL account number and last four digits of your social security number" ([fpl.com](https://www.fpl.com/support/contact/start-stop-move/start-service/topic-not-listed1.html)).
- The **service address**, including the ZIP code. The signup asks for it first, and the agent asked for it first too.

Or skip the call and [start service online](https://www.fpl.com/my-account/start-service.html).

## How the FPL deposit works

From [FPL's residential deposit page](https://www.fpl.com/rates/residential-deposits.html), checked on October 1, 2026.

- **Whether you pay one:** a credit scoring system decides if a deposit is required. The agent said the same: the Social Security number runs a credit review.
- **How much:** two months of the new home's expected use, based on the property's prior usage history. If the home has used about $100 a month, the deposit is $200.
- **When it's due:** once the account is opened, sometimes before your service is connected. The agent told us 10 days after the account is established.
- **Interest:** it earns 2% interest after 6 months.
- **Getting it back:** it's refunded after 23 months of good payment, or when you close the account.
- **Without cash:** a guarantor contract is a non-cash alternative.
