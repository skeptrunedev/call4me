**The short answer:** Etihad customer service in the US is **+1 914-303-8393**, open **24 hours a day, every day** (that's what [etihad.com's help page](https://www.etihad.com/en-us/help) shows under Call us, then Contact us, with United States of America selected). Press **1** for English, then your **cabin class**, then **2** if you have an existing booking. On our call a person picked up **2 minutes 21 seconds** in.

There's **no Guest Relations option** in the menu, and asking for it out loud at the menu does nothing. The first agent sent us back to a menu. The second agent, reached at **5:44**, found the complaint in a few minutes and told us:

- The family's follow-up email about the broken seat **was attached to their existing complaint case** and the team was reviewing it.
- The outcome takes **"up to 28 days"**, counted **from the day the case was opened**. There was "no certain date."
- There's **no separate reference number** for the follow-up. "This is only the case number."
- There's **no dedicated Guest Relations email**. The call ran out of time while the agent was answering that, so we didn't hear the rest.

The call was made on Thursday, October 1, 2026, from about 11:12 to 11:22 am Eastern, by a call4me user's AI phone assistant. It was following up on a complaint about a business-class seat that wouldn't lie flat on a flight from Chicago to Abu Dhabi, with a refund of the $1,512 paid upgrade requested. Nothing was accepted or changed on the call: the brief said not to accept a settlement, voucher or waiver. The user is okay with us sharing the call. Their name, the traveler's name, all three Etihad staff names, the booking reference, the case number, the flight number and date, seat numbers, the last digits of a card, and a name and email address in an email the agent read out are muted in the audio.

## How to follow up on an Etihad complaint by phone

1. **Have your case number and booking reference ready.** The first agent asked for a name, the cabin class and the booking reference. The second asked for the case number and "what is it going on for."
2. **Give a name when they ask.** Our caller only said whose assistant it was, and the agent ended up repeating a misheard name back.
3. **Spell the booking reference with a phonetic alphabet from the start.** The agent read one letter back wrong, and fixing it took 30 seconds.
4. **Don't ask the menu for Guest Relations.** There's no option for it. Pick your cabin and "existing booking," then ask the agent to look up your complaint case.
5. **If you sent an email, name it by date and subject before anything else.** One case can hold several emails. The agent first read out a different one and asked about the wrong refund.
6. **Ask three things:** is my email attached to the case, who's handling it, and when will I get a written answer. We got yes, a name, and "up to 28 days" from the day the case was opened.
7. **Don't expect a separate reference number** for a follow-up. The agent said the existing case number "is only the case number."
8. **Do the date math yourself.** The case was opened September 23, so 28 days from then is about October 21, 2026. That's our math, not Etihad's: the agent said there was "no certain date."

## Other ways to contact Etihad

All of these are on [etihad.com/en-us/help](https://www.etihad.com/en-us/help) and its [complaints and feedback page](https://www.etihad.com/en-us/help/share-feedback):

- **Online chat:** an AI assistant, labeled "Recommended."
- **WhatsApp:** also "Recommended." The help page links to +971 56 688 9003. The phone menu offers WhatsApp too: press 0 at the cabin menu.
- **Raise a complaint:** an online form at [etihad.com/en-us/help/share-feedback/raise-a-complaint](https://www.etihad.com/en-us/help/share-feedback/raise-a-complaint).
- **Check a complaint's status:** log in at [etihad.com/en-us/help/share-feedback/track-complaint](https://www.etihad.com/en-us/help/share-feedback/track-complaint).
- **Baggage claims, Etihad Guest feedback, and claims filed on someone else's behalf** each have their own link on the complaints page.

The family in our call had emailed their follow-up to feedback@etihad.ae, and the agent found it in the case. We didn't see that address on the Etihad help pages above, which point to the online form.

## Where our caller fell short

- **It ran out of time.** The call was set to stop at 10 minutes, and it hung up at 9:55 while the agent was answering the question about a Guest Relations email. call4me has since dropped that cap: a call now runs as long as the balance covers.
- **It asked the recording for Guest Relations** at 1:06. It's a keypad menu, and nothing happened.
- **It didn't give a name** when the first agent asked, which led to some back and forth over a misheard name.
- **It sat on the second menu without pressing anything** after the transfer, so the menu said it didn't recognize the entry and played again. That cost about 30 seconds.
- **It read its whole request in one go,** from 6:04 to 7:13: over a minute of booking reference, case number, flight, seat, refund and three questions. The agent then pulled up the wrong email. Leading with "the September 25 email about the seat" would have been faster.
- **It never finished one request.** At 7:11 it started "And please ask the reviewer," meaning to ask Etihad to check the crew and maintenance records for the seat, and the agent put it on hold. It didn't come back to it.
- **It introduced itself as "[name]'s assistant,"** not as an AI assistant, which is what its brief asked for.
