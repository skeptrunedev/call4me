**To check whether a prescription is ready at Farmacia Luma in Viejo San Juan, call its pharmacy counter at (787) 722-0335.** The pharmacy is at Calle San Francisco 255 in Viejo San Juan, Puerto Rico. Staff picked up with "Buenos días, recetario Luma" on October 15, 2026. **The recetario (prescription counter) is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:45 PM,** according to the pharmacist who answered.

Where that number comes from: the pharmacy's [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/farmacialuma/) gives the address as Calle San Francisco 255, and the federal [NPI registry record](https://npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov/provider-view/1689701443) for Farmacia Luma lists 787-722-0335 at 255 Calle de San Francisco. The pharmacy's own domain, farmacialuma.com, redirected to a page that did not load when we checked on October 17, 2026, so we could not confirm the number on a Farmacia Luma website. The call itself reached the Luma recetario.

On Thursday, October 15, 2026, at about 10:02 AM Puerto Rico time, a call4me customer's assistant called in Spanish to ask whether a prescription sent by the customer's doctor had arrived. Staff said **yes, it was received and ready to pick up**, and quoted **$1 without insurance** ("sin plan médico"). They also said the counter would run special hours the next day, Friday, October 16: **9:30 AM to 4:00 PM**.

## What the call did not confirm

- **Whose prescription it was.** The assistant named the medication and the customer, and staff said they had it. Nobody read back the patient's legal name, date of birth or the prescribing doctor. The customer's own brief listed those details, but the assistant never needed to give them.
- **Weekend hours and store hours.** The answer covered the recetario on weekdays and one special day. The assistant did not ask about Saturday or Sunday, or about hours for the rest of the store, which may differ.
- **Why the hours changed.** Staff called October 16 "un horario especial" without saying why. Treat it as a one-day change, not the regular schedule.
- **Pickup requirements.** ID, payment methods and whether someone else could collect it were not discussed.
- **That the pickup happened.** The recording ends with the call. It does not show the prescription being collected or the price being charged.

The $1 price is what staff said on October 15, 2026, for this one prescription without insurance. It is not a price list.

## Calling Farmacia Luma about a prescription

This was a short call because the questions were specific and the order was sensible: did it arrive, is it ready, what does it cost without insurance, when can someone come. Each answer took one line.

A few things made it work, and a few are worth doing differently:

- **Lead with the medication.** Staff found the prescription from the medication and the customer's name alone. Have the patient's legal name and date of birth ready anyway, in case the counter needs them.
- **Ask for the cash price explicitly.** "Sin plan médico" got a direct number. If you have insurance, say so, since the copay may differ.
- **Ask about the day you will actually go.** The regular hours were not the whole answer: the next day had special hours. If you plan a Saturday pickup, ask about Saturday.
- **Confirm in one sentence before hanging up.** The assistant repeated the four answers back, and staff said "Correcto".
