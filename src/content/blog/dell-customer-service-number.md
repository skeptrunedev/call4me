**The short answer:** Dell's customer service number for home consumers is **1-800-624-9897**. Dell's [Limited Hardware Warranty page](https://www.dell.com/en-us/legal/lp/art-limited-hardware-warranties) lists it under "Individual Home Consumers", "U.S. Only", as "Customer Service", next to Hardware Warranty Support at 1-800-624-9896. **But on both of our calls to it, without an order number, Dell's AI voice assistant sent us to dell.com/orders and hung up.**

**What reached a person was Dell's sales line, 1-877-275-3355**, the only phone number on Dell's [contact page](https://www.dell.com/en-us/lp/contact-us). It lists sales hours as "Home: 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. USA CT, All Days". This got us to a person:

1. At "how may I help you today?", **say "make a purchase"**.
2. At "Which product are you interested in purchasing today?", **say "something else"**. It says "I'll transfer you to one of our sales agents who can help."
3. **Hold.** For us, about 3 minutes.
4. **A person at the "Dell Sales Reception Desk" answered 4 minutes 38 seconds into the call.** When we said we had general questions, not a purchase, they transferred us to customer care.
5. **A customer care representative answered at 7:10**, after about a minute and a half more on hold.

What the customer care representative told us:

- **Sales can transfer you to customer care.** "If you're connected with the sales team and you're requesting to connect with customer care, then they will be able to connect the call internally."
- **Have your order number ready.** "If the call is going to be connected with the customer care, then you can call us with the customer number or the order number." For the technical team, "service tag is mandatory."
- **Consumer orders get a 30 day return.** "If the order is placed in consumer, for a consumer purchase, then the orders are eligible for 30 days return policy." Business or commercial account orders, they said, are "sold under no return and refund policy".
- **Returns start in My Account.** If "the order is eligible and the order is placed in consumer", there's "an option to submit a return request."
- **Hours:** "Central Standard Time, ma'am, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m." That was their answer after we asked about sales and customer care hours, and it isn't clear which team it was for. It doesn't match the sales hours on Dell's contact page (8 a.m. to 10 p.m. Central, all days).

We called on Thursday, October 22, 2026, between about 1:09 and 1:35 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Dell sales line phone tree, step by step

This is the menu as it played on October 22, 2026, calling 1-877-275-3355.

1. **Call 1-877-275-3355.** "Thank you for choosing Dell. If you do not consent to this call being recorded for quality, training, and security purposes, press nine. Otherwise, please remain on the line."
2. **"If you have an extension for a specific person or department, say extension or press one now. If not, how may I help you today? You can say things like check my order status, make a purchase, or technical support."** We said "representative" and "make a purchase" together, and it answered "I'm sorry, I didn't get that". **Say "make a purchase"** on its own.
3. **"Which product are you looking to purchase today?"** When we asked for a sales representative instead, it listed the options: "computer, server, electronics or accessories, spare parts, warranty, or you can say something else". **Say "something else".**
4. **"I'll transfer you to one of our sales agents who can help. Thanks for choosing Dell."** Then hold, with "All of our agents are currently busy. Please continue to hold for our next available agent" every minute or so.
5. **A person,** at 4:38: "Welcome to Dell Sales Reception Desk. How can I assist you?" They asked whether we were looking to buy or had already bought.
6. **Ask for customer care.** The agent said "The customer care department will help you", asked "You're calling from U.S. or Canada?", and transferred us.
7. **More hold:** "All of our representatives are currently assisting other callers. Please stay on the line as calls are answered in the order they are received."
8. **Customer care answers,** at 7:10: "Thank you for calling Dell. Hello."

## Things that don't work

**Calling 1-800-624-9897 without an order number.** We called Dell's listed customer service number twice that afternoon, three minutes apart, and neither call reached a person. Both ended at Dell's "AI-powered voice assistant", which sent us to www.dell.com/orders or "my account" and then hung up. Neither call played hold music or offered a callback.

On that line, on October 22, 2026:

- **Saying "representative" at the first prompt.** It asked for more information, confirmed "You said you'd like to speak to a representative, is that right?", then played a five option menu instead: "order support or press one. Make a purchase or press two. Technical support or press three. Make a payment or press four. Or say billing or press five."
- **Saying "it's something else"** at the "order status, return, exchange, cancellation" menu. That's what sent us to the AI voice assistant. Asked for customer service hours, it said "I'm not seeing information on Dell's customer service hours right now", then "Goodbye".
- **Saying "return" at the first prompt.** It asked "Is this order for a purchase you made with a credit card?" and "Was the purchase financed with a lease or a loan?", then went to the same AI voice assistant.
- **Asking the AI voice assistant for an agent.** We asked twice ("I need to speak to an agent", then "a live agent about general order and return questions"). Both times: "please visit www dot dell dot com forward slash orders or log into my account."

What we didn't try on that line: pressing 1 for order support, 3 for technical support, or 0, answering "yes" to the credit card question, or giving a real order number. With an order number the menu may well route you to a person. Without one, it didn't.

## What to have ready

- **Your order number,** for customer care. The representative said customer care can also use your customer number.
- **Your service tag,** if your call is going to technical support. "If this call is going to be connected to the technical team, then service tag is mandatory."
- **Whether you're in the U.S. or Canada.** The sales agent asked before transferring us.

## What Dell's website says

These come from dell.com, not from the calls:

- **Sales: 1-877-275-3355**, "Home: 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. USA CT, All Days" and "Business: 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. USA CT, (Mon-Sat)", on Dell's [contact page](https://www.dell.com/en-us/lp/contact-us).
- **Customer Service: 1-800-624-9897, Hardware Warranty Support: 1-800-624-9896**, for individual home consumers in the U.S., on Dell's [Limited Hardware Warranty page](https://www.dell.com/en-us/legal/lp/art-limited-hardware-warranties). The same page lists other lines for employee purchase programs, businesses, government and education customers, and Alienware.
- **Returns:** "The 30-day return period is calculated from invoice date", a footnote on the same warranty page, which adds "Exceptions to Dell's standard return policy still apply, and certain products are not eligible for return at any time."
- **Order help online:** Dell's [order support FAQ](https://www.dell.com/support/contents/en-us/article/esupport-order-support/frequently-asked-questions) says to go to My Account, then Orders, then "Get Help". You can look up an order as a guest at [Dell order support](https://www.dell.com/support/order-status/order-support).

A few things to know about our side of the calls:

- **It didn't follow up on hours.** The representative's "8 a.m. to 5 p.m." answer came after we asked about sales and customer care hours together, and our caller didn't ask which team it was for.
- **It didn't ask for the customer care number.** The sales agent transferred us straight away, so we don't know if customer care has a direct line other than 1-800-624-9897.
