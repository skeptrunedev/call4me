**The short answer:** Afternic's [official contact page](https://www.afternic.com/contact) lists **866-829-9361** (toll free) and **+1 339-222-5145** (international) for sales, which is the team that handles buying a domain. The same page's site data lists **866-351-9586** and **+1 781-839-7990** for support. It doesn't list hours.

The call on this page went to a different number: a London line, **+44 20 7255 0717**. It was answered with "Thank you for calling Afternic, the world's largest premium domain marketplace", and a person introduced himself as being "at GoDaddy". Is Afternic owned by GoDaddy? Yes: Afternic's own site footer carries a GoDaddy Operating Company copyright. **We couldn't find that UK number on Afternic's or GoDaddy's own websites**, so if you're calling from scratch, use the sales numbers above.

On Tuesday, October 13, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called that line to make an offer on a domain listed for sale on Afternic. **A domain broker answered 44 seconds into the call.** What he said:

- **An offer far below the list price "probably" won't work.** Domains listed at a set price are often sold "for their full price", so those sellers "aren't looking to start lower to negotiate."
- **Sellers would likely consider offers "within like 10 to 20%" of the list price.** Making an offer "isn't a bad idea", but it needs to be "quite close to what they're looking for."
- **He asked whether this was a first inquiry,** because someone else had already been in touch about the same domain.
- **The deal moves to email.** He took a contact email and said he'd send "what I recommend as the next step."

The domain name, the prices, the customer's name and company, the email address and the broker's first name are muted in the recording.

## Making an offer through the GoDaddy domain broker

The broker's answers are useful if you want to buy a domain listed on Afternic's domain marketplace:

- **Know the list price before you call.** It was the first thing the conversation turned on. A listed price is the seller's asking figure, and the broker said these sellers often sell at it.
- **Expect a lowball to get turned down quickly.** The opening offer here was well under the list price, and the broker said it wouldn't work before discussing anything else. A second, higher figure got the same answer.
- **Aim within 10 to 20% of the list price** if you want the broker to take the offer to the seller. That's his estimate, not a policy.
- **Be ready for an email follow-up.** The broker asked for an email address and moved the deal there. Have the address you want the paperwork sent to ready before you call.
- **Ask about fees directly.** This call never reached commission, escrow or transfer timing. If those matter to your budget, ask before you name a figure.

When the broker asked whether he was talking to a person or an AI, the assistant said it was an AI assistant working for the customer, and he said he didn't mind. He did ask whether replies by email would also come from an AI.
