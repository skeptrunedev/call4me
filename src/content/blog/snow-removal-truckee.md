## How snow removal works in Truckee: routes, triggers, berms and roofs

If you're new to a Truckee house, or used to calling a plow guy after each storm somewhere else, this is what the companies' own terms say.

- **You buy a season, for a route.** A-1 runs November 1 to April 30 with unlimited clearings. [True North](https://www.truenorthsnowremoval.com/) runs the same dates "with no cap on the number of services in a season." SPE runs to May 1, Stars to April 15. Taylors sells two plans: up to 17 plow visits, or unlimited at one plow per 24 hours from November 1 to April 15. Each company covers named neighborhoods, so start with who plows your street.
- **There's a trigger.** Most start at about 4 inches: SPE measures on Glenshire Drive, and True North's route "activates when our reference site, at roughly 5,920 feet, indicates 4 inches of new natural snowfall." Stars starts at 3 to 4 inches in your driveway.
- **Once a day, often at night, with no set time.** Stars guarantees "one driveway clearing per 24-hour period." Elements says "We cannot guarantee a specific clearing time for every driveway," and Taylors aims "to complete full driveway clearing by 8:00 AM or earlier" as a goal, not a promise. In a multi-day storm, that's one pass a day.
- **The Town plow's berm is often yours.** When the Town clears the street it leaves a berm across your driveway. Stars removes berms left after its route "the following day" but warns "There will be times that you will need to shovel these berms." Ask every company how it handles the berm.
- **Decks, walkways and roofs are a different hire.** Stars "concentrates its efforts exclusively on driveway snow removal," Royal's Yelp profile says "Driveway Snow Removal Only. No decks or Walkways," and Elements says its operations "focus on driveways" and points customers to "local resources" for "shoveling and roof-shed clearing." Hand crews do the rest: [Dependable Snow Removal](https://www.dependablesnowremoval.com/) lists walkways, decks and stairs, roofs and chimneys, ice dams and propane tanks; [Rake](https://raketruckee.com/) does walkways, patios, decks and roofs with "small blowers or hand shoveling"; North Lake Construction charges by the hour per worker. Roofing companies such as Mills Roofing do roof snow and ice.

## How we ranked them

One scoring method for every business in all six of our winter lists, from public facts only.

1. **A rating adjusted for how many reviews it has.** We blend each business's star rating with a 4.3, as if it had 10 more reviews at 4.3, so a 5.0 from 3 reviews counts for less than a 4.8 from 200. Here, Stars' 5.0 from 4 reviews counts as 4.50 and Estabrook's 4.8 from 22 as about 4.64. A business with no rating anywhere starts at 4.0.
2. **+0.30 for an actual dollar price** the business publishes on its own site or listing for the plowing itself. A pricing model ("competitive rates", "seasonal flat rate"), a price someone quoted in a review, or a fee for something else doesn't count. SPE's $100-per-plow cancellation charge is that kind of fee, so SPE doesn't get it.
3. **+0.15 for being based in Truckee.**
4. **+0.15 if it says it's taking new customers**, or told us so on the phone on November 27, and **−0.60 if it says it's full or not taking new customers.** We read each business's own words to decide. Royal's "season contracts are no longer availabe" counted as not taking customers; no notice either way counts as neither.
5. **+0.05 for activity dated 2026.**
6. **−0.40 for a serious red flag:** signs of a broker, lead-gen site or front; a pattern of no-show complaints, or one dated 2024 or later; signs of fake reviews; or a listed number that's disconnected.

The calls only filled in facts that weren't online, mostly whether a business is taking customers. All three of ours went to voicemail, so they changed nothing, and whether a company picked up isn't part of the score. Ratings and prices are as of November 27, 2026.

## Snow plowing near me: how to hire in Truckee

1. **Start with your neighborhood.** A 5.0-star plower that doesn't run your street can't help. Tahoe Donner, Glenshire, Prosser, Northstar and Olympic Heights each have their own route companies above.
2. **Ask the five questions that decide it:** the season price for your driveway, how many clearings it covers, the trigger depth, the target clearing time, and whether the street berm is included.
3. **Ask what happens in a multi-day storm.** Once per 24 hours is the norm. The January 2026 Estabrook review and the 2026 Elements complaints are both about long storms.
4. **Hire decks, walkways and roofs separately**, and before the first big storm, not during it.
5. **Check you're talking to the plower.** If a site has no company name, no Truckee address or license, and a toll-free or out-of-state number, it's probably passing your request on.
6. **Check it's still running.** For a company with no website and no review since 2023, call before you count on it. A voicemail in the business's name means the number works, not that the routes have room.
7. **Read the cancellation terms.** SPE charges $100 per plow if you cancel before January 1 and refunds nothing after. Royal's [payment page](https://royalsnowremoval.com/contact-2/) says "there are NO REFUNDS."

## Where our caller fell short

- **Its voicemail said too little.** The message asked for a callback about "residential snow removal in Truckee this winter" but didn't ask the questions. A company that listens to voicemail could have answered them on the callback if it had heard them.
- **It guessed wrong on the callback.** Our Truckee number had also called firewood sellers that morning. When someone called back, our caller asked "Who am I speaking with?" but not which business, then asked about firewood delivery. The caller answered, "Um, this doesn't pertain to me," and, "You called about snow removal." Our caller then asked if it had reached a firewood seller, and when the person asked for a human, it couldn't reach one and took a message. It never asked the snow questions it had been given.
- **It didn't try again.** Each call was one try at the same minute on a holiday. Estabrook's line played "Please hold for the next available agent" before going to voicemail. A second call on a weekday morning would likely have reached someone.
