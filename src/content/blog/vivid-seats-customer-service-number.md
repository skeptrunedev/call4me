**The Vivid Seats customer service number we tested is 1 866 848 8499.** We called on **November 2, 2026**, using [call4me](/). The existing order route asked for an order number or the contact phone number on the order. Without either, the recording directed us to **support@vividseats.com** or recorded answers to common questions. We did **not** reach a person.

We had no order to look up. This test shows the menu and its identifier requirement, not what happens after a valid order lookup or how long an actual buyer would wait. Our planned general question about ticket delivery never reached a representative.

## What if you do not know your order number?

The menu offered another way to look it up: **press 2 at the order lookup prompt to give the contact phone number on the order**. That is a different menu from the opening menu, where 2 means existing order support. Listen for the prompt before choosing an option.

We did not provide a phone number or test that lookup. If you have neither identifier, the recording's instruction was to email **support@vividseats.com**. The website also provides the account and app routes below. We did not send an email or test how quickly it receives a response.

## Start with the contact route for your task

Vivid Seats' [contact page](https://www.vividseats.com/contact-us) separates **Existing Orders**, **Shopping For Tickets** and **Selling Tickets**. Choose the one that matches what you need. A question about tickets you already bought belongs with existing order support; it is different from asking a sales representative to help you buy tickets.

When we checked on November 2, 2026, the [Existing Orders page](https://www.vividseats.com/contact-us/existing-orders) asked visitors to sign in to view orders and directed them to chat in the Vivid Seats app. It displayed support hours of 24/7. The page did not display a public phone number in the signed out version we retrieved. Its general support hours do not establish the hours of every phone department.

We sourced **1 866 848 8499** from Vivid Seats' [official SMS terms](https://www.vividseats.com/sms-terms/), which lists the number for assistance. The call below shows what answered that number on the date tested.

## If your tickets have not arrived

Check the delivery date and method in your order first. Vivid Seats' [purchase guide](https://support.vividseats.com/support/solutions/articles/1000210309-i-ve-never-purchased-from-vivid-seats-before-what-is-the-process-) says the confirmation email includes an estimated shipping or delivery date, with another email when tickets are ready. You can also check the order in your account.

Its [estimated delivery date definition](https://support.vividseats.com/support/solutions/articles/1000213827-estimated-delivery-date) describes that date as when tickets are expected to be ready to send. Tickets might arrive by a different method than an email attachment. The [delivery methods guide](https://support.vividseats.com/support/solutions/articles/1000210347-how-are-tickets-on-your-site-delivered-) includes electronic transfers through other ticketing services as well as mobile tickets, PDF delivery, shipping and special delivery arrangements.

Before contacting support, gather:

1. The order number and email address used to buy the tickets.
2. The event name, venue, date and start time.
3. The estimated delivery date and the delivery method shown in the order.
4. What the order currently says, what you have already tried, and any exact error message.

These details help you describe the problem clearly. Do not treat the absence of a PDF as proof that tickets are missing if your order calls for a mobile transfer. Follow the instructions for the actual delivery method. We did not buy tickets or resolve a real delivery problem in this test.

## A clear request for existing order support

> I need help with my Vivid Seats order [order number] for [event, date and start time]. The order lists [delivery method] and an estimated delivery date of [date]. It currently shows [status]. I have tried [steps], and the error is [exact message]. Please tell me what to do next and whether you need to check the order. Ask me before accepting any replacement, refund, credit or order change.

Use your own order details and the support channel offered in your account. An assistant can help prepare the request and summarize the answer. Keep passwords and verification codes out of its instructions; join the support conversation yourself if account verification is needed.
