**The short answer:** Windstream customer service is now Kinetic. windstream.com's contact page redirects to [gokinetic.com/support](https://www.gokinetic.com/support). **To reach a person, call Kinetic Sales at (866) 703-8175 and press 1** ("new residential deals and offers"). On our call a live sales representative answered 55 seconds in, not a recording and not an AI assistant. They didn't ask for an account number.

**What the rep told us:**

1. **No contract and no early termination fee.** "There is no contract with Kinetic. So there is no contract to sign, so no early termination fee or something like that."
2. **A rented modem has to go back when you cancel.** On some plans ("the plans of one gig to two gigs") you rent Kinetic's modem: "They should have to pay $10 for the modem, and they should have to return it whenever, you know, they decide to take it out of the service."
3. **You can use your own modem.** "If they want to use their own modem, of course, you know, we just go and install the internet."
4. **A customer service number: 833-811-9530.** The rep read it out and offered to transfer us. We haven't found that number on gokinetic.com, and we didn't call it.

This is a sales line. Our caller told the rep that Nick wasn't a current customer and was comparing providers, and the rep said "you're calling the right department because this is the sales department." We don't know how the same rep would handle an existing customer's billing or repair problem.

**Our two earlier calls never reached a person.** One on the support line, (800) 347-1991, and one on the sales line where our caller pressed 3 (existing customers). Both ended at the same prompt: "Please enter either your phone number, including area code, or enter the account number located on your bill, and then press pound." [Those calls are below](#if-you-call-the-support-line-or-press-3).

We made all three calls on Thursday, October 8, 2026, at 4:25 pm, 4:30 pm and 5:02 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) placing them.

## The Kinetic phone menus, step by step

**On the sales line, (866) 703-8175:**

1. **"Thank you for calling Kinetic Fiber Internet. To ensure your call is routed properly, please select one of the following three options."**
2. **Press 1** for "new residential deals and offers". This is the option that reached a person for us.
3. Press 2 for "new business promotions". We didn't try it.
4. Press 3 if "you are an existing customer calling for upgrades or have recently signed up for service". On our earlier call, this led into the main Kinetic menu below and its account number prompt.

After pressing 1, there was about 30 seconds with no speech, then the rep picked up.

**On the support line, (800) 347-1991** (and after option 3 on the sales line):

1. **"Thanks for calling Kinetic. Para continuar en español, oprima ocho."** Press 8 for Spanish.
2. **The main menu.** "If you're already a Kinetic customer, press 1. To ask about becoming our customer, press 2. To check order status, press 3. If you're calling to report a downed telephone line or pole, press 4."
3. **Existing customers: the account prompt.** "Please enter either your phone number, including area code, or enter the account number located on your bill, and then press pound." Enter it here.
4. **If it doesn't take,** you hear "We couldn't identify your account by the number you entered" and the same prompt again.
5. **Then it asks residential or business.** "To ask about service for a residential account, press 1. For a business account, press 2."
6. **Then it offers to wait.** "Do you need some extra time to find your account number associated with your Kinetic account? If so, I will wait 30 seconds. Ready to look at your account number? Press 1."
7. **If you still have nothing to enter, it hangs up.** "Sorry, I will now end the call. Goodbye."

We didn't try option 2 on the main menu ("To ask about becoming our customer").

## How we made these recordings

call4me is a phone for your AI agent: your agent (Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT) tells it what to get done and it makes the call. For the first two calls, the brief was to reach a person, without logging in or giving account details, and ask three general questions: whether a residential internet customer can cancel by phone and whether there's an early termination fee, whether the modem has to be returned and how, and (on the support line) whether there are offers for customers thinking about cancelling, or (on the sales line) the best number for an existing customer to reach a person. If a menu asked for an account number, it was to say it had a general question and ask for a representative.

The first two calls failed because of our caller:

- **On the support line it stalled and never asked for a representative.** It only said "One second", "Hang on", "Hmm, one sec" and "Just a quick sec" until Kinetic hung up. That didn't follow its brief.
- **On the sales line it picked the existing customer option.** The brief was to reach sales, and option 1 (new residential deals) was the option for people who aren't customers. It chose option 3 because its questions were about cancelling. That's why this call ended at the same account prompt as the first one.

The third call fixed that. Our caller pressed 1 for new residential deals and offers, introduced itself as my assistant comparing internet providers, and asked about contracts, early termination fees, the modem, and the customer service number. It gave the rep call4me's own number when asked for one in case of a disconnect (muted in the recording). It turned down the rep's offer to transfer us to customer service, so we don't know where that transfer goes. Nothing was ordered or changed.

Our call log has a few short lines from our caller that aren't audible on the recordings, and they're marked in the transcripts.
