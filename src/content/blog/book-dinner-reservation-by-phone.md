To **book a dinner reservation by phone**, give the restaurant the full date, preferred time, party size and reservation name. Ask staff to repeat the booking before hanging up, and ask about any deposit or cancellation terms that matter to your plans.

On October 6, 2026, a call4me customer's AI assistant asked a restaurant for **a table for four on Thursday, October 8 at 7:30 PM**. Staff checked, took a callback number and reservation name, then explicitly confirmed the table for that date and time.

**The table was booked.** No deposit or card was requested in the conversation, but the assistant did not ask staff to confirm cancellation terms. The recording therefore does not establish a free cancellation policy or the absence of later charges. No confirmation number was provided.

## The confirmation is the useful part

A restaurant saying that a time is available is only the start of a booking. Here, staff collected the reservation details and repeated the final date, time and party size. That is the evidence for a completed reservation.

The name needed to be repeated because staff did not hear it clearly the first time. The assistant repeated it and waited for confirmation. When someone is writing a booking into their system, that small clarification is more useful than ending the call as soon as availability sounds promising.

The unanswered cancellation question is also useful. If your evening could change, include that question in the calling brief. A restaurant not asking for a card during one conversation does not answer every policy question.

## Give the caller a booking window and clear limits

1. State the number of guests and the complete date.
2. Give your preferred time and the earliest and latest acceptable alternatives.
3. Provide the reservation name and a callback number privately.
4. Say whether a deposit, card guarantee or cancellation fee is acceptable.
5. Ask for the confirmed date, time, party size and any confirmation reference.

If your assistant is also helping plan the evening, have it return the restaurant's confirmed time before making the rest of the itinerary depend on it.
