**The Phoenician Golf Club is at 6000 East Camelback Road, Scottsdale, Arizona.** For Phoenician golf tee times, The Phoenician's [official golf page](https://www.thephoenician.com/golf/) says "To make a reservation, please click the button below or call" **(480) 423-2450**. That is the number dialed in the call below. For a group, the golf shop transferred the call to the tournament coordinator.

On October 9, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called The Phoenician about a small company golf morning: 12 to 16 golfers, three or four consecutive tee times around 8:00 to 8:30 AM on **Friday, November 20, 2026**, with carts, range balls and something to eat. The budget was about $165 per golfer, all in.

The coordinator said that Friday was **"basically sold out"** in the morning, with the first open tee times at 2:00 PM. The golf rate was **$247 plus tax per golfer**, and he said they could not go lower. He agreed to call back and place a soft hold if a morning slot opened up.

The same customer's assistant also called two other Phoenix area courses where a person answered with real answers: Stonecreek Golf Club, through the Arcis Golf sales office, and Camelback Golf Club at the JW Marriott Camelback Inn. Their answers are compared below.

## What these calls did not settle

- **No tee times were booked or held anywhere.** The Phoenician offered a soft hold only if a morning opened up. Stonecreek's 8:00 to 8:27 block was on the tee sheet during the call, but the recording ends before any hold terms, deposit or written confirmation.
- **No all in price was confirmed.** Every quote was before tax, and Stonecreek's was also before a service charge. None of the three gave a total with tax and service included.
- **The Phoenician did not quote a group package.** Food goes through the hotel, and the $15 to $20 breakfast figure was the coordinator's rough estimate.
- **Camelback gave no package food price, deposit terms or service charge.** It agreed to email information, and the recording does not show whether that email arrived.
- **No Grayhawk price came up.** The assistant mentioned the sales office's earlier email saying Grayhawk was above the budget; the call did not quote Grayhawk.
- **Prices are statements from October 9 and October 12, 2026.** The Phoenician's coordinator said its rates are dynamic.

## Tips for booking Phoenician golf tee times for a group

Ask for the tournament or group coordinator when you have three or more foursomes. At The Phoenician, the golf shop transferred the call straight away.

Ask early about the morning you want. On a November Friday booked six weeks out, The Phoenician's morning was already taken by a large group, regular play and an afternoon tournament.

Ask whether food is part of the golf quote. The Phoenician quoted golf only, with food through the hotel. Stonecreek built food into its per player price. Camelback said food would be arranged with whoever books.

Ask about minimums before you commit. Stonecreek explained a 75 percent minimum: hold 16, and the minimum is 12.
