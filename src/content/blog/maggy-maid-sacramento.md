**Maggy Maid of Sacramento's number is (916) 277-9047.** That's the number on its [official Sacramento page](https://maggymaid.com/sacramento/), which also lists the company wide line (888) 624-4962. Maggy Maid calls itself "a referral agency": it refers vetted cleaners rather than describing them as its own staff. Maggy Maid's service areas around Sacramento, per that page, include Midtown, East Sacramento, Land Park, Natomas, Elk Grove and Roseville. The page says pricing is flat rate, "set for your home", based on "your home's size and condition". It posts no dollar amounts.

So we have numbers. On October 1, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called that line to get recurring cleaning quotes for a two story apartment in Midtown Sacramento: 2 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms, about 1,014 square feet, three cats and no dogs. It asked for prices only and booked nothing.

A recorded greeting played first. **A person answered about 21 seconds into the call**, with no menu to get through. She quoted:

- **First service: $315.** Then, per visit: **$220 weekly, $236 biweekly, $284 monthly.**
- **Deep clean first if it's been over 3 months.** The assistant said the last professional cleaning was about four months ago, and she suggested a **$375 deep clean** for the first visit. For six months or more she "highly suggest[s]" a double deep clean.

At the same time, the assistant placed a second call to [Belleza's Home Services & Management](https://bellezashomeservices.com/) at **(916) 469-1075**, the number on that company's own site, with the same home details. Its prices came in lower. Both calls are below in full.

## Get house cleaning quotes by phone

Quote calls go faster when the caller has every detail the office will ask for. Both receptionists asked for the ZIP code, bedrooms, bathrooms and square footage. Maggy Maid also asked about stories, pets and the type of clean, and how long since the last professional cleaning. Belleza's asked how you found them and whether you already have a cleaner. The assistant here didn't know the last cleaning date at first and had to come back with "about four months", which changed the first visit quote.

Have it ask for per visit prices at each frequency, whether the first visit must be a deep clean, what a standard clean covers, and the cancellation rule. Tell it not to book.
