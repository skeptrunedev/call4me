**The short answer:** call the store and ask a person to check, because the answer changes from store to store and day to day. On Saturday, November 21, 2026, at 10:10 am Pacific, a **GameStop in San Diego said it had "10" new PS5 Slim Digital Edition consoles (825GB)** in stock for pickup that day, no Disc Edition, and that "they're being sold pretty fast." That same morning, a **Best Buy Express in Rosemère, Quebec, said PS5s were "sold out partout,"** sold out everywhere, even online. And in Tokyo on November 19, **six stores that answered all said no PS5 Pro**, new or used.

That's 16 calls from three call4me users, each looking for a PS5. Here's what it took:

1. **GameStop, San Diego area: busy twice, then a yes.** Two calls to one GameStop got a recording that all associates were busy. The third, 43 minutes after the first, got an associate who checked and said about 10 Slim Digital units, no Disc, closing at 8 pm. A second GameStop in the San Diego area gave the same busy recording.
2. **Best Buy, Montreal area: sold out.** Best Buy Canada's main line quoted a 14-minute wait. A Best Buy Express in Rosemère picked up right away and said there were none, blaming the GTA release and Black Friday. A bigger Best Buy in Rosemère played a recording that the store was closed.
3. **Tokyo: no PS5 Pro at any store that answered.** 9 calls in Japanese to 7 stores and lines in 17 minutes. Yodobashi Camera, Kojima x Bic Camera, Bic Camera, Sofmap's used-stock line and two Janpara shops all said no. GEO's phone system said it doesn't take game stock questions by phone. Nojima never gave our caller an answer.

Each region is one call4me user: one calling stores in the San Diego area, one calling stores in the Montreal area, and one calling stores in Tokyo. Our caller didn't buy, reserve or pay for anything. Stores are named by brand and city only. Store staff aren't named, and no one's name or phone number comes up in either recording. The two spots where a store's location is spoken are muted in the audio and shown in brackets in the transcripts. Stock and prices are what each store said that day, and they can change any hour.

## Best Buy: PS5 sold out in Quebec, "even online"

Call 6 lasted a minute and a half. The associate at the Best Buy Express in Rosemère said there were no new PS5s, "parce que depuis qu'il y a eu la sortie de GTA, puis pour Black Friday, vraiment sold out partout" (because since GTA came out, and for Black Friday, really sold out everywhere). "Même en ligne, c'est sold out": even online, it's sold out.

When will more come in? "C'est vraiment de la chance," it's really luck. Units come in and people come straight in to get them. The associate's advice was to watch the website, and as soon as a PS5 shows as available, "vous la commandez en ramassage puis vous venez la chercher": order it for pickup, then come get it.

Our caller asked whether the bigger Best Buy in Rosemère might have one. The associate had no information: "est-ce qu'il y a un monde où ils en ont? Oui" (is there a world where they have some? Before that, call 5 went to Best Buy Canada's main line. ## PS5 Pro in Tokyo: 6 stores said no

The Tokyo calls went out in Japanese, the first four within about a minute, asking for a PS5 Pro. On most of them our caller said "日本語専用モデルではなく、Proです": not the Japanese-language-only model, the Pro. That's a real distinction there. Sony's Japan site says the [PS5 Digital Edition 日本語専用](https://www.playstation.com/ja-jp/ps5/buy-now/ps5-digital-edition-Japanese-only/) lists at 55,000 yen, runs in Japanese only, and works only with PlayStation accounts whose country or region is Japan.

Every store that answered said no:

- **Kojima x Bic Camera** took 28 seconds: "プロは、もう、ずっとないです," the Pro, there just hasn't been any for a long time.
- **Yodobashi Camera** played about two minutes of hold messages. One said yodobashi.com shows each store's stock in real time, and another pushed its store pickup service. Then the game section: "在庫はございません," no stock. The next delivery: "未定です," not decided.
- **Bic Camera** double-checked the model, "プロフェッショナルのプロの方でよろしいですか" (the Pro, as in professional, right?), and checked for about 30 seconds: "当店PS5のProに関しては、今品切れの状況でございまして、次回入荷も未定でございます" (at this store, the PS5 Pro is sold out right now, and the next delivery isn't decided either). Could they hold one when it comes in? "おそらくできないですね," probably not: "基本的にはもうやはり先着順とかにはなってしまうと思います," basically it'll be first come, first served. Its hold message said the Bic Camera app checks store stock.
- **Sofmap's used-stock line** (a Bic Camera Group number just for in-store used stock) put our caller on hold for about a minute and came back with none at any Sofmap store: "新品、中古ともに" (new or used), "全店で、ご用意がございません."
- **One Janpara**: "プロ自体、うち今在庫ないですね" (we don't have the Pro at all right now), "一台も," not a single one.
- **A second Janpara**: "通常モデルしかうち今ないですね," only the regular model right now.

**GEO** never put a person on. Its menu went 2 for games, then 2 for stock and reservations, and then a recording: "ゲームの在庫、予約については、誠に申し訳ございませんが、お電話でのお問い合わせはお受けしておりません" (we're very sorry, but we don't take phone inquiries about game stock or reservations). Check in the store or in the GEO app.

**Nojima** was called twice, at 2:33 and 2:37 pm. Both times our system flagged a call-screening prompt as our caller started talking, and our transcript has no reply from the store.

So the Japan calls didn't fail. They got clear answers, and the answer was no. For more calls to Japanese stores in Japanese, see [how to check store stock in Japan](/blog/check-store-stock-in-japan).

## How to check if a PS5 is in stock near you before Black Friday

Everything here comes from what these stores said on these calls.

1. **Call again when the line is busy.** The San Diego GameStop sent two calls to "all store associates are currently assisting other customers." The third call, 43 minutes after the first, had a person on the line 27 seconds in.
2. **Name the exact console.** "A new PlayStation 5 Slim Digital Edition 825 gigabyte" got a check and a count. Ask about the Disc Edition and the Pro separately: the GameStop with 10 Digital units had no Disc. In Japan, say you want the Pro and not the Japanese-language-only model.
3. **Ask "roughly how many?"** "10" plus "they're being sold pretty fast" tells you to go today.
4. **Ask what time they close.** It's the deadline for your trip: GameStop said 8 pm.
5. **Don't count on a hold.** Bic Camera said a hold after a restock would probably not be possible, first come, first served, and EB Games said it "definitely cannot" hold a launch game without a preorder. Best Buy Express's advice was to order online for pickup the moment a PS5 shows up. Some stores do hold other things: see [how to ask a store to hold an item](/blog/ask-a-store-to-hold-an-item).
6. **Try the smaller store.** Best Buy Canada's main line quoted 14 minutes, and the bigger Rosemère store's line played a closed recording. The Best Buy Express in Rosemère picked up within seconds and had an answer 10 seconds into the call.
7. **Use the app when the phone won't help.** GEO's phone system sends game stock questions to its app. Yodobashi's and Bic Camera's hold messages point to their website and app for stock by store.
8. **Ask about used, and use a chain's central line.** Sofmap's used-stock line checked every Sofmap store in about a minute.

## Where our caller fell short

- **At Nojima, it talked first.** On both calls it started speaking as the line connected, our system flagged a call-screening prompt, and our transcript never has the store's side. We can't tell from the transcript whether anyone at the store heard the question. A third try later, after letting the line speak first, might have worked.
- **At Best Buy Canada, the call left the queue.** With a 14-minute estimate, that's a call to leave on hold. call4me can [wait in the queue for you](/hold-for-me) and bring back the answer.
- **At Kojima x Bic Camera, it stopped at "no."** The call lasted 28 seconds, and it didn't ask about a restock date, which Yodobashi and Bic Camera both answered.

## What to say: a script that worked

> Hi, just wondering, do you have a new [PS5 model] physically in stock for pickup today? Roughly how many? And what time do you close?

That's close to word for word what got the GameStop answer. If they ask whether you ordered it, say you're hoping to pick one up today and are just checking stock.
