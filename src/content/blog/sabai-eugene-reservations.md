**Sabai in Eugene accepts reservations by phone at (541) 654 5424.** The restaurant is at **27 Oakway Center, Eugene, Oregon**. Its [official reservations page](https://www.sabaieugene.com/reservations) welcomes walk ins and recommends reservations.

On October 10, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called to book dinner for two on Friday, October 16, preferably around 7:00 PM. It reached the phone menu and reservation queue, waited while the restaurant handled other reservations, then spoke to a staff member.

Staff offered 6:45 PM or 7:15 PM. The assistant chose **7:15 PM**, which was within the customer's allowed 6:30 to 7:30 PM window. The representative took the booking details and repeated the final date, time and party size.

## Why a time window helps

A request for exactly 7:00 PM could have required the assistant to ask the customer what to do next. Here, the acceptable window was already part of the brief. When staff offered two alternatives, the assistant could choose one and finish the booking during the same conversation.

That flexibility should come from the customer. Tell the caller whether an earlier dinner, a later dinner or a different day is acceptable. Give separate limits for time, seating and party size instead of assuming that flexibility in one area applies to everything.

Waiting was also part of the work. This recording shows the assistant staying through one real reservation queue. It is not a claim about Sabai's usual wait, the average length of an AI call or how quickly another restaurant will answer.
