## What the Wyoming consent form is for

The consent records that the named registered agent agrees to serve as the agent for your business. The state's [registered agent guidance](https://sos.wyo.gov/Business/Docs/HowToFindOrBecomeARegisteredAgent.pdf) says signed consent must accompany new entity filings. For online filing, the filer certifies that they have received similar written consent and keeps it for future reference.

That makes it different from your Articles of Organization. The [Wyoming LLC filing form](https://sos.wyo.gov/Forms/Business/LLC/LLC-ArticlesOrganization.pdf) includes the consent page, but the articles and the agent's consent serve different purposes. A copy of your registered agent order or a verbal agreement on the phone is also a different document.

When requesting the consent, ask for it to be completed for your exact LLC name and the registered agent's exact legal name. The official form has fields for the agent's physical registered office address, signature, date, printed name, title, phone and email, plus a mailing address if different.

The signature belongs to the registered agent. If you are using a provider, ask them to supply the completed consent rather than signing for them.

## How to request your signed consent

Start with your provider's account portal. If the completed consent is already available, download it and keep it with your filing records. Otherwise, contact the provider through its published support channel and ask for the specific document.

You can use this request by phone or in a support message:

> Please send the signed and dated registered agent consent for my Wyoming LLC. It should name our LLC exactly as it will appear on the filing and identify the registered agent that is consenting to serve. Please email the completed document to the address on our account, or upload it to our account's documents section.

Have the LLC name, provider account details and delivery destination ready. If the support business name differs from the legal registered agent name on your filing, make that distinction explicit. Ask the provider to confirm which entity will appear on the consent.

When the file arrives, check:

1. The LLC name matches your filing.
2. The registered agent name and physical office address match the agent you appointed.
3. The agent's signature, date, printed name, title and required email are present.
4. The document states the agent's consent to serve for your business and includes the compliance certification shown on the official form.
5. You have saved the completed document with your records.

If a detail is wrong or missing, ask the provider to correct it before relying on the document. Obtaining the consent is one part of the filing process. Check your formation separately in the [Wyoming Business Center](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/Default.aspx).
