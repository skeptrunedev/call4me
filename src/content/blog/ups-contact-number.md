**The short answer:** the UPS customer service number, which is also the main UPS phone number and contact number, is **1-800-742-5877** (1-800-PICK-UPS). UPS lists its hours as "Monday–Friday: 7 AM – 11 PM ET, and Saturday: 8 AM – 8 PM ET" ([ups.com](https://www.ups.com/us/en/support/contact-us)). A voice menu answers. To reach a person, say **"change my delivery"**, then say **"representative"** twice at the tracking number prompt, then say whether you're the **receiver or sender**. On our call, a person picked up about 6 minutes in, after about 3 minutes 20 seconds of hold music.

We tested this on Thursday, October 1, 2026, at 12:33 pm and 12:52 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) placing the calls. We asked one question: how do you change or intercept a UPS delivery that's already on the way, who can do it, and what does it cost? The first call never reached a person. The second did. This page is what we heard.

What the UPS agent told us:

1. **Both the sender and the receiver can change a delivery.** "Both can do it from their side."
2. **You need the tracking number.** It usually starts with 1Z and is 18 characters long, the phone menu said. The agent also mentioned numbers starting with 92. If you're the receiver and don't have it, ask the sender.
3. **The receiver can do it online.** Go to ups.com or the UPS My Choice app, enter the tracking number, pick the delivery change option, then choose from the options, which include changing the address and holding the package at a UPS Access Point.
4. **It may cost something.** "Sometimes it has a cost and sometimes it doesn't have a cost." The agent's figures: about **$7.99** if UPS does it for you, and about **$5.99** if you do it yourself on the website, including holding it at an Access Point. More on the price [below](#what-it-costs-to-change-a-ups-delivery).

## The UPS customer service number's phone tree, step by step

This is the path that reached a person on October 1, 2026.

1. **Call 1-800-742-5877.** "Thanks for calling UPS. Para español, marque dos." Say "English" or just wait.
2. **"Can we text to the mobile phone you're using?"** UPS offers to text you links during the call. Yes or no both move on.
3. **"How may I help you today?"** Say what you need in a few words. We said we wanted to change a delivery that's already on the way.
4. **"Which sounds right for you?"** For us it offered: 1, change delivery. 2, intercept a package I sent. 3, number of delivery attempts. 4, none of those. Pick **1** to change a delivery. Our caller picked 3 and heard about delivery attempts first.
5. **Say "change my delivery."**
6. **"Does your tracking number begin with 1Z?"** If you have the tracking number, give it. If you don't, say **"representative."**
7. The menu answers: "I understand you'd like to speak to someone. I can help right away once you share your tracking number." Say **"representative"** again.
8. **"Let me connect you to a live agent."** It asks if you're the receiver or the sender. Say which.
9. **Notices, then hold.** A survey invitation, a recording notice, a notice that "AI assisted technology may be used to help our representatives", "Please hold", a pitch for ups.com, then music.
10. **A person picks up.** On our call that was 5:57 into the recording, about 3 minutes 20 seconds after "Please hold". The agent asked for a name, a tracking number, and again whether we were the shipper or the receiver.

## What it costs to change a UPS delivery

Two sources, two different sets of numbers.

**What the UPS agent said on the phone.** "Sometimes it has a cost and sometimes it doesn't have a cost." Without a tracking number they couldn't tell us our case. "Most of the cases, you have to pay a charge of $7.99" if UPS does it, "but if you are doing it on your website, it would cost you around $5.99." Holding the package at a UPS Access Point: "in most of the cases it's not free", "$5.99 if you do it by yourself." The agent was talking to us as the receiver.

**What UPS publishes.** UPS's [list of domestic accessorial charges](https://assets.ups.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:f8d97e17-7ef9-48af-b8ce-b5c9bbf0be6a/original/as/preview-accessorial-us-en.pdf) prices **Delivery Intercept at $27.00 by phone and $21.00 on the web**. Delivery Intercept is the service the **shipper** requests to reroute, hold or return a package already in transit. UPS's intercept page says "you'll only be charged if the request is completed".

We don't know which number applies to your package. The published $27 and $21 are the shipper's Delivery Intercept fee. The agent's $7.99 and $5.99 were what they told a receiver, without looking up a package. Your price may also depend on the shipper and on your account. If you have the tracking number, ask for the price before you confirm the change.

## What to have ready before you call

- The **tracking number**. Most start with 1Z and are 18 characters long. The menu reads them one character at a time.
- Whether you're the **receiver or the sender**. The menu asks, and so does the agent.
- If you're the receiver without a tracking number: **ask the sender for it first.** Both the menu and the agent told us that.
- What you want done: a **new address**, **hold at a UPS Access Point**, or something else.

## Things that don't work

On October 1, 2026, these sent our caller in circles:

- **Saying "none of those" at the first numbered menu.** It goes to "Let's try another way" and the main list of options.
- **Saying "something else" at the main list.** You get quotes, refunds, billing, main menu, or something else. "Main menu" takes you back to the main list.
- **Telling the tracking number prompt you don't have one.** In both calls, "We don't have a tracking number" got "I'm sorry, I didn't understand that."
- **Asking for a representative in a long sentence.** "Could I just get to a representative to ask about intercepting or rerouting a delivery in transit?" got a new numbered menu instead of a person.
- **Picking "change delivery" from the follow up menus.** It read out another menu with change delivery as option 1, and choosing it again repeated that menu.

What worked was the single word "representative," said twice, at the tracking number prompt.

## How we made these recordings

call4me is a phone for your AI agent: your agent (Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT) tells it what to get done and it makes the call. These were our own test calls. We had no package to change. We asked only how the process works and what it costs.

On the first call our caller kept taking the options the menu offered and never reached a person, so we hung up after about 4 minutes. That was a bug on our side. We changed the caller so that when a spoken phone menu keeps offering routes that don't fit, it asks for a representative. The second call, about 18 minutes later, reached a person.
