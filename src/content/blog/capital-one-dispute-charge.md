**The short answer:** to dispute a charge on a Capital One credit card by phone, call **1-800-CAPITAL (1-800-227-4825)**, which Capital One's [contact page](https://www.capitalone.com/contact/) lists for "Capital One credit card customer service", "For servicing of existing Capital One accounts". Capital One's [dispute page](https://www.capitalone.com/help-center/fraud-disputes/dispute-credit-charge/) says to "Call the phone number on the back of your card", and the representative we reached confirmed: **"Yes, this is the correct number for that."** He said this line is **open 24/7**.

How it went on our call, with no card:

1. At "Please say or enter your 16 digit card number", **say "I don't have a Capital One card."** The menu says: "If you do not have a Capital One card, if it is lost or stolen, or you need time to search for it, just let me know." Cardholders enter the card number instead.
2. "Let's get you to someone who can help." **A live representative answered 47 seconds into the call.**

What the representative told us:

- **The phone team takes the dispute and submits it.** "We will investigate the charge that you need to dispute, and then after that, we'll provide you with the necessary steps." Capital One will "manage the communications with the merchant if necessary."
- **What they may ask for:** "their date of birth, their first and last name, their credit card number, possibly. But if only needed, their social security number or the last four digits of it."
- **Provisional credits:** "Capital One does provide a provisional credit." It "can take up to three business days for the credits to post."
- **Calling may beat the app.** You can file in the Capital One app, but "we also recommend them giving us a call instead, so we can insert all of the information with regards to the dispute."
- **Hours:** the general servicing team is "open 24-7." The **specialist dispute team**, for ongoing cases, is open "Sunday to Saturday, 7am to 1am Eastern Time."

**About deadlines:** the representative said you can dispute "so long as it is visible on our end." Capital One's own page is stricter: file "ideally within 60 days of the statement date." That 60 days is also the window for your federal billing error rights (see [how to dispute a charge](/blog/how-to-dispute-a-charge)).

## What Capital One's dispute page says

Capital One's [Credit card dispute process](https://www.capitalone.com/help-center/fraud-disputes/dispute-credit-charge/) page:

- **Ways to file:** in the app or on the website, "Select the transaction from your transactions list" and answer the questions; or call the number on the back of your card; or "Complete, print and mail the dispute form to" **Capital One Disputes, P.O. Box 30279, Salt Lake City, UT 84130-0279.**
- **When:** "ideally within 60 days of the statement date." Capital One may still help after that.
- **How long:** "The dispute process may take up to 90 days to reach a resolution."
- **Temporary credit:** "Capital One may provide a temporary credit on your account for the disputed amount," and may hold that amount against your available credit.
- **Evidence:** "Receipts and invoices", "Shipping or tracking info", "Photos of the product or service", and "Communications with the merchant regarding the issue."

## What to have ready

- **Your 16-digit card number.** It's the first prompt.
- **Your full name and date of birth.** The representative listed both.
- **Your Social Security number or its last four,** "if only needed."
- **The charge:** merchant, date, amount, and what went wrong.
- **Your evidence:** receipts, tracking, photos, and emails with the merchant.
