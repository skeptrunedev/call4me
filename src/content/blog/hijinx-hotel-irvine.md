**To change a Hijinx Hotel Irvine booking with the wrong mix of adults and children, call the venue with the booking reference and ask staff to check the exact session before changing it.** Hijinx Hotel Irvine is at Irvine Spectrum, 517 Spectrum Center Drive, Irvine, California. The [official location page](https://hijinxhotel.com/locations/irvine-spectrum) describes teams of two to six players and recommends booking ahead.

On October 10, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called about that evening's 7:30 PM session. The booking listed eight adults. The actual group was four adults and four children. Staff found the booking, agreed that it could be adjusted, and said they would email confirmation after the change.

The conversation also answered two practical questions: whether the children could attend that session, and how eight people would play when a room takes at most six. **The email itself was not verified, and the call does not show the group attending.**

The customer's brief mentioned an 8:00 PM cutoff. The employee instead stated 9:00 PM. When checked on October 11, the [published house rules](https://hijinxhotel.com/house-rules) also required guests entering from 9:00 PM to be over 21. The useful result was a specific answer about the booked session, not an assumption based on the original brief. Check the current rules and your own session directly before buying tickets for children.

## What to have ready before calling Hijinx Hotel

Have the booking name, reference, date and start time available privately. Explain what is wrong with the existing booking and the replacement headcount. For a mixed age group, give the children's ages and ask whether the restriction applies to arrival, the activity or remaining at the venue afterward.

For a group larger than one room, ask how staff will divide the players and whether the groups still do the same activity set. On this call, staff suggested two groups of four as a way to split the party. That answer does not guarantee another time slot or a particular allocation on a different booking.

No additional payment or price adjustment was settled in this conversation. If changing ticket categories could affect the amount paid, ask staff to explain that before authorizing a charge.
