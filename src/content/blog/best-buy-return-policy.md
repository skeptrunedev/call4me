**The short answer:** the **Best Buy return policy** gives **15 days for most products**, counted from "the day you receive your product". **My Best Buy Plus and My Best Buy Total members get 60 days.** Activatable devices like phones get 14 days, and Verizon activatable devices get 30 days for everyone. **Past the window, a Best Buy agent told us "there's only one exception that can be done": a store manager can approve it at their own discretion, "only if the item is unopened."** Calling doesn't help. "You would need to be at the store physically."

**Without a receipt,** another agent said Best Buy finds the purchase with "just your phone number, first and last name, and email," and that not finding it is "highly impossible." Best Buy's policy page is stricter about returns with no proof of purchase: those "will be in the form of store credit," and "may be denied or limited."

We called Best Buy customer service at **1-888-237-8289** (1-888-BEST BUY) twice on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at about 5:24 and 5:31 pm Central, with [call4me](/) placing the calls. They were general questions for this guide, with no order or account involved. **An AI assistant answers first.** Ask it for a live agent and it transfers you: **a person answered at 3:15** on the second call and at 4:19 on the first.

## What Best Buy's return policy says

From Best Buy's [return and exchange policy](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/help-topics/return-exchange-policy/pcmcat260800050014.c), checked October 24, 2026 (the page shows "Effective date: February 23, 2026"):

| | Standard | My Best Buy Plus and Total members |
|---|---|---|
| Most products | 15 days | 60 days |
| Activatable devices | 14 days | 14 days |
| Verizon activatable devices | 30 days | 30 days |

- **When the clock starts:** "the time period begins the day you receive your product."
- **Longer windows for everyone:** litter boxes 90 days; pet training devices and hearing aids 60 days.
- **Shorter:** "Products in Halloween Decorations and Christmas Decorations categories can be returned within 15 days for all customers," members included.
- **Marketplace:** "My Best Buy Plus and My Best Buy Total extended return timeframes do not apply to any Marketplace purchases."
- **Restocking fees:** $45 on activatable devices (not prepaid phones), and 15% of the price on drones, digital cameras, camera lenses, camcorders, projectors, golf launch monitors, mobility scooters, special-order products and a few other categories. "There is no restocking fee if the product is unopened," or for purchases in AL, CO, HI, IA, MS, OH, OK or SC.
- **No proof of purchase:** "Reimbursements on returns lacking proof of purchase may require an email address, will be in the form of store credit, may be denied or limited, and state sales taxes and fees will not be reimbursed."
- **Refunds:** Best Buy "will reimburse you for returned items in the same way you paid for them." Mailed returns are processed in 7 to 10 business days. Cash over $800, or a check or non-logo debit card over $250, is refunded by check within 10 business days.
- **Where:** "Return any in-store or online purchase from Best Buy to any Best Buy store," or ship it with a free prepaid FedEx label from your account. Major appliances, TVs over 50 inches, kegerators and SodaStream makers can't be shipped back.

The policy page doesn't describe exceptions past the window or mention a manager. That part comes from the calls.

## What the Best Buy agents told us

From the second call, about the return window:

- **Exceptions are a store manager's call.** "If it is an exception after the return window has been ended, there's only one exception that can be done, and it is mostly done by a store manager. But also, it is not something compulsory. They do then do that out of their own discretion, and only if the item is then unopened."
- **A phone agent can't approve one.** Asked whether she could, or only a store manager: "It's only a store manager."
- **Go in person.** Asked whether calling helps: "No, I'm so sorry. So, mostly, you would need to be at the store physically to then have that done."
- **Defective just past the window: warranty first.** "We would advise reaching out to the manufacturer first, and if maybe they are not able to help, then if you do have a plan, or a membership, or extended protection through Best Buy, we do then assist," or Geek Squad helps with the repair.
- **Membership windows:** "The normal Best Buy customer, you do have a standard return window of just 15 days." With Plus or Total, she said "an additional 60 days." The policy page says 60 days in total, not 60 on top of 15.

From the first call, about receipts and refunds:

- **No receipt:** to look up a purchase, "just your phone number, first and last name, and email."
- **If it can't be found:** "That's highly impossible, ma'am. So, if you made a purchase at Best Buy, either if it's an over-the-counter purchase, online order, or store order, the purchase will always be on the system." She added that you "need to have a profile."
- **Refunds** go "back to the original form of payment." "24 hours for our end to process the funds," then a "maximum of 7 to 10 business days for the money to reflect back into your account."
- **Online orders:** start the return "on the Best Buy website or on the app," then print a shipping label.

## How to return something to Best Buy after the window

Putting the policy and the calls together:

1. **Check your window first.** 15 days, or 60 if you're a Plus or Total member. It starts the day you received the item. If you're inside it, no exception is needed.
2. **If it's unopened, go to a store and ask for the store manager.** That's the one exception the agent described, at the manager's discretion. Bring the item in its packaging and the card you paid with.
3. **Don't expect the phone line to approve it.** Both the AI assistant and the agent pointed to the store or bestbuy.com.
4. **If it's defective, use the warranty.** Contact the manufacturer first. If you have a Geek Squad protection plan or a Best Buy membership, Best Buy may repair it.

## Returning to Best Buy without a receipt

- **Your Best Buy account is the receipt.** The agent said a purchase is found with your phone number, name and email. The policy page asks you to bring "your packing slip (if you received one), or your receipt," and the credit card you paid with, "for faster processing."
- **If there's truly no proof of purchase,** the policy page says you get store credit, maybe not the full amount, and no sales tax back, and it can be refused. Neither agent mentioned that, so it's worth knowing before you go.

## Getting to a person at Best Buy

1. **Call 1-888-237-8289.** "Hi, thanks for calling Best Buy. I'm your AI customer care assistant." It's a voice assistant, not a keypad menu.
2. **Say what it's about and ask for a live agent.** On both calls, saying it was about a return and asking for a live agent got a transfer. The second time it asked "making changes to an order, a return, or something else?" first, and offered to text a link to bestbuy.com/returns.
3. **Hold.** A recorded notice plays about Pokemon cards and Zelda inventory, then "There's a short wait right now." About a minute on the second call, about 2 minutes on the first.

The AI assistant checks the number you call from: "I don't see an account associated with the number you're calling from." If you call from the phone on your Best Buy account, it may find your orders. We didn't test that. Best Buy's policy page lists the number. We didn't find phone hours on bestbuy.com, and both of our calls went through on a Saturday evening. For how Best Buy compares with other companies we've called, see our [hold times](/hold-times).

## Where our caller fell short

- **On the first call, it never asked the agent about exceptions.** The AI assistant said it couldn't answer that and transferred us for exactly that question. Once a person answered, our caller asked about receipts, refunds and online returns, and then ended the call. That's why we called a second time (call `call_v47988kj4uwlr41c`, the first recording above).
- **It didn't push on store credit.** The policy page says no-proof returns get store credit. Our caller asked about it, but took "we are then able to assist" and moved on.
- **It didn't correct "an additional 60 days."** The policy says 60 days in total.
