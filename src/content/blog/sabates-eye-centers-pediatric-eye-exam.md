**Sabates Eye Centers' Leawood office is at 11261 Nall Avenue, Leawood, Kansas 66211.** Its [official Leawood page](https://www.sabateseye.com/locations/leawood/) lists **(913) 261-2020** and hours of Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. That is also the only number on the [Sabates Eye Centers contact page](https://www.sabateseye.com/contact-us/). Press **1** to schedule, then answer a second menu about insurance. On these calls a scheduler answered about **1 minute 15 seconds** after the line picked up.

If your child is on **TRICARE**, the short version from the calls is: **get the referral first, and make sure it names the Sabates doctor.** A scheduler said that without one the visit is self-pay, "starts around three fifteen," and needs a signed TRICARE waiver.

Between October 8 and October 13, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant made these calls to book a routine eye exam for a five year old new patient at Leawood. Two calls on October 8 found the slots and the referral rule. A third call asked the child's primary care office for the referral. A fourth call on October 13 booked **Tuesday, January 12, 2027 at 8:30 AM with Dr. Baumfalk**.

## The Sabates Eye Center phone menu

1. **Call (913) 261-2020.** A recording notice plays, then "Thank you for calling Sabates Eye Centers."
2. **Press 1** at "To schedule, reschedule, or confirm an appointment, press one." The other options are referring providers (2), prescription refills (3), medical questions (4), medical records (5), patient billing (6), optical (7) and the surgical center (8).
3. **Answer the insurance menu.** It offers employer insurance (1), Medicare (2), Medicaid (3) or no insurance (4). TRICARE isn't one of the choices. Key presses don't show up in these recordings, so they don't show which key the assistant pressed.
4. **A scheduler answers.** After a survey notice, a person in scheduling picked up at 1:12 on the October 13 call and at 1:16 on both October 8 calls.

The two October 8 calls dialed (913) 395-6591, a number from the customer's own request. It reached the same Sabates scheduling menu, but it isn't listed on the Sabates website. Use (913) 261-2020.

## What these calls did not settle

- **Referral coverage for January 12 was not confirmed.** Asked whether the referral on file covers the date, the scheduler said "it should," that the insurance department would reach out if not, and that they would "try to get the authorization taken care of before her appointment."
- **The referral's arrival wasn't checked.** The primary care office said it would start and submit it. No call confirmed Sabates received it.
- **The $315 self-pay figure is a starting point,** not a quote. It "goes up from there," and no exam components were priced.
- **No confirmation number was given,** and nothing here shows the exam took place.
- **"Thursday, July seventh"** is what the scheduler said on October 8. That date doesn't fall on a Thursday in 2027, so treat it as unclear.
- **The Sabates website doesn't say which doctors see children.** Dr. Baumfalk's [profile](https://www.sabateseye.com/doctors/ramona-baumfalk-od/) lists her as an optometrist at Leawood. That she sees pediatric patients comes from the schedulers on these calls.
- On the October 8 calls, a scheduler asked whether the TRICARE sponsor is active duty and whether the plan is Prime, Standard or West. The answer about the sponsor is muted.

The customer's assistant also made four other calls to Sabates in this stretch that aren't published here. One dropped while the scheduler was collecting details. Three stalled for different reasons: the customer couldn't be connected, the assistant gave a date of birth that wasn't the child's, and it didn't have the mailing address. On that last call the scheduler said the system "won't let me save without an address." **Have the child's full name, date of birth, mailing address and TRICARE details ready before you call.**

## Booking a TRICARE eye exam for your child

The order that worked here was: find out which Sabates doctor sees children at your office, get the referral naming that doctor, then book. Booking before the referral exists isn't blocked outright (the scheduler checked and said she could book two weeks out), but without the referral the visit is billed as self-pay.

When you ask the primary care office for the referral, give the Sabates office and the doctor's name, and say it's a routine eye exam. The coordinator here asked for the child's birthday, name, where to send it and the appointment date.

When you book, have ready everything the new chart asked for: the child's full name and date of birth, mailing address, a phone number, the primary care doctor, and the TRICARE ID. Ask whether the referral covers the appointment date, and plan for the visit to include dilation. For what an exam costs without coverage elsewhere, see [eye exam cost without insurance](/blog/eye-exam-cost-without-insurance).
