## Asking about tonight without booking

When you call a barbershop to ask "do you have anything tonight", many shops will simply put you down. That is helpful if you want the slot. It is a problem if you only wanted to compare options, because the barber may stay late or turn away someone else for you.

If you only want availability and price, tell your assistant what to say when the shop starts booking: thank them, say you are still deciding, and promise to call back to confirm. If you are happy to take the slot, say so up front and give the name to book under. Either way, ask for the barber's name and whether they want a phone number for the booking.

If the slot is booked and you end up running behind, see how to [tell your barber you are running late](/blog/running-late-to-barber). For a regular booking, see [how to book a haircut appointment](/blog/book-haircut-appointment).
