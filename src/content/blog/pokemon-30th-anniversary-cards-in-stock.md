## Who had Pokémon 30th Anniversary cards in stock in Nov 2026

**A Shoppers Drug Mart in Waterloo had the Knock Out Collection on November 11.** Our caller asked for the "Pokémon thirtieth Knockout Collection, like, physically in stock right now." The associate repeated the name and said "Give me a second." After two holds, about three minutes in all: "Hi, we do have, yeah, we do have one in stock, but we don't put it on hold." The price? "No, we would have to scan that... you would be able to check it online." A purchase limit? "We just have one in stock."

When our caller tried that store again on November 20, nobody picked up before the call ended. Three other Shoppers Drug Marts in Waterloo said no. The second store: "We do not have any Pokémon thirtieth at the moment." The third: "We don't at the moment. I'm so sorry." The fourth: "No we don't."

**A Lids in Kitchener had the 30th ETB at $200 on November 11.** That was its only 30th product: no Ultra-Premium Collection. The associate didn't know if more was coming: "We don't order the product, it's just allocated to us."

**A Lids in Guelph had one 30th Celebration Battle Deck on November 20.** "Right now, we only have just the Delta... Oh, no, we do have, yeah, like the box. Yeah, thirtieth Celebration Battle Deck, we do have that right now." The price: "it's forty dollars." It was the only 30th box left. The rest, the associate said, was Delta Reign and other non-30th products. No Ultra-Premium Collection or booster bundles: "No, not at the moment."

**An EB Games in Kitchener had the Ultra-Premium Collection on November 25.** "Yes," for walk-in purchase that day, at "three hundred and eighty." Two weeks earlier, none of the three Lids stores had one.

**No 30th stock:** the Lids in Waterloo ("I don't have any Pokémon 30th... everything I have coming in is Delta") and the Lids in Cambridge ("I don't have any of the new release stuff," asked about the Ultra-Premium Collection, the Ditto Premium Collection and the Mew and Mewtwo figure collection).

## Restock days: no store knew

No store gave a restock day or time. The closest was "weekly."

- **Lids, Waterloo:** "They don't tell us when anything comes in." Not later that day: "It'll be another day." Its advice: "You can or you can just come into the store and check."
- **Lids, Kitchener:** "It's just allocated to us," with no tracking information.
- **Lids, Cambridge:** "We can get it at any point within the next like three weeks. We never actually get these on the actual date of the release."
- **Lids, Guelph:** "It's pretty often, I believe it'd be weekly... I'd say maybe check back next week."

The Cambridge answer matches what we saw. On November 11, five days after the Ultra-Premium Collection's date on Pokémon's US site, none of the three Lids stores had one.

Every Shoppers Drug Mart our caller tried had the same phone menu: a flu shot message, then **1** for prescription refills, **2** for health care providers, **3** for other pharmacy questions, **4** for "customer service, including beauty department and post office," **5** for hours, **6** for the management team. After 4, a second menu: **1** for customer service, **2** for beauty, **3** for postal services.

On the four calls that reached a person, it took about 70 to 95 seconds from the greeting to a human voice. At the first Waterloo store on November 11, the associate then put our caller on hold twice to go check, about three minutes in all. The greetings also gave that day's hours: on Friday, November 20, two of the Waterloo stores said they were open until midnight.

This part is from Pokémon's own pages, not the calls. Pokémon calls the line **30th Celebration**. Staff understood "30th anniversary" fine, and the associate at the Lids in Guelph said "thirtieth Celebration Battle Deck" on their own. [Pokémon's 30th Celebration product showcase](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-product-showcase) lists, among others:

- **Knock Out Collection:** a foil Eevee card, a plastic coin and two packs. Available September 16, 2026.

- **Booster Bundle:** six packs. October 2.
- **Battle Deck, Espeon ex or Umbreon ex:** an all-foil 60-card deck with a deck box, coin and playmat. October 30.

- **Ditto Premium Collection** and **Figure Collection, Mew & Mewtwo:** November 6.
- **Binder Collection:** December 4, 2026, the only one on that page still to come.

The page doesn't list prices. Those are dates from Pokémon's US site. The "Delta" cards two Lids stores mentioned are a different expansion, [Mega Evolution Delta Reign](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/the-pokemon-tcg-mega-evolution-delta-reign-expansion-arrives-november-6-2026), which Pokémon says arrived November 6, 2026.

## Tips for finding Pokémon cards in stock before Black Friday and Christmas

Built only from what these calls showed:

1. **Name the exact product.** "Thirtieth ETB," "thirtieth Celebration Battle Deck," "30th Knockout Collection": every associate knew the products by name, and asking for a named product got the Shoppers associate to go check the shelf.
2. **Ask what's physically on the shelf right now.** The Shoppers associate went to check and came back with "we do have one in stock." Two Lids stores had other Pokémon cards and few or no 30th boxes, so ask for the 30th specifically.
3. **Don't count on a hold.** None of the four stores asked would hold one. If a store says yes, go.
4. **Don't wait for a restock date.** Lids stores said stock is "allocated," they get no tracking, and it can come up to three weeks after release. Call again, or do what the Lids in Waterloo said: "just come into the store and check."
5. **Get the Shoppers price online or at the till.** "We would have to scan that... you would be able to check it online."
6. **At Shoppers Drug Mart, press 4, then 1, and allow 3 minutes.** The menus take more than a minute before a person answers.
7. **Call several stores at once.** The three Lids calls ran at the same time and each was done in about a minute.
8. **Don't stop at Lids and Shoppers.** On November 11 no Lids store had the Ultra-Premium Collection. On November 25, EB Games did.
9. **Use the Best Buy app for Best Buy.** Its phone line says agents can't give Pokémon inventory or release information.

## Where our caller fell short

- **Two Shoppers calls ended before anyone picked up.** The greeting and menus took 70 to 95 seconds on the calls that got through, and calls 8 and 10 ended just under 2 minutes in. Each call needed more time.
- **It heard a yes that wasn't one.** At the Lids in Waterloo, our caller asked if more stock was coming, heard "Yeah," and asked when. The associate meant the Delta cards: "We just got them in."
- **It didn't ask enough at the stores that had stock.** At EB Games it asked about one product and the price, not how many, a limit, or tax. At the Lids in Kitchener it didn't ask about the Ditto or Mew & Mewtwo collections. At the Shoppers stores on November 20 it asked only about the Knock Out Collection.
- **At Topps, it wouldn't give a first name.** It introduced itself as "with assistant for the customer," which isn't a sentence, and repeated it while the representative asked for a name four times. If an account needs verifying, plan to take the call yourself, or give your assistant a name it can use.

## What to say: a script that worked

> Hi, quick question. Do you have the Pokémon TCG 30th Anniversary Ultra-Premium Collection in stock for walk-in purchase today? And what's the price on it?

That got "Yes" and "three hundred and eighty" in 29 seconds at EB Games. Then ask what our caller didn't: "How many do you have, and is there a limit per person? Is that before tax? If you sell out, when do you usually get more?"
