**To order a cake from Café Demi in Teaneck, call (551) 501-2865 and press 2 for the café at the recorded menu.** Café Demi is at 1400 Palisade Ave., Teaneck, NJ 07666. That is the number on [the Café Demi's official website](https://www.thecafedemi.com), and it is the number both calls below dialed. On both calls a staff member picked up about 34 seconds after the call connected.

On Friday, October 16, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called Café Demi twice. The customer wanted a whole Pecan Maple dairy-free cake in New Milford, the next town over, that same day. The first call checked the price, asked about delivery and put a cake on hold. The second call, about an hour later, paid for two cakes by card and reconfirmed a courier pickup.

## Café Demi hours and location

The [official site](https://www.thecafedemi.com) lists one location, 1400 Palisade Ave. in Teaneck (next door to the therapy gym), with these hours:

| Day | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Sunday to Thursday | 8:00 am to 4:30 pm |
| Friday | 8:00 am to three hours before Shabbat |
| Saturday | Closed |

The site also says Café Demi is fully kosher and certified by the RCBC. Neither call discussed kosher certification. Online orders go through the link on the site, and the phone menu says the same: "To place an order, please visit our website."

## The Café Demi phone menu

1. **Call (551) 501-2865.** A recording says "Welcome to Café Demi. To place an order, please visit our website at www.thecafedemi.com."
2. **"For hours and operations, dial 1."**
3. **"For the café, please dial 2."** Both calls pressed 2.
4. **"For catering inquiries, please email us at info at thecafedemi.com."**

A person answered "Café Demi" right after the menu, about 0:34 into each recording. There was no queue message.

### What the calls did not establish

- **That the courier pickup happened.** The recordings end when the calls end. They don't show the cakes leaving the café.
- **The hold length.** The first call asked for about an hour. Staff said yes to holding one but did not state a time.
- **How the order grew from one cake to two.** The first call held one. The second call asked to pay for two that were being held, and staff quoted for two without objecting. Whatever happened between the calls is not in the recordings.
- **A written receipt.** Staff said the receipt would go in the bag. No receipt or confirmation number was read out.
- **Ingredients or kosher status.** The assistant asked for the cake by its dairy-free name and staff said they had it. Nobody discussed ingredients, allergens or certification on either call.
- **Delivery through Uber Eats or Grubhub.** Staff suggested trying those apps. The assistant did not check them.

## Paying a café by phone for courier pickup

The useful part of these calls is the order of operations. The first call found out whether a courier pickup would work before anyone paid. Only after staff said a courier could come in and give a name did the second call hand over a card.

A few things made the second call go smoothly:

- **A hard spending limit.** The assistant had a $48 ceiling and said so. Staff quoted $46.90, under the limit, and charged that amount.
- **One name for everything.** The hold, the payment and the courier pickup all used the same name. Staff said there was no order number, so the name is the only thing the courier has to give.
- **A pickup deadline said out loud.** The assistant asked staff to confirm the courier would come before 2:15 PM, and staff agreed. On a Friday, Café Demi closes three hours before Shabbat, so a same day pickup has a real cutoff.

Card details read over the phone are sensitive. In this recording they are muted. If you have an assistant pay this way, a single use or low limit card keeps the risk small, and you should only give card details to a person at the business, never to a menu or voicemail.
