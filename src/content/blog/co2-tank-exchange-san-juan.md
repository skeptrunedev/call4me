**The short answer:** Hydro Warehouse PR does a **20 lb CO2 tank exchange** at **44-46 Calle Progreso, inside Corona Comercial Park in Santurce, San Juan, PR 00907**. Its [official website](https://hydrowarehousepr.com) lists **(787) 506-8385** and **(787) 688-8384**, and hours of **Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM**, closed Saturday and Sunday. The assistant dialed (787) 506-8385, the first number on that site, and a person answered with the store's name in the first second. There is no phone menu.

What staff told a call4me customer's assistant on Friday, October 16, 2026:

- **It's an exchange, not a refill.** You bring your empty tank and leave with a full one. They don't refill your own cylinder.
- **On the spot, while you wait.** "No, es de inmediato. Yo tengo los tanques llenos." (No, it's immediate. I have the full tanks.)
- **$50.18 total.** "Cuarenta y cinco dólares más tax, cincuenta con dieciocho." That's $45 plus tax. When the assistant asked about a deposit or extra charges, staff said "correcto" to $50.18 with none.
- **20 lb only.** "Solamente trabajo el tamaño de veinte libras." A full tank to buy outright is **$350 plus tax**.
- **Your tank's hydro test must be within 3 years.** Staff said the empty has to be "certificado, al día", and confirmed that means a hydrostatic test date within three years.
- **Food grade CO2 with a CGA 320 valve.** Staff said "sí" when the assistant asked about both.
- **The full tank comes sealed** with a plastic seal from the gas company, not a cap.
- **Someone else can bring the tank.** A driver can drop the empty and take the full one, paying in person and showing ID.
- **Pay in person.** Staff preferred that over paying by phone, and said they don't offer or recommend a delivery or courier service.
- **Finding it:** staff described the store as inside the Corona industrial complex, with a "Cervecería Corona" sign outside and a gated entrance. "Si viene y se acerca y le preguntan, le dejan pasar." (If you come up to it and they ask you, they let you through.)

Staff also said they speak English, so you don't have to call in Spanish.

The assistant then called two local delivery services to take the tank across town. One quoted **$120** and was booked for a pickup about an hour later. The other quoted **$200** by PayPal and was not booked. The rest of this page has the details and all five recordings.

## How the exchange works

Ask about a **co2 tank exchange** rather than a refill. When the assistant asked whether they could refill the customer's own tank, staff corrected it: "No es un relleno, es un intercambio." (It's not a refill, it's an exchange.) If you need your own cylinder filled and returned, this store is the wrong stop. If you just need a full 20 lb tank, it's quick.

Check the hydro test stamp before you go. Staff's first condition, repeated in both calls, was that the tank be "certificado, al día", and the follow-up call pinned that down to a test date within three years. The calls didn't cover what happens to a tank that fails, beyond the assistant's own plan to bring it back unpaid.

Bring cash or a card and an ID if you're sending someone. Payment is in person, and staff said a driver can do the swap as long as they pay there and show identification. Staff didn't ask for a reference name in the answer, though at the end of the second call they asked for the customer's name and the assistant gave it.
