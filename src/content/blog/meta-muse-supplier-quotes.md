## Define the order before asking for a winner

Adapt this brief after supplying the missing buyer details. The [complete text download](/static/blog/resources/meta-muse-supplier-quotes/supplier-research-prompt.txt) retains the detailed calling sequence.

> Prepare inquiries for 25 brown corrugated boxes advertised as 12 by 12 by 12 inches, rated 32 ECT. Use the three listings above. Ask me for the destination, deadline, required inside dimensions and approved caller identity. Verify Call4me access without dialing and find each supplier's sales number on its official site. Show me the recipients, sources and questions before requesting approval for those calls, each limited to five minutes. Ask about the exact SKU, pack quantity, merchandise, shipping, taxes, surcharges, arrival and quote validity. Follow each approved call to completion and return the transcript and available recording. Separate phone answers from published prices and unresolved costs. Do not order, subscribe, accept terms, leave voicemail or arrange callbacks. Keep the delivered total unknown while required charges are missing.

A phone answer may be an oral estimate or a request for a written proposal. Record who answered and any conditions; an automated price, voicemail or incomplete answer is not a firm delivered quote.
