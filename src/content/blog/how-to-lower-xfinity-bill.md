**The short answer:** to lower an Xfinity bill, the account holder calls **1-800-934-6489** (1-800-XFINITY), says **"billing"** when the voice assistant asks what the call is about, and asks the billing department for a new promotion, a lower internet speed, and the autopay discount. **A billing representative answered 6 minutes 29 seconds into our recording.** He said "the offer will come from the billing department," since "all the billings are handled from the billing department, mobile billing, internet billing, cable billing."

What he said can lower the bill, on Saturday, October 24, 2026:

- **A new promotion when yours ends.** "If a customer [is] on the promotion which is ending... after that, the new promotion will be updated on the account."
- **A lower speed.** For streaming, TV and email, "500 Mbps will be good for them." He quoted 500 Mbps promotions of "$65, $70, $75," depending on the area and term, and a 1 gig promotion "of $80 monthly." These were examples, "the promotion depends on the area."
- **Autopay and paperless billing.** "If customer turn on the AutoPay with the checking account, then also there will be [an] AutoPay paperless billing discount of $10."
- **A smaller TV package.** He listed a $65 package with "around 30-plus" basic channels, TV+ at $105 with "125-plus channels," TV Premium with "185-plus," and Sports & News at about "$90 and $95." An entertainment add-on was "$11 extra."
- **A mobile line.** Moving a cell phone line to Xfinity Mobile: "she will get a line for the next one year $0," and then a promotion "in which the bill is going to be only $30."

Xfinity's own [autopay and paperless billing page](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/automatic-payment-paperless-billing) confirms the $10: you get "a $10 discount on your monthly bill" if you "have Xfinity Internet" and "sign up for automatic payments and paperless billing with a stored bank account." It says "the discount starts within 45 days after you sign up" and "it may take up to two bills to show up." The prices above are the representative's examples, not quotes for any account. We're not Xfinity customers and gave no account details.

**Can it be done by phone?** "Yeah, this packages, this changes, we can do it on the phone," he said, "if customer have the details of the account."

We called on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at about 6:27 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking general questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Xfinity number to call

Xfinity's [rights and contact page](https://www.xfinity.com/support/articles/rights) gives **1-800-xfinity (1-800-934-6489)** "for inquiries related to residential services." Xfinity Mobile has its own number on that page, **1-888-936-4968**. We only called the residential line.

Asked about hours, the representative said "customer [can] call at any time," including Saturdays. On our [earlier Xfinity customer service call](/blog/xfinity-customer-service-number), a different representative said agents are "available anytime." Xfinity's page doesn't list hours.

## What to have ready

From the representative:

- **Your Xfinity account number**, or "if the customer don't have account number, we ask for the service address."
- **A way to verify.** "If they register any [cell] phone number or email, we send a 6-digit verification on that." Or, if a bank account is stored on the account, "we ask for the last 4-digit to verify it."
- **Your current speed and TV package**, and what you actually use. His advice on speed depended on whether you stream, watch TV or just email.

## How we got through the voice assistant

Xfinity's menu is an "AI virtual voice assistant," and it wanted an account number before anything else. This is what finally reached a person:

1. **"Hello, and thank you for calling Xfinity."** An ad for a free Xfinity Mobile line, then "I was unable to find an account with your phone number."
2. **"Please say or enter the ZIP code where you have or want service."** Our caller didn't enter one, and the menu moved on.
3. **Press 1** for home services, then **press 1** again as an existing customer. Key presses don't show up in the recording; the next prompts are for existing customers.
4. **"I'll need a phone number or account number to look you up."** As the account holder, give yours here. Without one, we were asked for the account number four times. "Representative, please," "Agent," and "Speak to a person" each got "I understand you'd like to speak to an agent, but I need a little more information."
5. **"Are you calling to activate equipment you recently received?"** No.
6. **An Internet Essentials offer** for people on SNAP, Medicaid or the National School Lunch Program. It played three times while our caller kept asking for a person.
7. **"Is this about billing, making a payment, or an internet issue?"** Say **"billing."** "I am connecting you with an agent who can help."
8. **Text chat offer:** "Press 1 to connect with an agent via text messaging." Otherwise, press 2.
9. **"Please wait for the next available representative."** A person answered about 46 seconds later.

Our caller lost time by asking for an agent before naming a reason. Say "billing" as soon as you can. On our earlier call, choosing internet, then "something else," then "billing" reached a person in 3 minutes 27 seconds.

> Hi, I'm the account holder. My promotion [ended / ends on date] and my bill is now [amount]. I mostly use the internet for [streaming, work calls, email]. What promotions can you put me on? Would a lower speed save me money, and am I getting the autopay and paperless discount? I'm also looking at [competitor and price].
