**AI appointment booking starts with a clear decision: which time can your assistant accept on your behalf?** Give it the business, complete dates, acceptable alternatives and any cost limits. After the call, ask for the business's explicit confirmation and the questions still open.

## Decide whether to book, replace or notify

A scheduling request can require different actions. Make yours explicit before the assistant calls.

| Your situation | What to ask the assistant to do | What counts as the result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| You need a new appointment | Book one time within your stated limits | Staff explicitly confirms the booking details |
| You want to move an existing visit | Check its current status, then request the authorized change | The new slot and the old slot's status are both clear |
| You are running late | Give your expected arrival and ask for instructions | The desk's actual response, including any conditions |

An available time is an option. A confirmed booking is a commitment from the business. A message delivered to the desk is an update. Keeping those outcomes separate makes the assistant's report useful when you plan what happens next.

## Give your assistant the choices you would make

Use complete dates and the business's local timezone. Say whether the same provider or location is required. If several times work, specify how to choose: earliest calendar date, earliest time that day, or closest to your original visit.

For an existing appointment, provide its date, time and reference privately. Have the assistant check whether it is still active before changing it. If you want the original slot preserved until a replacement is available, put that instruction in the brief. The office may have its own process; an unresolved conflict should come back to you.

Also specify whether the caller can accept a deposit, cancellation fee, different provider or different location. Supply only the identity and contact details you authorize it to share. Check the saved caller profile as well as the prompt, since a profile can affect the caller's introduction.

Here is a suggested prompt to adapt:

> Use call4me to call [business and verified phone number] about [appointment purpose]. My acceptable dates and local time windows are [full dates, times and timezone]. I prefer [selection rule], and [provider and location requirements] must stay the same. The current booking is [details, or no existing booking]. First check its status. You may book one appointment within these limits using [authorized name and callback details]. For an active booking, ask to preserve it until a replacement is confirmed. Ask about deposits, cancellation terms and arrival instructions before agreeing. My permission for charges is [specific limit or none]. If a required detail, alternative or charge falls outside these instructions, collect the options and ask me. Return what staff confirmed, the original booking's status, unanswered questions and the available transcript or recording. Ask separately before changing my calendar.

## Check the result before changing your plans

The call ending successfully tells you that the conversation ended. Use the [confirmation checklist](/static/blog/resources/ai-personal-assistant-appointment-booking/confirmation-checklist.md) to inspect the actual scheduling outcome:

1. Did the assistant reach the intended business, location and scheduling desk?
2. Did staff explicitly confirm a booking, offer an option, acknowledge a message or request a callback?
3. Are the complete date, local time, timezone, provider or party size and location clear?
4. If there was an existing booking, is its final status known?
5. Which deposit, cancellation and arrival questions were answered, and which remain open?
6. What exact words or transcript passage support the result?
7. Is anything still waiting on written confirmation, your decision or another call?

Have your assistant put those answers back into the conversation where you planned the task. A concise result might contain: **status, confirmed details, evidence, unresolved questions, next decision**. This is a suggested reporting format, not a claim that these customer calls wrote to a planning app.

If you also want a calendar event, treat that as a separate action using the confirmed details and your permission. The phone recording establishes what the business said. It does not establish a calendar write, receipt of a text or attendance at the visit.
