**The short answer:** Capital One's general customer service number is **1-877-383-4802**. Capital One's [contact page](https://www.capitalone.com/contact/) says: "Not sure where to start? Call us at 1-877-383-4802 so we can direct your call to the right person." The same page lists **1-800-CAPITAL (227-4825)** for "Capital One credit card customer service", "For servicing of existing Capital One accounts". The contact page doesn't list hours. **The representative we reached said the line runs 7 am to 12 midnight Eastern.**

A spoken menu answers, not a person. This got us to a live representative:

1. At "Which of these can I help you with? Credit cards, bank account, auto loans, or CreditWise?", **say "credit cards"**. It transfers you.
2. At "Please say or enter your full 16-digit card number", we said "representative". It asked for the card number again, and said: "If you do not have a Capital One card, if it is lost or stolen, or you need time to search for it, just let me know."
3. **Say you don't have a card** (our caller said "I don't have an account"). It answered "Let's get you to someone who can help."

**A live Capital One representative answered 1 minute 22 seconds into the call.** There was no hold message.

What the representative told us:

- **Hours: 7 am to midnight Eastern.** "We start working at 7 a.m., and we close at 12 midnight, but Eastern time." That's for this line, which he called "the core service, so it's the main service."
- **Customers go through the automated system first.** "We have an IVR system, or automated system, when you need to put your information so we can identify your account." If that doesn't work, "then you will be connected with a real agent."
- **Callbacks are possible, if you ask.** "Sometimes, yes, we can," but "only if the customer asks us to call him later."
- **What they use to find your account:** your full name and ZIP code; if that doesn't work, your phone number; if that doesn't work, your Social Security number; plus your date of birth "to verify your identity."
- **A credit card dispute "can take up to 90 days."** Some merchants respond "in a couple of days, even sometimes on the same day." While it's open, Capital One issues credits for the amount "so that it doesn't impact what you owe," but they "may not apply to the available credit for you to spend." You get a letter with the result, and depending on it the credits "will become permanent."

We called on Friday, October 16, 2026, at 3:30 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Capital One customer service phone tree, step by step

This is the menu as it played on October 16, 2026, calling 1-877-383-4802.

1. **Call 1-877-383-4802.** "Thanks for calling Capital One. Para español, oprima el ocho." Then: "This call may be monitored and recorded for quality assurance, training and authentication purposes."
2. **"Which of these can I help you with? Credit cards, bank account, auto loans, or CreditWise?"** It's a spoken menu. We said "credit cards".
3. **"Okay, credit card. Please hold on and I'll transfer you."** A second greeting plays, this time with "Para español, oprima el dos".
4. **"Please say or enter your full 16-digit card number."** Customers enter their card number here. We said "representative", and it didn't transfer us.
5. **"Our agents will be able to help you much faster if I verify your card information first. If you do not have a Capital One card, if it is lost or stolen, or you need time to search for it, just let me know. Otherwise, please say or enter your 16-digit card number now."**
6. **We said we don't have an account.** "Let's get you to someone who can help."
7. **A person,** about 10 seconds later: "Hi, thank you for calling Capital One. This is [name removed]." He asked for a first and last name.

We only tried the credit cards branch, and only as a caller without a card. We don't know how the bank account, auto loans or CreditWise branches go, or whether a lost or stolen card goes to the same team.

## What to have ready

- **Your 16-digit card number,** if you're calling about a credit card. It's the first thing the credit card line asks for, and the representative said the automated system uses your information to identify your account before you reach an agent.
- **Your full name and ZIP code.** That's what the representative said they ask for first if the automated system doesn't identify you.
- **Your phone number, and your Social Security number** as a fallback, if the name and ZIP code don't find your account.
- **Your date of birth,** to verify your identity.

## Things that don't work

On October 16, 2026, at the card number prompt:

- **Saying "representative".** It didn't transfer us. It said agents can help "much faster" if it verifies your card first, and asked for the card number again.

Saying we didn't have an account is what got us through. If you do have a card, the representative's answer suggests the menu wants you to enter it, and that a failed lookup is what sends you to a person.
