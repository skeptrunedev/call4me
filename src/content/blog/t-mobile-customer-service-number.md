**The short answer:** the T-Mobile customer service number is **1-800-937-8997**. From a T-Mobile phone, you can **dial 611** instead. **An automated voice menu answers, not a person.** When it asks for your mobile number, say **"I don't have one"** (it asks twice). When it offers payment, tech support, billing or something else, say **"something else."** **A live representative answered 1 minute 49 seconds into our recording**, about 1 minute 52 seconds after we dialed, and about 20 seconds after the menu said it was connecting us. There was no hold music.

T-Mobile's [contact us page](https://www.t-mobile.com/contact-us) lists it this way: "Customer Service: Dial 611 from your T-Mobile phone or call: 1-800-937-8997." The same page says "Customer Care Representatives are available daily from 4 a.m. to 9 p.m. Pacific Time," and "Live agent chat is available in T-Life from 4 a.m. until 12 a.m. Pacific Time."

Here's the route that reached a person, with no account and no T-Mobile number:

1. Call 1-800-937-8997. After "Welcome to T-Mobile," a Spanish option and a privacy notice, the menu says: "To get started, please enter or say your mobile number, area code first, or say, I don't have one."
2. **Say "I don't have one."** It asks again: "To look up your account, I'll need the 10-digit mobile number you're calling about." **Say "I don't have one"** again.
3. **"Tell me, how can I help you today?"** Our caller started explaining, and the menu asked again: "In a few words, describe what you need."
4. We said **"Representative."** The menu didn't transfer us. It answered with a list instead: "Are you calling about a payment? Do you need tech support? Have a billing question? Or something else?"
5. **Say "something else."** "I'll get you right over to someone who can help. We care about your experience, so we may record this call."
6. About 12 seconds of quiet, then a person.

The first words from a live person, at **1:49** in the recording:

> "Hi, thank you for calling T-Mobile. This is [name removed]. How can I help?"

**What the representative told us** (the rep's words, not anything we checked against T-Mobile's policies):

- **Hours:** we asked whether care reps are available every day from 4 a.m. to 9 p.m. Pacific time. The rep said "Um, yes." That matches the contact page.
- **Billing credits and disputes:** asked how long they typically take to resolve, the rep said "around 7 to 10 business days."
- **Calling when you're not a customer yet:** "the automated system will ask them. There's a prompt, and they just have to follow it." That's the whole answer. The rep didn't say where the prompt sends you.

We called on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, at about 11:50 am Pacific, with [call4me](/) placing the call. [GitHub Copilot CLI](/blog/github-copilot-phone-calls) placed it through the call4me MCP server. It was a general question for this guide, not about any account, and we didn't say we were a T-Mobile customer. This page is what we heard.

## The T-Mobile phone tree, step by step

It's a speech menu. You can answer out loud, and the first prompt also says "enter," so the keypad works for the mobile number. We only used our voice.

1. **Call 1-800-937-8997.** "Welcome to T-Mobile." Then "Para español, marque asterisco," and a privacy notice: "As part of this call and subsequent interactions from this call, T-Mobile may collect information about you to provide the best service possible."
2. **The mobile number prompt.** "To get started, please enter or say your mobile number, area code first, or say, I don't have one." We said "I don't have one."
3. **The same prompt again.** "To look up your account, I'll need the 10-digit mobile number you're calling about. You can enter or say it now, area code first, or say, I don't have one." We said it again. After the second time, it moved on.
4. **"Tell me, how can I help you today?"** Our caller answered with a full sentence about a consumer guide. The menu replied "Let's try again. In a few words, describe what you need. You can say things like, I want to make a payment, I need to check my account balance, or whatever the reason is for your call."
5. **"Representative" didn't skip ahead.** The menu said "Let's try by choosing one of those options. Are you calling about a payment? Do you need tech support? Have a billing question? Or something else?"
6. **"Something else" got us through.** "I'll get you right over to someone who can help. We care about your experience, so we may record this call."
7. **A person answered** about 12 seconds after that message ended. The line was quiet the whole time. There was no music, no wait estimate and no callback offer.

If you call with your T-Mobile number, the menu will look up your account first, and the route may be different. We didn't test that.

## What the representative said, in detail

These are the rep's answers, as recorded.

**Hours.** Our caller asked "are care reps available every day from 4 a.m. to 9 p.m. Pacific time?" and the rep said "Um, yes." The rep confirmed our question rather than stating the hours. T-Mobile's contact page says the same thing: "Customer Care Representatives are available daily from 4 a.m. to 9 p.m. Pacific Time."

**People who aren't customers yet.** The rep didn't catch the first question ("I'm sorry?"), so our caller asked again. The answer: "Actually, the automated system will ask them. There's a prompt, and they just have to follow it." On our call, the prompt that mattered was saying "I don't have one" at the mobile number question. The contact page also lists Consumer Sales at 1-844-222-1938 for shopping, which we didn't call.

**Billing credits and disputes.** "Um, actually, around 7 to 10 business days." That's the rep's estimate. We didn't find a timeline on the contact page.

## What we didn't find out

- **Whether phone or T-Life chat is faster.** Our caller had this question but didn't ask it, so we have no answer from T-Mobile. The contact page lists longer hours for chat (4 a.m. until 12 a.m. Pacific) than for phone reps (4 a.m. to 9 p.m. Pacific).
- **Where the menu sends people who aren't customers yet.** The rep's answer was "follow the prompt," without naming a team.
- **Whether "Representative" ever works on its own.** On our call it led to the list of options, not a transfer.
- **Whether the wait is ever longer.** Ours was about 20 seconds from "I'll get you right over" to a person, on a Tuesday just before noon Pacific time.
- **What happens if you call with a T-Mobile number,** or dial 611 from a T-Mobile phone.
