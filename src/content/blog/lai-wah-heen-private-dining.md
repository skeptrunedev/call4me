**To book private dining at Lai Wah Heen, call the restaurant at (416) 260 8988.** It is at 108 Chestnut Street in Toronto, and its [official private dining page](https://laiwahheen.com/events/) lists several event spaces and gives that contact number. When arranging a group dinner, ask for the exact room, seated capacity, spending minimum and final total before treating a reservation as a finished event plan.

On October 9, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called about a private dinner for 12 to 14 people on Thursday, November 19, from approximately 6:00 to 9:00 PM. The caller asked for a courtesy hold without a deposit. Staff collected the booking details and explicitly said the reservation was made, with further details to follow.

**That is evidence of a reservation, not a settled C$140 per person dinner package.** The final menu, wine allowance, tax treatment, cancellation terms and written proposal were not confirmed in the recording.

## The room discussion and the unresolved capacity question

The employee discussed Silk Road, Lune and Sol. These are the amounts spoken on October 9, not current offers or a final bill:

| Silk Road | C$1,200 minimum plus 20% service charge | The full package and tax were not priced |
| Lune | C$1,500 minimum | The employee suggested it for roughly 12 to 13 guests; final charges were not itemized |
| Sol | C$2,500 minimum plus 20% service charge | Recommended in the conversation for 14 guests |

The caller then requested the Lune room for a group still described as 12 to 14. Staff made the reservation, but did not resolve whether 14 people would fit at one seated table. The [published room descriptions](https://laiwahheen.com/events/), checked October 11, list Lune for 12 seated guests and Silk Road for 14. **Confirm the final headcount and room in writing if the group grows beyond 12.**

The employee compared the customer's proposed C$140 per person budget with the room minimum. That comparison did not establish whether tax, wine and service were included. A spending minimum and a complete event quote answer different questions. Our [private dining cost guide](/blog/private-dining-room-cost) explains what to ask about each charge.

## What was reserved, and what still needed an answer

The useful result was getting the conversation past general availability. Staff entered the customer's name, contact details, company and requested evening into the booking system and said it was set up. No card or deposit was provided during the call.

The next step was still a written proposal. That should identify the room, final seated headcount, menu, drinks, minimum spend, tax, service charge, deposit deadline and cancellation terms. The call does not establish that the proposal arrived, that no deposit would ever be due, or that the quoted budget was accepted as a total price.
