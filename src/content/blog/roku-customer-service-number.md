## Roku never charges to activate: how to spot a fake Roku number

Searches like "Roku customer service number" are full of numbers that aren't Roku's. Roku's own [scam warning page](https://support.roku.com/article/protect-yourself-from-scams) says scammers build fake sites that "give you a fake support phone number to call," and that these sites "may even show up at the top of search results because scammers pay for ads." What it says to watch for:

- **Activation fees.** "Roku has never charged money to activate a device. If a site says your code failed and asks for payment, it's a scam."
- **Account fees.** "Creating a Roku account is 100% free. If someone tries to charge you for this, hang up."
- **Paid setup help or "support plans".** Roku offers free setup help, and it calls monthly or yearly tech support plans fake.
- **Activating your TV with their account,** then demanding money to "unlock" it.
- **Remote access.** "Never let anyone connect to your computer or install software."
- **Personal details.** "Roku support will never ask for your Social Security number, passwords, or to install software on your computer."

Roku's page says real Roku sites "always end in roku.com", such as support.roku.com and my.roku.com. The only US phone number we found on Roku's site is 816-272-8106. If you're handed a different "Roku" number, especially alongside a fee, don't call it.

And if "Roku" calls you out of the blue, be careful: the agent told us Roku doesn't normally call customers, only "if there's, like, a technical issue or a billing issue."

## Cancelling a subscription billed through Roku

You can do it by phone or yourself. The agent said Roku will cancel subscriptions it bills for you over the phone, with two exceptions he named, Hulu and Disney+, which "has the option to cancel" themselves.

Roku's [manage or cancel subscriptions page](https://support.roku.com/article/manage-or-cancel-subscriptions) gives the self-serve routes:

- **On the web:** go to **my.roku.com/subscriptions**, choose the subscription, select **Manage subscription**, then **Turn off auto-renew**.
- **On your Roku device:** press Home, highlight the app, press the **Star button** on your remote, select **Manage subscription**, then **Turn off auto-renew**.
- **You keep access until the end of the billing period** after you cancel.
- **Disney+, Hulu and Sling TV:** contact them directly "even if Roku is billing you". Roku lists Disney+ at 888-905-7888, Hulu at 877-824-4858 and Sling TV at 888-394-6698. We've called Hulu: see our [Hulu customer service guide](/blog/hulu-customer-service-number).

Roku's [refund policy](https://support.roku.com/article/refund-policy) says content and subscriptions bought directly through Roku are "pre-paid, final, and non-refundable," with no refunds for partial terms. Free trials have to be cancelled before they end. For more on cancelling by phone, see [cancel a subscription](/cancel-subscription).
