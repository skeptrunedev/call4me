## What this call did not settle

This was a walk-in check, not a booking. No appointment time, confirmation number or price was given, and staff didn't say how long a walk-in would wait. The call also didn't establish what was causing the leak, whether the tire could be repaired, or that the customer later went in. The store page lists flat repair and tire inspection among its services, but the call itself didn't discuss what a check would include.

"Walk-ins all day" was the answer for that Thursday. It's a reasonable sign the store handles drop-in tire checks, but it isn't a promise about every day or every kind of job. If you need a set time, ask to book one; the store page also offers online appointments.

## Calling a tire shop about a slow leak

A slow leak is the easy kind of tire call: the car is drivable, so the only real question is when the shop can look at it. The assistant led with the vehicle, which tire, and that it was losing air but not flat. That gave staff enough to answer in one word.

For your own call, add what matters to you before dialing. If you need a time slot instead of a walk-in, say so. If you want a rough idea of the wait or what a repair might cost, ask before hanging up, since this call didn't cover either.
