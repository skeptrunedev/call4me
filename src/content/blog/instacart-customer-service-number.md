**The short answer:** Instacart's customer service number is **1-888-246-7822**. Instacart's [Help Center "Get help" page](https://www.instacart.com/help/section/809794019/1992925010) lists it under "Get support for your order placed on Instacart": "To get help through Instacart: ... Call 1-888-246-7822." The page doesn't list hours. **The agent we reached said live agents are on the phones 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.**

Two key presses got us to a person:

1. At "Press 1 to continue in English. Press 2 for Spanish", **press 1**.
2. At "If you are calling about your customer account, press 1. If you are a shopper, press 2", **press 1**.
3. "Please hold while we transfer you."

**A live Instacart agent answered 24 seconds into the call.** There was no voice assistant and no account number prompt on our call.

**Is this the real Instacart number?** Yes: it's the number on Instacart's own Help Center, and we called it on October 22, 2026 and reached a live Instacart support agent. Also written 1 (888) 246-7822 or 888.246.7822.

What the agent told us:

- **Phone support is 24/7.** "We are on 24 hours, seven days a week. There is no time that the live agents are not on call."
- **This number is the way to reach a person.** "By phone to reach us the quickest is only through the phone number that is provided by Instacart." She added that "at times, you will be put through to a chat or an AI that will ask you for some questions." We didn't get one.
- **What they use to find your account:** your email or phone number, and your order number if you're calling about an order. If you call from the phone number on your account, they may find it "without even asking."
- **Missing or damaged items:** report them in the app, from the order's help option, or call this number and a live agent can "process a resolution."
- **Credits show up right away.** They "appear immediately" in your account "under the credits, gift cards, and promos page," equal to the charge for the item, and apply "automatically on your next order at checkout."
- **A refund to your card takes 7 to 10 business days,** "depends on your financial institution."
- **You can cancel Instacart+ over the phone,** but the agent has to transfer you: "we do have to put you through to our membership team."
- **No callback instead of waiting.** Asked about a callback option, she said holds after you reach an agent last "a maximum of two minutes" before they come back, and "if they are disconnected, we do call them back."

We called on Thursday, October 22, 2026, at 1:09 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Instacart customer service phone tree, step by step

This is the menu as it played on October 22, 2026, calling 1-888-246-7822.

1. **Call 1-888-246-7822.** "Press 1 to continue in English. Press 2 for Spanish." We pressed 1.
2. **"If you are calling about your customer account, press 1. If you are a shopper, press 2."** We pressed 1.
3. **"Please hold while we transfer you."** About 6 seconds of quiet.
4. **A person:** "Thank you for calling Instacart. You're through to [name removed]. How can I assist you today?" That was 24 seconds into the call.

We only tried the customer branch. We don't know how the shopper branch goes, and the agent said some callers get "a chat or an AI" first, which we didn't hear.

Instacart's Help Center also has a [senior and specialized support line](https://www.instacart.com/help/section/3760654223) "for customers who may need additional help setting up an account or placing an order". It says a live specialist answers **1-844-981-3433** daily from 8 am to 11 pm Eastern. We didn't call that one.

## What to have ready

- **The email or phone number on your Instacart account.** That's what the agent said they normally use to find you. Calling from the phone number on your account may let them find it without asking.
- **Your order number,** if you're calling about an order.
- **Which item was missing or damaged,** and whether you want Instacart credit (shows up immediately, applies at your next checkout) or a refund to your card (7 to 10 business days).

If you'd rather not call, the agent pointed to the app: go to your order and use the help option to report an item issue.

## Cancelling Instacart+ by phone

The agent said it "can be done over the phone," but the customer support agent doesn't do it: "we do have to put you through to our membership team. We do have membership team agents that handle cancellation of memberships." We didn't ask to be transferred, so we don't know how long that second wait is.
