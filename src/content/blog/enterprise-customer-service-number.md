**The Enterprise customer service number in the US is 1 855 266 9565.** Enterprise lists it on its [official contact page](https://www.enterprise.com/en/help/contact.html). We called on **November 2, 2026**, using [call4me](/), and reached a person about **1 minute 24 seconds** into the recording, after a voice menu asked four questions about our reason for calling.

We asked general questions for this guide about charges, deposits and extending a rental. We did not have a reservation or rental agreement, and no rental was booked, changed or extended. The representative answered the questions, but some of the answers were broader than Enterprise's published instructions. The distinctions below matter if you are dealing with your own rental.

## How we reached a person

The greeting asked us to have a rental agreement number ready and said the call could be recorded and analyzed. It offered Spanish by pressing 2. The English route then asked us to answer **yes or no** to four questions:

1. Are you calling to reserve a vehicle?
2. Would you like to update an existing reservation?
3. Are you calling about Enterprise Plus membership?
4. Are you in a rental vehicle and involved in an accident or experiencing mechanical issues?

Our caller answered **no** to each because this was a general inquiry. A survey notice followed, then the system routed us to a representative. We did not use a keypad shortcut to request a person.

Answer those questions for your actual situation. Our route does not establish what happens after a yes response or how long another department takes to answer.

## Deposit or billing question: central support or the branch?

The representative directed our general question to central customer service. Enterprise's [deposit refund FAQ](https://www.enterprise.com/en/car-rental-faqs/us-deposits-and-payments/car-rental-deposit-refunds.html) gives more specific instructions for a real refund or charge:

* **Credit card:** Enterprise says it releases the authorization when the vehicle is returned and charges the final rental amount.
* **Debit card:** The FAQ says the refund usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on the bank. If it has not appeared after 10 business days, it directs you to the branch where you rented.
* **A refund smaller than expected:** Check the rental receipt, then contact the renting branch with questions about the charges.

For those cases, start with the branch named in the FAQ. You can also ask central customer service which team handles your issue. Our general call did not establish that central support can issue every refund or resolve every charge.

Have the **rental agreement or reservation number**, the receipt and a clear description of the charge or missing refund. State whether the vehicle has already been returned. The representative asked us whether the question concerned a past rental, an upcoming rental or general information.

## Who can extend an Enterprise rental?

The person we reached said customer service could help. Enterprise's [US extension FAQ](https://www.enterprise.com/en/car-rental-faqs/us-reservations/extend-rental-reservation.html) separates the route by rental location:

* **Airport rental:** The FAQ directs customers to Enterprise's contact page for help.
* **Rental from a branch outside an airport:** Contact the renting location directly. Enterprise says its number is at the top of the rental contract.

The FAQ says extensions require a phone call, may change the rate and require an additional card authorization. Ask for the new return date, total and authorization amount before agreeing. Our recording provides no evidence that a particular extension would be available or that the original rate would continue.

## What are Enterprise customer service hours?

In our call, the representative checked and said **8 am to 5 pm Central**. We did not get the days of operation, so that answer is incomplete.

Enterprise's contact page separately lists **chat** hours as Monday through Friday, 8 am to 5 pm Central, closed weekends and holidays. It does not label those as phone hours. We would not treat that chat schedule as confirmation of the phone line's full weekly schedule.

Our call reached staff on a Monday morning. It does not verify evening, weekend or holiday phone availability.
