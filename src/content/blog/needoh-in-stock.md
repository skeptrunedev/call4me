**Is NeeDoh in stock?** Mostly not, at the stores we heard from. **Where to buy NeeDoh:** the only NeeDoh on a shelf in any of these calls was the **World's Smallest NeeDoh at a Five Below in Surprise, Arizona**. On the morning of Monday, October 26, 2026, a call4me user near Phoenix had their AI phone assistant call six Phoenix-area stores for NeeDoh squishies, especially the holiday ones. The Kohl's in Avondale said "We have no more, none," and the associate didn't expect more: "I really don't think that we're going to get anymore for this year. Because we got two huge shipments a few weeks ago." The Kohl's in Tolleson had none and no restock news. None of the stores that answered had a holiday NeeDoh.

To find out if NeeDoh is in stock near you before Black Friday or Christmas, the calls point to one method: phone each store when it opens, ask a person to check the shelf and the back, and ask when the next truck comes. Below are all 7 NeeDoh calls, then a second hunt for a hard-to-find holiday buy from November 2026:

1. **NeeDoh, Phoenix area, 7 calls:** two Kohl's had none, one Five Below had only the World's Smallest, and four calls ended without an answer.

The calls were made by two call4me users: one near Phoenix (NeeDoh) and one in the Jacksonville, Florida area (the energy drink). Our caller didn't buy anything or give a card. ## Where to buy NeeDoh: what Kohl's, Five Below and Walmart said

**At Avondale, Kohl's phone system answered the NeeDoh question before a person did.** Each Kohl's call during store hours opened with an automated assistant ("This call may be monitored or recorded by Kohl's and our AI-powered service providers"). At Avondale, when our caller asked about NeeDohs, it had an answer ready:

> Any NeeDohs or other squishy toys received by the store will be placed on the sales floor for purchase. We are unable to honor customer holds or reservations for these items.

So at that store, a phone call can tell you whether NeeDohs are there, but it can't get one set aside. Asking for "someone who can physically check the shelves in toys" got a transfer: "I'll connect you to our children and toys department so a store associate can check the shelves for you."

- **Avondale:** none, plenty of other squishies, and the "two huge shipments" quote above.
- **Tolleson:** the associate listed other squishies they had (the names are hard to make out in the recording), confirmed dumpling squishies "over here in the registers," and on NeeDoh said: "No, they haven't say anything that we're gonna receive any Needoh."
- **Surprise:** the phone system sent our caller to toys, the person who answered transferred it again, and after about 4 more minutes on hold, someone picked up and said, "I did transfer you, but let me try it again, okay? I'm sorry about that." Seven minutes, no answer.

**Five Below had a few NeeDohs, the small ones.** The associate in Surprise picked up almost an hour before opening and could see the shelf. The answer on restocks, "we never know until we break down our pallets," is worth remembering: that store didn't know what a delivery held until it opened the pallets. The Five Below in Goodyear played the same greeting ("Our crew members are busy wowing our customers. Please hold as we transfer your call") and then left our caller on hold for about 3 minutes.

**Walmart's phone line is an AI agent first.** "I'm the Walmart AI agent. I'm not perfect," it said, and offered to text a walmart.com link for "live availability and pricing." When our caller asked for a person who could look at the shelf, it tried a transfer and got: "We're currently experiencing unusually high call volume and we're unable to connect you with an associate at the moment."

## How to find NeeDoh in stock, and other hard-to-find Christmas toys in 2026

Everything here comes from the calls above.

1. **Call at opening, or just before.** At the Five Below in Surprise, an associate picked up at 9:09 am, before the 10 am opening, and could say exactly what was on the shelf. The Kohl's in Avondale at 8:57 am was a closed-store recording. At 9:03 am, its phone system answered and put our caller through to toys.
2. **Ask for the toy department.** "Someone in toys or customer service" got Kohl's phone system to transfer to "our children and toys department." The Jacksonville Beach Target's menu says "To reach a specific department, press two," and then lists "Toys, press eight."
3. **Ask about the back and the truck, not just the shelf.** "None in our back room either," "we never know until we break down our pallets" and "a truck on Monday, like Monday night" are the answers that tell you whether to call again tomorrow or give up.
4. **Name the exact item and what it isn't.** "World's Smallest NeeDoh" and "holiday NeeDoh" are different answers. Our caller said "not the cranberry or sugar plum," and at the fifth Jacksonville Five Below the associate said, "I know which one you're talking about."
5. **Don't plan on a phone hold for NeeDoh at Kohl's.** The phone system said: "We are unable to honor customer holds or reservations for these items." If a store says it has them, go.
6. **Don't trust an app listing for an item a vendor stocks.** The user had seen the drink listed in stock on Target's app the day before, and the store said it wouldn't be out until November 29.
7. **Call a lot of stores at once.** The Jacksonville hunt reached 16 stores in about 13 minutes. Most answers took about a minute, and a quick "no" is useful too.

## Where our caller fell short

- **It hung up too early at the Five Below in St. Johns.** The associate said, "let me go look. Hold on one second," and our caller said goodbye before they came back. On the second call, the associate said, "Yeah, I came back and you're gone."
- **It stayed 7 minutes in transfers at the Kohl's in Surprise.** After the second transfer failed, calling back and asking for toys again might have been quicker.

## What to say: a script that worked

> Hi, quick question. Could someone check the shelf for [exact item and variant]? Not the [similar item]. If you're out, is there any in the back, and do you know when your next truck comes?
