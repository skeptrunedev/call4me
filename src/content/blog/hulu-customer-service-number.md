**The Hulu customer service number is 877 824 4858.** Its [official billing contact page](https://help.hulu.com/contact-us/account-and-billing-billing?target=phone) displays the number when you select **Call Hulu**. Hulu says live support is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

We called on **October 8, 2026**, and reached a person **1 minute 28 seconds** into the recording. We pressed no keys and stayed on the line as the menu invited us to do. We checked Hulu's contact flow again on **November 2**, without signing in. This is a source refresh, not a new call or a claim about your current wait.

## Where Hulu lists its phone number and hours

Open Hulu's [contact page](https://help.hulu.com/contact-us), select **Account & Billing**, then **Billing**, then **Call Hulu**. The number appeared after that final selection in our November 2 check. The same flow offers account management and chat. Choose the topic that matches your own issue.

The [Hulu help article](https://help.hulu.com/article/hulu-how-to-contact-hulu) now opens as **Hulu corporate information**. Its support footer still states that live help is available around the clock. We verified the displayed hours, but our recording tests only one Thursday afternoon call.

The contact flow displayed a zero minute wait during our page check. That changing estimate is separate from our measured 1:28 call and does not guarantee an immediate answer. **855 738 6978** is Hulu's [privacy request line](#hulus-privacy-line-855-738-6978), not the billing support number we tested.

## What our Hulu support call established

**What the representative told us about cancelling:**

- **You can cancel online at hulu.com.** We asked whether it can be done over the phone. She first said she'd "be more than glad to help you with this process", but after we said we had no account, her answer was "you can do it online at hulu.com". She didn't say whether support cancels by phone.
- **If you cancel mid-month, you keep access until the end of your billing period.**
- **No partial refunds** for cancelling mid-month: "No, ma'am."
- **Pausing instead of cancelling** "will depend on the type of subscription that you have." She didn't say for how long.

We called on Thursday, October 8, 2026, at about 1:27 pm Pacific, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. We had no Hulu account and nothing was cancelled or changed. This page is what we heard.

## The Hulu customer service phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 877-824-4858.** "Thanks for calling Hulu. Phone calls may be recorded, including for quality assurance or training purposes." Then: "Para español, presiona siete."
2. **The menu.** "If you have questions about your plan, including bundle options, or need help signing up, press 1. For questions about your bill, including switching plans, managing your add-ons, updating your payment information, or canceling your account, press 2." Press 3 is general questions, log in, devices or home network. Press 4 is playback errors. Press 5 is loading Hulu, the app, My Stuff or Cloud DVR. Press 6 is shows and movies, live TV and premium add-ons. Star replays the options.
3. **Or don't press anything.** "Otherwise, stay on the line and we'll connect you with an agent shortly." That's what we did.
4. **A survey notice.** "Please stay on the line after your call to complete a short survey."
5. **A person answers.** On our call, a Hulu Support representative picked up about 17 seconds after the menu finished. She asked whether we were calling to cancel and whether we had an existing account.

We didn't test the numbered options, so we can't say whether press 2 reaches the same people.

## Hulu's privacy line: 855-738-6978

855-738-6978 is not a general support line. Hulu's [US State Privacy Rights page](https://www.hulu.com/your-us-state-privacy-rights) lists it for privacy requests, such as asking Hulu to access, correct or delete your personal information: "You can also call our toll-free number at 855-738-6978 to speak with one of our representatives." We didn't call it. For billing, cancelling or account help, use 877-824-4858.
