## Where the number comes from

## If your tickets have not arrived

First check the expected arrival date in the order confirmation or in **My Tickets, then Orders**. StubHub's [delivery guide](https://support.stubhub.com/articles/61000276297) says event organizers control when some tickets are released, so support cannot always speed delivery up.

If the expected date has passed, the [event today guide](https://support.stubhub.com/articles/61000276900) says to contact customer care. Tell support the event name, date, start time, order number and what the order currently says. Ask what the next action is and when you should check again.

If the order says a mobile transfer is ready, use its **Get Tickets** link to check where it was sent. StubHub's [transfer instructions](https://support.stubhub.com/articles/61000276384) explain that mobile transfer tickets arrive through an external account, such as Ticketmaster or AXS. Check that account's email address and your spam folder. If a retransfer option is offered, follow its instructions. If the option is missing or the transfer still fails, contact support.

These are the current help pages' instructions. We did not purchase a ticket, accept a transfer or resolve a missing order in this test.

## Buyer support and seller support need different details

| Situation | Have ready | Question to ask |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Buyer waiting for tickets | Order number, event and expected delivery date | Has the promised date passed, and what happens next? |
| Buyer unable to accept a transfer | The destination email shown in the order and the exact error | Which ticketing account should receive it, and is a retransfer needed? |
| Seller unable to deliver tickets | Sale number and the original ticket vendor | Which transfer step is incomplete? |
| Seller waiting for payment | Sale number, event date and payment status | Is a payout detail or proof of transfer still missing? |

StubHub's [seller transfer guide](https://support.stubhub.com/articles/61000276760) explains how to transfer through the original ticket provider, confirm the transfer in StubHub and upload proof. Its [payout guide](https://support.stubhub.com/articles/61000276591) directs sellers to check their payment status and expected payout date.

## Prepare a clear support request

> I need help with my StubHub [purchase or sale]. My event is [event and start time], and the order or sale number is [number]. The account currently shows [status]. I have already tried [steps], and the exact error is [message]. Please explain what I should do next and what you need to check the issue. Ask me before accepting a replacement, refund, credit or other change.

Use this in the support channel available through your account. An assistant helping you prepare the request does not need your password or verification codes. Give the actual event time and order details so support can assess your situation.
