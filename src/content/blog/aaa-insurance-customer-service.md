**The short answer:** there's no single AAA insurance phone number. AAA is a group of regional clubs, and aaa.com doesn't publish a number. Its [club finder](https://www.aaa.com/stop) says: "Enter your ZIP code to be directed to your local Club's website." Your club's site has its own phone numbers.

We called **AAA Mountain West Group** (formerly AAA Northern California, Nevada & Utah), which serves Alaska, Arizona, Northern California, Montana, Nevada, Utah and Wyoming. Its [contact page](https://mwg.aaa.com/contact-us) lists:

| What you need | Number | Hours (as listed) |
|---|---|---|
| Auto & Home Insurance New Policy Quotes & Sales | **877-323-4222** | Monday to Friday 7 am to 9 pm, Saturday and Sunday 8 am to 6 pm (no time zone given) |
| Claims, changes to an existing policy, Member Service Center | **800-922-8228** | "AAA Claims Phone Number Available 24/7" |
| Member services | **800-564-6222** | Monday to Friday 8 am to 10 pm MST |

**If you live somewhere else, your club has different numbers.** Use the ZIP code finder at aaa.com to get to your club's site.

On the quote line, this got us to a person:

1. Call **877-323-4222**. The menu offers English (press 1) or Spanish (press 8).
2. At the main menu, choose "To become a AAA member or obtain an insurance quote, please press 4."
3. **Enter your ZIP code** when asked: "To best direct your call, please enter your zip code."

A **live person** (not a voice assistant) answered "Thank you for calling AAA Insurance" 1 minute 24 seconds into the call. He told us:

- **Anyone can get a quote.** "Anybody can get a quote. That's no problem."
- **You have to be a AAA member to start a policy.** "To start a policy, you do have to be a AAA member, though."
- **A phone quote takes about 10 minutes** for one driver and one car, and "15, 20 minutes" for multiple drivers and vehicles.
- **Have ready:** the name and date of birth of each driver, the address where the vehicle is registered and kept, the coverages you want, and driver history.
- **The claims line is open 24/7.** "They would just call that department." That matches the contact page, which lists 800-922-8228 as "Available 24/7".

We called on Thursday, October 8, 2026, at about 2:17 pm Mountain time, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The AAA insurance phone tree, step by step

This is the AAA Mountain West Group quote line. Other clubs' menus may be different.

1. **Call 877-323-4222.** "Thank you for calling AAA. Let us help you outsmart life. For English, press 1. Para español, oprima 8."
2. **A survey notice plays.** "Should you be selected to receive our satisfaction survey, via a text message from AAA."
3. **The main menu.**
   - "If you know your party's extension, press 1."
   - Smart home security systems and services: press 7.
   - "For roadside assistance, please press 2."
   - "For billing, claims, or changes to your policy, press 3."
   - "To become a AAA member or obtain an insurance quote, please press 4."
   - "To book or make changes to your travel plans, please press 5."
4. **"To best direct your call, please enter your zip code."** We entered 89501, a Reno, Nevada ZIP code in the club's area, only to get routed.
5. **The survey notice plays again,** then the line rings once.
6. **A person answers.** "Thank you for calling AAA Insurance. My name is [name removed]. How can I help you?" That was about 25 seconds after the ZIP code prompt, with no hold message.

Our call log doesn't record key presses, so we can't show which keys our caller pressed. The menu went from the main menu to the ZIP code prompt, and the person who answered said "AAA Insurance", which fits option 4.

## What to have ready

From the representative:

- **The name and date of birth of each driver.**
- **The address where the vehicle is registered and kept.**
- **The coverages you want.**
- **Driver history.**
- **Your ZIP code,** for the phone menu.
- **About 10 minutes** for one driver and one car, or 15 to 20 minutes for more.

You can get a quote without being a member. To start the policy, you'll need to be a AAA member.
