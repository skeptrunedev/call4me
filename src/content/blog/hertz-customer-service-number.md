**The short answer:** the Hertz customer service number for a rental you already had is **1-800-654-4173**. A virtual assistant answers: "Hi, this is Haley, your virtual assistant." It's not a person. Say you don't have a confirmation number and ask for a representative. It will try to help you itself first, so keep asking. On our call it said "Let me connect you now" after our sixth request (2 minutes 44 seconds in), and **a live Hertz representative answered at 3:07**.

Don't go quiet once it says it's connecting you. On another call to the same number, Haley said "Let me connect you with a live agent", then nothing happened for 12 seconds, then it said "I haven't heard from you in a bit." Our caller stayed silent, and Haley ended the call at 2:43. If it comes back and talks, answer it.

Hertz has different numbers for different jobs. Its help center page ["Phone Numbers - U.S."](https://hertz.my.site.com/care/htz_faqsearchwebform?searchTerm=Phone+Numbers+U.S.&lang=en_US&pos=us) lists:

- **A rental you already had: 1-800-654-4173.** For customer service on past rentals, "please call 1-800-654-4173 (They are open from 6 am - 7 pm Central time, Monday - Friday)".
- **Reservations: 1-800-654-3131.** "Book Leisure/Business Reservations 1-800-654-3131 (US, Puerto Rico and Canada)". International reservations: **1-800-654-3001**.
- **Extending a rental: 1-800-654-4174,** open 24/7.
- **Roadside assistance: 800-654-5060.**

What the live representative told us about charges on a finished rental:

- **Have your final invoice ready.** It's "a PDF that shows all the information and the details for the charges", including the rental days, additional services, taxes and fees.
- **Deposits:** "in the following three to five business days, normally the deposit is released back" after the rental is closed. He added that it "depends to the financial institution" and can take "a couple of days or a couple of weeks". Hertz's virtual assistant on the reservations line told us something different: "7 to 10 business days" ([that call is below](#the-reservations-line-call)).
- **Damage or fuel charges can be disputed by phone,** with "the billing department".

### The past rentals phone tree, step by step

There was no keypad menu on either call to this number, and no keys were pressed.

1. **Call 1-800-654-4173.** "Thank you for calling Hertz. Your call may be recorded or monitored for quality assurance."
2. **About 6 seconds of silence.** Then: "Hi, this is Haley, your virtual assistant. To get you to the right place, could I please get your confirmation number?"
3. **Say you don't have it and ask for a representative.** It answered: "I can actually help with a lot right here. Looking up or changing a reservation, canceling, getting a receipt, explaining a charge, or roadside assistance. What's your question about?"
4. **Keep asking.** It asked for a confirmation or rental record number, then offered to look the rental up by "the phone number on the account", then asked what the charges were about, then gave its own advice. Our caller said it didn't have the numbers and asked for a person each time.
5. **"Let me connect you now."** On this call it came after our sixth request. Then silence, then a faint sound, and a person answered about 19 seconds later. Haley didn't speak again in between.
6. **If Haley speaks again instead, answer it.** On our other call, Haley promised a live agent, came back 12 seconds later with "I haven't heard from you in a bit", and hung up when our caller didn't reply ([that call is below](#the-call-where-haley-hung-up)).

The representative's first question was "May I know your rental record number or your reservation ID number, please?" Our caller said it didn't have one and had general questions, and he answered them anyway.

Two answers, two numbers: the representative said 3 to 5 business days for a deposit, and the virtual assistant on the reservations line said 7 to 10 business days. Both said it depends on your bank. ## What to have ready

For a past rental (1-800-654-4173):

- **Your final invoice,** the PDF with the charges, taxes and fees. That's what the representative asked customers to have.
- **Your rental record number or reservation ID.** Both Haley and the representative asked for one first. We didn't have one and still got through, but it's what they'll use to look up your charges.
- **The rental dates and pickup location,** which Haley listed, and the phone number on the reservation, which it offered to look you up by.

For a reservation (1-800-654-3131):

- **Your confirmation number,** if you're calling about a booking. It was the assistant's first question. Without one, saying you have general questions worked.
- **Which card you'll pay with,** credit or debit. It changes the hold and which cars you can rent.
- **Your rate type,** Pay Later or prepaid, if you're asking about cancellation fees.
- **The state you're renting in and your age,** if you're under 25.
- **Your AAA membership,** if you're 20 to 24.
