**The Chase credit card customer service number is 1 800 432 3117.** Chase lists it as Card Customer Service on its [official customer service page](https://www.chase.com/digital/customer-service). We called that number and reached a live specialist **2 minutes 14 seconds** into the recording.

This is the **credit card** line. Chase lists **1 800 935 9935** separately for personal banking, including checking, savings and debit card questions. Our recording does not test that banking line.

We called on **October 24, 2026**, at **6:27 pm Eastern**, using [call4me](/). We explained that we had no Chase account and wanted general information about credit card disputes. No card number, Social Security number or customer account was supplied. We checked Chase's official contact page again on **November 2**, but did not make another call for this guide.

## How we reached a person at Chase Card Services

This is the route that worked for our general inquiry without an account:

1. **Call 1 800 432 3117.** After a recording notice, the greeting identified Chase Card Services.
2. **Listen to the account prompt.** It first asked for a full card number. It also offered the pound key for a menu covering lost or stolen cards, replacements, disputes and application status.
3. **Use the option that matches your situation.** Because we had no card number, our route continued to the option for callers without an account number, **press 1**.
4. **The next prompt asked for a Social Security number.** Our caller said, "I don't have that. Agent."
5. **Wait for the specialist option.** The menu then said to press or say **0** to speak with a customer service specialist. Our caller said "0", and the system announced the connection.

The greeting from a person came at **2:14**. We asked general questions and received general answers. This does not show that Chase will access or change a card account without verifying the cardmember, or that saying "agent" always produces the same route.

If you are calling about your own account, follow the current prompts and have the cardmember available to verify directly. Do not use our lack of an account as a reason to misstate your situation.

## What are Chase credit card customer service hours?

When we asked about this line's hours during the October 24 call, the specialist said it was open **24 hours a day, seven days a week**. We reached a person on a Saturday evening.

That is the representative's answer for the line we called. The official contact page we checked on November 2 lists the number but does not state its hours. Our one call does not independently verify overnight or holiday coverage, or the hours of another Chase department.

## What should you have ready?

For your own credit card issue, have your card and a clear description of the question ready. The automated system may ask the cardmember to verify details before discussing the account. If an assistant handles the menu and hold, join when Chase needs you and supply sensitive details directly.

For a charge question, have the merchant, transaction date, amount and explanation ready. The specialist said the amount and reason were needed for a dispute. The [dispute guide](/blog/chase-dispute-charge) covers the rest of that conversation and links to Chase's current instructions.

Our call established access to a person who answered general questions. We did not open a dispute, obtain a credit, access an account or confirm a resolution.
