**The short answer:** you can cancel a Planet Fitness membership **online at planetfitness.com** or **in person at your home club**. The clubs we called did not offer cancelling over the phone. But the terms depend on the club, because each club is a franchise: on the same evening, one San Francisco club told us **Classic has a 12 month commitment and a $58 fee** to leave early, while the other described no commitment at all. Before you cancel, find out which one your club is.

We called both clubs on Wednesday, September 30, 2026, around 4 to 4:30 pm Pacific, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. Here's what they said.

| | Lakeshore, 1533 Sloat Blvd | Kearny St, 141 Kearny St |
|---|---|---|

| Early cancel fee | none mentioned | Classic: $58 if you cancel before 12 months. Black Card: "cancel it anytime" |

Both clubs' pages on planetfitness.com say **"Cancel Online, Anytime!"**

## The phone tree at Lakeshore, step by step

1. **Call the club** (Lakeshore: 628-303-3332, from its page on planetfitness.com).
2. **"If you are currently a member, press 1."** Para español, 8. To learn about Planet Fitness, 2.
3. **The member menu** starts by telling you to cancel at planetfitness.com. Then: 1 for help signing in, 2 for billing questions, 3 for hours and closures, 4 for corporate memberships, **5 for any other inquiries**, 8 to repeat.
4. **Press 5.** A staff member at the club picked up about 6 seconds later.

## How to cancel, step by step

1. **Find your home club.** It's the club where you signed up, and it's the one that holds your membership. Another club can't see it: "we don't have access to their membership."
2. **Cancel online** at planetfitness.com, signed in to your account. Or **go to your home club in person** and sign. Both clubs' pages say "Cancel Online, Anytime!"
3. **Check your commitment first,** if you're on Classic. At the Kearny St club, leaving before 12 months costs $58. Black Card there has no commitment.
4. **Watch the annual fee date.** At Lakeshore it's billed "on or shortly after November 1st", and the staff member said there's no way to avoid it once that date passes. If you're going to cancel anyway, do it before then.
5. **Keep using the gym** until the end of the billing period you've already paid for.
