**The short answer:** you can cancel an Audible membership **online**, in your membership settings at audible.com, or **by phone at 888-283-5051** (1-888-283-5051, the number in the title of Audible's [contact page](https://www.audible.com/contactus)). By phone, Audible verifies you by **sending an approval link to the phone number or email on your account**: you click approve, then ask them to cancel. The associate told us the line is open **24/7**.

Before you cancel, know these, all from the associate on our call:

- **Your cancellation takes effect on the last day of your current billing cycle,** with no further charges. The account closes that day.
- **Unused credits expire when the cancellation takes effect.** They "won't be able to be used again", so spend them first.
- **Books you bought with credits stay yours** after you cancel (Premium Plus).
- **Standard plan titles lock.** They're a monthly selection, not a purchase, so you can't listen to them again until you have an active membership.
- **You can pause instead, for 90 days.** After a pause there's a 12 month wait before the next one: pause in October, and the next pause is October of next year.
- **Signed up through Apple or Google?** Then you have to cancel with Apple or Google subscriptions, not Audible. If you signed up through Amazon, Audible handles it.
- **Ask about cheaper plans.** "We do have plans that are discounted", and you don't have to be cancelling to ask.

## The Audible customer service phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 888-283-5051.** "Hi, you've reached Audible. We look forward to assisting you. Stay on the line while we find someone who can help." Then a recording notice.
2. **"To connect to an Audible customer service associate, please press 2 7 0."** This is a keypad check, not a menu. Listen for the digits and type them. Our caller pressed 270.
3. **The main menu.** 1 if you're visually impaired (an accessibility specialist), **2 "to pause or cancel your Audible membership"**, 3 for your listening experience, 4 for technical difficulties, 5 for all other questions. Our caller pressed 2.
4. **A second keypad check: "please press 5 5 0."** The digits were different from the first one. Our caller didn't answer this in time (see below), heard a warning, and pressed 550 on the repeat.
5. **"Your call is in queue."** An associate picked up about 5 seconds later, 2 minutes after we dialed.
6. **Ask your question.** The associate said anything that touches your account needs verification first, but general questions don't.

## What to have ready

- **Your phone or your email inbox, whichever is on the account.** Audible sends a verification link "to verify the account". You "just need to click approve", then ask to cancel.
- **Where you signed up.** If it was through Apple or Google subscriptions, call Apple or Google instead.
- **Your credits, spent.** Unused credits expire when the cancellation takes effect.
- **Your plan name** (Premium Plus or Standard), since what happens to your books depends on it.

## Things that don't work

On October 2, 2026:

- **Waiting at a keypad check.** After the first "press 5 5 0" went unanswered for about 10 seconds, Audible said "Sorry, we have received no response. This call will be disconnected after the next attempt without response." Type the digits as soon as you hear them.
- **Typing the same code twice.** The two checks used different digits (270, then 550). Listen each time.

We didn't try saying "representative" or pressing 5 for all other questions, and we didn't cancel anything: we had no account to cancel. So we can't tell you whether Audible offers you a deal at the moment you cancel. The associate's answer to "are there any offers" was that discounted and cheaper plans exist and you can ask about them anytime.
