**The short answer:** the MetLife Pet Insurance phone number is **(855) 270-7387**. MetLife Pet's [customer support page](https://www.metlifepetinsurance.com/customer-support/) lists it under "Call Us", open **Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. EST** and **Saturday and Sunday 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. EST**. For a mailing address change, the same page says to "call (866) 937-7387 or (855) 270-7387 and speak with a customer service representative." This call used (855) 270-7387.

This got a call4me customer's assistant to a person:

1. Listen to the menu. **Press 4**, "another question about an existing MetLife Pet Insurance policy". Our caller said "four" at the prompt.
2. Wait through an app message and a "Ruh-roh! It looks like all of our team members are busy" notice.
3. **A MetLife Pet Insurance customer service representative answered 2 minutes 2 seconds into the call.**

What the call established, on Tuesday, October 13, 2026:

- **Customer service changed the account email by phone.** The customer had lost access to the old address. The representative found the policy by name, old email and home address, then said "I did update the email."
- **There was no password reset link.** To sign in, the representative said to open the MetLife Pet website or app and choose **New User Register** with the new email: the existing policy "will be connected to the new email address."
- **A new ZIP code needs a licensed sales agent.** "Premiums are based on the zip code in which you live," so customer service could only change an address within the same ZIP code. She transferred the call.
- **The licensed agent moved the policy and quoted the new premium:** **$144.56 a month** for the same plan ($5,000 annual limit, $250 deductible, 80% reimbursement, injury and illness) at the new ZIP code, starting the next day.
- **The revision resets the deductible and annual limit,** and the old premium is refunded pro rata to the original payment method "within 10 business days."

## What the call did not do or confirm

- **Nobody signed in.** The assistant didn't register the new email on the website or app during the call, so the recording doesn't show that the existing policy actually appears after registering.
- **No password reset link was sent.** The representative said that isn't how they do it.
- **The new address wasn't read back.** The agent asked for the new ZIP code and street, recapped the plan and premium and said the revision would start the next day, but didn't repeat the full address.
- **The old premium wasn't stated.** The call doesn't show whether $144.56 is more or less than before. Before the transfer, the assistant told customer service it could accept any premium change.
- **The refund amount and timing weren't confirmed.** "On the fourth" may refer to the old payment date; it wasn't explained.
- **Email delivery wasn't checked.** The agent said documents were emailed; the recording can't show they arrived.
- **Only option 4 was tried,** on one weekday. Hold times on other days and branches may differ.

## Before you call MetLife Pet Insurance

- **Have the policy number if you can.** It was the first thing customer service asked for. Without it, the lookup used the name, the old email and the home address on the policy.
- **Know the name exactly as it appears on the policy,** and spell it.
- **If you're moving, know whether the ZIP code changes.** Same ZIP code: customer service said they can update it. Different ZIP code: expect a transfer to a licensed agent, a new premium, and a reset deductible and annual limit.
- **For the licensed agent, have the phone number on file,** plus the name, address and email on file.
- **Spell email addresses letter by letter.** The representative misheard one letter of the new address, and it took three passes to get it right.
- **Decide on the premium first.** The agent quotes the new price and asks you to go ahead on the same call, after reading a fraud warning and asking you to certify the information is true.

The support page says you can change your email, name or phone number yourself in your MyPets account under "Account", then "Edit Account Info". That only helps if you can still sign in. This customer couldn't, which is why the call was needed.
