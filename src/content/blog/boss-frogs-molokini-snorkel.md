**Boss Frog's Maalaea Harbor store is at 300 Ma'alaea Road, Building 2, Shop 2N, Wailuku, Hawaii.** Its [official store page](https://bossfrog.com/store-locations/maalaea-harbor-snorkel-rentals/) lists **(808) 242-0088** for the shop and store hours of 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM daily. The same page gives 1-888-700-3764 for general questions and (808) 661-3333 once you are in Hawaii. The calls below dialed (808) 242-0088, the number on that page.

On Saturday, October 10, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called Boss Frog's Maui to find out whether two adults could still snorkel Molokini that day. Staff answered right away on both Boss Frog's calls; there was no phone menu.

**The short answer from the call:** the Malolo afternoon snorkel trip out of Maalaea Harbor was running, with check-in at **12:30**, but it was **booked solid** for adults. Whether it would go to **Molokini or Coral Gardens** was not fixed in advance: staff said it depends on the weather, and the captain decides when the boat leaves the harbor. Staff offered **10 spots the next day** and room on Monday instead.

The availability call took 1 minute 14 seconds, and staff answered the first question after a lookup pause of about nine seconds. Prices and availability here are historical statements from that morning. They are not current seat counts.

## What these calls did not settle

- **No booking was made.** The customer's brief said information only, and the assistant did not reserve the Sunday or Monday spots.
- **No price was quoted** for the Malolo trip, because no adult seats were left.
- **The destination was not confirmed** for any day. Molokini versus Coral Gardens is the captain's weather call at departure.
- **The conditions call did not name a calm spot.** Staff said "everywhere should be calm today" right after rating the south side a three, and the call ended at its two minute limit before that was explained. It also does not establish what the one to ten scale measures.
- **The caller stumbled on the conditions call.** After about 12 seconds of silence on the line, the assistant reintroduced itself and repeated the question staff had already answered. Staff asked whether they were talking to an AI, and the assistant said yes.

## Calling Boss Frog's about a same-day Molokini snorkel

The useful questions were short: is the trip running today, what time is check-in, are there seats for your party, and where is it going. Staff answered all of them in about a minute. A few things made the answers more useful:

- **Name the trip and the harbor.** "The Malolo afternoon snorkel trip out of Maalaea Harbor" left no doubt about which boat.
- **Ask about Molokini versus a backup site.** Staff said the captain chooses Molokini or Coral Gardens by weather when leaving the harbor, so nobody can promise Molokini ahead of time.
- **Decide in advance whether another day works.** Staff offered Sunday and Monday. This brief said information only, so the assistant took the answer and hung up. If you would take the next day, say so, and say whether the assistant may book.
- **Give the call enough time.** Two of these three calls hit a two minute cap mid-answer. A three to five minute limit fits a quick availability and conditions check.
