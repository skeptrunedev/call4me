**To cancel HelloFresh, sign in on the website, open Account Settings, then Plan Settings, and choose Cancel Plan under Status.** Finish the prompts and save the confirmation. HelloFresh's [US cancellation guide](https://www.hellofresh.com/about/how-to-cancel-hellofresh-subscription) says to do this by **11:59 pm PST, five days before your next scheduled delivery**. A box already being processed can still be charged and delivered.

We called **(646) 846 3663** on Saturday, October 3, 2026, with [call4me](/) asking a human about the deadline, refunds, confirmation and a missing cancel button. This was a general inquiry for a guide. We had no subscription to cancel, and no account was changed.

## How to cancel a HelloFresh subscription online

The [official website instructions](https://www.hellofresh.com/about/how-to-cancel-hellofresh-subscription) are:

1. Log into your HelloFresh account.
2. Select your name at the top right, then **Account Settings**.
3. In **Plan Settings**, find **Status** and choose **Cancel Plan**.
4. Complete the prompts until you see the cancellation confirmation.

The representative also told us to log in and cancel the subscription. We did not test a signed in account, so the menu labels above are sourced from HelloFresh's guide rather than an account walkthrough.

## When should you cancel to avoid the next HelloFresh box?

The human emphasized cancelling **at least five days before delivery**. Their example was an October 8 delivery that needed cancellation on October 3.

We asked for the timezone, but the representative did not answer that part. The **11:59 pm PST** time above comes from the official guide. Check the deadline displayed for your next order, especially if you are close to the cutoff, and ask support to confirm that particular box's status.

## What happens if you cancel after payment or after a box is processed?

The representative said the **final box would still be delivered** if it had already been generated and the cutoff had passed. Our saved live call transcript captured a statement that they could not issue a credit or refund, but the key word in that answer is unclear in the recording. HelloFresh’s published guide says orders processed before cancellation remain chargeable. Ask support to review your actual order before relying on a refund. The representative also reminded us that the plan is a weekly subscription, so cancelling before a later delivery still matters.

That answer concerned a generated box after its cutoff. It does not establish the outcome for every payment, damaged delivery or refund request. We did not provide an order for review, and no refund was requested or issued.

If you have already paid, ask support to check the actual order before assuming cancellation stops it. Get separate answers to **“Is my subscription cancelled?”** and **“Will this next box still be charged or delivered?”**

## How to cancel HelloFresh on the app or your phone

HelloFresh's [app page](https://www.hellofresh.com/about/app) advertises skipping weeks and cancelling, but it does not give a specific sequence of app menu labels. We did not test the native app, and our call did not establish that sequence.

If the app does not show a cancel option, use the website route above in a browser. On our call, we asked what to do when the option is missing on the website or app. The representative said to **call customer care**, which can look up the account using **the shipping address, email address and account holder's name**.

Deleting the app does not complete the account cancellation described by support. Finish the cancellation process and look for confirmation.

## HelloFresh customer service phone number and the human route

HelloFresh publishes **(646) 846 3663** for Customer Care on its [accessibility page](https://www.hellofresh.com/about/accessibility). We reached a person at that number on Saturday morning Pacific time. That establishes availability when we tested it; our call did not establish a complete current support schedule.

The menu on our call asked for:

1. A phone number, starting with the area code, followed by the pound key.
2. Whether the caller was the account holder: **1 for yes, 2 for no**.
3. A topic: **1 for delivery, 2 for ingredients, 3 for technical support, 4 to cancel the account, 5 for other inquiries**.

We followed the cancellation route and heard a transfer announcement before a representative answered. These are the menu choices we heard, not a claim that every future call will use the same menu. Answer account questions truthfully. The representative answered our general questions without locating an account; an actual account request needs verification.

## How do you confirm HelloFresh cancellation?

The representative said a **confirmation email should arrive within 24 hours**. Save that email and the cancellation date.

If you do not receive it, contact support and ask them to confirm the subscription status and the status of the next order separately. Our inquiry did not generate a confirmation email because we did not cancel an account.

## Is skipping a week the same as cancelling HelloFresh?

HelloFresh presents skipping selected deliveries and cancelling the plan as separate actions. Its [guide](https://www.hellofresh.com/about/how-to-cancel-hellofresh-subscription) says to use **My Menu**, select a delivery week and choose **Skip Week** for a temporary break. Use **Cancel Plan** when you want the subscription cancelled.

We did not ask the representative to demonstrate skipping or cancel a real plan. Check each scheduled delivery when using a temporary skip.

## Common HelloFresh cancellation questions

### Can I cancel HelloFresh after the first box?

Ask support to check the next order's deadline and status. The representative described cancellation based on the delivery schedule, not on how many boxes you have received. Any promotional terms belong to your particular offer and were not reviewed on our call.

### Why did I receive a box after cancelling?

The representative said an already generated box can still become the final delivery when the cancellation cutoff has passed. Ask support to identify when the order was processed and when the subscription cancellation took effect.

### What if the HelloFresh cancel button is missing?

Try the website route. The representative said customer care can help locate the account using the account holder's name, email and shipping address. Call with that information ready.

### Can I get a refund just because I cancelled?

HelloFresh’s published guide says orders processed before cancellation remain chargeable. Our saved live call transcript captured a refusal of a credit or refund after the cutoff, but the key word is unclear in the recording and the representative did not review an actual order. Ask about your specific charge rather than assuming plan cancellation refunds it.

### Does cancelling HelloFresh delete my personal data?

We asked about stopping the subscription and deliveries. Our call did not establish a data deletion process, so this guide does not treat plan cancellation as proof that personal data has been erased.
