**Palmeri Ristorante is at 11650 San Vicente Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90049, in Brentwood.** Its [official About page](http://www.palmeriristorantespa.com/about/) lists that address and **310.442.8446**, the number our customer's assistant dialed. The [reservations page](http://www.palmeriristorantespa.com/reservations/) says "Reservations are made by phone only", and the [private events page](http://www.palmeriristorantespa.com/privateEvents/) says the restaurant offers "seating for over 80 guests as well as a private room that seats 12." The site calls the restaurant Palmeri S.p.A. Ristorante. It doesn't use the word Brentwood, but 11650 San Vicente is in Brentwood.

On October 12 and 13, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called Palmeri twice about a private dinner for 8 at one table on Thursday, November 5, 2026, from 6:00 to 9:00 PM. It was a quote request only. The assistant was told not to hold, book or agree to any charge.

**The second call, in Italian with the staff member who handles private dining, quoted the wine room at $135 per person for a set menu, plus a $250 room fee, about 20% gratuity and 10% tax.** The first call, a day earlier in English, had said $100 per person plus tax and gratuity. Get the final number in writing.

Before you compare totals, note the two price points. The $100 figure came from someone else at the restaurant who was standing in for the private dining contact. The $135 figure came from the private dining contact herself and came with a menu list. Neither call shows that the $100 menu and the $135 menu are the same thing.

## What these calls did not settle

- **Nothing was booked or held.** On the first call the host took the customer's name, phone number, party size and time, and said he could "take the information". The recording doesn't show whether that went in as a reservation or a note. On the second call the assistant said it was asking only for a quote, with no reservation yet.
- **No written quote is shown.** The first call ended while the assistant was asking for the package to be emailed. On the second call the assistant got the contact's email but didn't ask her to send the package. Nothing on these recordings shows a proposal arriving.
- **Wine and alcohol weren't priced.** The $135 menu named soft drinks and coffee only.
- **Gratuity and the room fee were hedged.** Staff said "usually" 20% and that they "usually" take $250 for the room, "depending". Tax was stated as 10%.
- **The 6 to 9 PM time wasn't repeated on the second call.** The first call covered it.
- **Room size wasn't asked.** Staff called it the wine room. The website lists one private room that seats 12, which fits 8, but the call didn't confirm they are the same room.
- **The cancellation term is as spoken.** The $250 charge for canceling inside 48 hours was explained in passing, not read from a contract.

For what to ask about every charge in a private dinner, see our [private dining room cost guide](/blog/private-dining-room-cost).

## Why the second call was worth making

The first call got availability and a ballpark, but the person who handles private dining wasn't in, and the call was cut off before the package email request finished. The customer's brief for the second call listed exactly what was still missing: the room fee or minimum, the gratuity percentage, deposit terms and an email address spelled back letter by letter. The assistant asked each in turn and got an answer to each.

Two things to copy if you book here. Ask for the person who handles private dining, and call in the evening: the assistant had been told to call back after 5 PM, and on October 12 staff said she wasn't working that day but would be in the next day. And ask whether the per person price includes drinks, because the $135 menu named only soft drinks and coffee.
