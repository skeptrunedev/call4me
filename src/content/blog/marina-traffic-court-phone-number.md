## What to have ready before you call Marina traffic court

- **Your case or citation number.** It was the clerk's first question, before any greeting or name.
- **The name the case is under.** She asked for it next and used it to confirm the right case.
- **The amounts on your paperwork, and which count each one belongs to.** The clerk answered by count ("count one", "count two"), and what the family had been told didn't match what she saw.
- **Whether you're doing a trial by written declaration.** That decided which amount she treated as due.
- **A way to pay online or a checkbook.** The phone line can't take a card.

Calling between 8 am and 4 pm on a weekday is the only window the court lists. Expect a hold: this one was close to 7 minutes on a Friday morning. The menu's online 60 day extension notice is worth hearing if you only need more time and your citation doesn't require a court appearance.
