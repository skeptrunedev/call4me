## Ask your AI agent to find a sauna you can actually visit

Connect [call4me](/mcp) to your assistant using the [Claude Code setup guide](/blog/claude-code-phone-calls) or [Codex setup guide](/blog/codex-phone-calls). Then give it your location, arrival window and the exact amenity you want:

> Find gyms with a sauna within [travel time or distance] of [ZIP code]. I want to visit on [date] between [times]. I specifically want [dry sauna, steam room or either], and [a hot tub is required, optional or unnecessary]. I am [a nonmember, a member of this chain or a member's guest]. Read official branch pages first, then use call4me to call up to [number] locations about missing details. Confirm which rooms exist, whether they are operating that day, their hours and last entry, and whether my admission includes them. Ask about total entry price, age and guest restrictions, required ID, reservations, towels, lockers and any room access rules. Return a comparison with dated sources and unresolved questions. Do not purchase a pass or start a membership.

If a call goes unanswered, keep availability marked as unconfirmed rather than borrowing another branch's answer.

## Finding gyms with hot tubs near you

If you want a hot tub as well as a sauna, make it a separate requirement. Search official pages for both hot tub and whirlpool, then ask the branch what that label means at its location.

Life Time Reston's published schedule distinguishes an indoor whirlpool from an outdoor whirlpool. On our check, it listed the outdoor whirlpool as closed every day while publishing operating hours for the indoor one. An outdoor photo alone would have been a poor basis for a visit. This is a website observation, not a staff confirmation of same day availability.

Keep separate columns for sauna, steam room and hot tub. A yes in one column should not fill the others. Ask whether amenities are in shared areas or particular locker rooms if your group wants to use them together.

## Can you use the sauna without a membership?

The Reston employee confirmed the **$30 day pass** and included amenities. Asked about residency or first visit requirements, they described a simple arrival and locker orientation. However, the YMCA's [published guest policy](https://www.ymcadc.org/membership/get-a-guest-pass/) requires photo ID and a waiver, and its day pass section limits local guests to three visits per calendar year. Separate trial wording on that page gives different limits. The phone answer does not establish an exemption from those written rules.

Have your assistant clarify the terms for your situation. For passes and pool access, use our [gym day pass guide](/blog/gym-day-pass).

## Confirm the sauna type and the final arrival details

When a website only says sauna, ask whether staff mean a dry sauna, infrared sauna or steam room. Preserve their answer instead of treating these labels as interchangeable. Our YMCA call established dry sauna and steam room access; it did not establish a hot tub.

Before leaving, ask for the branch address, confirmed amenity, arrival deadline, total price, what to bring and unresolved questions. For a later visit, check again for closure notices closer to departure.
