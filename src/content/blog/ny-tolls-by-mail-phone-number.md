**The short answer:** the Tolls by Mail NY phone number is **1-844-826-8400**. The MTA's [how to pay tolls page](https://www.mta.info/fares-tolls/tolls/how-to-pay-tolls) lists it as "For toll bills: Tolls by Mail, 844-826-8400". The same page also gives **800-333-8655** "For help with both E-ZPass and Tolls by Mail", and **718-313-9414** "For notices of violation". tollsbymailny.com now redirects to e-zpassny.com, which blocked our automated check, so the MTA page is the official listing we could read.

**You don't need a bill number.** On October 8, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called 1-844-826-8400 with only a California license plate, the registered owner's name and their address. This got it to a person:

1. **Press 1** at "If you are calling regarding a Tolls by Mail bill, to pay a violation, or to make a one-time payment on your E-ZPass account, press one."
2. **Press 1** at "For Tolls by Mail, press one."
3. **Press 1** at "To convert now or talk to an agent regarding Tolls by Mail, press one."

Key presses aren't audible in the recording, but the menu or transfer that followed each step matches option 1. **A representative at the E-ZPass New York Service Center answered 2 minutes 37 seconds into the call.** There was no hold queue, only a transfer message.

What the representative did with just a plate:

- **She found unpaid bills.** "I did pull up some toll bills and violation."
- **Her first offer was to email the details.** "I can put a case in to have the information emailed out to you." When asked, she read them out instead.

## The E-ZPass NY customer service phone tree, step by step

This is the menu as it played on October 8, 2026, calling 1-844-826-8400. If you want the E-ZPass NY customer service number for a live person about a toll bill, this is the route that worked.

2. **A notice for Orange County residents** about a Governor Mario M. Cuomo Bridge plan, then a list of what to have ready: "Your account number, E-ZPass tag number, case number, invoice number, violation number, or license plate number."
3. **It points you to chat and the app.** e-zpassny.com "to speak with a live agent via chat", or the Tolls NY app.

- **Hours weren't asked.** The MTA page doesn't list them for 1-844-826-8400.
- **A clean result is only for that day and those agencies.** Tolls can post weeks after travel, and other states' toll roads weren't checked.
