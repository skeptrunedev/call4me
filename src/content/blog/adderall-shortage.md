**The short answer:** the Adderall shortage is still real where we checked. On the morning of October 1, 2026, a call4me user's AI phone assistant called 10 pharmacies in Miami-Dade and Broward counties about **Adderall 30 mg**, immediate release (IR) or extended release (XR), brand or generic, for a 30-day supply. **Five pharmacies answered the question, and all five said they didn't have it.** One independent pharmacy said **every Adderall product is on back order from its wholesaler**, that it is "cut off until further notice," and that it is "estimating about a year." At the other five pharmacies, the call ended before anyone answered the question.

What the five answers had in common:

1. **A flat no.** Nobody offered a partial quantity or a different strength. (Our caller didn't ask about other strengths. More on that below.)
2. **No restock date.** Nobody had one. The one pharmacy that guessed said its wholesaler gives it no date.
3. **No referral.** Of the four pharmacies we asked, none knew of another pharmacy nearby that had it. One pharmacist said, "I'm pretty sure every other place is in the same boat."
4. **One real lead:** the same pharmacist suggested calling the **mail-order pharmacy or member services number on your insurance plan**, because "mail order facility rules are different from retail pharmacies."

These calls were made on Thursday, October 1, 2026, between 9:36 and 10:32 am Eastern, by a call4me user who has a prescription and was checking stock. Our caller only asked about stock: it didn't fill, transfer or reserve anything, and it didn't give a date of birth, address or insurance details. The user is okay with us sharing the calls. We've left the pharmacies unnamed. Each pharmacy's name and address, the user's name and the staff names are muted in the audio and shown in brackets in the transcripts. Stretches of dead air longer than about 3 seconds are shortened to about 1 second, and each cut is marked in the transcript. Stock is what each pharmacy said on that date, and it can change any day.

## Is there still an Adderall shortage in 2026?

As of October 1, 2026, yes, by the FDA's own list. The [FDA Drug Shortages database](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/drugshortages/default.cfm) lists the mixed amphetamine salts tablet, the ingredients in Adderall and its generics, as **"Currently in Shortage,"** first posted on October 12, 2022. Its [detail page](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/drugshortages/dsp_ActiveIngredientDetails.cfm?AI=Amphetamine%20Aspartate%20Monohydrate,%20Amphetamine%20Sulfate,%20Dextroamphetamine%20Saccharate,%20Dextroamphetamine%20Sulfate%20Tablet&st=c&tab=tabs-1) lists each manufacturer, by strength, as available, limited or unavailable, and most makers were reverified in September 2026. The American Society of Health-System Pharmacists keeps its own [Adderall shortage entry](https://www.ashp.org/drug-shortages/current-shortages/Drug-Shortage-Detail.aspx?id=857), written for pharmacists.

Those lists tell you what manufacturers report. They don't tell you what's on the shelf at the pharmacy down the street. That's what these calls are.

## When will the Adderall shortage end?

Nobody we called knew. Three pharmacies were asked directly when they'd get more, and all three said they didn't know. Pharmacy 4 called it a back order. Pharmacy 9 said its wholesalers "do not provide us that information," and that its own guess was "about a year."

The FDA doesn't publish a single end date either. Its [detail page](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/drugshortages/dsp_ActiveIngredientDetails.cfm?AI=Amphetamine%20Aspartate%20Monohydrate,%20Amphetamine%20Sulfate,%20Dextroamphetamine%20Saccharate,%20Dextroamphetamine%20Sulfate%20Tablet&st=c&tab=tabs-1) shows availability one manufacturer and one strength at a time. If you want to know whether things are improving, that page and the [ASHP entry](https://www.ashp.org/drug-shortages/current-shortages/Drug-Shortage-Detail.aspx?id=857) are the places to check, not a pharmacy's guess.

## What to do if your pharmacy is out of Adderall

1. **Call before you drive.** A stock check took under a minute at every pharmacy that answered. Ask for the exact strength and form on your prescription, and say "IR or XR" out loud: they're different products.
2. **Ask about the quantity, not just the drug.** "Do you have enough for a 30-day supply, quantity 60?" A pharmacy can have some and still not have enough to fill it.
3. **Ask when they expect more, and whether they know a pharmacy that has it.** Nobody had an answer for us, but it costs one sentence.
4. **Call your insurance plan's mail-order pharmacy or member services.** That was Pharmacy 9's suggestion: "Mail order facility rules are different from retail pharmacies." The number is usually on your insurance card.
5. **Talk to your prescriber.** If the strength or form on your prescription isn't anywhere you call, your prescriber is the one who decides whether a different strength, a different form, or a different medication makes sense for you, and can send a new prescription to a pharmacy that has it in stock. Don't change how you take it on your own.
6. **Check the [FDA shortage list](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/drugshortages/default.cfm)** to see which manufacturers report the strength as available, and mention it to your pharmacist or prescriber.
7. **Expect to be asked who you are.** Pharmacy 9's automated assistant wouldn't check stock for a phone number it didn't recognize, and Pharmacy 3's phone menu asked for a date of birth (pressing nothing still got us to a person). Some pharmacies hung up before the question was finished.

## Where our caller fell short

- **It never asked about other strengths or forms.** Every pharmacy was asked about 30 mg only. "Do you have any other strength of Adderall in stock?" would have given the user something to take to their prescriber.
- **At Pharmacy 2,** it didn't say IR or XR, and after the "No" it said goodbye without asking about a restock date or another pharmacy, even though its instructions said to.
- **At Pharmacy 10,** a person answered after about a minute of menu and hold, our caller asked the question, and then our side ended the call mid-sentence. The call records show the call4me side hung up, not the pharmacy. We're looking into why. We don't count Pharmacy 10 as out of stock.
- **Its opener was long.** "This is [name]'s assistant. I'm just checking whether Adderall 30 milligram IR tablets or XR capsules, brand or generic..." Three pharmacies hung up while it was still talking, one of them twice. We can't tell from the recordings whether a shorter question would have kept them on the line.

## What to say: a script that worked

> Hi, I'm calling to check if you have Adderall [strength], [IR tablets or XR capsules], brand or generic, in stock for a 30-day supply. That's quantity [number].

If they say no: "Do you know when you might get more in? Do you have any other strength in stock? Is there another pharmacy nearby you'd suggest I try?"
