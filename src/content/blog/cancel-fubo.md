**To cancel Fubo, first check who manages your subscription.** A human at Fubo told us direct subscribers can cancel on the Fubo website or call **(844) 441 3826**. If Roku manages the subscription, cancel through Roku. The representative said the Fubo mobile app has no cancellation control.

We called on Saturday, October 3, 2026, with [call4me](/) asking general questions. The first attempt ended with Fubo's automated assistant. The second reached a human after we explicitly asked for one twice and chose customer service. We did not have a subscription to cancel, and no account was changed.

## How to cancel a Fubo subscription online

For subscriptions managed directly by Fubo, its [US cancellation guide](https://support.fubo.tv/hc/en-us/articles/360021527991-How-do-I-Cancel-My-Fubo-Subscription) gives this route:

1. Sign in at [fubo.tv](https://www.fubo.tv/welcome) and choose a profile.
2. Open the settings icon, then **My Account**.
3. Choose **Subscription**, scroll down and select **Cancel**.
4. If a discount offer appears, select **Cancel Subscription** to continue cancelling.
5. Wait for the confirmation page.

The human we spoke with also directed subscribers to the website. Having the Fubo app on an iPhone or Android phone does not mean the app manages the billing. Use a browser if you cannot find a cancellation option in the app.

Not sure who manages the subscription? Fubo's guide says to check [fubo.tv/subscription](https://www.fubo.tv/subscription): a subscription managed by Roku is labelled **Roku Subscription**.

## Can you cancel Fubo by phone?

Yes, for a direct subscription. The representative said support can cancel it in their system after verification using **the account email and the last four digits of the payment card**.

Fubo publishes **(844) 441 3826** for phone support, open **10 am to 10 pm Eastern, seven days a week**, with possible holiday changes. Those hours come from its [contact page](https://support.fubo.tv/hc/en-us/articles/360015151672-How-do-I-Contact-Fubo-Support), checked October 3, rather than from our representative.

### How we reached a human at Fubo

The route on our second call was:

1. The opening menu offered **1 for English** and **2 for Spanish**.
2. After the recording notice, Alex introduced itself as Fubo's AI assistant and requested the billing ZIP code and last four card digits.
3. We asked to be transferred to a human customer service representative for a general cancellation question.
4. Alex offered to answer instead and warned that a representative might take five minutes or more. We repeated our request for a human representative or supervisor.
5. Alex offered **customer service, technical support, sales or cancellation**. We chose **customer service**, and it transferred us to a person.

That five minute warning was the assistant's estimate, not our measured wait. General questions were answered without an account lookup. An actual cancellation needs verification.

The first attempt shows why the distinction matters. We asked for a representative but then accepted Alex's offer to answer. When we asked about free trial timing, the automated assistant said it could not help and the call ended. The cancellation answers below come from the human on our second call or the named official policy, not that failed attempt.

## Does cancelling a Fubo free trial end it immediately?

**For a trial taken directly through Fubo, yes.** The representative said cancellation takes effect immediately and the remaining trial days are forfeited. For a paid plan, they said access continues until the end of the paid billing period.

There is an important exception: Fubo's [cancellation policy](https://support.fubo.tv/hc/en-us/articles/360004457571-What-Is-Fubo-s-Cancellation-Policy) says a **Roku free trial continues until its scheduled end** after cancellation. That exception is website evidence; the representative did not explain it during our call.

## How to cancel Fubo on Roku

The representative said subscriptions started through Roku must be cancelled through Roku. Roku's current [subscription instructions](https://support.roku.com/article/manage-or-cancel-subscriptions) give these options:

* **Online:** open [my.roku.com/subscriptions](https://my.roku.com/subscriptions), select the active Fubo subscription, then **Manage subscription** and **Turn off auto renew**. Follow any confirmation prompts.
* **On the device:** highlight Fubo in the Apps section, press the remote's **Star** button, choose **Manage subscription**, then **Turn off auto renew**. Enter a subscription PIN if prompted.

Fubo's own guide still uses older labels such as **Unsubscribe** and **Cancel subscription**. Roku's instructions were updated September 14, 2026. Follow the confirmation shown in your account and save it.

## Does Fubo refund prepaid or unused time?

We asked the representative about prepaid charges. They referred refunds to another department and did not establish eligibility.

Fubo's [published policy](https://support.fubo.tv/hc/en-us/articles/360004457571-What-Is-Fubo-s-Cancellation-Policy) says it does **not refund prepaid or partial months**. Most plans have no cancellation fee, but a promotional offer with a commitment period can carry an early cancellation fee. Check the terms of your own offer before assuming cancellation is free.

## How do you confirm Fubo cancellation or resolve a later charge?

The representative said a successful cancellation produces an **email confirmation**. Save it with the cancellation date and any support reference.

Fubo's [guide](https://support.fubo.tv/hc/en-us/articles/360021527991-How-do-I-Cancel-My-Fubo-Subscription) also says the Subscription page shows an end date and time when cancellation is scheduled. An ended subscription shows reactivation options. Merely logging in does not reactivate it.

If a charge appears after cancellation, the representative said **billing handles the issue**. Contact support with your confirmation and the charge details, and ask them to explain which account and service period the charge belongs to. Our calls did not test a billing dispute or obtain a refund.

## Common Fubo cancellation questions

### Can I cancel Fubo on my iPhone or Android phone?

Use a web browser for a direct subscription. Our representative said the Fubo app does not have a cancellation option. If another company manages the subscription, follow that company's route instead.

### Can Fubo support cancel a Roku subscription?

Our representative directed Roku subscribers to Roku. Check who manages your subscription before calling so that you reach the right company.

### Is uninstalling the Fubo app enough?

Use the cancellation route and get confirmation. The representative described an account action on the website or through verified phone support; removing an app was not a cancellation method.

### What if I cannot find the cancel button?

Check the billing provider and try the website rather than the mobile app. For a direct subscription, call support with the account email and last four card digits. Ask for a human if the automated assistant cannot resolve it.
