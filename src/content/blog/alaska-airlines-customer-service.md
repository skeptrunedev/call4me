**The short answer:** the Alaska Airlines customer service number for reservations in the United States and Canada is **1-800-252-7522** (1-800-ALASKAAIR). Alaska's [Contact us page](https://www.alaskaair.com/content/about-us/help-contact/contact-us) lists it as the "Reservations / pre-flight questions department" with "Service hours: Available 24/7". **A virtual assistant answers, not a person.** Tell it what you need, and when it offers to connect you to reservations, say yes. **A live agent answered 12 minutes 44 seconds into our call**, after about 9 minutes 20 seconds on hold. The hold announcement had said about 9 minutes.

This got us to a person, with no booking and no Atmos Rewards account:

1. Call 1-800-252-7522. The virtual assistant says "How can I help you today?"
2. We asked for a reservations representative. It didn't transfer us. It asked: "Can you tell me what you're calling about so I can get you to the right person?"
3. It answered our questions about hours, fees and callbacks itself, then asked: **"Did you want me to connect you to reservations now to confirm for today?"** We said yes.
4. It asked for a **first and last name**, then: "One moment while I connect you to a team member." The wait estimate was about 9 minutes.

What the agent told us:

- **Reservations is open 24 hours a day.** "We're 24 hours a day." The virtual assistant said the same: "Reservations for pre-flight questions are available 24-7."
- **Have your confirmation code ready.** Without one, a name and travel date can work "if they're the passenger."
- **Callbacks depend on the wait.** "Our system automatically will do a callback option if it's required by our hold times." We weren't offered one.
- **No fee to change or cancel a Main cabin ticket.** "Well, we don't have any fees." A change can cost the difference in fare. If you cancel outside of 24 hours, "the value is issued as credit for future travel."
- **The 24-hour rule covers all tickets.** "All our tickets within 24 hours of booking, if you're not traveling within those 24 hours, are fully refundable back to the original form of payment."

## Change and cancel fees on a Main cabin ticket

What we heard, from the virtual assistant and then the agent:

- **No change or cancellation fee.** The assistant: "For most Alaska Main and First Class fares, there are no change or cancellation fees." The agent: "Well, we don't have any fees."
- **You pay any fare difference.** The assistant: "If the new flight costs more, you'd just pay the fare difference, and if it costs less, you may get credit back depending on the ticket rules."
- **Cancel after 24 hours and you get credit, not cash.** The agent: "if you cancel outside of 24 hours, the value is issued as credit for future travel."
- **Within 24 hours of booking, a full refund to your card,** as long as the flight isn't within those 24 hours. The agent said this applies to "all our tickets."
- **Saver fares are stricter.** The assistant said Saver "may only be eligible for a 50% credit if you cancel at least 14 days before the first flight." Alaska's [Saver fares page](https://www.alaskaair.com/content/travel-info/flight-experience/saver) says the same: "Saver fares are eligible for a 50% credit if canceled at least 14 days before departure of the first flight on the ticket."

## What to have ready

- **Your confirmation code,** if it's about a trip you booked. It's the first thing the agent named.
- **The passenger's name and travel date,** if you don't have the code. The agent said that works "if they're the passenger."
- **Your first and last name** for the virtual assistant. It asks before it transfers you, to "share with our team."
- **Time.** Our hold was about 9 minutes 20 seconds, and the agent put us on a second hold of about 2 minutes to check an answer.

## Things that don't work

On October 22, 2026, with the virtual assistant:

- **Asking for a representative straight away.** It didn't transfer us. It asked what we were calling about.
- **Asking for the fastest way to a person.** "I can't help with strategies to bypass our phone system."
- **Counting on a callback.** The assistant said it couldn't promise one, and the agent said the system offers it only "if it's required by our hold times." Neither offered one on our call.

What did work was telling it our question, letting it answer, and saying yes when it offered to connect us.
