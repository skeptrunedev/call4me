**The short answer:** the DoorDash customer service number is **855-431-0459**. DoorDash's [customer help page](https://help.doordash.com/en-us/consumers) lists it under "Can't find what you are looking for?": "Our chat and call support are available 24/7" and "Call Us at 855-431-0459". So the DoorDash phone number for customers is open **24 hours a day, 7 days a week**, and the representative we reached confirmed that a real person answers around the clock.

This got us to a live person in 2 minutes 8 seconds:

1. At "To continue in English, press 1", **press 1**.
2. At "If you are a customer, please press 1", **press 1**.
3. An automated voice asks "How can I help you?" Asking for a representative doesn't skip it: it asks you to name the problem instead. **Say "missing item".**
4. **Say "yes"** when it repeats the problem back to you.
5. When it asks whether you want a refund, a redelivery, or the status of an existing refund, **say "refund"**.

After a recording notice and a short hold, a DoorDash customer service representative picked up. He told us:

- **You can report a missing item by phone or in the app.** "You can just call the DoorDash customer service. And there's also a self-service app that you can just report it through DoorDash application."
- **Refunds can come back as DoorDash credit right away.** If the refund is approved, it shows up "as soon as possible" as DoorDash credit.
- **Or back to your card in 3 to 10 business days.** Customers can choose the original payment method instead, and that takes "three to ten business days, depends on your bank."
- **Phone support with a real person is 24/7.** We asked "is your phone support available 24/7 with a real person?" and he answered "That's correct."
- **He'll want the phone number on the order.** His first move was to offer to process the refund himself: "You just need to provide me your phone number associated to the order."

We called on Friday, October 9, 2026, at about 4:08 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The DoorDash customer service phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 855-431-0459.** "You've reached the DoorDash's phone concierge. To continue in English, press 1." Press 1. The Spanish ("oprima dos") and French ("appuyer sur trois") options play right after it.
2. **The caller-type menu.** "Please listen carefully as our menu options may have changed. If you are a customer, please press 1. If you are a Dasher, please press 2. If you are a restaurant on our platform or are currently onboarding, please press 3. If you are a Drive merchant or are calling about our white label fulfillment service, please press 4." Press 1.
3. **"How can I help you?"** This is an automated voice that listens for what you say. When we asked for a representative, it answered: "I understand you'd like to speak with an agent. To ensure that you get to the right person to help, you can say things like, Dasher never arrived, missing item, or refund." Name your problem in a few words.
4. **It confirms the problem.** For a missing item: "I understand that you're looking for help with a missing or incorrect item on your order. Is that correct?" Say yes.
5. **It narrows it down.** "Are you looking to receive a refund, an order re-delivery, or are you asking about the status of an existing refund?" We said "refund".
6. **A recording notice.** "This session may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance and training purposes," then "Please stay on the line after your call for a brief survey about your experience."
7. **A short hold.** About 16 seconds of sound with no speech on our call, likely hold music.
8. **A person answers.** "Thank you for calling DoorDash. This is [name]. How can I assist?" On our call that was 2 minutes 8 seconds into the recording.

If you stay on after the representative hangs up, a two-question survey starts: "Did we resolve your issue? If yes, press 1. If no, press 2."

## What to have ready

- **The phone number on your DoorDash account.** It was the first thing the representative asked for: "your phone number associated to the order".
- **Which order and which item was missing,** so you can say "missing item" at the voice prompt and explain it once a person picks up.
- **Whether you want DoorDash credit or a refund to your card.** Credit comes "as soon as possible". Your original payment method takes 3 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

If you'd rather not call at all, the representative pointed to the app, and DoorDash's help page also offers "Chat with Us in the App". Looking for other DoorDash support numbers? On this line, Dashers, restaurants and Drive merchants use the same number and pick their own option at the second menu.

## Things that don't work

- **Asking for a representative at "How can I help you?"** It didn't transfer us. It asked us to name the problem instead and offered "Dasher never arrived, missing item, or refund" as examples. Naming the problem is what got us through.
