**The short answer:** Hertz has different numbers for different jobs. Its help center page ["Phone Numbers - U.S."](https://hertz.my.site.com/care/htz_faqsearchwebform?searchTerm=Phone+Numbers+U.S.&lang=en_US&pos=us) lists:

- **Reservations: 1-800-654-3131.** "Book Leisure/Business Reservations 1-800-654-3131 (US, Puerto Rico and Canada)". International reservations: **1-800-654-3001**.
- **A rental you already had: 1-800-654-4173.** For customer service on past rentals, "please call 1-800-654-4173 (They are open from 6 am - 7 pm Central time, Monday - Friday)".
- **Extending a rental: 1-800-654-4174,** open 24/7.
- **Roadside assistance: 800-654-5060.**

We called **1-800-654-3131**, the reservations line. **We did not reach a person.** A virtual assistant answered, introduced itself as "Haley, your virtual assistant", and answered all three of our questions itself. Our caller never asked for a representative, so we can't tell you how to get to a human on this line.

What the virtual assistant told us:

- **Credit card deposit hold:** "up to $200 on top of your estimated rental charges", varying by vehicle class: "a small SUV is $300, a standard SUV is $400", and more for larger or specialty vehicles.
- **Debit card deposit hold:** "a flat $500 hold in addition to the estimated charges". Debit cards only work "up to a small SUV", not for standard SUVs or larger, or the Adrenaline, Prestige or Dream cars.
- **When the hold comes back:** after the car is returned and the rental closed, but "it can take 7 to 10 business days" depending on your bank.
- **Changing or canceling:** "Yes, you can change or cancel a reservation. Whether there's a fee depends on the rate type." Pay Later: no fee before pickup. Prepaid: free within 24 hours of booking, then $100, or $200 within 24 hours of pickup.
- **Age:** 20 at most U.S. locations, 18 in New York and Michigan, 19 in Alabama and Nebraska. A young renter fee of $52 a day in those four states and $25 a day elsewhere.

## The Hertz reservations phone tree, step by step

There was no keypad menu on our call.

1. **Call 1-800-654-3131.** "Thank you for calling Hertz. Your call may be recorded or monitored for quality assurance."
2. **About 8 seconds of silence.** Then: "Hi, this is Haley, your virtual assistant. To get you to the right place, could I please get your confirmation number?"
3. **Say you don't have one.** Our caller said "I don't have a confirmation number. It's just some general questions." The assistant said "No problem at all. What questions can I help you with today?"
4. **Ask your question out loud.** The assistant answered each question in full itself. It didn't transfer us or offer a person.

## What Hertz's own pages say, next to what the assistant said

Hertz's "Rental Deposit Hold" help article says the hold is up to $200 for credit cards, more for SUVs and premium vehicles, and $500 for debit cards, on top of the estimated rental charges. That matches what the assistant told us.

The rest of the assistant's answers (the 7 to 10 business day release, the $300 and $400 SUV holds, the debit card vehicle limits, the cancellation fees, the minimum ages and the young renter fees) we only have from the call. We haven't checked them against a Hertz page, so treat them as what Hertz's phone assistant said on October 8, 2026, and confirm on your own reservation.

Two small things in the audio:

- To "can a reservation be changed or canceled by phone without a fee?", it started with "Yes", then said "Oh, sorry", and explained that the fee depends on the rate type. So the honest answer is "sometimes".
- On the AAA waiver it said you need "the AA discount code in your reservation". Three of our four transcripts hear "AA" there, though it said "AAA member" a moment earlier.

## What to have ready

- **Your confirmation number,** if you're calling about a booking. It was the assistant's first question. Without one, saying you have general questions worked.
- **Which card you'll pay with,** credit or debit. It changes the hold and which cars you can rent.
- **Your rate type,** Pay Later or prepaid, if you're asking about cancellation fees.
- **The state you're renting in and your age,** if you're under 25.
- **Your AAA membership,** if you're 20 to 24.
