**Adobe Animal Hospital Los Altos is at 4470 El Camino Real, Los Altos, California 94022, and its phone number is (650) 948-9661.** That is the number on the hospital's own [locations page](https://www.adobe-animal.com/locations), and it is the number both calls on this page dialed. The same line also answers for the Los Gatos office, which the site lists separately at (408) 357-8000.

To reach a person, **press 5** ("regarding all other inquiries"). Press 6 is for a life-threatening emergency, press 1 is the pharmacy, and press 2 is for people who are not clients and are calling about a charge.

On October 6, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called to book a regular, non-emergency exam for a one year old husky mix with a small wart-like lump on his chest. **A team member answered 3 minutes 3 seconds into the call.** The account owner then joined the call himself, and staff booked **Thursday, October 8 at 10:00 AM at the Los Altos hospital**, "the big building on El Camino."

## The phone tree and the hold

1. A recording names both locations, Los Altos and Los Gatos.
2. **Press 5** for a team member. The recording also points to web chat at adobe-animal.com.
3. Hold. The queue announces your place ("You are number one") and offers a callback: "please press star one and follow the prompts. Do not hang up until the system has confirmed your number to call you back."

Two calls from the same assistant show how long that hold can be:

Both calls sat at "number one" in the queue for most of the wait. In the booking call, the assistant said it would take the callback option, but a team member picked up about 40 seconds later, so the recording does not show a callback being set up.

## What the calls did not settle

- **No price was discussed.** Not for the exam, and not for any tests on the lump.
- **No confirmation number, reminder, or text was mentioned**, and staff did not say what to bring or how early to arrive.
- **No medical advice was given.** Staff booked a regular exam; nobody on the call judged whether the lump was serious.
- **Attendance is not established.** The recording shows the booking, not the visit.
- **The callback option was not tested.** The assistant said it would take it, but a person answered first.
- **The account owner did the talking once he joined.** The assistant got through the menu and the hold, then handed the call to the owner when a person answered. His voice is disguised in the recording.

## Book your own vet visit by phone

What made the booking call quick once a person answered:

- **Know the account.** Staff found the dog by the phone number on file. If you are not sure which household number is on the account, give both.
- **Say why, briefly.** "A small new lump, no pain, want it checked" was enough for staff to book a regular exam rather than urgent care.
- **Give a window, not a single time.** The owner gave 9:30 to 11:30 AM and either location; staff offered four times and he took the first one.
- **Ask what you care about before hanging up.** This call never covered price or what to bring. If those matter, ask.
