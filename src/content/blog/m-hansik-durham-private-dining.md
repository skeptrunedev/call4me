**To book M Hansik's private dining room in Durham, email the events team at mhansikdurham@gmail.com; staff quoted a $350 room fee and a $1,000 deposit at booking.** M Hansik is at 501 Washington Street, Suite C, Durham, North Carolina. Its [official locations page](https://mhansik.m-restaurants.com/locations) lists **(919) 813-2338**, the number our calls dialed, and dinner hours of 5:00 to 9:30 pm Wednesday and Thursday, 5:00 to 10:00 pm Friday and Saturday, and 4:00 to 9:00 pm Sunday. It is closed Monday and Tuesday. The site's [private parties page](https://mhansik.m-restaurants.com/party) is an inquiry form; it does not publish a room fee or deposit.

The phone line opens with a menu. **Press 3 to speak to a team member.** On our call, a person answered about 43 seconds after the call connected.

On Thursday, October 1, 2026, at about 9:43 pm Eastern, a call4me customer's assistant called M Hansik to move a company dinner forward: a private room for 12, possibly 14, on Thursday, November 5, 2026 at 6 pm. The person who answered said:

- **The private room is enclosed** and "we can accommodate up to 35."
- **A $350 room fee and about $65 per person for a set menu** are correct. The assistant read both numbers back and he said "That's correct."
- **The deposit is "just a $1,000 deposit at the time of booking."**
- **The room is booked by email,** not over the phone. He gave the events team's address as **mhansikdurham@gmail.com**, spelled it out, and confirmed the assistant's read back. "Once we get that email, we'll get the contract and everything together for the party."

He did not agree to pencil in or hold the room on the call. Nothing was paid and no card was given.

## Vin Rouge: a covered, heated garden with no room fee

**Vin Rouge is at 2010 Hillsborough Road, Durham.** Its [official private events page](https://www.vinrougerestaurant.com/private-events/) lists **(919) 416-0466**, the number both calls dialed, and describes two private dining rooms plus indoor and outdoor event spaces. The page does not mention the garden by name or list capacities, minimums or deposits.

## What these calls did not establish

- **No booking was made at either restaurant.** M Hansik declined to hold the room until it received an email. Vin Rouge's "Yes" on October 7 is a spoken answer, and the recording does not include a hold confirmation or any document.
- **M Hansik's deposit terms are incomplete.** The $1,000 figure came with no word on crediting, refunds or a cancellation deadline.
- **"Up to 35" was the answer to a question about seated capacity.** The host did not say "seated," and did not say whether the room has a minimum headcount.
- **The set menu itself was not discussed.** Neither were drinks, tax, gratuity or service charges at M Hansik.
- **Vin Rouge's minimum and deposit are unknown.** Both recordings end before those answers. We also did not confirm that the promised emails arrived.
- **No human listening verification is claimed.** The transcripts were reviewed against the saved call text and independent automatic transcriptions.

## How to book M Hansik's private room

1. **Call (919) 813-2338 during dinner hours and press 3** if you want to ask questions first. Staff will send you to email for the booking itself.
2. **Email mhansikdurham@gmail.com** with the date, time, headcount and the occasion. That is the address staff gave on October 1; it is not published on the restaurant's website, so confirm it on the phone if your email bounces.
3. **Ask the contract to state** the room fee, the set menu price per person, the $1,000 deposit, how the deposit is credited, the cancellation deadline and what happens if the headcount changes.
4. **Do not treat a phone conversation as a hold.** At M Hansik, the contract comes after the email.

## Where our caller fell short

- **It said "next year" at Vin Rouge** for a date five weeks away. The events manager had to ask whether it meant 2027 or next month.
- **It led with "the 50% deposit"** at M Hansik instead of asking openly. The answer was different anyway, but an open question is cleaner.
- **It never got Vin Rouge's minimum.** The first recording ends while the events manager checks, and the second call put the hold ahead of the money questions.
