**Virgin Hotels New York City is at 1227 Broadway, New York, NY, and its only parking is valet.** On October 15, 2026, guest services quoted **$80 plus tax per 24 hours for a sedan or smaller car and $90 plus tax for an SUV or larger car**, with no in and out privileges. The number dialed was **(212) 213 0090**. When we searched the hotel's own site on October 16, its New York contact and events pages listed 212.213.0090 as the main line at 1227 Broadway, and a separate reservations line, 877.499.5277.

One thing to know before you budget: the hotel's own FAQ, as indexed on October 16, listed valet at **$75 a night for standard vehicles** and $90 for SUVs, plus tax. The staff member on the call said $80 for a sedan. The phone answer is newer than whatever the page last said, but the two don't match, so ask for the current rate when you check in.

A call4me customer's assistant made both calls with information questions only. Nothing was reserved or paid.

## What the calls did not settle

- **The oversized limit.** The staff member said six or seven feet, offered to check with the garage, put the call on hold for about six minutes, then came back without an answer because the garage was busy at check in. Her best guess was over seven feet tall. Width was not answered.
- **The Model Y rate.** She said it looks like a sedan, so $80, but that they would confirm it when the car arrives. That is not a guarantee.
- **The $75 or $80 question.** The FAQ figure and the phone figure differ. Neither call asked about it.
- **Tax and any extra charge** for taking the car out mid stay were not quantified.
- **Event parking** and holiday rates were not asked about.
- No parking was reserved, and the calls don't show anyone parking there afterwards.

## The phone menu and time to a person

Both calls went to the same automated greeting. The menu offers 1 for reservations, 2 for dine and drink, 3 for sales, marketing and events, 4 for Exhale spa, 5 for "people, not human resources", **6 to "chat to a real live lovely human being"** and 0 for guest lost and found or anything else.

A guest services staff member answered **1 minute 2 seconds** into the first call and **1 minute 6 seconds** into the second. The recordings don't show which key the caller pressed. If you're calling about valet, 6 is the option the menu names for a person.

## Before you drive to 1227 Broadway

- **Budget per 24 hours, not per night.** A 6 PM drop off runs to 6 PM the next day. Staff quoted $80 plus tax for a sedan and $90 plus tax for an SUV.
- **Plan errands around no in and out.** Each time the car comes out and goes back in costs extra.
- **Tall vans and roof boxes: ask first.** The only answer on height was a guess of seven feet, and nobody had a width limit.
- **Charge your EV before you arrive.** The garage has no charging.
- **Call 45 minutes ahead** for the car, using the hotel's number.
- **If both valet spots out front are full,** park on that street or the adjoining street, and a bellman moves the car when a spot opens.
