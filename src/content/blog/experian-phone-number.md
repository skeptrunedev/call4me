**The short answer:** Experian's credit freeze line is **1-888-397-3742** (1-888-EXPERIAN). It is fully automated. It asks for your **complete Social Security number** before it will do anything, and we found no option that reaches a live person without it. After you pass its identity check (SSN, ZIP code, street number, date of birth), it still steers you to freeze **online at experian.com/freeze** or **by mail**. In our tests it never placed the freeze over the phone.

We tested this on Wednesday, September 30, 2026, between about 3:45 and 5:45 pm Central, with [call4me](/) placing the calls and me joining to type my own Social Security number. This page is what we heard.

## The phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 1-888-397-3742.** A recording thanks you for calling and plays a consent notice about recording, AI transcription, and biometric technology. Staying on the line counts as consent.
2. **"You've reached our self-service line."** It says you can add or remove a security freeze or order a credit report here.
3. **"Please say or enter your complete Social Security number."** All 9 digits, on the keypad. This comes before any menu.
4. **"Now please say or enter your ZIP code."** This has to match the address on your credit file. Mine is a mail forwarding address, not where I live, and that is the one that worked.
5. **"We also need the numeric portion of your street address."** For 1878 Main Street you enter 1878. Same address as the ZIP.
6. **"Please enter your date of birth"** as a two digit month, two digit day and four digit year: July 20, 2009 is 07202009.
7. **It reads your date of birth back** and asks "Is that correct?" Say yes.
8. **It offers to text you instructions** if you're on a mobile phone.
9. **"Have you already attempted to complete a freeze online or through the mail?"** Then it recommends online or mail as "the simplest and most effective way", and offers the text again.
10. **If you ask it to repeat,** it says you can freeze "online, by phone, or by mail", then reads out only the online and mail steps.
11. After a couple of unanswered yes or no questions it ends the call: "I'm afraid I can't proceed without valid information."

If any identity step fails (we missed the date of birth on the first pass), you get: "We weren't able to verify your identity." Then **say verify or press 1** to start over, **say self-help or press 2**, or **say instructions or press 3** for instructions on freezing by mail or online.

## What to have ready before you call

- Your full **Social Security number**
- The **ZIP code and street number of the address Experian has on file**, which may be an old or mailing address
- Your **date of birth** as MMDDYYYY
- A phone with a keypad. The prompts time out in a few seconds, so have the numbers in front of you.

## Things that don't work

These routes did not reach a consumer support agent on September 30, 2026.

- **Saying "representative" at the Social Security prompt.** It answers "I don't recognize that entry" and asks for the number again.
- **Pressing 0 at the Social Security prompt.** It skips ahead to a spoken menu (freeze or unfreeze, fraud alert, report or score). Asking for anything there sends you to experian.com/help.
- **Saying "other questions" or "representative" at that menu.** You get "visit experian dot com slash help", then "I'm currently unable to provide support on this matter", and the call ends.
- **Calling Experian's headquarters line, 714-830-7000.** It's a corporate switchboard "intended to assist Experian business partners". Press 1 and you land in the same automated system. Press 2 and a real operator answers, but only to transfer you into that same system, which asks for your Social Security number again.

## How to actually freeze your Experian credit

This is what Experian's own phone system told us to do:

- **Online:** go to **experian.com/freeze** and use the Credit Freeze Management Center. The phone system calls this "the fastest and easiest" way.
- **By mail:** send a written request to **Experian Security Freeze, P.O. Box 9554, Allen, TX 75013** with your full name, Social Security number, addresses for the past two years, date of birth, a government issued ID, and a utility bill or bank statement.

A freeze is free, and you'll need to lift it before applying for new credit.

## How we made this recording

call4me is a phone for your AI agent: your agent (Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT) tells it what to get done and it makes the call. For this test, it dialed Experian, and when Experian asked for my Social Security number it rang my phone so I could join and type it myself. The agent never saw or heard the number. It took the call back once I was through, and wrote down every prompt.

Some of our 15 calls that afternoon ended early because of bugs on our side, which we fixed the same day. The phone tree above held the same on every call that got that far.
