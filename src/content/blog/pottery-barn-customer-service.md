**The short answer:** the Pottery Barn customer service phone number is **1-888-779-5176**. Pottery Barn's [contact page](https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/contact-us/) lists it as "U.S. Customer Service 1.888.779.5176", and the site header says "Call Us 1.888.779.5176". Neither page we checked lists phone hours. The site also offers Chat Now.

**A live person answered.** Here's how we got to her:

1. Call 1-888-779-5176. A recording says the call may be monitored, then asks why you're calling: "You can say track my order, received a furniture scheduling notification, or customer service."
2. **Say "customer service".** It answered "Please hold while I transfer your call. All agents are currently busy."
3. A customer service representative picked up **49 seconds after the call connected**, about 27 seconds after the transfer started.

**What the representative told us:**

- **Order status without an order number:** "You can't get a status on an order without seeing the order." Her advice was to get the order number and look it up. Pottery Barn's own [order tracking page](https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/list-order/) says something different: "If you do not have your order number, please call 1.888.779.5176". So the website sends you to the phone line, and the phone line asked us for the number. We didn't ask whether she could find an order another way (more on that [below](#how-we-made-this-recording)).
- **Returns:** "You have a 30-day window to return either", meaning furniture and regular items alike. We could not confirm the 30 days on potterybarn.com: the [returns page](https://www.potterybarn.com/customer-service/return/) we were able to read doesn't state a return window.
- **Return shipping:** "It depends. It can be a return shipping fee," for both regular items and furniture. The returns page agrees: "Depending on the reason for return, you may be responsible for return shipping costs." Neither she nor the page gave an amount.
- **Furniture delivery time:** "You get estimated a delivery date with your order." She couldn't give a typical range "without knowing the item."

We called on Thursday, October 8, 2026, at about 4:22 pm Eastern (1:22 pm Pacific), with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Pottery Barn phone menu, step by step

1. **Call 1-888-779-5176.** "Thank you for calling Pottery Barn. Your call may be monitored or recorded for quality and training purposes."
2. **"Please let us know why you are calling. You can say track my order, received a furniture scheduling notification, or customer service."** This menu takes spoken answers. We said "customer service". We didn't try the other two options.
3. **"Please hold while I transfer your call. All agents are currently busy. Please remain on the line and the next available agent will be with you as soon as possible."** No hold time estimate was given.
4. **A representative picks up** at 0:49 on our recording. No account or order number was asked for before the transfer.

## What to have ready

- **Your order number.** It was the representative's first question when we asked about delivery status: "Can we get the order number?" Without it, she said she couldn't give a status.
- **The item you're asking about,** if you want a delivery estimate. She said it depends on the item and the estimated delivery date that comes with your order.
- **The reason for your return.** Both she and the returns page said whether you pay return shipping can depend on it.
