**The short answer:** Windstream customer service is now Kinetic. windstream.com's contact page redirects to [gokinetic.com/support](https://www.gokinetic.com/support), which lists **account support at (800) 347-1991**. Both lines we called answered "Thanks for calling Kinetic."

**Have the phone number on your account, or the account number from your bill, before you dial.** On both of our calls the menu asked for one of them before anything else, and neither call reached a person. No live representative and no AI assistant picked up: only a recorded menu.

Have these ready:

1. **The phone number on your Kinetic account, including the area code.** The menu's words: "Please enter either your phone number, including area code, or enter the account number located on your bill, and then press pound."
2. **Or the account number from your bill.**
3. **Whether it's a residential or a business account.** The menu asked that too.

We made both calls on Thursday, October 8, 2026, at 4:25 pm and 4:30 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) placing them. We had no Kinetic account to give, so we couldn't get past the prompt. If you have yours, enter it at that prompt. We don't know what comes after it.

## The phone menu, step by step

This is the menu on (800) 347-1991. The sales line led to the same menu (see below).

1. **"Thanks for calling Kinetic. Para continuar en español, oprima ocho."** Press 8 for Spanish.
2. **The main menu.** "If you're already a Kinetic customer, press 1. To ask about becoming our customer, press 2. To check order status, press 3. If you're calling to report a downed telephone line or pole, press 4."
3. **Existing customers: the account prompt.** "Please enter either your phone number, including area code, or enter the account number located on your bill, and then press pound." Enter it here.
4. **If it doesn't take,** you hear "We couldn't identify your account by the number you entered" and the same prompt again.
5. **Then it asks residential or business.** "To ask about service for a residential account, press 1. For a business account, press 2."
6. **Then it offers to wait.** "Do you need some extra time to find your account number associated with your Kinetic account? If so, I will wait 30 seconds. Ready to look at your account number? Press 1."
7. **If you still have nothing to enter, it hangs up.** "Sorry, I will now end the call. Goodbye."

At step 2 on our call, the menu restarted and cut off mid-sentence ("If you're already a...") right into the account prompt. That's what you'd hear if our caller pressed 1. Key presses don't show up in our recordings, and our call log doesn't list them, so we can't confirm it.

The menu said "we couldn't identify your account by the number you entered" on both calls, though we had no account number to enter. We can't tell from the recording whether anything was entered or whether that's simply what it says when nothing is.

## The sales line: (866) 703-8175

If you are interested in hearing about new business promotions, press 2. If you are an existing customer calling for upgrades or have recently signed up for service, press 3."

**Our caller chose the existing customer path, not new residential deals.** Our questions were about cancelling service, so it said "upgrades sounds like customer service," and our call log says it took the existing customer option. We didn't try option 1 (residential deals) or option 2 (business promotions), the options aimed at new customers, so we don't know whether either one reaches a sales person.

## Things that didn't work

On October 8, 2026, at Kinetic's account prompt:

- **Saying "representative".** The menu answered "we couldn't identify your account by the number you entered" and asked for the number again.
- **Saying you have a general question and no account number.** Same answer.
- **Waiting.** On the support line our caller only said "Hang on" and "one sec". The menu asked residential or business, offered to wait 30 seconds, and then ended the call.

We have no record of our caller pressing 0, and we didn't try the residential or business options on the sales line's first menu.

## Is there an early cancellation fee?

We didn't get to ask anyone. That page doesn't say how to cancel or whether you need to return equipment, and the support page lists no separate cancellation number. We didn't find out either one.

## How we made these recordings

call4me is a phone for your AI agent: your agent (Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT) tells it what to get done and it makes the call. For both calls, the brief was to reach a person, without logging in or giving account details, and ask three general questions: whether a residential internet customer can cancel by phone and whether there's an early termination fee, whether the modem has to be returned and how, and (on the support line) whether there are offers for customers thinking about cancelling, or (on the sales line) the best number for an existing customer to reach a person. If a menu asked for an account number, it was to say it had a general question and ask for a representative.

What it got wrong:

- **On the support line it never asked for a representative.** It only said "One second", "Hang on", "Hmm, one sec" and "Just a quick sec" until Kinetic hung up. That didn't follow its brief.
- **On the sales line it picked the existing customer option.** The brief was to reach sales, and option 1 (new residential deals) was the option for people who aren't customers. It chose option 3 because its questions were about cancelling. That's why this call ended at the same account prompt as the first one.

Our call log also has two short lines from our caller on the sales call that aren't audible on the recording, and they're marked in the transcript.
