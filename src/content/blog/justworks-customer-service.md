**The short answer:** the Justworks customer service number is **(888) 534-1711**. Justworks' own [Help Center](https://help.justworks.com/hc/en-us) lists it under "Call Us", and its help articles give the same Justworks support number next to support@justworks.com. That's the number this call dialed.

If you're a company administrator with a benefits question, this got a person:

1. **Press 2** ("If you are a Justworks administrator calling on behalf of your company").
2. **Press 1** ("For questions related to benefits").

**A live Justworks representative answered 1 minute 3 seconds into the call,** right after a recording notice. There was no queue message before her.

The first thing she did was check identity. She asked for the caller's name and the company name, then said she had to send **a six-digit verification code** to the admin's cell phone or work email on file before she could help with the account. The admin didn't join the call to read it, so everything below is the general guidance she gave without looking at the account. She said so more than once: "this is all extremely general."

What Justworks benefits support said on October 12, 2026:

- **COBRA usually lasts up to 18 months** after a standard qualifying event such as termination or reduced hours. A disability extension can bring it to **29 months**; a second qualifying event (divorce, legal separation, death of the employee, a dependent aging out) to **36 months**. State programs such as New York continuation and Cal-COBRA can also reach 36 months.
- **An employer can pay for an employee's COBRA. Justworks calls it a COBRA severance.** How it's handled depends on whether the company is on Justworks' master plan policy, an open market policy, or uses Justworks as employer of record. On the master plan, Justworks asks for the length of time and how much of the premiums to cover, as a percentage or an exact dollar amount per tier.
- **For a company that is closing or dissolving, she said employer-paid COBRA is limited to 3 months.** It "must be invoiced and prepaid upfront" before the company's closure, and severance paid through payroll must run before the official termination date. She said the guidance is different if the company stays active and only an employee leaves.
- **Medical, dental and vision coverage runs through the last day of the company's final termination month,** and Justworks requires **30 days' termination notice**.
- **COBRA itself goes through HealthEquity.** She also said dissolving companies are exempt from a monthly COBRA management fee.
- **For account-specific answers,** the admin should call back on the line, chat with Justworks, or reply to the existing support email thread. She also pointed to the [Justworks Help Center](https://help.justworks.com/hc/en-us) and its "COBRA coverage" article.

## What to have ready

- **The account admin, on the line or reachable.** Justworks sends a six-digit code to the admin's cell or work email on file before it will discuss the account. If an assistant is calling for you, the representative said it can only get account-specific help "as long as he is on the line and has provided express authorization."
- **Your company name** as it appears on the Justworks account.
- **Any existing ticket or support email.** She suggested replying to that thread for tailored answers.
- **For COBRA severance:** how many months you want to cover and how much of the premium, as a percentage or a dollar amount per tier, plus your planned termination date.

## How the call went on our side

- **The assistant introduced itself by its own name** and said it was calling on behalf of the account owner. It never claimed to be them.
- **It asked for the code to go to the owner's cell,** said it was connecting the owner, and rang them twice. They didn't answer. When the representative asked whether the code had arrived, it said "Let me check. Not yet," which was a stall: it had no way to see the code itself.
- **It went back and forth on email or cell,** and the representative sent a second code to the work email.
- **Then it asked for general information,** and got through all its questions plus the representative's name and a reference number.
