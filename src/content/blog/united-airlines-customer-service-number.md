**The United Airlines customer service number for US reservations is 1 800 864 8331.** United lists it as **1 800 UNITED 1** in its [official phone help information](https://cloud.enews.united.com/text-messaging-terms.html). On our recorded call, saying **"flight change fee"** got us past the voice assistant and into the reservations queue. A person answered **19 minutes 27 seconds into the original call**.

We made that call on **October 1, 2026**, at about **4:09 pm Eastern**, using [call4me](/). We asked general questions about changing a flight and told the representative we had no trip booked. This guide uses the existing recording from our [United flight change article](/blog/united-change-flight). We checked United's published phone help information again on **November 2**, but did not make a new call.

The recording proves that this reservations route reached a person on the date tested. It does not establish the route for every department, a typical wait or a completed booking change.

## How we reached a live United representative

This was the route for our general flight change question:

1. **Call 1 800 864 8331.** The voice assistant asks why you are calling.
2. **Describe the issue in a few words.** We said "flight change fee". A longer question about Basic Economy and regular Economy fees was not understood.
3. **Answer the travel region prompt.** The menu offered **1** for travel within the United States and Mexico, or **2** otherwise. We pressed 1.
4. **Choose whether to take the survey.** We pressed **2** to decline it.
5. **Stay in the queue.** The system later offered **9** for a voice assisted experience for rebooking after disrupted travel. We stayed on the line because we were asking a general question.

Use the reason and region that match your actual trip. We did not test the disrupted travel option or the route after choosing another region. The phrase we used is evidence of what worked for our inquiry, not a universal shortcut for baggage, refunds or every reservation issue.

## How long did United keep us waiting?

Our total time to a person was **19 minutes 27 seconds**. The time from the transfer announcement to the live greeting was about **18 minutes 10 seconds**. The automated estimate had been **10 to 15 minutes**.

Those measurements describe one Thursday afternoon call. They are not an average hold time, a promise that calling at that hour is faster or evidence that the same menu still has the same queue. When asked for a faster way to reach someone without a confirmation number, the representative said it depended on the queue.

## What should you have ready?

The representative's first question was for our **confirmation number**. We had none, so she could only answer general questions. For a question about your own trip, have the booking confirmation and a clear description of the issue ready.

If you want to change a flight, identify the flight or date you want instead and ask the representative to check the rules for your ticket before you approve a change. Our original call discussed several fare types, but some answers differed from the United pages checked that day. The [flight change guide](/blog/united-change-flight#what-united-com-says) keeps those differences visible. A general answer from that recording does not establish the price or eligibility of your own change.

## Is this the right contact for every United issue?

We tested the **reservations desk**. For another country or language, start with United's [official reservations directory](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/help/contact-reservations.html). For delayed, lost or damaged bags, use United's separate [bag help page](https://www.united.com/en/us/baggage/bag-help). For feedback about a past trip, use the [Customer Care form](https://www.united.com/en/us/customer-care).

These links are alternative official routes, not additional services we tested. Our call did not verify baggage support, a refund claim, MileagePlus account service or a response to the Customer Care form.

For current phone hours, check the reservations directory for the country and language you need. The number in United's phone help information does not by itself establish the hours of every department.
