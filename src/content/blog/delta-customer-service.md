**The short answer:** the Delta customer service phone number for reservations is **800-221-1212**. Delta's [Need Help page](https://www.delta.com/us/en/need-help/overview) says: "To speak with a representative about a new or existing reservation, call 800-221-1212," and "You may contact Reservation Sales 24 hours/day, 7 days/week."

**The first voice you hear is not a person.** It introduces itself as "Delta's AI-powered voice assistant." Here's what got us from it to a live representative, with no confirmation number:

1. Call 800-221-1212. When the assistant asks why you're calling, **say you have a general question for reservations.**
2. It asks what kind of help: "Find an existing reservation, change or cancel a trip, or book a new flight." We said "change or cancel a trip."
3. It asks for your **six-character confirmation number**. We said we didn't have one, had a general question, and needed a representative.
4. "I hear you. Give me a moment. I'll transfer your call now."
5. It offers to **message with a representative** ("the wait time is zero minutes") or a **callback** "within 14 minutes." We said **"no thanks"** and stayed on hold.
6. After about 14 minutes of hold, with recorded announcements every couple of minutes: "You are now being connected to a Delta representative."

A live representative answered **16 minutes 15 seconds into the recording**, about 16 minutes 20 seconds after we dialed. On our call, the hold lasted about as long as the callback estimate it had offered.

**What the representative told us:**

- **No fee to change a Main Cabin domestic ticket.** "There is no fee to change it." You pay or get credit for the fare difference.
- **Fare difference:** today's price is compared with what you originally paid. "If it's lower, then a credit will be issued out for the remaining balance. If it's higher," the difference comes from your card.
- **Cancel, and you get an eCredit,** not a refund. It lasts "for a year from the original purchase date."
- **Refunds:** "a ticket needs to be canceled 24 hours from the original booking date to get a refund, or purchase a refundable ticket."
- **No extra fee for using a phone agent.** "It'll be the same online as it will be with the agent."

## The Delta phone tree, step by step

It's a voice assistant that asks questions. The only keypad options are at the wait offer, and you can answer that by voice too.

1. **Call 800-221-1212.** "Thanks for calling. I'm Delta's AI-powered voice assistant. See delta.com slash AI terms for our terms of use. To get you to the right place, please tell me with as many details as possible why you're calling today."
2. **Say why you're calling.** We said "I have a general question for reservations." It answered "Got it. One moment."
3. **"What kind of reservations help do you need? Find an existing reservation, change or cancel a trip, or book a new flight?"** We said "change or cancel a trip."
4. **"To pull up the right reservation, can you share your six-character confirmation number?"** If you have one, this is where it goes. We didn't, and said so, and asked for a representative.
5. **"I hear you. Give me a moment. I'll transfer your call now."** No second attempt to get the number.
6. **The wait offer.** "We're experiencing longer than usual wait times. You can choose to message with a representative where the wait time is zero minutes, or to receive a callback when it's your turn within 14 minutes. Please note, standard text and data rates apply." Press 1 for messaging, 2 for a callback, or say "no thanks."
7. **The hold.** It asks you to stay on the line after the call for a one-question survey. Then it's mostly music, with recorded announcements about SkyMiles, messaging Delta ("delta.com slash need help" or the Fly Delta app), companion certificates, having your confirmation, ticket or SkyMiles number ready, and free Wi-Fi.
8. **"You are now being connected to a Delta representative."** On our call, that was 14 minutes after we said "no thanks".

## What to have ready

- **Your six-character confirmation number,** if your question is about a booking. The voice assistant asks for it first. Without one, ask for a representative.
- **Your ticket number or SkyMiles number.** An announcement on hold says: "For quicker service, please have your confirmation number, ticket number, or SkyMiles number ready. You can find this information in the Fly Delta app and delta.com under My Trips, or in your confirmation email." Delta's [Need Help page](https://www.delta.com/us/en/need-help/overview) also says: "If you are a SkyMiles member, have your SkyMiles account number available to expedite your call."
- **The card you'll pay a fare difference with,** if you're changing to a more expensive flight. The representative said it comes "from whatever card being used at that point in time."

## What the representative said, in detail

We asked three general questions about a Main Cabin domestic ticket. The answers are the representative's, as recorded. We didn't compare them with Delta's published fare rules.

**Changing a ticket.** "There is no fee to change it, but fare difference goes between what the prices of the ticket as of today compared to the original purchase date look like. And we compared those pricings between the two. If it's lower, then a credit will be issued out for the remaining balance. If it's higher, then that will just have to come from whatever card being used at that point in time."

**Canceling.** "If you cancel it, it will be for an e-credit. That e-credit will last for a year from the original purchase date. So let's say a ticket was purchased today, you would have till 10-8-2027 to use the new credit, if it's not canceled within 24 hours."

**Refunds.** "When it comes to refundability, a ticket needs to be canceled 24 hours from the original booking date to get a refund, or purchase a refundable ticket." Her wording was "24 hours from the original booking date"; together with the line before it, we read that as canceling within 24 hours of booking. That reading is ours.

**Phone versus online.** "No, it'll be the same online as it will be with the agent."

## Things we didn't test

- **The messaging option** ("the wait time is zero minutes") and **the callback** ("within 14 minutes"). We stayed on hold instead.
- **Saying "representative" at the very first question.** We gave a reason first, then asked for a representative when it wanted a confirmation number.
- **The SkyMiles and flight information lines.**

## How we made this recording

call4me is a phone for your AI agent: your agent (Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT) tells it what to get done and it makes the call. For this one, the brief was to reach a Delta reservations representative, ask three general questions, and not log in, give account details, or change anything.

Two things our caller did weren't ideal:

- **It picked "change or cancel a trip"** at the second question, though it had no trip. That's what led the assistant to ask for a confirmation number. It worked out, because the assistant transferred us as soon as we said we didn't have one, but "book a new flight" or a plain request for a representative might have been a cleaner path. We didn't test either.
- **It said "Just a minute. No thanks. Sure."** at the callback offer. The assistant answered "I'm sorry, I didn't catch that" and repeated the offer. A plain "no thanks" the second time went through.
