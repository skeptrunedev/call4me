**The short answer:** Uber's main rider help page says "While we don't offer a customer service number for Uber riders," and sends you to log in for help instead. The one rider support number in Uber's help center is **1-800-314-2308**, the "Call premium support" button on Uber's [Reach out to support](https://help.uber.com/riders/article/call-uber-now?nodeId=d2b17464-b912-4978-a7ee-9e46987616be) page. That page says: "Our support team is here to help our most dedicated riders, 24 hours a day," and "When you call, you'll need to confirm the phone number that's connected to your Uber account."

**We didn't reach a person.** The line's first and only prompt asks for the 10-digit phone number on your Uber account, followed by the pound key. We don't have an Uber account to give it. After three entries it didn't recognize, it said "For security reasons, we'll unfortunately need to end this call" and hung up, 1 minute in. There was no menu, no option for a representative, and no callback offer before that.

A second number on Uber's help center, **+1 833-315-8991** on its [Report rider inquiries](https://help.uber.com/en/riders/article/report-rider-inquiries?nodeId=8c4671e3-2972-418d-88d8-8a60a8795ab8) page, turned out to be a line "for riders in the state of Colorado". Everyone else is told to use the help center in the app.

Both lines end the same way: **"Please contact our support team through the help section of your app."**

We called on Thursday, October 22, 2026, at about 1:10 pm Eastern, with [call4me](/) placing the calls. This page is what we heard. We never got to ask our questions about hours, callbacks, lost items or refunds, so what we say below about lost items comes from Uber's own help pages, not from the calls.

## The Uber premium support line, step by step

This is the line as it played on October 22, 2026, calling 1-800-314-2308.

1. **Call 1-800-314-2308.** "Hello. Thank you for calling Uber's dedicated premium support line." No language menu and no recording notice played before the next prompt.
2. **"Please enter the 10-digit phone number associated with your Uber account, followed by the pound key."** This is the only prompt. Enter the phone number on your Uber account and press #. Uber's help page says to do this "manually" if you're calling from a different phone than the one on your account.
3. **If it doesn't recognize the number:** "We didn't recognize that entry. Please try again," and the same prompt plays again.
4. **After the third miss,** "There have been too many invalid entries. For security reasons, we'll unfortunately need to end this call. Please contact our support team through the Help section of your app. Goodbye." It hangs up.

We don't know what happens after a phone number that Uber does recognize, or whether the line checks that your account counts as one of its "most dedicated riders". Our call never got past step 2.

## The Colorado rider line

Uber's "Report rider inquiries" page says "You can let us know about any rider inquiries by calling +1 833-315-8991." We called it two minutes after the premium line. The whole call was 48 seconds:

1. "Thank you for calling Uber Support. This line is for riders in the state of Colorado. If you are not located in Colorado, please reach out to our general support team via the Help Center in the app."
2. A recording notice: "this call will be recorded for quality and training purposes."
3. **"If you are a rider and a Colorado resident, press 1. Otherwise, press 2."** We aren't in Colorado, so our caller didn't choose 1.
4. "This line is currently only available for riders in Colorado." It added that drivers should use "Contact Support" in the Uber Driver app ("chat, phone, or email"), and "We are no longer accepting driver calls at this number. Goodbye." Then it hung up.

We don't know what's behind option 1.

## What to have ready

- **The phone number on your Uber account,** typed on the keypad, then #. It's the first thing the premium line asks for, and Uber's page says you'll need to confirm it.
- **The Uber app, signed in.** Both lines send you there, and Uber's rider help page sends you to log in for support.

## Things that don't work

On October 22, 2026:

- **Calling 1-800-314-2308 without a phone number Uber recognizes.** There's no other option at the prompt. Three misses and it ends the call.
- **Calling +1 833-315-8991 from outside Colorado.** It tells you to use the help center in the app and hangs up.

## Lost an item in an Uber, or charged the wrong amount?

We meant to ask a person about both and couldn't. Here's what Uber's help pages say.

**A lost item:** Uber's [lost item page](https://help.uber.com/en/riders/article/forgot-something-in-an-uber-we-will-help-you-find-it?nodeId=feab9beb-9eac-47a2-b63a-6915d8821e1d) has you call the driver from the app: "Tap on Activity at the bottom of your Uber App", "Select the trip on which you lost your item", "Tap on Find Lost Item", "Tap on I need to contact my driver about a lost item", enter your contact number, and "Tap on Call". If you can't reach the driver, it says to "raise a concern through the in-app Help section with the correct trip in reference."

**A wrong charge or a refund:** we didn't find a phone route for this. Both lines we called point you to the help section of the app, which is where Uber handles trip issues.
