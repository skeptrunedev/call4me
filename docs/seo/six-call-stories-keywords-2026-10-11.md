# Keyword targeting for six named venue call stories

October 11, 2026. Research uses the installed `ahrefs` CLI, with its help and
local README checked before execution. The user authorized all six stories and
asked to name the venues. The location checks below independently match the
actual dialed business numbers. Customer contact details are not SEO content.

## Target decisions

These are monthly Ahrefs estimates, not expected visits. KD is Ahrefs keyword
difficulty. Unknown means null, never zero. US and Canadian estimates are
separate markets. Overlapping phrases must not be added into a traffic forecast.

| Article path | Primary phrase | Market | Volume | KD | Intent and use |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `/blog/hijinx-hotel-irvine` | hijinx hotel irvine | US | 2,900 | 0 | Name the Irvine Spectrum attraction and explain the recorded family booking correction. The stored results include questions about children. |
| `/blog/lai-wah-heen-private-dining` | lai wah heen | Canada | 1,300 | 0 | Toronto venue research and reservations. Explain the actual private dining request and separate the reservation from pending pricing and proposal. |
| `/blog/pin-on-grand-reservations` | pin on grand | US | 150 | Unknown | Exact venue and real table reservation. Do not label this a proven low difficulty keyword. |
| `/blog/salon-per-se-bellaire` | salon per se | US | 2,200 | 0 | State the Bellaire branch in Houston immediately. Explain rescheduling with the preferred stylist; the brand phrase includes other branches. |
| `/blog/sabai-eugene-reservations` | sabai eugene | US | 700 | 1 | Give the official Eugene reservation contact and the real alternative time outcome. |
| `/blog/icebar-orlando-tickets` | icebar orlando | US | 700 | 0 | Explain the recorded admission and walk in inquiry; use tickets as supporting task language. No purchase or reservation was completed. |

The higher demand opportunities are venue terms with a useful calling task,
not six interchangeable generic AI articles. A named case study can answer
specific booking questions but is not the venue's official website. Put the
official venue link and city near the opening. Preserve existing broad dinner,
haircut and private dining guides and link to them where useful. Introduce the
existing assistant workflow without asserting which assistant these customers
used. Suggested prompts are instructions for future calls, not transcript text.

## Reproduction and evidence

Raw files are outside the repository at
`/home/skeptrune/git_projects/call4me-seo-evidence/six-2026-10-11/`.
Do not commit raw SERP files because they contain temporary provider signatures.
The initial US batch measured 26 exact phrases; the Canadian batch measured four.
Five matching reports cover restaurant reservations, hair appointments, Hijinx,
ICEBAR and Canadian private dining. Eight SERP reports include two null responses.
The exploratory `pin on grand oakland` phrase was rejected after the official
phone and address established St. Petersburg; it is not a location claim.

```bash
ahrefs keyword-batch 'hijinx hotel irvine,salon per se,sabai eugene,icebar orlando,pin on grand' --db us --json
ahrefs keyword-batch 'lai wah heen,private dining toronto,private dining rooms toronto,lai wah heen private dining' --db ca --json
ahrefs keyword-ideas 'restaurant reservation' --type matching --db us --limit 100 --json
ahrefs keyword-ideas 'hair appointment' --type matching --db us --limit 100 --json
ahrefs keyword-ideas 'hijinx hotel' --type matching --db us --limit 100 --json
ahrefs keyword-ideas 'icebar orlando' --type matching --db us --limit 100 --json
ahrefs keyword-ideas 'private dining' --type matching --db ca --limit 100 --json
ahrefs keyword-serp 'lai wah heen' --db ca --limit 10 --json
ahrefs keyword-serp 'hijinx hotel irvine' --db us --limit 10 --json
```

Measure actual Search Console query impressions and clicks after publication,
then assistant connections, completed first calls and repeat use. Search volume
alone is not evidence of qualified demand or a promise of traffic.
