## Keep the stylist, make the timing flexible

The customer gave several acceptable windows instead of a single replacement time. That allowed the assistant to move past Tuesday immediately when staff said the stylist was off. It then asked for the earliest Thursday option after 5:00 PM and accepted 5:30 PM.

For your own call, say whether keeping the stylist matters more than getting the earliest appointment. If you would accept another stylist, make that explicit. If you would not, the caller should return available dates rather than quietly changing the person doing the service.

Include the old appointment details privately so staff can find the right booking. Ask them to move it, not create a second appointment, and confirm the replacement date before ending the call. If a rescheduling fee matters, ask about it before accepting. This conversation did not settle fees or a cancellation policy.
