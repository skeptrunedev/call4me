**The short answer:** the Vivint customer service number for existing customers is **1-800-216-5232** (also written 800.216.5232 or (800) 216-5232). Vivint's [support site](https://support.vivint.com/) shows "Call (800) 216-5232 for support" when its chat is offline, and its call button reads "Call 800.216.5232". The numbers on Vivint's [contact page](https://www.vivint.com/company/contact-us), 844.481.8630 and 855.749.2969, are for people who aren't customers yet ("Not a customer? Get peace of mind and call us now to get started."). Neither page lists phone hours.

To reach a live person at Vivint customer service billing, this route took **2 minutes 14 seconds**:

1. At "Please enter the 10 digit phone number associated with your account", **enter the account phone number**, then **press 1** to confirm it.
2. At the main menu, **press 6** ("For payments, questions about your bill, or changes to your account").
3. At the billing menu, **press 4** ("For questions about your Vivint bill, press 4 to speak to the next available billing specialist").

A representative from **account services** answered after a short hold message. The menu also offers billing help by text chat (press 1 at the billing menu), which we didn't try.

On Tuesday, October 13, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called that number to ask for a loyalty discount. The account was month-to-month at **$43.64 a month**, and the customer was weighing whether to keep it. The assistant was told not to accept any offer, plan change or new contract, and to bring everything back. Here is what happened:

- **Nothing could be offered or applied without the verbal passcode.** The representative had the account pulled up, but said "for us to offer you something, we need the account fully verified", and the verbal passcode "is the first step".
- **If the account holder doesn't remember the passcode,** he offered to send a passcode reminder to the email on file, or a passcode confirmation by mail.
- **He named a possible offer anyway:** "You guys could be eligible for five dollars for six months of discount." He was clear that he could "only give you the information", not apply it.
- **Nothing was accepted.** The assistant said it would take the offer back to the account holder.

## What this call did not settle

- **The discount was not applied or confirmed.** "Could be eligible" is what the representative said. He didn't say whether $5 a month for 6 months is the most Vivint offers, or whether a verified call would get more.
- **The passcode reminder may not have been sent.** The assistant asked for the reminder to go to the email on file. The representative didn't say he sent it, and the recording doesn't confirm it.
- **Nobody asked for the cancellation department or a retention team.** The call stayed with the billing specialist the menu reached. We don't know whether saying "cancel" routes you somewhere else.
- **Phone hours weren't asked.** Searches for a "Vivint customer service phone number 24/7" are common, but neither this call nor the pages we checked state hours for the phone line.
- **We only know the route with an account phone number.** The first prompt asks for it. We didn't try the line without one, and we don't know where the other menu options lead.

## Before you call Vivint support about your bill

These tips apply to the Vivint security customer service line for existing customers, based on this one call.

- **Have the verbal passcode ready.** It was the first thing the representative asked for, and without it he wouldn't apply anything. If you're the account holder and you've forgotten it, ask for the reminder by email before you call back.
- **Call from, or know, the account phone number.** The menu asks for it before anything else.
- **Know your current monthly price and plan.** The assistant opened with $43.64 a month, month-to-month, which framed the request.
- **Decide in advance what you'd accept.** The representative's offer was $5 a month for 6 months. If that isn't enough, say so on a verified call and ask what else is available, or ask whether a different team handles retention.

An assistant calling on someone's behalf hit the same wall any third party would: the account was found, but nothing could change without the passcode. If you want your assistant to do the whole call, give it the passcode privately, or have it ring you so you can say it yourself.
