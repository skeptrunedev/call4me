**The short answer:** Vanguard's [IRA recharacterization page](https://investor.vanguard.com/investor-resources-education/iras/ira-recharacterization) says to "call one of our experienced retirement specialists" at **800-205-6189**, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern. Vanguard's main customer service phone number for personal investors is **877-662-7447**, with the same hours, per its [corporate contact page](https://corporate.vanguard.com/content/corporatesite/us/en/corp/contact-us.html). The call below dialed 800-205-6189, the number on the recharacterization page.

A spoken menu answers first. On this call, a live person picked up **1 minute 30 seconds** in:

1. At the prompt for the last four digits of your Social Security number, the caller said **"I'm just getting started"** (the menu offers that phrase if you're not calling about an existing account).
2. It played "If you are calling about a 401k or employer sponsored plan, press or say 1. For anything else, press or say 2." The caller gave no spoken choice.
3. It offered new accounts and transfers, said "I didn't get that", repeated itself, then said **"One moment while I connect you with someone who can help."**

The first person was an **Account Maintenance and Beneficiary Specialist**. She needed the account owner on the line to verify, then transferred the call to **Retirement Services**, even though we had dialed the retirement specialists line. The retirement specialist came on at **11:42**, after about 4 minutes of waiting for the transfer.

On Wednesday, October 14, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant made this call to check a 2025 Roth IRA contribution recharacterization the customer had signed through DocuSign the day before. The customer joined briefly to pass Vanguard's identity check, then handed the call back. Hear it on the [examples page](/examples#vanguard-customer-service-number).

**This page is not financial or tax advice.** It reports what one Vanguard representative said on one recorded call about one account. Ask Vanguard or a tax professional about your own situation.

Vanguard's recharacterization page says the earnings figure uses the method in IRS Notice 2000-39 and that "the calculations are based on ALL assets in your IRA plan, not just the fund contributed to." That is why the earnings on a $7,000 contribution can look large or small relative to what that money alone did.

## What this call did not confirm

- **It did not complete the recharacterization.** The specialist said it was pending and expected to finish the next day. The recording doesn't show it finishing.
- **No exact effective date was given.** The assistant asked for "the effective transfer date". The specialist gave the amount and "completed by tomorrow", and said pre-deadline rules apply.
- **No written confirmation.** Vanguard said there wouldn't necessarily be one beyond the transaction record.
- **No tax filing questions.** Nobody discussed Form 8606, an amended return or how the tax return should report it. Vanguard's page notes you "may need to file an amended return."
- **Only one menu path.** We don't know where the 401k branch or an existing account login goes, or whether calling 877-662-7447 would reach Retirement Services faster.

## The Vanguard phone tree to a live person, step by step

This is the menu as it played on October 14, 2026, calling 800-205-6189.

1. **"Thank you for calling Vanguard. As a commitment to quality service, calls are recorded and monitored."**
2. **"If you have an existing Vanguard account, please enter the last four digits of your social security number or employer ID number. If you are not calling about an existing account, you can say, I'm just getting started."** Account holders would normally enter digits here. Our caller said "I'm just getting started."
3. **"If you're calling about a 401k or employer sponsored plan, press or say 1. For anything else, press or say 2."** The saved call log shows no spoken answer. Key presses aren't audible in the recording, so we can't say whether a digit was pressed.
4. **"Let's look at your options. For opening or transferring into a new account, like an IRA, individual brokerage account, or 529, press 1."** Then "I didn't get that. Can you try again?" and the same option again.
5. **"One moment while I connect you with someone who can help."** A person answered about 10 seconds later.

The menu route we took is the one for people without an account, so the first person was not the right team. She verified the owner, looked at the record, and then transferred to a retirement specialist. If you have an account and enter your digits, the route may differ.

## Getting through verification with a third party on the line

The first representative would not discuss the account with the assistant alone: "We need the client for the authentication." call4me rang the customer, who joined and was asked for:

- his first and last name,
- whether he was comfortable sharing security information with a third party on the line, and whether the third party could "speak, act and transact",
- a name for the person assisting him,
- the last four digits of his Social Security number and his ZIP code,
- a security code texted to the phone number on file, read back.

He then said "I will let my assistant take over now" and the assistant carried on. If you're having an assistant make this call, expect to join for a few minutes at the start.
