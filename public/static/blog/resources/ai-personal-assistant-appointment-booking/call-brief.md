## Suggested task prompt

Use call4me to call the specified business using this brief. First check whether an existing booking is active. You may book one appointment within the dates, times, provider, location and cost permissions above. Ask to preserve an active original slot until a replacement is confirmed, according to my instructions. If the business cannot follow that process, report the options and ask me. Read back the full date, local time, provider or party size and location. Ask about cancellation terms, arrival instructions and written confirmation. Return the exact outcome, the old booking's status, available transcript or recording, and all unresolved questions. Ask separately before changing my calendar.

For an arrival update, tell the desk my original appointment time and expected arrival, ask whether I should still come in, and preserve any conditions in its response. Collect other options without accepting an appointment change unless I authorize it above.
