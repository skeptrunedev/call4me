**The short answer:** the DIRECTV customer service phone number is **800-531-5000**. DIRECTV's [contact page](https://www.directv.com/support/contact-us/) says "Call us at 800.531.5000," under "Prefer to talk?", with "Business hours: Mon-Sun, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM CT". It's the only phone number on that page, and there's also a Chat Now option. directv.com doesn't publish a separate number for cancelling.

**We did not reach a person.** An AI agent that introduced itself as "Eva, DIRECTV's AI agent" answered right after the greeting. It answered our general questions itself. When it offered to connect us to a live agent, it first asked for "the phone number associated with your DIRECTV service." We had no account, so we had nothing to give it, and the call ended there. We did not try any other route to a person.

**What the AI agent told us** (this is the AI agent talking, not a DIRECTV representative):

- **You can cancel by phone.** "Yes, a customer can cancel their DIRECTV service over the phone."
- **The early termination fee is up to $20 a month.** "If a customer cancels while still in a contract, there's an early termination fee of up to $20 for each month left in their commitment." We weren't able to load DIRECTV's fee pages to check this figure ourselves, so treat it as unconfirmed.
- **Leased equipment usually has to go back.** Receivers, remotes and power cords, "to avoid a non-return fee." It said you either get a return kit in the mail or bring the equipment to a FedEx Office location.
- **Offers for people thinking of cancelling exist, but a person has to tell you about them.** "The details depend on your account and situation. If you want to know about current offers, a live agent can review them with you directly."

We called on Thursday, October 8, 2026, at about 3:20 pm Central, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## What happened when we called DIRECTV customer service

1. **Call 800-531-5000.** "Thank you for calling DIRECTV. Para español, oprima el ocho. This call is being recorded for quality assurance."
2. **An AI agent answers.** Apart from the Spanish option, there was no keypad menu on our call. About 15 seconds in: "Hi there. I'm Eva, DIRECTV's AI agent. How can I help you today?" You talk to it in plain sentences.
3. **It answers general questions itself.** Each answer came about 7 to 9 seconds after the question, after a short filler line ("Let me check on that", "Let me look into that").
4. **Asking for a person means giving your account phone number.** When we said yes to "Would you like to connect with someone for that?", it said "Got it", went quiet for about 9 seconds, then asked: "Please say or enter the phone number associated with your DIRECTV service."
5. **Without one, it goes back to answering itself.** We said we didn't have it. It replied "I'll look into general offers for you", repeated its earlier answer, and offered to connect us again.

So, on our call, the step between the AI agent and a person was the phone number on the account.

## How to cancel DIRECTV by phone

Based on our call, plus what DIRECTV's own forum users say:

1. **Call 800-531-5000 during business hours,** Mon-Sun, 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM CT, according to DIRECTV's contact page.
2. **Have the phone number on your DIRECTV account ready.** That's what the AI agent asked for before it would connect us to someone.
3. **Tell it you want to cancel.** On DIRECTV's community forum, users in a thread about being [unable to cancel service](https://forums.directv.com/conversations/satellite-account/unable-to-cancel-service/691cc13ffaba3f1aaea38832) report the voice menu looping, and advise saying "cancel" to reach an agent. That's advice from forum users, not DIRECTV policy, and we didn't test it.
4. **Ask a person about offers before you confirm.** The AI agent said only a live agent can review current offers.
5. **Ask how your equipment goes back.** The AI agent said it's either a return kit in the mail or a drop-off at a FedEx Office location.

## What to have ready

- **The phone number associated with your DIRECTV service.** The AI agent asked for it before connecting us to a person.
- **How many months are left on your contract,** if you have one. The AI agent said "If you want to know the exact amount, you'd need to check how many months are left on the contract."
- **Your leased equipment:** receivers, remotes and power cords, in case you're cancelling.

## What we didn't test

- **Getting through to a person.** We had no account, so we couldn't give the phone number it asked for.
- **Saying "cancel", "representative" or "agent",** or pressing 0. Our caller never asked for a person directly; the AI agent offered.
- **Chat.** directv.com lists a Chat Now option. We only called.
- **Whether the answers match your contract.** The AI agent spoke in general terms ("usually", "up to", "sometimes").
