**To cancel a Crunch membership, check the cancellation options for your home club and agreement.** At Crunch Polk Street in San Francisco, a representative told us members can use the **app, an iPad at the club, or an email request to operations or a sales member**. They said a phone call provides instructions but does not itself complete cancellation.

We obtained those answers with [call4me](/) on October 6, 2026. This was a general inquiry, not a member account cancellation. Crunch's [official FAQ](https://www.crunch.com/faq) says cancellation policies depend on the location and agreement. Do not assume the Polk Street answer applies to every franchise or membership.

## How to cancel Crunch membership online or in the app

Crunch's [FAQ](https://www.crunch.com/faq) says some clubs support cancellation through **Member Portal, then Manage Membership**. Our Polk Street representative specifically said the app was an option.

We did not sign in to a membership or test the buttons. If the cancellation control is missing, ask your club whether your agreement supports it and which alternative it accepts. At Polk Street, staff said an email request to operations could be processed when the online route was inaccessible.

## Cancelling by email or at the club

Polk Street staff described two alternatives to the app:

* **At the club:** use the club's iPad to make the cancellation request.
* **By email:** send the request to the operations manager or a sales member so it can be processed.

The representative did not provide an email address during the recording. Ask your home club for its current cancellation recipient; do not assume an address found on a third party website is accepted. We did not submit an email or observe the club iPad flow.

Before sending anything, ask what information is required and how you will receive confirmation. Do not send payment card details merely because an unsolicited address asks for them. Our call did not establish a document checklist or a confirmation turnaround time.

## What notice is needed before the next charge?

**The exact deadline remains unresolved.** We asked about the next monthly charge or annual fee, and the representative said cancellation needed to happen **by the 30th of the month**. They sounded uncertain when asked whether the same answer applied to a fixed term agreement.

That does not establish a universal 30 day notice policy, whether a request on the 30th stops a particular payment, or how February works. The annual fee question was not answered separately. Do not convert this brief statement into a guaranteed cutoff.

For your own membership, ask the club to check:

1. The next monthly payment and any upcoming annual fee.
2. The date it must receive your cancellation request.
3. Whether an initial commitment changes the effective date or creates a fee.
4. How you will confirm the account is cancelled and whether another payment remains.

Get the answers for your agreement, and keep the confirmation. We did not ask staff to review an actual bill or waive a fee.

## Finish the task with your agent

After you review the instructions, choose the accepted route. For an email request, have your assistant check the draft against the club's requirements before you authorize sending it. For an app or club visit, use its checklist while you complete the required step. If a representative needs to verify the account holder, be ready to take part.

Ask your assistant to keep the submitted request, effective date, final charge and confirmation together in the task. Leave it marked as awaiting confirmation until the club actually confirms cancellation.
