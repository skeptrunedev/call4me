**Couch reupholstery cost needs a definition of what is included.** Modern Memory Design in New Jersey publishes **$1,000 to $2,500 including labor and standard fabric**. Threadful Things in Austin lists **labor from $1,658 for a sofa with three seats**, with fabric separate. Those figures describe different scopes, so they are not directly comparable. Sources: [Modern Memory Design](https://www.modernmemorydesign.com/pages/upholstery-cost-calculator) and [Threadful Things](https://threadfulthings.com/services/pricing/).

Check those inclusions before comparing either with another shop's total.

## Chair reupholstery cost: dining seats versus armchairs

**A removable dining seat and a fully upholstered armchair need separate comparisons.** Threadful Things lists labor from **$95 for a removable dining seat**, **$670 for a club chair** and **$995 for a wingback chair**. Fabric is additional. Modern Memory Design lists **$100 to $250 for a dining chair seat and back** and **$300 to $1,100 for an armchair or club chair**, including standard fabric and labor. Sources: [Threadful Things chair pricing](https://threadfulthings.com/services/pricing/) and [Modern Memory Design's price guide](https://www.modernmemorydesign.com/pages/upholstery-cost-calculator).

Identify the chair construction before asking for a price. Tell the shop whether the work covers only a seat pad, the seat and back, or the whole upholstered body. Include whether the seat comes out and whether you can deliver just that part. For a set, give the exact quantity and flag any chairs with different damage.

Ask for a price per chair and a total for the set. Have the assistant check whether fabric, padding replacement and installation are included in both figures. If the shop needs to inspect the chairs, record that condition rather than converting a starting price into a final total.

For exposed wooden arms, legs or frames, request a separate woodwork estimate. Our [furniture refinishing cost guide](/blog/furniture-refinishing-cost) covers that related task.

## How much upholstery fabric will you need?

[Extra Touch Upholstery's yardage guide](https://extratouchreno.com/cost-guides/how-much-fabric-do-i-need/) estimates **12 to 16 yards of plain fabric for a standard seven foot sofa**, compared with **16 to 22 yards for patterned fabric requiring matching**. For a single dining seat, its estimates are **0.75 to 1.25 yards plain** or **1.25 to 1.75 yards patterned**. These are that shop's planning ranges.

Ask the upholsterer to confirm yardage for the specific furniture and fabric before buying material. Include the fabric width, pattern and product name when available. If you want to supply your own fabric, have the assistant check whether the shop accepts it and how that changes the estimate.

## What should the written estimate include?

Give every shop the same scope so the comparison answers a useful question: what will it cost to complete the work you actually want?

* **Covering:** Specify the fabric and whether the price includes every cushion, back, arm and skirt you want recovered.
* **Interior work:** Ask whether foam, padding, springs or frame repairs are included, optional, or dependent on inspection.
* **Transport:** Request the charge for both pickup and return, including any access requirements at your building.
* **Timing and payment:** Check the estimated completion date, deposit amount, balance due date and cancellation terms.

Send clear front, back, side and damage photos through the shop's requested channel. Ask what could change after inspection and how you would approve extra work. A photo estimate is useful for narrowing the shortlist; confirm its conditions before arranging collection.
