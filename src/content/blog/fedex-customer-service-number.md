**The short answer:** the FedEx customer service number in the U.S. is **1-800-463-3339** (1.800.GoFedEx), as listed on [fedex.com](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/customer-support/call-us.html). A voice assistant answers.

**Our three calls without a tracking number never reached a person.** The voice assistant requested a tracking or door tag number each time. We did not test every route.

**With a tracking number, here's what got us a person:**

1. Call 1-800-463-3339 and say you're calling about a package with a tracking number.
2. Say or enter the tracking number. The voice assistant reads you the latest status.
3. Ask for a representative. It may first ask about a missed delivery and offer to text you a claim link. Then it asked us "You want to speak with a representative, right?" twice. We said yes both times.
4. It said "It looks like we weren't able to catch the reason for your call" and offered a callback: "If you'd like for us to call you back at this number, please press 1."
5. **Press 1.** It said "We will call you back as soon as an agent becomes available."
6. FedEx called back within seconds of that call ending, about 4 minutes after we first dialed. An agent was on the line 23 seconds into the callback.

**What the agent said:** request Hold at Location on fedex.com, at a FedEx location or a participating store like Dollar General or Walgreens. There's no fee to hold it. The request is accepted "within 24 to 48 hours", and then the package is held "for the next five business days." Bring a government-issued photo ID and the tracking number. And yes, the shipper can block hold and redirect changes. Details are [below](#what-the-fedex-agent-told-us), next to what fedex.com says.

We made these calls on Thursday, October 1, 2026, with [call4me](/) placing them. The tracking number was from my own package from an online order that FedEx tried to deliver four times in June before returning it to the seller.

## What the FedEx agent told us

We asked how a recipient holds a package at a FedEx location, or redirects one that's already on the way. Here's what she said, next to what fedex.com says. We checked fedex.com on October 1, 2026.

|---|---|---|

| Can the shipper block it | "Yes, he will" | "Some shippers may restrict certain delivery options" |

Where they agree: hold it online, holding is free, bring a photo ID and the tracking number, and the shipper can block changes.

Where they differ:

- **How long it's held.** The agent said five business days. fedex.com says up to 7 days.
- **How fast it's accepted.** The agent said 24 to 48 hours. fedex.com says a request made before midnight the day before delivery "should be available for pickup the next day."
- **The cost of redirecting.** When we asked "what the fee would be" after asking about redirecting, the agent said "There is no any specific fee, because every time, because you hold the package at the station, so there is no any fee." That's about holding. fedex.com lists a fee for delivering to another address, starting at $5.55.

When we asked how to redirect a delivery that's already on the way, she described holding it instead: "When the package should be returned back to the station, you should get the message. And then by the fedex.com, you should hold your package, any location where you want."

At the end she asked us to take a short survey, "with a positive feedback." ## How to hold or redirect a FedEx package online

You don't need a person for this. From FedEx's [Hold at Location page](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/hold-at-location.html), checked on October 1, 2026:

1. Go to FedEx's tracking page and enter your tracking number.
2. Select **Manage Delivery**, then **Hold at Location**.
3. Choose the FedEx location you want it sent to.
4. Wait for a text, automated phone call or email saying it's ready.

By phone, the same page says to call 1.800.GoFedEx and **say "track my package,"** then follow the prompts.

What else fedex.com says:

- **How long it's held:** "FedEx will hold your package for up to 7 days." After that it goes back to the shipper.
- **What to bring:** a government-issued photo ID. If your name doesn't match the label, bring the tracking number too. If the address on your ID doesn't match, bring another proof of address, like a utility bill.
- **The shipper can block changes:** "some shippers may restrict certain delivery options. Or they may not allow recipients to change the destination once the shipment is in transit."

## What it costs to hold or redirect a FedEx package

From FedEx's [Delivery Manager FAQ](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/faq/delivery-manager.html), checked on October 1, 2026:

| Option | Cost |
|---|---|
| Hold for Pickup at a FedEx location | Free |
| Redelivering your package | Free |
| Vacation hold (up to 14 days) | Free |
| Deliver to another address | Starts at $5.55 per package |
| Deliver another day | Starts at $5.55 per package |
| Schedule a delivery time | Starts at $5.55 per package |

FedEx says "Fees vary based on location and will be determined when services are requested." Redirecting more than 120 miles costs more.

**The deadline:** "You have until midnight the day before delivery to change your delivery location." For Hold for Pickup, requests made before midnight the day before delivery "will be available for pickup the next day."

## If you call without a tracking number

We called three times without one: at 1:01 pm, 1:13 pm and 2:39 pm Eastern on October 1. We had no package to ask about, just the question of how holding and redirecting work. None of the three reached a person, and none got a callback offer.

FedEx's own [phone menu page](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/customer-support/phone-menu.html) lists **"Representative" as 0**. On our calls, saying "representative" still led back to the tracking number. **We didn't try pressing 0, and we didn't try saying "help me find it" or pressing star.** We don't know where those go.

Have these ready for the route we used:

1. **A tracking number,** from your shipping confirmation or the sender's email. If you don't have it, the voice assistant says to ask the shipper.
2. **Or a door tag number,** from the note FedEx leaves after a missed delivery. It starts with **DT** and has **12 digits**.

## Where to find your tracking number or door tag number

This is what the voice assistant told us, word for word:

- **Tracking number:** "The tracking number is included in your shipping confirmation or email from the sender." And: "The shipper of your package should have the tracking number. We recommend that you get in touch with the shipper to get this number and call us back once you have it."
- **Door tag number:** "A door tag is a notification left at your door by FedEx after a delivery attempt. It starts with the letters DT, followed by 12 digits."

If you got a door tag, fedex.com says you can also text "follow" and your door tag number to **48773** (for example, follow DT999999999999) to get your delivery options.
