## Questions for one call

1. Can an adult work quietly on a personal laptop in general seating during the supplied window?
2. Which general seating areas have power outlets, if any? Do not ask for a guaranteed seat.
3. How does a guest connect to WiFi, and does that require a library card?
4. Is there any current branch specific restriction the visitor should know about?

Published study room rules already prohibit reservations and do not intend rooms for use lasting multiple hours. Do not ask to reserve one. Source: https://sfpl.org/services/meeting-rooms/study-rooms
