**The Paradox Hotel Vancouver phone number is (236) 900-6001.** The hotel's [official FAQ page](https://www.paradoxhotels.com/vancouver/faq) lists it as the Front Desk line, and gives the hotel address as **1161 West Georgia Street, Vancouver, BC V6E 0C6**. The same page lists a concierge email, concierge.vancouver@paradoxhotels.com, a 3:00 PM check in time and a standard check out before 12:00 PM.

There was no phone menu on this call. A staff member answered "Thank you for calling the Paradox Hotel" about 7 seconds into the recording.

Just after midnight Vancouver time on October 5, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called that number about an upcoming October 17 to 19 stay for a special occasion trip. The guest had accepted an upgrade to a Paradox Signature King room by email earlier that night. The assistant had two questions and was told not to finalize anything, give card details or agree to charges:

1. Is there any flexibility on the $30 per night upgrade fee?
2. Will the romantic room setup already arranged with the concierge (rose petals, balloons and chocolate covered strawberries) be set up in the Signature King now that the room type changed?

**Staff said the upgrade fee was "pretty set in stone", and that concierge would set up the romantic room package in the new room as long as it is selected on the guest's profile.**

## Calling about a Paradox Hotel Vancouver room upgrade

The brief did two useful things. It asked a direct question about the fee instead of hinting, and it accepted the first answer politely, so the call stayed short. It also limited the call to information: no upgrade changes, no card numbers and no new charges.

If you are calling about your own Paradox Hotel Vancouver room, ask staff to look up the booking and read back what is on it: room type, nightly rate after the upgrade, and any packages or setups attached to your profile. A general answer like "as long as it's selected" is helpful, but a read back is what tells you it actually is. For a romantic or surprise setup, the official FAQ lists the concierge email, which gives you something in writing.
