## What to check when comparing ticket options

Ask what the ticket actually covers. A lower admission price and a package that includes drinks are different purchases, so the useful comparison depends on what the group plans to buy. On this call, staff mentioned packages but did not give enough detail to compare their totals.

Also distinguish closing time from guaranteed last admission. The employee described staying out or returning before 2:00 AM, but did not give a separate final entry deadline. A group arriving late should ask directly about its expected arrival time rather than assuming doors stay open until the minute the venue closes.

This was a successful information call. It answered whether the group needed a reservation and identified two admission prices to check. It was not a completed night out, a guaranteed place in a queue or a ticket purchase.
