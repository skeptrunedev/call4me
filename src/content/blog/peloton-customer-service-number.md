**The short answer:** the Peloton customer service number, the Peloton member support phone number for US and Canada members, is **1-866-679-9129**. Peloton's [privacy policy](https://www.onepeloton.com/privacy-policy) says to "contact our member support by telephone, US & CA: 1.866.679.9129", and its [membership terms](https://www.onepeloton.com/membership-terms) give the same number to change or cancel a membership and for billing questions. Neither page lists phone hours.

For privacy requests, Peloton lists a second number, **1-844-559-0051**. The same privacy policy says: "If you are a U.S. resident and would like to exercise one of your rights, you can also contact us at 1-844-559-0051." Its [US states privacy notice](https://www.onepeloton.com/us-states-privacy-notice) offers the same number alongside an online Privacy Request Form.

That second number is the one in this recording. On October 1, 2026, a call4me customer's assistant called 1-844-559-0051 to stop postal catalogs and marketing mail to a household in Seattle. What happened:

1. **No menu.** A recorded greeting said "Thank you for reaching out to Privacy at Peloton" and that the caller "will now be connected to our member support team."
2. **A short queue message,** "Your call is very important to us and will be answered in the order it was received."
3. **A person answered 22 seconds into the call.** She greeted the caller with "thank you so much for contacting Supervisor Support", as both independent transcriptions heard it.

## What the call established

| Detail | What the conversation established |
| --- | --- |
| Number dialed | 1-844-559-0051, the privacy request line on Peloton's own privacy policy |
| Phone tree | None. A privacy greeting, a queue message, then a person |
| Time to a person | 0:22 from the start of the recording |
| Who answered | A Peloton member support representative |
| Request | Stop all postal catalogs and marketing mail to one address, under any of the names there |
| What she asked for | The account holder's first and last name, then the email address on the account |
| Lookup | About 17 seconds of silence while she checked |
| Her answer | The request needs an escalation, and she said she could escalate it |
| Confirmation number, timeframe, written follow-up | Not given |
| Recording length | 2 minutes 49 seconds |

The representative described the escalation as a request "to stop the information or notifications about sales or something like that." When the assistant repeated that the goal was no more catalogs or marketing mail to that address under any of the names, she said "I can escalate it for you. This is not a problem."

## What the call did not confirm

This is a partial result, and the recording supports no more than that:

- **No confirmation that the mail is stopped.** She offered an escalation. She did not say it was submitted, done or permanent.
- **No reference number and no timeframe.** Peloton's privacy policy says "We process requests to be placed on do-not-mail and do-not-contact lists within the timescales required by law", but the representative gave no timeframe on the call.
- **"Any of the names" was not addressed directly.** The assistant said it twice. Her answers ("not a problem", "that is correct") came after it, but she never said the escalation covered every name at the address rather than the one account she looked up.
- **The 1-866-679-9129 member support line was not called.** We don't know its menu, hours or hold time.
- **Nothing was cancelled or changed on a membership.** If that's why you're calling, the membership terms point to 1-866-679-9129.

If you need a record of an opt out, Peloton's privacy policy also lets you "submit an 'Object to Marketing' request via our Privacy Request Form", which gives you something in writing. The call didn't test that form.

## Which Peloton support phone number to call

Peloton customer support has two published phone lines for US members, plus written options:

- **Membership, billing and cancelling:** 1-866-679-9129, the member support number on Peloton's privacy policy and membership terms.
- **Privacy rights, including stopping marketing:** 1-844-559-0051. On this call it played a privacy greeting and connected to the member support team, with no menu.
- **In writing:** the Privacy Request Form linked from Peloton's [US states privacy notice](https://www.onepeloton.com/us-states-privacy-notice), or privacy@onepeloton.com, which the privacy policy lists.

## What to have ready

- **The account holder's first and last name.** It was the first thing asked.
- **The email address on the Peloton account.** It was the second, and the lookup ran on it.
- **The full mailing address,** if the request is about postal mail.
- **Every name that receives mail there,** if you want all of them covered. Ask the representative to say whether the request covers each one.

Before hanging up, ask for a reference number and how long the change takes. This call ended without either.
