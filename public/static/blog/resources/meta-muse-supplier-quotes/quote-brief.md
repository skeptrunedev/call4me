## Questions for each approved supplier

Please identify the exact SKU, material, dimensions convention, strength and pack quantity that meet the supplied specification. What is the merchandise total for the requested quantity? Separately state shipping, tax and every required surcharge for the supplied destination. What arrival estimate and quote expiration apply? Identify relevant returns terms and any required minimum order. If any specification cannot be met, describe the difference before suggesting a substitute.

## Compare fairly

Calculate delivered total only when every required charge is known. Sum merchandise, shipping, tax and required fees. Divide by usable units for delivered cost per unit. Keep supplier statements separate from agent calculations and assumptions. Check all offers against the same quantity, destination, date and specification. Reject an automatic winner if an essential term remains unknown.

## Phone brief after authorization

Ask only the unresolved questions above for the exact supplied scope. Do not order, accept terms, create an account, arrange a callback or share buyer details beyond the approved identity and fields. If the recipient needs information not supplied, record the gap and return it to the buyer. Follow call4me_get_call until the call ends, then collect the transcript and any available recording. Preserve who answered, the stated facts and the unanswered questions. Do not treat an automated repeat of a website price or a voicemail as a completed quote.
