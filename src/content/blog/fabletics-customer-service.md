**The short answer:** the Fabletics customer service number is **1-844-322-5384** (1-844-FABLETICS). Fabletics' help center lists it as open **24 hours, 7 days a week**, and says you can cancel your VIP membership "by phone, chat, or online" with "no charge to cancel."

Getting a person is quick. On our call, an automated assistant answered, tried to match the number we called from to an account, and asked twice for the phone number on the account. When we said we didn't have it, it said **"I will transfer you to a consultant for more help."** A consultant picked up **1 minute 8 seconds** after dialing.

What the consultant told us:

- **The VIP charge is $69.95 a month,** and it's optional: **skip the month between the 1st and the 5th** or you're charged. Fabletics' [help center](https://help.fabletics.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058964072-How-much-does-Fabletics-cost-per-month) says the same, and that the charge lands between the 6th and 12th.
- **The charge becomes a VIP credit** you can spend on an item, outfit or bundle "for value up to a hundred dollars."
- **To cancel by phone, they need your first name, last name, email address, and confirmation that you're the account holder.** Without an account, they won't go further.
- **Retention offers and the return policy depend on your account.** The consultant wouldn't describe either without one.
- **Unused VIP credits:** the consultant said you can spend them, or "if you want them to be refunded, they will be refunded." That's what the consultant said on the phone. Fabletics' [help center](https://help.fabletics.com/hc/en-us/articles/25291944893332-What-happens-to-my-VIP-credits-if-I-cancel-my-membership) only says you can still use your credits after cancelling and that unused credits expire after 12 months. It doesn't mention refunds, so if you want your money back for unused credits, ask for it on the call.

We called on Friday, October 2, 2026, at about 2:18 pm Pacific, with [call4me](/) asking the questions. This page is what we heard.

## The Fabletics customer service phone tree, step by step

1. **Call 1-844-322-5384.** "Thank you for calling Fabletics. Calls are recorded. Para español, marca el ocho."
2. **"How can I assist you today?"** This is a voice assistant, not a keypad menu. Say what you need in a sentence.
3. **It looks you up by the number you're calling from.** If that number isn't on a Fabletics account, it says "I couldn't find your info with the number you're calling from" and asks: "Please say the phone number connected to the account you're calling about."
4. **If you have an account, say its phone number.** We didn't have one, so we said so. It asked once more, then said "Just a moment. I will transfer you to a consultant for more help."
5. **A short queue.** "You're the next caller in the queue," and a higher-than-usual-volume message. A consultant answered about 7 seconds later.
6. **The consultant asks for your first name, last name, email address, and whether you're the account holder.** This is the verification you'll need before they'll touch your membership.

Fabletics' help center adds a few details we didn't hear on the call: you can skip the month on the site (a "skip this month" window shows on your homepage between the 1st and 5th) or by calling, you can only skip one month at a time, and returns are accepted within 30 days of shipment, unworn with tags on.

## How to cancel a Fabletics membership by phone

1. **Call 1-844-322-5384** from the phone number on your Fabletics account. The assistant looks you up by it, which saves a step.
2. If it can't find you, **say the phone number on your account.**
3. **Ask to cancel your VIP membership.** If the assistant doesn't hand you to a person, ask for a consultant.
4. **Give your first name, last name and the email on the account,** and confirm you're the account holder.
5. **Ask about your unused VIP credits before you hang up.** You can keep spending them after you cancel (they expire 12 months after purchase), or ask for the refund our consultant described.
6. If it's between the 1st and the 5th and you haven't been charged yet, **skip the month too,** so the cancellation and the next charge don't cross.

## What to have ready

- **The phone number on your Fabletics account.** The automated assistant asks for it first.
- **Your first and last name and the email on the account.** The consultant asked for all three.
- **Confirmation that you're the account holder.** They asked, so calling for someone else may not work.
- **What you want done with unused credits,** if you're cancelling.

## Things that don't work

On October 2, 2026:

- **Asking general questions without an account.** The consultant answered the pricing questions, but wouldn't describe cancellation offers or the return policy without "an account that I can identify." Expect to be asked for your name and email before anything specific.
- **Calling from a number that isn't on your account.** The assistant can't find you and asks for the account's number. That's one extra step, not a dead end: saying we didn't have it, twice, got us transferred to a person.
